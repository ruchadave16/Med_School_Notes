*Humor has complete access to trabecular meshwork but ↑ intraocular pressure is from ↑ resistance to outflow in open angle*
### Etiology
- Aging, family history
- Nearsightedness
- Diabetes
- HTN
- Steroid use
### Pathophysiology
- ↑ resistance to aqueous humor flow -> ↑ IOP -> stress to optic nerve head
- Retinal ganglion cell apoptosis -> nerve damage and vision loss

### Clinical Features
- Asymptomatic initially
- Progressive **peripheral vision loss**
- Late stage -- tunnel vision
- ↑ IOP

### Diagnosis
- Peripheral vision testing
- OCT
- Imaging
	- Tonometry -- measure IOP
	- Ophthalmoscope:
![[Screenshot 2026-04-15 at 2.43.33 PM.png]]

### Treatment
- [[Prostaglandin]] analogs
- [[Beta blockers]]
- [[Alpha agonists]]
- Carbonic anhydrase inhibitors
- Cholinergic agonists

- Laser therapy -- enhance outflow

Surgery:
- Trabeculectomy
- Tube shunt surgery
### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
