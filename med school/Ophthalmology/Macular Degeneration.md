### Etiology
- Age related
- Smoking increases risk

### Pathophysiology
- Damage to [[macula]] causes progressive loss of central vision

1) Dry (atrophic)
	- Deposition of drusen on Bruchs membrane
2) Wet (neovascular)
	- Choroidal neovascularization under the RPE or between RPE and retina
	- New vessels are leaky -> hemorrhage -> fibrosis -> vision loss

### Clinical Features
- Severe vision loss (starting with central)

- Anti-VEGF for wet
