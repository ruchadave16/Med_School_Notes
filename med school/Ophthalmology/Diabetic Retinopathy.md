### Etiology
- [[Diabetes Mellitus]]
- **Most common cause of blindness in 20-40 y/o in US**

### Pathophysiology
- Non-proliferative:
	- ↑ vascular permeability -> macular edema
	- Capillary occlusions -> micro-infarcts
	- Thickened basement membranes
	- Loss of pericytes, retinal hemorrhages, hard exudates in outer plexiform layer, 
![[Screenshot 2026-04-15 at 3.11.14 PM.png]]

- Proliferative:
	- Advanced -> [[VEGF]] released to promote vascularization
	- New vessels are more leaky, more fragile, 
	- Must breach inner limiting membrane
