### Etiology
- Most common primary intraocular neoplasm in adults
- Correlation with BAP1 mutation
- Activating mutation in GNAQ or GNA11 genes
- Onset 50s or 60s
- Risk: light eye, fair skin
### Pathophysiology
- Choroidal melanocytes or stem cells
- Uveal nevi most common (10% of caucasian populations)
- Come up from choroid and can burst through Bruchs membrane

- Microscopic:
	- Spindle cells (elongated cells with taper on both sides)
	![[Screenshot 2026-04-15 at 3.40.14 PM.png]]
	- Epithelioid cells (polygonal cells)
	![[Screenshot 2026-04-15 at 4.01.13 PM.png]]
### Clinical Features
- Blurry vision (37%)
- Asymptomatic (30%)

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Globe preserving therapy: Plaque brachytherapy
- Surgery depending on growth
### Complications
- 50% will have metastases to liver (60%), lung (25%) soft tissue or bone
- 5 yr survival rate: 69%
