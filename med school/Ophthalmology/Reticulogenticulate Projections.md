- Ganglion cells send -> [[Optic Nerve|optic n.]] (converge at **optic disc**)
	- *Lesion: ↑ ICP causing [[papilledema]]*
- Optic n. enters skull through optic canal in sphenoid
	- Encased in dura and arachnoid
	- I/L information 
	- **Blood: [[ophthalmic a.]] <- [[Internal Carotid Artery|ICA]]**
	- *Lesion: I/L blindness and no pupillary reflex* 
		- Causes = [[optic neuritis]], central retinal a. occlusion, compression
- Some cross at optic chiasm
	- From medial retina cross, lateral retina do not
	- R visual field enters L optic tract
	- Brain receives C/L half of visual field
	- **Blood: AComm and 1st part of ACA**
	- *Lesion: [[bitemporal hemianopia]]*
		- Temporal visual fields of both eyes lost
		- Central part of chiasm is above ant. pituitary <- compression from tumors
		- AComm aneurysm
	- *Lesion: I/L [[nasal hemianopia]]*
		- ICA lateral to optic chiasm so aneurysm  affects lateral part
- Optic Tract goes around cerebral peduncles -> [[lateral genticulate nucl.]] in [[thalamus]]
	- Information from C/L FOV
	- Blood: anterior choroidal artery off of ICA
	- *Lesion: C/L [[homonymous hemianopia]]*
		- Tumor of temporal
		- Compression against cerebral peduncle
- Lateral genticulate nucl. in thalamus
	- Blood: [[Posterior Cerebral A.|PCA]]
	- *Lesion: C/L homonymous hemianopia*
