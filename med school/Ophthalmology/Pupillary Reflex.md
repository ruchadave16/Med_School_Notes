Afferent: [[Optic Nerve|CN II]]
Efferent: [[Oculomotor Nerve|CN III]]

**Direct reflex** and **consensual reflex**
#### Pathway
- Retina -> CN II to synapse in pretectal area of midbrain  -> [[Edinger-Westphal nucl.]] synapse
- Preganglionic -> [[Oculomotor Nerve|CN III]] -> ciliary ganglion
- Postganglionic -> [[iris]]
#### Lesions
- R optic n. 
	- Shine in R and neither constricts
	- Shine in L and both constrict
- R oculomotor n. 
	- Shine in R and only left constricts
	- Shine in L and only left constricts

- Anisocoria -- asymmetric 
	- [[Horner’s Syndrome]]
	- Tonic pupil
	- [[CN III palsy]]
	- Can be normal in 20% of population
- Blown pupil
	- Fixed and dilated, no rxn to light
	- Aneurysm, uncal herniation, drugs
- Marcus-Gunn Pupil
	- Dilates when light swung from unaffected to affected eye
	- Defect in direct response 
	- Optic neuritis, severe glaucoma, direct optic nerve damage, retinal detachment, retinal infection
- Tonic Pupil
	- Parasympathetic ciliary ganglionopathy
	- Damage to short ciliary n.
	- Dilated at normal light, no response to light in 1 eye
	- Constricts on near response but slow to redilate
