#### Dorsal 
- Pass in parietal through retrolenticular limb of internal capsule -> superior bank of calcarine sulci
- Blood: PCA
- *Lesion: C/L inferior quadrantanopia*
- "Pie on floor"

#### Ventral
- Loop over inferior horn of lateral ventricles into temporal lobe (Meyer's loop) -> inferior bank of calcarine sulci
- Blood: MCA
- *Lesion: C/L superior quadrantanopia*
