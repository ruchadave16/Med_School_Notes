### Etiology
- Most common primary ocular malignancy
- Tumor suppressor **RB** -> loss of 1 allele inherited in 40% of cases, need a 2nd hit
### Pathophysiology
- Small, round blue cells growing in sheets
- Flexner-Wintersteiner Rosettes: Cells lined up in a circle around an empty lumen
(no blood vessel) with fine cytoplasmic processes

![[Screenshot 2026-04-15 at 3.34.54 PM.png]]

### Clinical Features
- Leukocoria -- white pupillary reflex

![[Screenshot 2026-04-15 at 3.31.57 PM.png]]

- Strabismus -- both eyes don't look in the same direction
- ↓ vision
### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Chemotherapy to reduce size and ablation afterwards
- Removal of eye otherwise
### Complications
- Metastasis to brain, CNS, liver, bone
- Monitor for other common diseases
	- Osteosarcoma
	- Fibrosarcoma
	- Melanoma
