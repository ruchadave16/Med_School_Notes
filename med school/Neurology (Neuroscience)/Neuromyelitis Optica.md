### Etiology
- Risk
	- Autoantibody for **Aquaporin 4** -> seen in 70% of NMO cases
- Onset 40s and 50s
	- Women > men

### Pathophysiology
- Early destruction of perivascular [[astrocytes]] -> causes demyelination
- [[IL-6|IL 6]] is key here
- Pathology shows necrosis, similar to MS
	- Acute inflammation with neutrophils
### Clinical Features
- Bilat. optic neuritis
- Spinal cord lesions -- demyelination
- Much more severe and acute than [[Multiple Sclerosis|MS]]
