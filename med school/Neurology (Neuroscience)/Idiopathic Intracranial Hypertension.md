↑ [[ICP]] with no secondary cause

Risks: female, overweight, [[Vitamin A]], dural venous sinus stenosis

Symptoms:
- Intractable headache (worse laying down)
- ↑ ICP symptoms
- Pulsating tinnitus
- Increased opening pressure on lumbar puncture
