↑ [[ICP]] with no secondary cause

Risks: female, overweight, [[Vitamin A]], dural venous sinus stenosis
- [[Tetracyclins]], [[Lithium]], [[Oral contraceptives]]

Symptoms:
- Intractable headache (worse laying down)
- ↑ ICP symptoms
- Pulsating tinnitus
- Papilledema
- Transient blurred vision

Treatment:
- Increased opening pressure on lumbar puncture
- Removal of CSF
- Weight loss
- Carbonic anhydrase inhibitors, Loop diuretics
