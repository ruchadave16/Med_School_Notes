### Etiology
- Repetitive use injury- Office work, Metabolic- Hypothyroidism, diabetes, amyloidosis, pregnancy

### Pathophysiology
- Mechanism of disease
- Relevant pathways (e.g. immune, hormonal)

### Clinical Features
- Pain and tingling over palmar thumb, index, middle, and ½ ring finger
- Motor deficit: APB (Abductor Pollicus Brevis) weakness and atrophy at the later stage

### Diagnosis
- Phalen’s maneuver (Wrist flexion test) dorsum of the hands held together with wrists flexed for period of time, may acutely worsen symptoms
- Tinel’s Sign- Percussion over the carpal tunnel may elucidate symptoms
- NCS shows low conduction over [[carpal tunnel]]
### Treatment
- Wrist splint
- Carpal tunnel release

- Short-term
- Long-term
