### Signs
- **Miosis**
- **Respiratory depression**
	- ↓↓ RR
	- Shallow breathing
- **Low consciousness**
- Truncal rigidity
- Hypotension, bradycardia
### Management
1) AIRWAY and VENTILATION
	- Respiratory depression = #1 reason for death
2) Supportive care
	- Monitor oxygen, BP, HR
	- Supplemental oxygen if needed
	- [[IV fluids]]
3) [[Naloxone]]
	- Competitive antagonist
	- IV, IM, or intranasally
