### Etiology
- Small, asymptomatic strokes

### Pathophysiology
- Accumulation of strokes over time

### Clinical Features
- Subcortical deficits
- Step-wise decline
- Can have UMN findings

### Diagnosis
- Multiple white matter subcortical lesions
