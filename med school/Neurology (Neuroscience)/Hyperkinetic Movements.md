↑ movement (Loss of inhibition)

- Chorea -- jerking, purposeless movements
	- [[Huntingtons Disease]] or [[Wilson Disease|Wilsons disease]]
	- Drugs, toxins
	- Immune -- [[Systemic Lupus Erythematosus|SLE]]
	- Vascular damage
- Athetosis -- slow writhing movement of hands, feet
	- Striatum lesions, [[Huntingtons Disease]]
- Ballismus -- violent flinging movements, large amplitude
	- STN stroke
- Dystonia -- involuntary contractions that are sustained
	- Cervical dystonia
		- Most common
		- Idiopathic, genetic, or secondary condition 
	- Focal dystonia
		- Seen in repetitive movements
		- “Writer’s cramp”
- Myoclonus
	- Sudden muscle contraction or loss of tone
	- "shock / jolt"
- Tics 
	- Brief coordinated action that is involuntary and repeated with alternating contraction of agonist and antagonist
	- [[Tourette Syndrome]]
- [[Tremor]]
