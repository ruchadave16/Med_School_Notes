- Survival > 10 year
- Very commonly progresses to high grade tumors and almost always comes back eventually
- Better prognosis than astrocytomas
### Etiology
- IDH mutation
- Chromosomal deletion of 1p and 19q

### Pathophysiology
- Grade II or III based on mitosis activity

**Microscopy**
- Round uniform cells
- Perinuclear clear -- fried egg appearance
- abundant delicate capillary network: “Chicken Wire Vasculature”

![[Screenshot 2026-03-29 at 6.54.10 PM.png]]
### Clinical Features
- More superficial than astrocytomas, often frontal cortex
- Seizures
- Headaches
### Diagnosis
- Ill-defined mass with no enhancement at low levels
- Calcifications on CT

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle
