*Collecting center in [[brain]] for where information is processed and converted to signals for [[pituitary gland]]*

- Below thalamus, above substantia nigra

![[Screenshot 2026-04-12 at 12.15.47 AM.png]]
#### Vasculature
- Branches of [[Anterior Cerebral A.|anterior cerebral a.]], posterior communicating a., [[Posterior Cerebral A.|posterior cerebral a.]]
### Nuclei
- Anterior Nucleus
	- Anterior 
	- Heat dissipation -- sweating and vasodilation
- Preoptic Nucleus
	- Anterior
	- Thermoregulation
	- [[GnRH]] release
- Supraoptic Nucleus
	- Anterior
	- [[Vasopressin|ADH]] produced
- Paraventricular Nucleus
	- Anterior
	- [[Oxytocin]], [[Vasopressin|ADH]] made
	- Controls [[Pituitary Gland|pituitary]]
- Suprachiasmatic Nucleus
	- Anterior
	- [[Circadian rhythm]]
- Lateral Nucleus
	- Lateral zone
	- [[Hunger]]
- Ventromedial Nucleus
	- Medial
	- [[Satiety]]
- Dorsomedial Nucleus
	- Medial
	- Feeding, emotional behavior (rage)
- Arcuate Nucleus
	- Medial
	- Hormone release regulation
- [[Mammillary bodies]]
	- Posterior
	- [[Memory]]
- Posterior nucleus
	- Posterior
	- Heat conservation
