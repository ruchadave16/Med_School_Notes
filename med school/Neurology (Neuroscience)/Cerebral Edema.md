*Fluid accumulation in [[brain]]*
#### Cytotoxic Edema
*Increase in ICF secondary to neuronal, glial, or endothelial membrane injury*
- Seen in [[cerebral ischemia]] or [[hepatic encephalopathy]]
- Imaging
	- CT might not be visible, could be normal
		- Might see loss of gray-white differentiation
		- Cortical ribbon swells
	- MRI DWI
#### Vasogenic Edema
*Increase in ECF from [[BBB]] disruption and ↑ vascular permeability*
- Fluid moves to extracellular
- Causes
	- Brain tumor
	- Abscess
	- Contusions
	- Infarct / Hypoxia
- Imaging
	- [[Chest CT Scan|CT]]: **Grey-white differentiation maintained**
		- Edema in white matter
		- Extends finger like
		- Secondary effects like cytotoxic  -- effacement of cerebral sulci
	- [[MRI]]: Same as above but hyperintense T2 and FLAIR WITHOUT restricted diffusion
