>[!Warning] Red Flags
>- Acute onset -- vascular
>- New character 
>- New onset past age 50 -- malignancy
>- Worse with laying down, coughing, Valsalva -- ↑ ICP
>- Worse sitting up -- [[intracranial hypotension]]
>- Fever and chills -- infection
>- Neck stiffness, eye movement pain -- meningitis
>- Neuro signs -- intracranial pathology
>- Vision changes -- ↑ ICP
#### General

- [[Migraines|Migraine]]
#### Trigeminal Autonomic Cephalgias
-  [[Cluster Headache]]
- SUNCT
	- Stabbing cluster headaches but 1-600 seconds, up to 20 attacks a day
- Paroxysmal hemicrania 
	- 2-30 min in duration
	- Treatment: [[indomethacin]]
- Hemicrania Continua
	- Cluster headaches but > 3 months in duration
	- Treatment: [[indomethacin]]
