### Etiology
- Swelling of central canal due to cyst or cavity formation

### Pathophysiology
- Compression of anterior white commissure first
- Can expand to [[Ventral Horn|ventral gray horn]]
- If T1-T4, lateral horn impact
- Possible DCML

### Clinical Features
- Anterior white commissure -> bilateral loss of pain and temp for 2-3 levels
	- If at cervical region, cape distribution (C4 - C6)
	- NO loss of fine touch and vibration
- Ventral gray horn -> bilateral flaccid paralysis at level(late)
- Lateral horn if T1-T4 lesion -> [[Horner’s Syndrome]] (late)
- If DCML -> loss of fine touch and proprioception below lesion
- Spinothalamic tract -> descending sensory loss
