*Filtrate from blood formed by [[choroid plexus]]*

- Drains through arachnoid villi into [[Dural Venous Sinuses|dural venous sinuses]] to venous system
- Volume = 150 cc
- 500 cc made and drained daily

**Function**
- Shock absorber
- Delivers nutrients and takes waste
- Allows for [[ICP]] changes
#### Nature
- Clear = normal
	- Red = hemorrhage, cloudy = infection
#### Composition
99% water
Protein <40 mg/dL
Glucose 40-70 mg/dL
Cell count -- no RBC, barely any lymphocytes
pH = 7.3 - 7.4
Electrolytes
- K+ and Ca2+ < plasma
- Cl- > plasma
- Na+ and HCO3- similar
