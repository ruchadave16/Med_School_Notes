*Slowly progressing disease associated with cognitive disturbances and motor issues*
### Etiology
- HIV reaching CNS
### Pathophysiology
- HIV reaches CNS through [[microglia]], astrocytes can be infected too
- Neuronal loss
- Mononuclear cell infiltrates
- Microglial nodules + macrophage giant cells
- Gliosis
- Loss of myelin in subcortical areas
