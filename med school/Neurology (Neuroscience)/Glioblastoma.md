- Grade II: 6-8 years prognosis
- Grade IV: 9-12 month prognosis
### Etiology
- Secondary tumors: [[isocitrate dehydrogenase]] mutations (IDH) <- generally better prognosis

### Pathophysiology
- ↑ mitosis activity determines grading (between II and IV)
- Presence of [[necrosis]] or microvascular proliferation = Grade IV
- Primary appear as Grade IV vs Secondary start at lower and go up to Grade IV

**Micro**
- Giant cells
- Necrosis with palisading -- necrosis with tumor cells on periphery
- Microvascular proliferation
### Clinical Features
- Seizures
- Headache

### Diagnosis
- Ill-defined mass
	- Contrast enhancement = higher grade
	- Ring enhancing w central necrosis
