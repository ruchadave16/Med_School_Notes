### Etiology
- [[HSV1]] most common >>> HSV2
- [[Arbovirus]]

### Pathophysiology
- Inflammation / damage to brain parenchyma
	- Can also include meningitis
### Clinical Features
- Headache
- Altered mental status
- Fever

### Diagnosis
- If CSF looks like [[Aseptic Meningitis|viral meningitis]], suspect viral
- HSV encephalitis causes hemorrhage
	- RBC might be positive
- Antibodies to the virus -- IgM first

### Treatment
- HSV -- IV [[acyclovir]] (70% -> 19% mortality)
### Complications
- Mortality 70% without treatment of acyclovir
