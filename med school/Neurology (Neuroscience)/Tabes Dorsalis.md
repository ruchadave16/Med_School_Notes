### Etiology
- [[Syphilis]] -- late stage (months to years after)

### Pathophysiology
- Severe demyelination of posterior columns and dorsal root gangion

### Clinical Features
*DORSALIS*
- Dorsal column degeneration
- Orthopedic pain ([[Charcot Joint Disease]]) -- neuropathic arthropathy
- [[Reflexes]] ↓ (sensory loss)
- Shooting episodic pain 
- Argyll-Robertson pupils
- Locomotor ataxia
- Impaired proprioception
- Syphilis

### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- + [[CSF]] VDRL to rule in (specific but not sensitive)
	- + CSF treponemal test to rule out (sensitive but not specific)
- Imaging
