Smallest glial cells with small, elongated nuclei
- [[Macrophage]]-like
- Part of phagocytic system
- At rest, highly branched and amoeboid processes
- Active during injury
	- Prune unused synapses
- Derived from [[Mesoderm]] -- [[Yolk Sac]] or fetal [[Liver]]

![[Screenshot 2025-08-09 at 11.14.37 PM.png]]
#### Reaction to Injury
- Proliferation
- **Elongated nuclei**
- Aggregate around tissue necrosis -- microglial nodules

	![[Screenshot 2026-03-24 at 10.16.30 PM.png]]

- Congregate around dying neuron bodies -- neuronophagia
