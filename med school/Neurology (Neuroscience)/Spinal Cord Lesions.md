-  Complete transverse: Bilat. sensory loss of all at and below
- [[Syringomyelia]] or other central cord
	- No pain, sensation, motor weakness arm > leg, proprioception preserved
- [[Anterior Cord Syndrome]]
	- Bilat loss of pain and temp below lesion
- PSA Syndrome
	- I/L dorsal column and dorsal horn loss
- Hemisection ([[Brown Sequard Syndrome]])
	- I/L loss of all sensation and weakness (LMN) AT level
	- I/L UMN and vibration and proprioception loss BELOW level
	- C/L loss of pain and temp (2-3 levels below)
	- I/L Horner's if above T1
- [[Conus medullaris syndrome]]
	- Saddle anaesthesia, [[UMN Lesion]] and [[LMN Lesion]] symptoms
- [[Cauda Equina Syndrome]]
	- Saddle anaesthesia (might be unilat.), [[LMN Lesion]] findings
