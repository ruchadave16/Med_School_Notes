- 2 hemispheres (L and R)
- 5 lobes (frontal, parietal, temporal, occipital, limbic)

**Components**
- Gyrus -- bump
- Sulcus -- trough
- Fissure -- deep groove
#### Main Landmarks
- Central sulcus -- divides frontal and parietal lobe
- Lateral sulcus (Sylvian) -- divides temporal from frontal and parietal 
- Longitudinal fissure -- divides left and right
- Parieto-occipital sulcus -- (medial view) divides parietal and occipital

![[Screenshot 2026-03-18 at 10.33.54 PM.png]]

- Corpus Callosum: largest commissure in brain separating L and R
	- Rostrum (connects frontal)
	- Genu (connects frontal)
	- Body (connects parietal)
	- Splenium (connects occipital)
- Anterior commissural fibers (connects temporal)
- Fornix (hippocampus to hypothalamus)
- Septum pellucidum -- membrane separating 2 lateral vesicles

![[Drawing 2026-03-18 22.42.55.excalidraw]]

- [[Internal capsule]] -- white matter tract that brings info to and from cerebral cortex
	- Anterior limb 
	- Genu
	- Posterior limb -- sensory from thalamus to cerebrum

![[Drawing 2026-03-18 22.47.14.excalidraw]]

- [[Basal ganglia]] -- nuclei series that controls movement and behavior
- Corona radiata -- white matter with descending axons
### Frontal Lobe
- Function: Motor areas, personality, motivation, reasoning
- Gyri: Superior, middle, inferior frontal gyri
- Precentral gyrus has **[[primary motor cortex]]**

![[Screenshot 2026-03-18 at 10.36.12 PM.png]]
### Parietal Lobe
- Function: [[Somatic sensation]], awareness of self and surroundings
- Gyri: Postcentral gyrus (has **primary [[somatosensory cortex]]**), supramarginal gyrus, angular gyrus, etc

![[Screenshot 2026-03-18 at 10.38.30 PM.png]]
### Temporal Lobe
- Function = language, smell, hearing
- Gyri: Superior, middle, inferior temporal gyri, occipital temporal gyrus

![[Screenshot 2026-03-18 at 10.39.45 PM.png]]
### Occipital Lobe
- Visual processing
- Calcarine sulcus, lingual gyrus, cuneus

![[Screenshot 2026-03-18 at 10.40.18 PM.png]]
### Limbic Lobe
- Olfaction, emotion, memory
- Cingulate gyrus, parahippocampal gyrus, cingulate sulcus
- Uncus -- bump that is medial part of cerebrum

![[Screenshot 2026-03-18 at 10.41.22 PM.png]]
### Vasculature
- Lateral temporal, frontal, parietal -- [[Middle Cerebral A.]]
	- **Infarct** = C/L motor and sensory loss of **UE and face and aphasia**
- Medial frontal, parietal -- [[Anterior Cerebral A.]]
	- **Infarct** = C/L motor and sensory loss of **LE**
- Occipital and medial temporal -- [[Posterior Cerebral A.]]
	- **Infarct** = C/L **visual field loss**
	
![[Screenshot 2026-03-20 at 12.46.42 AM.png]]
#### Watershed Zones
- ACA - MCA: bilateral proximal arm and leg hemiparesis and aphasia
- MCA - PCA: higher order visual deficits
