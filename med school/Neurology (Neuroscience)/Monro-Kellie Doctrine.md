- Skull is rigid with fixed volume made of tissue, blood, and CSF
	- If volume of 1 increases, other 2 must decrease
	- First compensation by displacing CSF into spinal canal and ↑ absorption of CSF
	- Can also displace venous blood

**Important to understand [[ICP]]**
- ↑ ICP from edema, hemorrhage, tumor, hydrocephalus
- Elevated ICP can cause brain herniation
