*Involuntary, rhythmic and oscillatory movements from alternating contraction of agonist and antagonist muscles*

#### Resting Tremors
- Tremor at rest, resolve with movement
- [[Parkinson's Disease]]
- ↓ frequency, seen in hands
#### Action Tremors
- Positional
	- When body part maintained against gravity
	- Drug induced, disease based (hyperthyroid)
	- Worse with sympathetic stimulation
- Essential tremor
	- Genetic risk factor
	- Common in elderly
	- Postural in hands, can spread to head or voice
	- Seen with voluntary movement
	- Worse with movement, anxiety, caffeine
	- Improve with alcohol
	- Treatment: [[Propranolol]], [[primidone]]
- Intention tremor
	- Appear during action with goal oriented movement
	- ↑ amplitude during terminal part of movement
	- Cause = cerebellar lesions
- Flapping tremor
	- Flapping of hand when wrist extended on outstretched open hand
	- Brief interruption of sustained contraction
	- Causes:
		- Metabolic encephalopathy
		- [[Wilson disease]]
		- Metabolic diseases
- Function Tremor
	- Distractable
	- Acute onset
	- Conversion disorder
