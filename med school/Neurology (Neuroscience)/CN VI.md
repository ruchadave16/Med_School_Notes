Motor nerve -- lateral rectus n. (EOM)

*Lesion: I/L abducens palsy, eye drift medially*
*Lesion of nucleus: I/L horizontal gaze palsy -- *
### Pathway
- Nucleus in [[pons]] internal to facial colliculus -> path in pink through tegmentum and basilar pons
- Through medial leminscus and CST
- Leaves brain at middle pontomedullary junction
- Subarachnoid path on surface of basilar pons -> pontine cistern -> Dorello canal -> cavernous sinus -> superior orbital fissure

*If ↑ [[ICP]], CN VI stretched at Dorello canal -> abduction weakness*
