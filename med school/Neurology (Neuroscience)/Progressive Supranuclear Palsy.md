### Etiology
- Onset = 60s
- Sporadic

### Pathophysiology
- Atrophy of midbrain, pontine tegmentum, superior cerebellar peduncle
- Tau + inclusions in neurons

### Clinical Features
More bilat. symptoms
- Unsteadiness and frequent falls -- fall backwards
- Rigidity and axial dystonia
- [[Ophthalmoplegia]]
	- Difficulty with vertical eye movement
	- Doesn't show till 1 yr after other symptoms
### Diagnosis
**Commonly mistaken for [[Parkinson's Disease|Parkinson's]]**
- Labs
- Imaging
	- Atropy of midbrain much more than expected for Parkinson's

- Criteria

- Pharmacologic
