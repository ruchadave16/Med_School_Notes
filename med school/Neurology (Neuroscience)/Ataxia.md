#### Cerebellar Ataxia
- Intact strength and sensation but impaired coordination
- Incoordination EVEN WITH eyes open AND closed
- Symptoms I/L to lesion
- **Limb Ataxia**
	- Cerebellar hemispheres and paravermis damaged
	- ↓ coordination to distal limbs
	- Dysmetria, intention tremor, dysdiadochokinesia
- **Truncal Ataxia**
	- Midline vermis damage
	- Can't maintain posture
	- ↓ proximal limb damage
	- I/L falling to that side
- **Gait Ataxia**
	- Wide based unsteady gait
#### Sensory Ataxia
- ↓ proprioceptive input because of posterior column damage
- ↓ ankle reflex
- Incoordination **BETTER** with eyes, worse without
- + Romberg sign

#### Vestibular Ataxia
- Vertigo + nausea
- Nystagmus
- Gait instability but no limb dysmetria
- Worse symptoms with head movement
