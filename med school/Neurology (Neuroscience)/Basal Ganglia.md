*Group of nuclei in cerebrum, diencephalon, and midbrain that ensure smooth movement without completing movement*

- Works by disinhibition, NO synapse on LMN
- *Lesion* = 
	- [[Hyperkinetic Movements]]
	- [[Hypokinetic Movements]] 
### Parts
- Caudate nucleus
	- Works with frontal lobe
	- On lateral wall of lateral ventricles
	- Input from [[Primary Motor Cortex|motor cortex]]
	- [[GABA]] -> globus pallidus
	- Blood:
		- head and body -- medial striate
		- tail = anterior choroidal a. 
- Putamen
	- Lateral part of basal ganglia
	- Combines with caudate through [[nucleus accumbens]]
	- [[GABA]] -> globus pallidus
	- Blood: lateral striate a.
- Globus Pallidus
	- Medial to putamen
	- Striatum inputs
	- TONIC FIRING GABAerngric (-) neurons
		- Internal segment (GPi): Output to VA/VL of thalamus
		- External segment (GPe): Output to STN
	- Blood: GPi = anterior choroidal a. GPe = lateral striate
- Nucleus accumbens
	- Input = prefrontal cortex -- dopamine
- Subthalamic nucleus
	- Behind substania nigra
	- Glutamatergic neurons
	- Blood: branches PCA
- Substania Nigra
	- Rostral midbrain
	- Dopaminergic neurons
	- Compact Part
		- Output to striatum; Input from striatum
		- Movement
	- Reticular Part
		- Input: striatum
		- Output: [[superior colliculus]], thalamus, striatum
		- Controls head and eye movement
	- Blood: [[Basilar A.]]

### Function
- Works by disinhibition -- BG normally inhibiting movement so when something inhibits it, movement is allowed
- Pathway
	- Cortex -> basal ganglia -> [[thalamus]] -> cortex
	- **Input**: cortex -> striatum
	- Interconnection: striatum -> pallidum 
	
Direct Pathway: facilitates movement
- Striatum -> (inhibit) GPi -> (releases break) thalamus -> cortex
- 2 inhibitory

Indirect Pathway: inhibits movement
- Striatum -> (inhibit) GPe -> (inhibit) STN -> (excites) GPi -> thalamus -> cortex
- 3 inhibitory

Substantia nigra -- excites direct by releasing dopamine onto D1, inhibits indirect by releasing dopamine onto D2
