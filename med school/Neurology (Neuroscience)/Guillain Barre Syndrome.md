### Etiology
- [[Campylobacter Jejuni|Campylobacter]]
- [[Influenza Virus|Influenza]]
- [[Cytomegalovirus|CMV]]
- [[EBV]]
- [[Mycoplasma Pneumoniae]]

### Pathophysiology
- Demyelinating polyneuropathy -> Progressive weakness over days->weeks
	- Immune system attacks [[myelin]]
	- Segmental demyelination
- Usually preceded by illness up to 2 weeks before hand
### Clinical Features
- **Ascending** weakness
- Flaccid paralysis
- Absent reflexes early on
### Diagnosis
- CSF shows (not needed)
	- Albuminocytologic dissociation -- Normal cells but high proteins
- Bilat. symptoms, weakness and ↓ reflexes, acute onset
- Nerve conduction (not needed)
	- Shows slowing
### Treatment
- Supportive care
- Plasmapheresis -- clean plasma of antibodies
- IV immunoglobulin
- Ventilation if needed
### Complications
- **Respiratory paralysis causes death**
