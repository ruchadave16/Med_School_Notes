*Innervates skin of face – cutaneous*
- **Sensory**: touch, proprioception, vibration, pain, temp of face, mouth, anterior 2/3 tongue, nasal sinuses, supratentorial dura mater
- **Motor**: [[Chewing|mastication]] muscles

>[!Warning] 
>[[Mandible]] = great auricular n. (C2-3)

**Location**
- Sensation through [[trigeminal ganglion]] (NO synapse here)
	- In Meckel's cave posterior to cavernous sinus
	- Motor information NOT here
- Exits brain between lateral basillar pons and middle cerebellar peduncle

1) [[Ophthalmic Nerve]] (CN V1)
2) [[Maxillary Nerve|Maxillary n.]] (CN V2)
3) [[Mandibular Nerve|Mandibular n.]] (CN V3)
#### Nuclei:
**Blood Supply: AICA**
- Chief sensory trigeminal nuc. 
	- Middle [[pons]] lateral to motor nuc.
	- Fine touch, pressure synapses, 2nd axons cross ascend to VPM in thalamus → Primary somatosensory
- [[Spinal trigeminal tract]]
	- Lateral caudal pons
	- Temp, pain for face, tongue, pharynx
- Motor nuc. V
	- Middle pons
	- Input from bilat. [[corticobulbar tract]]
	- *Lesion: LMN means I/L jaw deviation when opening, UMN means no symptoms*

>[!Clinical]
>[[Trigeminal Neuralgia]]
>- Maxillary or mandibular affected
>- Intense stabbing (1-2 min), generally unilateral
>- Attack from just sensation
