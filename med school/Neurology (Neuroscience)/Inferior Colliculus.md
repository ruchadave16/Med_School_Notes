*Gray matter for integration of sound and reflexes*

- Dorsal side of caudal [[midbrain]] 
- Inputs
	- Superior olivary nucl – spatial information
	- Dorsal cochlear nucl – frequency/pitch information
	- Ventral cochlear nucl – intensity/loudness
- Output
	- Tectospinal tract -- moves head towards sound
	- Medial geniculate nucl. -- relay to the cerebrum to transverse temporal gyrus
#### Sound Attenuation Reflex
- Superior olivary nucl. -> CN VII or CN V3 -> [[middle ear]] muscles
- Attenuation: stapedius contracts in response to loud sounds
- Tympanic: in anticipation of loud sounds, both tensor tympani and stapedius contract
