*Protective mechanism against ↑↑ tension*
#### Pathway
- ↑ tension causes [[Golgi Tendon Organ|GTO]]to be stimulated
- Ib afferent -> DRG to spinal cord -> synapse on GABAergic neurons -> inhibit alpha motor neurons -> ↓ signal along LMN
- Relaxation of muscle AND stimulates antagonist muscle to contract

>[!Clinical] Clasp-Knife Response
>- Seen in [[UMN Lesion]]
