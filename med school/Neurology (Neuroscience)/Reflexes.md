*Involuntary and instantaneous reaction to a stimulus that does not use conscious thought*

![[Screenshot 2026-03-31 at 6.20.06 PM.png]]

#### Types
 - Superficial reflexes -- stroking skin
	 - [[Plantar reflex]]
 - [[Deep tendon reflexes]] -- quick stretch of muscle
 - [[Primitive reflexes]] -- In infants but disappear in 1 year
 - [[Golgi tendon reflex]] -- elicited by tension in muscle
 - [[Flexor Reflex]] -- elicited by pain
 - Visceral reflexes -- elicited by change to stimulus
