*Recurrent headache disease with moderate - severe pain and neurologic + autonomic symptoms*
### Etiology
- More common in women
### Pathophysiology
- Activation of parts of the brain involved with pain modulation
- Cortical spreading depression and inhibition across cerebral cortex

1) Vascular Hypothesis 
	- Intracranial vasoconstriction -> aura and then extracranial
	- Mainly disproved
2) Neuronal Dysfunction Hypothesis
	- Cortical spreading depolarization
	- Associated with aura
3) Neurovascular Hypothesis (*Current*)
	- Trigger turns on trigeminovascular -> Calcitonin gene-related peptide ([[CGRP]]),  Substance P, Neurokinin A
	- Pain path: trigeminal nucleus caudalis -> thalamus -> cerebral cortex
### Clinical Features

>[!Note] https://ichd-3.org/1-migraine/1-1-migraine-without-aura/

- Throbbing head pain (pulsing) -- generally unilateral but can be bilateral
	- Retroorbital or occipital
- Lasts 4-72 hours
- Aggravation by routine physical activity, such as climbing stairs or bending over
- Nausea (90%)
- Vomiting (30-40%)
- Photophobia
- Phonophobia
- Sensitivity to movement

**Migraine Phases**
1. Prodrome (hours–days before headache)
2. Aura (in ~25–30% of patients)
3. Headache phase
4. Postdrome

**Categories**
- With Aura (~30%)
	- Flashing lights
	- Zigzag patterns (fortification spectra)
	- Scintillating scotoma (shimmering spots or lines)
	- Scotoma (blind spots)
	- Hemianopsia (loss of vision in one-half of the visual field)
- Without Aura
### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria
### Treatment
- Avoid triggers
- Sleep, meals, exercise regularly
- ↓ Caffeine and smoking
- Cold packs to neck and head

>[!Warning] Do stroke workup before starting triptans

**Acute Therapy**
- [[NSAIDs]]
- [[Triptans]] -- 5-HT1B/D selective
- [[Ergot Alkaloids]] 
- [[Ditans]] -- 5-HT1F selective
- [[Gepants]] -- CGRP signalling antagonists

**Additional Side Therapies**
- [[Antiemetics]]
- [[Synthetic Corticosteroids]] -- [[Dexamethasone]] for prolonged migraine > 72 hours
- [[Opioids]] -- [[Butorphanol]] for refractory cases

**Prophylactic**
- > 4 migraine days a month
- Severe attacks that reduce quality of life
- [[Medication Overuse Headache]]

1) [[Beta blockers]] -- 1st line
	- [[Propranolol]]
	- [[Metoprolol]]
	- [[Timolol]]
2) [[Antiepileptic Drugs]] -- 1st line
	- [[Topiramate]]
	- [[Valproate]]
3) [[Antidepressants]]
	- [[Amitriptyline]]
	- [[Venlafaxine]]
4) [[CGRP Monoclonal Antibodies]]
5) [[Clostridium Botulinum|Botulinum]]
6) Angiotensin II blockers -- [[Candesartan]]
#### Role of [[Serotonin]] in Therapeutics
5-HT1D and 5-HT1F = presynaptic on trigeminal sensory
- Release calcitonin gene-related peptide ([[CGRP]]), Substance P, Neurokinin A
- ↓ inflammation and pain transmission

5-HT1B on cranial blood vessels
- Vasoconstriction
- Counters vasodilation of migraine
