### Etiology
- Primary causes
- 1 in 1000 people
	- Women 2x more than men
	- Diagnosis in 20s or 30s, peak at 35
- Genetics
	- 15x more likely if present in 1st degree relative
	- 150x ↑ risk if monozygotic twin
	- DR2 haplotype in MHC
	- IL2 and IL7 SNPs
- Environment
	- High latitude
	- ↓ [[Vitamin D]]
	- Tobacco smoke
	- Obesity
	- [[EBV]]

### Pathophysiology
- Demyelinating disorder with lesions (white matter damage) separated by time and space
- [[Th1]] -> IFN-y -> activates macrophages
- [[Th17 Cells]] -> recruit leukocytes
- Immune system attacks [[oligodendrocytes]] -> demyelination
	- Repeated attacks cause scarring -- schlerotic plaques
- Damage to nerve fibers underneath

![[Screenshot 2026-03-28 at 12.55.03 PM.png]]

**Pathology**
- White matter lesions that are depressed, gray, irregularly shaped
- Microscopic
	- myelin stain shows loss of myelin
	- "Active" plaques have infiltration by lipid laden macrophages and lymphocytes
	- "Inactive" have no macrophages but reactive astrocytosis
### Clinical Features
- [[UMN Lesion]] mixed motor and sensory
	- U/L [[optic neuritis]] <- 10-50% end up getting MS
	- Vision loss
	- Eye movement pain
	- Loss of color vision
	- Flashing lights
- [[INO]] (Internuclear ophthalmoplegia)
- Fatigue
- Sensory disturbances -- ascending numbness
- Ataxia
- Electric shock down spine and hands when neck is flexed -- Lhermitte’s sign
### Diagnosis
- Clinical
	- **>1 neurologic episode separated in time and space**
- Imaging
	- MRI shows white matter plaques (↑ intensity on T2 and low on T1)
	- Below is Dawson's Fingers -- FLAIR intensities sticking up from ventricles
- [[CSF]]
	- **Oligoclonal bands**: ↑ [[IgG]] with non-clonal pattern
![[Screenshot 2026-03-28 at 1.05.06 PM.png]]

**Differential**
[[Neuromyelitis optica]] (NMO), [[Acute Disseminated Encephalomyelitis]] (ADEM- Monophasic), [[Myelin oligodendrocyte glycoprotein antibody disorder]] (MOG)
