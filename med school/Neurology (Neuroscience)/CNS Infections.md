**Categories**
- Meningitis -- meninges infection

- Cerebritis -- inflammation of brain parenchyma (non-viral infections)

#### Routes
- Hematogenous
- Direct implant -- trauma, iatrogenic (post op), local extension
- Migration
	- [[Rabies Virus]] and [[HHV3|varicella-zoster virus]]  -- retrograde from nerves in periphery
	- [[Corynebacterium Diphtheria|Diphtheria]] -- peripheral and cranial neuropathies
	- [[Clostridium Tetani]] -- tetanus toxin
	- [[Clostridium Botulinum]] -- botulinum toxin
	- [[Shigella]] 
	- [[Legionella Pneumophilia]]
