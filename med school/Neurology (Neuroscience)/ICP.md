*Pressure in intracranial / sinus space*

>[!Note] Normal = 4-20 mmHg or 5-25 cmH2O in lateral position
### Etiology
**Differential**
- Tumor, hematoma
- Non-communicating hydrocephalus
- Communicating hydrocephalus
- Venus sinus thrombosis
- [[Idiopathic Intracranial Hypertension]]
### Pathophysiology
- Mechanism of disease
- Relevant pathways (e.g. immune, hormonal)
### Clinical Features
**Symptoms**

>[!Warning] THIS IS A MEDICAL EMERGENCY

- Headache, nausea, vomiting
	- Worse with Valsalva, laying down, worse in morning
- [[Papilledema]]
- Somnolence
- Altered mental status
- Vision loss and pupillary reflex loss
	- [[CN VI]] stretched so can have diplopia
	- Pathognomonic for ↑ ICP
- Diplopia
- Unequal pupils
- **Cushing's reflex** -- HTN, bradycardia, irregular respirations
### Diagnosis

### Treatment
- Raise bed 
- Hyperventilation
- [[Mannitol]]
- Hypertonic saline
- Clinicians must avoid lumbar puncture in the presence of midline shift or obstructive hydrocephalus
- **Decompressive craniectomy**
