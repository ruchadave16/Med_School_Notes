*Pressure in intracranial / sinus space*

>[!Note] Normal = 5-15 mmHg

### Increased ICP
- ↓ blood flow so ischemia
- herniation

**Symptoms**

>[!Warning] THIS IS A MEDICAL EMERGENCY

- Headache, nausea, vomiting
- [[Papilledema]]
- Somnolence
- Altered mental status
- Vision loss and pupillary reflex loss
- Diplopia
- Unequal pupils
- **Cushing's reflex** -- HTN, bradycardia, irregular respirations

**Treatment**
- Raise bed 
- Hyperventilation
- [[Mannitol]]
- Hypertonic saline
