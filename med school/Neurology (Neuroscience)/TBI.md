*Change to normal brain function*

Closed head TBI vs penetrating TBI
### Incidence
- ~2-3 million per year
- >50,000 deaths per year
- 50% visits related to alcohol, 2.5 million visits to ED

- Unintentional falls = #1 cause
- Struck in head and motor vehicle accidents
### Diagnosis
- Glasgow Coma Score
- Imaging
	- CT = first line
	- MRI 
### Types
- [[Concussion]]
	- Mild cuz not acute danger but can have awful effects
	- Trauma from quick acceleration / deceleration
- [[Diffuse Axonal Injury]]
	- Rotation or acceleration / deceleration -> distortion of axons
	- Microscopic damage
- [[Intracranial Hemorrhages]]
- [[Contusion]]
	- Superficial hemorrhage
	- Coup = at site of injury
	- Contrecoup = opposite side from rebound
	- Found on surface of gyri
- Laceration
	- Damage depends on area and energy transfer
	- Energy transferred outward
### Complications
- Post-traumatic [[seizures]]
	- Common
	- Within 1 week of injury
	- Can turn into epilepsy
- [[Hydrocephalus]]
- [[Meningitis]]
- Vascular damage
- [[Headache|Headaches]]
- [[Vertigo]]
- [[Addiction]]
- [[Chronic Traumatic Encephalopathy]]
	- Multiple tbis causing potential aggession and depression
