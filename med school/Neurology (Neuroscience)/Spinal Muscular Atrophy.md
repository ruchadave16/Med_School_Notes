### Etiology
- Genetic: autosomal recessive from defect in SMN1 gene
- **Leading genetic cause of infant mortality** (1/6000)
	- 1/50 in US = Carriers
	- Newborn screening in 36 states

### Pathophysiology
- Neurodegenerative affecting [[LMN Lesion]] only
- Lost both copies of SMN 1 gene -- the more SMN2 genes you have, the better off you are (3 max)
- ![[Screenshot 2026-04-08 at 11.22.17 PM.png]]

### Clinical Features
- Type 1 < 6 months
- Type 2 onset 6 month - 18 month
- Type 3 onset 18 month -> 17 year

- Proximal muscle weakness and atrophy

	- Antisense oligonucleotide (Nusinersen, Risdiplam)
