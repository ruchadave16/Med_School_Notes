*Unpaired artery on [[pons]] that comes off of [[Vertebral Artery|vertebral a.]]*

**Branches**
- Anterior inferior cerebellar a. (AICA)
	- Around middle cerebellar peduncle
	- Lateral cerebellum, dorsolateral pons, middle cerebellar peduncle
	- Labyrinthine aa -> [[Inner Ear]]
- Pontine aa. 
	- Ventral medial pons
- Superior cerebellar a. 
	- Inferior to CN III
	- Superior cerebellum, rostral pons, caudal part of midbrain

Bifurcates into [[posterior cerebral a.]]
