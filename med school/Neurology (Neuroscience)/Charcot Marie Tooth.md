**Most common inherited neuropathy**
### Etiology
- Duplication of PMP22 gene

### Pathophysiology
- Deficient myelination

### Clinical Features
- Weakness
- Muscle atrophy
- Sensory loss
- **High arch and hammertoes**
- Onset in teenage years
