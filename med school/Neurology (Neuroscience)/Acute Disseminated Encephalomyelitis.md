### Etiology
- Most often follows viral infection or rarely viral immunization
	- Used to be mostly after measles
	- Now mainly after URI reflection
- Children and young adults 
	- 1-2 weeks after event
	- M slightly more than F

### Pathophysiology
- Believed to be T-cell mediated hypersensitivity rxn
- Swelling of brain, **vasculo-centric demyelination**
- Perivascular microhemorrhages common

![[Screenshot 2026-03-28 at 1.26.57 PM.png]]
### Clinical Features
- Headache + meningeal signs
- Fever
- Lethargy
- Rapid progression -> coma

### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- CSF: ↑ cell count but NO oligoclonal bands
- Imaging
	- Widespread T2 white matter lesions
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- Mortality = ~20%
- Long term neuro deficits in survivors
