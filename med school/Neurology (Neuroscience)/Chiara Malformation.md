### Etiology
- Congenital, can be acquired
### Pathophysiology
- Cerebellum herniates through foramen magnum
### Clinical Features
- Chiara I: 
	- Cerebellar tonsil herniation
	- [[Syringomyelia]] due to compression of cervical cord
- Chiara II:
	- Tonsil and vermis of cerebellum AND medulla herniate
	- [[Myelomeningocele]] in lumbosacral region

- Occipital headaches that radiate worse with cough
- Cerebellar ataxia
- Nystagmus
- ↑ ICP
### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- Noncommunicating hydrocephalus
- Breathing and HR issues

### High-Yield Associations
- 1:1000 live births
