*Separates blood from CNS extracellular fluid*
- Formed by capillary endothelial cells with [[tight junctions]]
- [[Astrocytes]] end feet
- Continuous [[basement membrane]] with pericytes
- Stops diffusion of macromolecules into brain tissues

>[!Note] Incomplete in neonates
>- Unconjugated [[bilirubin]] can penetrate brain tissue and stain deep nuclei -- kernicterus
#### Drug Permeability
- Must be **highly lipophilic** or use transporters, cannot be substrate of [[P glycoprotein]]

>[!Example]
>- First Generation Antihistamines
>	- Lipophilic: pass and cause drowsiness
>	- 2nd gen are less and cause minimal effects
>- Glucose and essential AA
>	- Cross using transporters
>- [[L DOPA]]
>	- Precursor to dopamine, crosses using AA transporter
#### Exception
Circumventricular organs (CVOs) -- near wall of 3rd and 4th ventricle
- Neurohypophysis (post. pituitary)
- Area Postrema
	- Part of [[vomiting center]], detects toxins through chemoreceptor trigger zone
- Regions that Secrete Neurohormones
	- Hypothalamus
	- Pineal Gland

>[!Warning] Disrpution
>High BP, TBI, [[stroke]], neoplasm, infections
>[[Mannitol]]
>
>Can cause edema, mental confusion, convulsions, coma
