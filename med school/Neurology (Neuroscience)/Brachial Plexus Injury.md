- Radial n. 
	- Axilla -- [[Saturday Night Palsy|Saturday night palsy]]
	- Humerus -- Lose wrist extension and sensory to dorsal hand
	- [[Posterior interosseous syndrome]] 
	- [[Wartenberg's Syndrome]] -- superficial radial n. 

- Median n.
	- [[Carpal Tunnel Syndrome]] or wrist laceration
	- [[Anterior interosseous syndrome]]
	- [[Pronator Teres Syndrome]]

- Ulnar n.
	- [[Cubital Tunnel Syndrome]]
	- [[Guyon Canal Syndrome]]

- Musculocutaneous n. 
	- Damaged due to upper trunk compression or at coracobrachialis
	- Motor: weak flexion of elbow, low biceps reflex
	- No sensory lateral forearm
	
- Axillary n.
	- Most commonly damaged with fracture of surgical neck of [[humerus]] or [[shoulder dislocation]]
	- Cannot abduct arm past 15
	- No sensation over lateral surface of arm

#### Plexus Injury
Plexopathy = disorder of brachial, lumboscarla plexus
- [[Erb-Duchenne Palsy]]
- [[Klumpke's Palsy|Klumpke’s Palsy]]
- [[Thoracic Outlet Syndrome]]
