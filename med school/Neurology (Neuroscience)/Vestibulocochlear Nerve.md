- Vestibular division
	- 4 nuclei
	- Signals retrograde from [[vestibule]] and [[Semicircular Ducts|semicircular ducts]] through internal auditory meatus to cranial cavity
	- Vestibulothalamic path -- bilat to thalamus -> cerebral cortex
	- Vestibulocerebellar path -- through inferior cerebellar peduncle -> I/L flocculonodular lobe
	- Vestibuloreticular path -- to reticular formation for autonomous regulation
	- Vestibulospinal -- lateral for I/L to extensors, medial bilat to head and neck
	- Vestibulonuclear -- synapse for [[vestibuloocular reflex]]

Function: Sensory for [[hearing]] and vestibular
