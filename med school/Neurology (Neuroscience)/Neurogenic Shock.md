### Etiology
- Primary causes
- Risk factors

### Pathophysiology
- ↓ MAP and perfusion from spinal cord injury -> loss of vasomotor tone to peripheral

### Clinical Features
- ↓BP + bradycardia 
- Warm extremities from no peripheral vasoconstriction
- Motor and sensory deficits

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
	- Fracture / injury to spinal cord
- Criteria

### Treatment
- [[IV Fluids]]: MAP goals of 85 to 90 mmHg for 5 to 7 days should be considered
- [[Norepinephrine]] / [[Phenylephrine]] only if hypovolemia excluded

- Short-term
- Long-term
