**Course**
- Exits through hypoglossal canal
- Passes deep to [[mandible]]
- Supplied by ASA

**Function**
- Somatic motor to [[tongue]] intrinsic and extrinsic muscles (movement of tongue)
### Path
- Medulla (fibers lateral -> medial leminscus and CST)
	- *Damage with ASA lesion*
- Preolivary sulcus -> hypoglossal canal -> floor of mouth

**Motor Nuclei**
- Hypoglossal
	- Rostral (open medulla near midline)
- Input from C/L [[Corticobulbar Tract|corticobulbar tract]]

*Lesion*
- LMN: tongue towards damaged side *Lick your wounds*
- UMN: deviate **away** from damaged side but towards limb weakness side (before crossing)
