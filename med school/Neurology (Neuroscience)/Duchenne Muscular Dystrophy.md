### Etiology
- Mutation of DMD gene that makes [[dystrophin]]
	- 8-10% heterozygous females show symptoms
		- Skewed X inactivation (DMD/WT) ← only express mutant
		- Uniparental isodisomy (DMD/DMD) ← both copies from mother
		- New germline mutation 
	- Weaker mutation = Intermediate MD and Becker MD
- 1/5000 live male births
### Pathophysiology
- Onset 5 years
- Loss ambulation 13 years
### Clinical Features
- Weakness progresses, loss of muscle
- Impairment in respiratory and cardiovascular function

Year 1:
- Proximal muscle weakness, leg before arms
- EOM sparing
- Short stature

Year 2:
- Clumsiness and falls constantly
- Gower's sign -- **using hands to rise up and climb up body**
- Waddling gait
- Scoliosis
### Diagnosis
- 20x HIGHER creatine kinase (>800)
- + Gower's sign
- Genetic testing -- gold standard

### Treatment
- [[Synthetic Corticosteroids|Corticosteroids]] -- slows decline
- PT and rehab
- By 18, need ventilation at night
- Many gene therapy options

### Complications
- Death by 19 years from respiratory and cardiac failure
