### Etiology
- Crossed legs, leg cast, fibular neck fracture

### Pathophysiology
- Compression of peroneal n. at fibular head

### Clinical Features
- Motor weakness of foot dorsiflexion and eversion
- **Difference from [[L5 Radiculopathy]], where inversion and eversion are affected**
- No sensation at bottom of foot
