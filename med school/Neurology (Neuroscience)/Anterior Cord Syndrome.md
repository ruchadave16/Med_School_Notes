### Etiology
- ASA infarct
- Trauma
- Vertebral burst fracture
### Pathophysiology
- Initial [[Spinal Shock|spinal shock]]: flaccid paralysis below lesion but UMN symptoms few days later

### Clinical Features
- Bilat. loss of pain and temperature below lesion
- LMN loss at level of lesion
- UMN loss below level of lesion
- Incontinence
### Diagnosis
- **Post. column** preserved -- vibration proprioception intact

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- Watershed infarct

- Classic buzzwords
- Boards clues
