*Impairment of [[CSF]] flow in or outside ventricles causing dilation*
- ↑ CSF causes expansion of unfused skull in infants
#### Communicating Hydrocephalus
- Flow of CSF blocked **after** ventricles
- All 4 ventricles enlarged
- Causes: SAH, infection, inflammation, post meningitis
- Result: ICP ↑, papilledema, herniation

#### Noncommunicating Hydrocephalus
- Block **in** ventricles
- Causes: tumors, cyst blocking foramen of Monroe, hemorrhage, scarring post meningitis, [[Dandy-Walker Syndrome]], [[Chiari Malformation]]
	- MOST COMMON: aqueduct stenosis
#### [[Normal Pressure Hydrocephalus]]
- Chronically dilated ventricles
- No change to subarachnoid space volume, compression of corona radiata
- Enlarged lateral or 3rd ventricle
- Cause: trauma, infection, surgery, idiopathic
- **Symptoms**:
	- Urinary incontinence
	- Progressive cognitive decline
	- Gait ataxia
