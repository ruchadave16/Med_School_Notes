- Myxopapillary ependymoma -- Grade I
- Subependymoma -- Grade I
- Regular ones are Grade II or III

Grade II: 60-70% 5 year survival
### Etiology
- [[Neurofibromatosis Type 2]] association
- Alterations on Chr22

### Pathophysiology
- 2-16 yrs -- posterior fossa (floor of 4th ventricle)
- 30-40 yrs -- supratentorial

**Micro**
- Perivascular Pseudorosettes: tumor cells around blood vessel

### Clinical Features
- [[Obstructive hydrocephalus]]
- Headache
- Seizure
- Spinal cord symptoms
