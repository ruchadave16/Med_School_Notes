#### [[Brainstem Development]]

*Lesion*:
- If [[Corticospinal Tract|CST]] signs (C/L UMN symptoms) and I/L CN signs (facial), brainstem lesion

Midline Features (4M)
- CN 3, 4, 6, 12 motor nuclei
- Medial leminscus -- C/L loss of fine touch and proprioception in arm and leg
- Medial longitudinal fasciculus -- I/L INO (internuclear ophthalmoplegia)
- Motor pathway (CST) -- C/L weakness in arm and leg

Lateral (4S for Side)
- Sympathetic pathway -- I/L Horner's
- Spinocerebelllar pathway -- I/L ataxia of arm and leg
- Spinothalamic pathway -- C/L loss of pain and temp of arm and leg
- Sens. nuclei of CN V -- I/L loss of pain and temp of face

- All other motor nuclei (5, 7, 9, 10, 11) are lateral
- Sensory are most lateral (5, 7, 8, 9, 10)
