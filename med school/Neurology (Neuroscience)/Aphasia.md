*Deficit in [[language]] development acquired after injury*

>[!Note] Dysarthria = difficulty speaking from muscle weakness

#### Causes
- Stroke
- Infectious disease
- Tumor
- Toxins
- Hydrocephalus
- Nutrition
- Metabolic disorders

6 TESTS – spontaneous speech, naming, comprehension, repetition, reading, writing

- [[Wernicke's Aphasia]]
- [[Broca's Aphasia]]
- [[Conduction Aphasia]]
- Global Aphasia = damage to all
- [[Transcortical Sensory Aphasia]]
- [[Transcortical Motor Aphasia]]
- [[Transcortical Mixed Aphasia]]
