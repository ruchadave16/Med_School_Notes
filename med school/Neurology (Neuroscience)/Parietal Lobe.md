#### [[Somatosensory Cortex]]
#### Posterior Parietal Cortex
- R side gets bilat. information
- L only gets C/L information
- Integrates tactile somatosensory info to understand objects in space
- Stereognosis -- 3D depth perception
- Spatial orientation and awareness
#### Inferior Parietal Cortex
- High order integration of sensory and language info
	- What does word mean
	- What do numbers, letters words symbolize
	- How do symbols convey meaning
	- Writing from language processing

### Lesions
**Dominant**
- Language deficits
	- Astereognosis -- can't identify tactile alone
	- Agraphesthesia -- can't recognize letters drawn on skin
	- Alexia -- can't read
	- Anomia -- can't name object but recognize it
	- Agnosia -- can't recognize objects
	- Ideomotor Apraxia -- can't execute on command
	- Ideational Apraxia -- can't conceptualize 
- [[Gerstmann Syndrome]]

**Non-Dominant**
- Spatial awareness and attention deficit
	- Asomatognosia -- can't recognize position of body parts 
	- Constructional apraxia -- can't draw or construct objects but strength and comprehension = intact
	- ↓ spatial orientation -- lost, can't tell distances
- Hemispatial Neglect
	- *Parietal eye field*
	- Doesn't pay attention to L
	- Denial of defect
