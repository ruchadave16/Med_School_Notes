**Blood Supply**: SCA
- 4 nuclei in cerebellum, 1 outside
- All synapse in cerebellar cortex and then Purkinje fibers -> these nuclei

![[Screenshot 2026-04-09 at 12.29.51 PM.png]]
**L -> M: Don't Eat Greasy Foods**
#### Dentate
- Most lateral
- Input from lateral hemispheres of cerebellar cortex
- Function: motor planning and initiation of voluntary movement
- *Lesion = I/L limb ataxia, impaired motor planning*

#### Interposed (Emboliform and Globose)
- Input from paravermis
- Real time correction of limb movements
- *Lesion = I/L limb dysmetria, intention tremor*

#### Fastigial
- Most medial
- Input from vermis
- Function: axial position, gait stability, eye and head correction
- *Lesion = trunchal ataxia, gait instability*
#### Vestibular Nuclei
- Pontomedullary junction
- Input from Flocculonodular lobe
- Function: balance, gaze stability
- *Lesion = vertigo, nystagmus, impaired gaze stabilization*
