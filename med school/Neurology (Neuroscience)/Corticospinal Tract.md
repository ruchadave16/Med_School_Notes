**2 motor neuron pathway**
UMN:
- C/L [[primary motor cortex]] -> [[Internal Capsule|internal capsule]] (posterior limb) -> [[brainstem]] -> cross in caudal [[medulla]] -> descend in lateral column of spinal cord -> I/L ventral horn
- Synapses on I/L ventral horn

![[Screenshot 2026-03-26 at 11.09.45 PM.png]]

LMN:
- [[ventral horn]] -> ventral root -> spinal nerve -> muscle

*Lesion* -- [[UMN lesion]] **below** injury level I/L
- Spastic hemiparesis

![[Screenshot 2026-03-26 at 10.42.31 PM.png]]

- Most commonly talk abt LCST
	- Cervical cord medial, LE is lateral
	- Blood = ASA
