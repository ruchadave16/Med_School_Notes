*Abnormal electrical activity in brain that causes change in movement, consciousness, or behavior*

- Provoked Seizure
- Focal Seizure
	- Isolated to 1 location of brain
	- Can generalize secondarily
	- Aura before seizure
- Generalized Seizure
#### Terms
- Tonic: stiffening
- Clonic: rhythmic jerking
- Myclonic: arrhythmic jerking
- Atonic: no tone, falling to ground

**Postictal Period**: minutes to hours of depressed neurological state before
returning to baseline
- Todds paralysis- transient focal weakness following seizure, corresponds to motor neurons involved during seizure
