#### Saccular Aneurysms
- 35-40%: junction of [[Anterior Cerebral A.|anterior cerebral a.]] and anterior communicating a. 
	- **Result:** Optic chiasm compression with bitemporal hemianopia
- 30-35%: [[Middle Cerebral A.|middle cerebral a.]] bifurcation
- 20%: inferior cerebellar a. to PCom
	- **Result**: [[Oculomotor Nerve|CN III]] compression 
- 5% at [[basilar a.]] bifurcation to [[Posterior Cerebral A.|PCA]]

#### [[Charcot-Bouchard Microaneurysm]]
- Most common in [[basal ganglia]] from chronic HTN
- Like lacunar stroke
- Ruptures into hemorrhagic stroke
#### [[Arteriovenous Malformation]]
- Large tangle resulting in multiple connections exist between arteries and veins
- Enlargens over time
- High flow low resistance shunt
