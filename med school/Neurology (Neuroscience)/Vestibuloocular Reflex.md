**Pathway**
L semicircular canal ↑ -> [[Vestibulocochlear Nerve|CN VIII]] -> Vestibular nucl. -> R PPRF and R CN VI -> L MLF -> L CN III
- Head moves towards signal, eyes away

Can be suppressed by cerebellum 

**Lesions**
- Disrupting = nystagmus to same side
- Ablative = nystagmus to opposing side
#### Clinical
- Acute vestibular syndrome
	- Stroke
	- [[Vestibular neuritis]]
- Episodic, provoked
	- [[orthostatic hypotension]]
	- [[BPPV]]
- Episodic, unprovoked
	- TIA
	- [[Meniere disease]]
	- Vestibular migraine
- Chronic vestibular syndrome
	- Bilateral vestibulopathy
	- Cerebellar degeneration
- [[Labyrinthitis]]
