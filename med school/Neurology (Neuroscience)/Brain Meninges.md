*Coverings of brain right under calvarium*

1) [[Dura Mater]]
2) Arachnoid Mater
	- Has granulations of villi
	- Subarachnoid space has [[CSF]] and major blood vessels
	- **Origin**: [[Neural crest cells]]
	- No pain receptors
	- [[Subarachnoid cisterns]]
3) Pia Mater
	- Connects to brain parenchyma through glial end feet
	- **Origin**: [[Neural crest cells]]
	- No pain receptors

#### Spaces
- Epidural Space: potential between dura and skull
	- Has middle meningeal a. 
- Subdural Space: potential between dura and arachnoid
	- Veins that go to dural venous sinuses are here
- Subarachnoid Space: exists normally, filled with [[CSF]]
	- Cerebral arteries here
