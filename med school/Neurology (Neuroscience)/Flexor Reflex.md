*Withdraws body from [[Pain]] stimulus*
I/L flexion with C/L extension

**Absence = significant brain damage**

- Receptor activation -> $A\delta$ and C fibers
- Nociceptor afferents -> excitatory interneurons activated to I/L flexors AND inhibitory to extensors
	- Commissural interneurons activate C/L extensors 
	- Ex: If you step on a nail, you pull up left leg so right leg extends to accomodate
- Contraction of flexors pulls away AND inhibition of antagonist
