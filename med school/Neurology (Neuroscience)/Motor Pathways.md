- UMN: upper motor neuron -- from [[Cerebrum|cerebral cortex]] -> LMN in [[ventral horn]]
- LMN: lower motor neuron -- [[ventral horn]] / brainstem -> [[skeletal muscle]]
	-  Alpha (A$\alpha$) -- large cell bodies in [[ventral horn]]
		- Project to extrinsic muscle fibers to contract, part of LMN
- $A\gamma$ -- small cell bodies that project to intrinsic muscle fibers
	- NO contraction of large muscle, only intrinsic muscle fibers
	- Innervated by [[reticulospinal tracts]]
	- Set length at which muscle spindle will response to change  
- [[Neuromuscular Junction|NMJ]] -- synapse between LMN and muscle
### Hierarchy
- Direct pathways (pyramidal)
	- Conscious control of [[Skeletal Muscle|skeletal muscle]]
	- [[Corticospinal tract]]: cerebral cortex -> ventral horn (SN synapse)
	- [[Corticobulbar tract]]: cerebral cortex -> brainstem nuclei (CN synapse)
- Indirect pathways (extrapyramidal)
	- Involuntary control of [[Skeletal Muscle|skeletal muscle]]
	- Rubrospinal tract: 
		- Red nucleus in [[midbrain]] -> C/L in spinal cord 
		- **Limited in humans**
	- Tectospinal tract
		- [[Superior colliculus]] of midbrain -> C/L in anterior column
		- Head movement with gaze
	- Reticulospinal tract (M/L)
		- [[Reticular formation]] -> anterior column
		- Posture, muscle tone
		- M: extensors excited
		- L: inhibits extensor, allows flexors
	- Vestibulospinal tract (M/L)
		- Pons at vestibular nuclei 
		- Extend antigravity muscles for eye and head posture
		- In anterior column of spinal cord
