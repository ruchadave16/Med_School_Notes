- [[DCML]]
	- Carries fine touch, proprioception
	- Lesion at this level = ipsilateral loss of fine touch, proprioception **below** lesion level
	- If only fasciculus gracile injured, only damages below T6
	- Blood supply: posterior spinal a. (PSA)
#### Anterolateral
- [[STT]]
	- Carries pain and temperature, crude touch
- Spinoreticular tract
	- Synapse in [[reticular formation]] of brainstem
	- Controls descending analgesic pathways
	- Controls emotional rxn to pain
- Spinomesencephalic tract
	- Synapse on superior colliculus and PAG (periaqueductal gray)
	- Function: turn eyes to pain, activates descending tracts to control pain
- Lateral Corticospinal Tract
- Anterior Corticospinal Tract
