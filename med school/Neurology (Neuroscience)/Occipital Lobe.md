#### Primary Visual Cortex
*Lesion: C/L homogeneous hemianopia* -- macula sparing generally from PCA stroke

Lingual gyri: inferior to calcarine sulcus
- Receives info from C/L superior FOV
Cuneus gyri: superior to calcarine sulcus
- Receives info from C/L inferior FOV

>[!Note] If individual gyri have lesion, then quadrantanopia
#### Visual Association Cortex
- Input from primary visual cortex
- Dorsal "Where" (goes -> parietal lobe)
	- Optic ataxia: can't localize object in space
- Ventral "What" (goes -> temporal lobe)
	- Prosopagnosia -- can't recognize faces
	- Visual agnosia -- can't recognize what is seen or copy objects
	
**Blood Supply: [[Posterior Cerebral A.|PCA]]**
