#### [[Primary Motor Cortex]]

#### Premotor Cortex and Supplementary Motor Cortex
- Plans motor activities
- Initiates voluntary activities -> Sends signal to motor cortex
- Blood supply: [[Middle Cerebral A.|MCA]] (superior division)
- *Lesion*:
	- Motor programming deficit -- no execution of learned behaviors
	- Bedside Test: Ask pt to imitate learned actions

#### Frontal Eye Field
- C/L conjugated eye movement
	- L FEF moves eyes -> right
- Blood supply: [[Middle Cerebral A.|MCA]] superior division
- *Lesion: Destructive (eyes move towards lesion), Irritative (eyes move away from lesion)*
#### [[Broca's Area]]
- **In dominant hemisphere**
- Motor production of [[language]]
- Blood supply: [[Middle Cerebral A.|MCA]] superior division
- *Lesion = [[Broca's Aphasia|Broca's aphasia]]*
#### Prefrontal Cortex
- Personality, initiative, judgement, feelings, abstract thinking
- Social behavior
- Working memory and conscious learning
- Suppresses primitive reflexes
- Executive function = Sequencing, Organization, Abstraction, Planning (SOAP)

**Dorsolateral Prefrontal Cortex (PFC)**
- Working memory and cognition
- Test: Draw clock with hands at 10 past 11
- *Lesion: issues with problem planning, solving, attention, drive*

**Ventromedial Prefrontal Cortex**
- Connects to limbic structures
- *Lesion = impulsive behaviors*

>[!Note] 
>R PFC has withdrawal bias -- activated by fearful activities
>L PFC has approach bias -- being engaged in activities
