*white matter tract that brings info to and from [[Cerebrum|cerebral cortex]]*

![[Screenshot 2026-04-09 at 4.37.38 PM.png]]
- Anterior limb 
	- Frontothalamic fibers
	- Thalamocortical fibers
	- Supplied by ACA
- Genu
	- [[Corticobulbar Tract|Corticobulbar tract]]
	- Supplied by [[Lateral Striate A.|Lenticulostriate aa.]]
	- *Lesion*: only CN motor symptoms: 
		- C/L lower face paralysis
		- Dysarthria and/or dysphagia due to tongue paralysis
	- Supply from [[Lateral Striate A.|lateral striate a.]]
- Posterior limb -- sensory from thalamus to cerebrum
	- [[Corticospinal Tract]]
	- [[Sensory Pathways]]
	- Supplied by [[Lateral Striate A.|Lenticulostriate aa.]]
	- *Lesion*:
		- C/L loss of all sensation of body and face
		- C/L hemiparesis

![[Drawing 2026-03-18 22.47.14.excalidraw]]
- Sublenticular limb
	- Below lentiform nucleus
	- Auditory radiations
	- Supplied by Anterior choroidal a. 
- Retrolenticular limb
	- Posterior to lentiform nucleus
	- Optic radiations
	- Supplied by Anterior choroidal a. 
#### Vasculature
- Striate aa. so **highly susceptible** to hypoxia and lacunar infarct
- Anterior limb -- medial striate aa. (recurrent a. of Huebner <- [[Anterior Cerebral A.|anterior cerebral a.]])
- Genu -- [[Lateral Striate A.|lateral striate a.]]
- Posterior limb -- mainly [[Lateral Striate A.|lateral striate a.]] (from MCA) but also anterior choroidal a.
