*Mediated by reticular activating system*

### Brain Death
- Irreversible cessation of [[brainstem]] function
	- No cortical activity
	- No CN reflexes
	- Exclude reversible causes (drugs, metabolic derangement)
	- Apnea testing -- put on 100% oxygen, ventilator turned off

#### Coma
- Eyes closed, immobile
- Dysfunction of corticothalamic loops from diffuse cellular dysfunction, disconnection, or loss of upper brainstem arousal tone
- No motor or cognition
#### Vegetative State
- Alternating eyes closed and open, reflex movements
- Some functioning of upper brainstem
- No motor or cognition
#### Minimally Conscious State
- Low level, intermittent interaction with environment
- Diffuse injury to white matter / thalamus
- Motor movement but non-goal oriented
#### Akinetic Mutism
- Severe: eye tracking only
- Mild: ↓ goal oriented behavior
- Partial responses
#### [[Locked In Syndrome]]
- Loss of corticospinal tract from complete pontine stroke
