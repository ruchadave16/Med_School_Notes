*Diffuse network of neurons in [[brainstem]] from [[medulla]] through pons to midbrain*

- Midbrain Lesion: ↓ consciousness, impaired arousal
- Pons Lesion: No REM sleep, abnormal breathing, extensor rigidity
- Medulla: ↓ respiration, CV instability, vital reflexes
#### Functions
- Arousal and consciousness
	- ARAS
	- Mainly in [[midbrain]]
- Sleep-wake regulation
	- [[Pons]]
- Motor control and Posture
	- [[Pons]]
- Autonomic regulation
	- [[Medulla]] and [[pons]]
	- respiration, HR, BP
- Sensory modulation and pain control
	- [[Midbrain]] starts [[pain]] perception
- Attention and habituation
- Reflex and CN integration
	- [[pons]] and [[medulla]]
