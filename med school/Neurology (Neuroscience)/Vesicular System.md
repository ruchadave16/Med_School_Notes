*Interconnected space in [[brain]] that has [[CSF]]*
- Lateral ventricles (L and R)
	- In [[cerebrum]]
	- Frontal (anterior), body, occipital (posterior), temporal (inferior), atrium (trigone)
	- Septum pellucidum  -- membranous body separating 2 lateral ventricles
- Interventricular foramen of Monroe-- connects each lateral ventricle to single 3rd ventricle
- 3rd ventricle -- surrounded by diencephalon between 2 thalami
- Cerebral aqueduct (of Sylvius) -- in midbrain
- 4th ventricle -- between medulla and cerebellum
- Lateral apertures (foramen of Luscka) -- 2 posterolateral openings to subarachnoid space
- Median apertures (foramen of Magendie) -- 1 opening posterior to cisterna magna
- Central canal -- continues into spinal cord
