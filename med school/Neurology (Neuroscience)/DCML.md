*Pathway that carries perception of mechanical stimulii*
- Fine touch
- 2 point discrimination
- 3D recognition
- Vibration -- pacinian corpuscle
- Conscious proprioception
- Pressure -- Merkel cell
### Pathway
**3 Neuron Pathway**
1) Cell body in [[dorsal root ganglion]] -- pseudo-unipolar
	- Ascend in dorsal column (fasciculus cuneatus (C2-T6) or fasciculus gracilis (T7-S5))
	- Synapse in cuneate or gracile nuclei in caudal [[medulla]]
	- *Lesion* = ipsilateral loss of fine touch and proprioception
		- Gracile nuc. would be below T6; cuneate nuc. Above T6

![[Screenshot 2026-03-25 at 3.44.49 PM.png]]

2) Cell body in cuneate / gracile nuclei
	- Cross in internal arcuate fibers in caudal medulla
	- Ascend in [[brainstem]] in [[medial lemniscus]]
		- *Lesion* here = C/L loss of fine touch and proprioception of body
	- Synapse in [[VPL]] nucleus of [[thalamus]]

![[Drawing 2026-03-26 10.29.31.excalidraw]]

3) Cell body in [[VPL]]
	- Blood Supply: [[Posterior Cerebral A.|PCA]] in VPL
	- Ascend in posterior limb of internal capsule through [[thalamocortical fibers]] -> corona radiata -> primary [[somatosensory cortex]]
		- [[Lateral Striate A.|Lenticulostriate aa.]] in Post. limb of IC
		- Medial cortex -- [[Anterior Cerebral A.|ACA]]
		- Lateral cortex -- [[Middle Cerebral A.|MCA]]
