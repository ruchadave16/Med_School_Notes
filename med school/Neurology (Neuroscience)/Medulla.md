- Pyramid -- ridges on ventral surface which have descending motor tracts
	- Most of [[Corticospinal Tract|CST]] cross and enter lateral CST
	- Remaining in anterior CST
	- *Lesion*: C/L [[UMN lesion]] for UE/LE
- Olive -- swelling of inferior olivary nucleus
- Pre and post olivary sulcus
	- [[Glossopharyngeal Nerve|CN IX]], [[Vagus Nerve|CN X]], [[Accessory Nerve|CN XI]] exit post olivary sulcus
	- [[Hypoglossal Nerve|CN XII]] exits pre olivary sulcus
- Inferior cerebellar peduncle -- dorsal
- 4th ventricle
- Foramen of Luschka

#### Nuclei 
- Gracile and Cuneate Nuclei
	- 2nd order neurons 
	- Blood Supply: PSA -- posterior spinal a.
	- *Lesion* here = ipsilateral loss of fine touch and proprioception
		- Gracile nuc. would be below T6; cuneate nuc. Above T6
- Nucleus Ambiguus ([[Glossopharyngeal Nerve|CN IX]] and [[Vagus Nerve|CN X]])
	-  Bilat. EXCEPT C/L to soft palate and uvula
	- *Lesion* -- uvula deviates **away** from weak muscles
		- LMN: deviate away towards normal
		- UMN (CBT): minimal
-  [[Hypoglossal Nerve|CN XII]]
	- Bilat. but C/L genioglossus
	- *Lesion* -- tongue **towards** weak side
		- UMN: away towards lesion side, no atropy
		- LMN: towards lesion side WITH atropy
- [[Accessory Nerve|CN XI]] motor nuclei.
	- Bilat. no UMN symptoms

#### Vasculature
- Medial: anterior spinal a.  (ASA)
- Lateral: posterior inferior cerebellar a. PICA (rostral) and [[Vertebral Artery|vertebral a.]] (caudal)
	- PICA infarct -- [[STT]] (C/L loss pain and temp of body) and [[Spinal Trigeminal Tract]] (I/L pain and temp of face)
