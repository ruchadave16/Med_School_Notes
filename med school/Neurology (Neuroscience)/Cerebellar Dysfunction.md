**NO PARALYSIS**, only weakness
#### Limb Coordination
Heel to shin, finger to nose
- Intention tremor = worsening as target is approached
- Dysmetria = over / undershooting target
- Dyssynergia = Breaking down multi-joint movement into sequential steps

Rapid alternating
- Dysdiadochokinesia = cannot perform

Rebound phenomenon -- arms out, eyes closed, push down and release
- + if overshoot

Cerebellar dysarthria = staccato speech

Difficulty with stable gaze and nystagmus
- Vertical, gaze-evoked, direction changing suggests central
#### Gait
- Wide Base = unsteady w tandem walking
#### Muscle Tone and Reflexes
- Cerebellar hypotonia = ↓ resistance to passive movement
- Pendulous myotonic reflex = ↓ damping of stretch reflex -- limb oscillates
### Causes
- Head trauma
- Vascular -- PICA, AICA, SCA
- MS, sarcoidosis
- Tumor
- Infection
- Creutzfeld-Jakob disease > degenerative
- Drugs -- antiepileptic, heavy metals, B6 toxicity
- Metabolic -- B1 deficiency, hypothyroidism
- Hereditary
