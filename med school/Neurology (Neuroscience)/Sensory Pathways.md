### Fine Touch, Vibration, Proprioception, 2 pt Discrimination
**3 neuron pathways**
- Body on [[DCML]] (dorsal column medial lemniscus)
	- Cross in caudal [[medulla]] in internal arcuate fibers
- Face on [[Trigeminal System]]
	- Cross in mid [[pons]]
- Synapse in [[thalamus]] -- VPL
- Destination = C/L primary [[somatosensory cortex]] (post central gyrus)
### Pain and Temperature
**3 neuron pathways**
- Body on [[STT]] (spinothalamic tract)
	- Cross in spinal cord in **anterior white commissure**
- Face **descends** in [[spinal trigeminal tract]]
	- Crosses in medulla and ascends in trigeminal lemniscus
- Synapse in [[thalamus]] -- VPM
- Destination = C/L primary [[somatosensory cortex]] (post central gyrus)
### Unconscious Proprioception
- Anterior spinocerebellar tract -- from interneurons in spinal cord
- Posterior spinocerebellar tract -- from muscles
- Destination = I/L [[cerebellum]]
