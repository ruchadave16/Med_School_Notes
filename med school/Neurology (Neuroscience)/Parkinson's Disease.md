### Etiology
- Onset = 60
- 15% have genetic link
- Risks:
	- Pesticide exposure
	- Cleaning chemicals
	- Heavy metal inhalation
	- Concussion history
### Pathophysiology
- Progressive degenerative disease of [[basal ganglia]]
- ↓ [[dopamine]] neurons from [[substantia nigra]]
	- Cannot excite direct path
	- Cannot stop indirect path
- ↓ pigmentation of substantia nigra
- **Lewy bodies**
![[Screenshot 2026-04-12 at 3.01.57 PM.png]]
### Clinical Features
Starts unilat. but progresses to other side
- Resting tremor
- Cog-wheel like rigidity
- Akinesia and bradykinesia
- Posture abnormal adjustments

- Festinating gait: small shuffling
- ↓ face expressions
- Excessive sleepiness
- Orthostatic hypotension
- Sexual dysfunction
- ↓ smell **early sign**
- Pain, paresthesia

![[Screenshot 2026-04-12 at 3.00.02 PM.png]]
### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria
	- Robust [[Levodopa]] response
**Other Causes of Parkinsonism**
- [[Multiple System Atrophy]] -- also has autonomic dysfunction and ataxia
- [[Progressive Supranuclear Palsy]] -- early falls, vertical gaze paresis, dementia
- [[Lewy Body Dementia]] -- dementia within 1 yr of onset, hallucinations
- [[Corticobasal Syndrome]] -- unilat. apraxia
### Treatment
- [[Levodopa]] -- increase [[dopamine]]
- [[COMT inhibitors]]
- [[Dopamine agonists]] -- stimulate receptors
- [[Amantadine]]
- [[MAO Inhibitors]] (B) -- inhibit metabolism of dpoamine

Advanced: [[deep brain stimulation]]
### Complications
- 85% will also have dementia
- 30-60% = depression
- 51% apathy
- 49% anxiety
- 44% hallucinations
- OCD
- 33% = REM Sleep Behavior disorder
