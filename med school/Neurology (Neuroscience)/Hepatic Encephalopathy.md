### Etiology
- [[Cirrhosis]]
- [[Hepatitis]]
- Liver shunt

### Pathophysiology
- Impaired [[liver]] function causes ↑ [[ammonia]] levels
- Alzheimer II cells: 
	- abnormal [[Astrocytes]] in deep grey nuclei
	- enlargened with cleared out chromatin and folded appearance

### Clinical Features
- Derangement of consciousness
	- Confusion
	- Drowsiness
	- Stupor
	- Coma
- Asterixis -- flapping tremor when sustained posture
	- Have pt. hold out arms with wrists extended
