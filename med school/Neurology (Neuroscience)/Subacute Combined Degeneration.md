### Etiology
- [[Vitamin B12]] Deficiency, [[Vitamin E]] deficiency, [[copper deficiency]]

### Pathophysiology
- Degeneration and demyelination of dorsal column, spinocerebellar tracts, and [[corticospinal tract]]
	- Dorsal column -> proprioception loss, sensory ataxia
	- CST -> + babinski and spastic paresis

### Clinical Features
- Numbness and tingling -- [[DCML]]
- [[Sensory ataxia]] -- spinocerebellar tracts
- Impaired position / vibration sense
- General weakness w upper motor neuron signs -- [[corticospinal tract]]
- + Babinski sign

### Treatment
- [[Vitamin B12|B12]] replacement
- Treat malabsorption cause
