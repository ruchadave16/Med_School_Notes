### Etiology
- Microvascular ischemia
- Tumor
- Demyelination
- Inflammation
- Compression from aneurysm

### Pathophysiology
- [[LMN Lesion]] of [[Oculomotor Nerve|CN III]]

### Clinical Features
- I/L eye abducted and depressed (down and out)
- Diplopia
- Mydriasis
- Non-reactive to light
- Near vision loss -- no accomodation
- Ptosis

### Diagnosis
>[!Warning] Acute CN III palsy WITH I/L mydriasis and headache = PComm aneurysm unless proven otherwise

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- Death or SAH
