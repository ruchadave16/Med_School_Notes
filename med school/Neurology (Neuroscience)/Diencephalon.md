>[!Clinical] [[Craniopharyngioma]]
>- Benign tumor from remnant of Rathke's pouch
>- #1 childhood supratentorial tumor
>- #1 cause of hypopituitarism in kids
>- Can compress causing
>	- Optic chiasm -- visual field defects
>	- Pituitary -- hypopituitarism
>	- Hypothalamus -- hyperphagia + obesity
>	- Interventricular foramen -- obstructive hydrocephalus
