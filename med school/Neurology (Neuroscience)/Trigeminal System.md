### Pathway
**3 neuron system**

1) Cell body in trigeminal ganglion
	- Enters [[pons]] and synapse on chief sensory trigeminal nuclei in ventral-lateral part
	- *Lesion here*: I/L loss of touch, vibration and pressure on face

2) Cell body in chief sensory trigeminal nuclei
	- Axon crosses midline -> trigeminal lemniscus
		- *Lesion*: Loss of ALL somatic sensation of C/L face
	- Synapse in [[VPM]] of [[thalamus]]
		- *Lesion*: Loss of all somatic sensation of C/L face
		- Blood supply = [[Posterior Cerebral A.|PCA]]
