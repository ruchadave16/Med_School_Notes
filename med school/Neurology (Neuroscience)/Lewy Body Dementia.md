### Etiology
- Primary causes
- Risk factors

### Pathophysiology
- Lewy bodies (+ for a-synuclein) in brainstem, neocortex

### Clinical Features
- + visual hallucinations
- Parkinsonian features
	- Dementia features onset within 1 year
- Extrapyramidal signs
- Fluctuating cognition
- REM sleep behavior disorder

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
	- Normal imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- [[Cholinesterase Inhibitors|AchE inhibitors]]
- [[Quetiapine]]
	- **Do NOT give [[haloperidol]] or other [[dopamine antagonists]] because can have worsening Parkinson's symptoms**

- Short-term
- Long-term
