#### Subdivisions 
- [[Cerebrum|Cerebral cortex]]
	- Peripheral gray matter, central white matter
	- 6 layers
	- Function: Integration of afferent and efferent information
	- Conscious mind
- [[Diencephalon]] 
	- [[Thalamus]]  -- relay station for info going to brain
	- [[Hypothalamus]] -- control of endocrine, ANS, water
- [[Brainstem]] -- [[midbrain]], [[pons]], [[medulla]]
- [[Cerebellum]] 
	- 3 layers:
		- [[Molecular Layer]]
		- [[Purkinjie Layer]]
		- [[Granular Layer]]
	- Function: Coordinate balance and muscle tone

#### Venous Vasculature
Superficial drain into **[[Dural Venous Sinuses]]**
- Superior cerebral vv.  -> superior sagittal sinus
- Superficial middle cerebral v. -> cavernous sinus
- Superior anastomotic v. (Trolard) -> superior sagittal sinus
- Inferior anastomotic v. (Labbe) -> transverse sinus

![[Screenshot 2026-03-20 at 1.06.48 AM.png]]

**Deep Veins**
- Merge to form internal cerebral v. -- dorsomedial thalamus location
- Basal veins (Rosenthal) -- ventral medial of temporal and occipital lobes
- Both merge into great cerebral v. (Galen) -> straight sinus
