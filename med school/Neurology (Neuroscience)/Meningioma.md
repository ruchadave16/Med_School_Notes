- Slow progressing, Grade I tumor
- Can grow to II and III
- Prognosis best if surgical excision of all tissue
### Etiology
- Common in 50s to 70s, 2:1 women : men
- Common with [[Neurofibromatosis Type 2]]

### Pathophysiology
- Source: meningothelial cells of the inner lining of the dura mater

**Micro**
- Lobular growth pattern
- Nuclear Pseudoinclusions (yellow)
- Meningothelial Whorls and Psammoma bodies (red)
