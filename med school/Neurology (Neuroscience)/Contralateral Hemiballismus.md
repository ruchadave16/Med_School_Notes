### Etiology
- Stroke to [[Posterior Cerebral A.|PCA]]

### Pathophysiology
- Subthalamic nucleus abnormalities
- Affects indirect pathway only 
	- Normally STN excites GPi, which inhibits thalamus stopping movement
	- ↓ inhibition of thalamus = ↑ movement

### Clinical Features
- Large amplitude movements, more rotary and flinging
- U/L because stroke
