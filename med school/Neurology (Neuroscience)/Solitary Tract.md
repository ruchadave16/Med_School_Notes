*Caudal [[pons]] -> sensory crossing in caudal [[medulla]]*

- [[Taste]] sensation (CN VII, IX, X)
	- Anterior 2/3 = [[Facial Nerve|CN VII]]
	- Posterior 1/3 = [[Glossopharyngeal Nerve|CN IX]]
	- Epiglottis = [[Vagus Nerve|CN X]]
- Carotid body sensation -- [[Glossopharyngeal Nerve|CN IX]]
- Baroceptor sensation -- [[Glossopharyngeal Nerve|CN IX]]
- GI and respiratory sensation -- [[Vagus Nerve|CN X]]
