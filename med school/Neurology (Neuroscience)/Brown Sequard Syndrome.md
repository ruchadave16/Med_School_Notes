### Pathophysiology
- Hemisection of spinal cord
### Clinical Features
- C/L loss of P&T below lesion (STT) -- this is because they cross in the anterior white
- I/L loss of fine touch and proprioception below lesion (DCML)
- I/L UMN loss below level (CST)
- I/L LMN loss at level (ventral horn)
- I/L loss of sensation at level (dorsal horn)
- If above T1, I/L [[Horner’s Syndrome]]
