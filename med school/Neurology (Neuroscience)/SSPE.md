### Etiology
- Follows exposure to the [[Measles Virus|measles virus]] by several years
- Onset 5-15 years old
	- High chance if measles before 18 months
	- **1-2 cases of every 10,000 measles cases**
	- **95% mortality rate**

### Pathophysiology
- Chronic encephalitis, with leptomeningeal, perivascular, and parenchymal infiltration by lymphocytes (predominantly T cells) and microglia
- [[Gliosis]] and loss of neurons
- Neurofibrillary tangles like Alzheimer

### Clinical Features
- Chronic progressive encephalitis
	- Intellectual impairment and behavior change
- Repeated symmetric myoclonic jerks every 5-10 sec
- ↑ mental deterioration
- Stupor, autonomic disturbances, eventual death

### Diagnosis
- Previous [[Measles Virus|measles virus]] infection
- CSF
	- ↑ [[IgG]]
	- Local synthesis of anti-measles antibodies in CNS
- EEG:
	- burst suppression pattern
- No focal CT patterns
- MRI showing asymmetric hyperintensities, often involving periventricular white matter 

### Treatment
- None -- supportive care
	1. Some progress to death within months
	2. Reports of survival >10 years
	3. Mean survival: 1.75 – 3 years
