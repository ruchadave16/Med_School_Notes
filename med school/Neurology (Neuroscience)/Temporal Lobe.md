#### Primary Auditory Cortex
- Heschl gyri
- Bilat. projection of auditory pathway with tone + volume
- *Lesion = no deafness but sound localization ↓ and auditary processing ↓*
- Blood supply: [[Middle Cerebral A.|MCA]] inferior division

#### Auditory Association Cortex
- Compares sounds with memories to sort them

[[Wernicke's Area]]
- **In dominant hemisphere**
- L -- dominant side, R -- nonverbal cues
- Blood supply = [[Middle Cerebral A.|MCA]]
- *Lesion = [[Wernicke's Aphasia]]*
