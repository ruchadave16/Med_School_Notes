### Etiology
- Primary causes
- Risk factors

### Pathophysiology
- Otoconia is dislodged and enters semicircular canal
	- Cupula deflected

### Clinical Features
- Episodic vertigo is brief (15-30 sec)
	- Provoked by head position

### Diagnosis
- Dix Hallpike maneuver 
	- In seated position, turn patient’s head to 45° and slightly extend. Then rapidly move patient from seat to supine with head extend off edge table with affected ear turn down. This will reproduce symptoms
