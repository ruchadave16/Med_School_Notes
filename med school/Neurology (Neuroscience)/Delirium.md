Waxing and waning mental status -- hyper to hypoactive
- Acute onset, reversible generally
- EEG might show slow background

#### Treatment
- Treat underlying condition
- Stop meds that are worsening
- Can use [[haloperidol]] if awful

**[[Benzodiazephines]] and [[Anticholinergic Drugs|anticholinergics]] make this worse**
