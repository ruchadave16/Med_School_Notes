### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria
	- Seizure > 5 min
	- Multiple seizures without return to baseline

### Treatment
- Stabilize pt (ABCs, glucose, IV access)
- Benzos (Lorazepam, midazolam, diazepam)
- Anti-seizure meds
	- Fosphenytoin IV (20 mg/kg)
	- Valproic acid (40 mg/kg)
	- Levetiracetam (60 mg)
	- Anesthetic w EEG
		- Propofol
		- Midazolam
		- Pentobarbital
### Complications
- Can cause permanent cortical injury
