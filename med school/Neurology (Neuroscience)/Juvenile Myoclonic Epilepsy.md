- Myoclonic jerks -- drop items from hands
	- Can start from absence seizures early on
- Can also have absence and generalized tonic-clonic seizures
- Triggers: flashing lights, alcohol, sleep deprivation
- Valproate (boys), Lamotrigine, Levetiracetam
- Treat throughout life
