### Diagnosis
- Biochemical test demonstrates increased erythrocyte transketolase activity after thiamine administration

### Treatment
- RAPID IV [[Thiamine]] **EMERGENCY**
- **GIVE THIAMINE BEFORE GLUCOSE**
- **[[Magnesium]]** = cofactor

### Complications
- HIGH morbidity, 75% permanent brain damage, 20% need lifelong care
