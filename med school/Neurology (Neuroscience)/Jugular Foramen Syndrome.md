### Etiology
- Primary causes
	- Tumors
	- Narrowing of foramen
- Risk factors

### Pathophysiology
- [[Glossopharyngeal Nerve|CN IX]], [[Vagus Nerve|CN X]], [[Accessory Nerve|CN XI]] palsy

### Clinical Features
- Symptoms
	- Hoarseness and dysphagia
	- Palate drop, uvula away
	- Tongue towards
	- Ear pain but NO infection (CN IX)
	- Headache
	- I/L weak SCM and trapezius
- Signs
	- [[Horner’s Syndrome]] if tumor is outside skull
- Variants
	- Can also impact XII
