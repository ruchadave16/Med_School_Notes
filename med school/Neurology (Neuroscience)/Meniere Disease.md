- Migraines
- Autoimmune dysfunction
- Inner ear trauma
- Viral infection
- Noise pollution

### Pathophysiology
- Endolymphatic hydrops -> ↑ endolymph in [[inner ear]]
- Distention of space causing ↑ pressure

### Clinical Features
- episodic vertigo (20 min to hr)
- sensorineural hearing loss
- non-pulsatile tinnitus
- Aural fullness
- Nystagmus
