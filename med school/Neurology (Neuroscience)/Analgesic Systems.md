*Reduction of pain mediated by descending inhibitory circuits*
### [[Endogenous Opioid Peptides]]

### Descending Analgesic Pathways
Trigger
- Spinomesencephalic path activates PAG
- Spinoreticular activates on locus ceruleus and raphe
#### Pathway
- PAG (Periaqueductal gray ), midbrain
	- Enkephalin producing neurons that stimulate raphe nuclei
- [[Raphe nuclei]], medulla
	- [[Serotonin]] neurons synapse on substania gelatinosa
	- Activates inhibitory neurons that release enkephalin to suppress pain
- Locus ceruleus, pons
	- [[Norepinephrine]] from ceruleospinal pathway reduces [[STT]] and suppresses substance P in dorsal horn
#### [[Opioids]]
- $\mu$ receptors normally inhibit GABA neurons and suppress descending inhibitory pathways
- In this case, descending inhibitory pathways are more active, DECREASING pain
### Ascending Pathways
- [[Dorsal horn]] to inhibit pain signals from peripheral neurons -> brain
### Peripheral Action
- Peripheral [[opioid receptors]] when activated, reduce nociceptor excitabiltiy
