*Symptom not a disease*

- Cause: disease of dorsal column -- [[Multiple Sclerosis]] or [[Tabes Dorsalis]]
- Result: loss of coordination, can be compensated by visual cues
- Test: + Romberg sign, + finger to nose test or heel-to-knee

Walk with **high steppage gait**
