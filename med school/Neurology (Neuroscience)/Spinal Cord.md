#### Location
- Adults: [[Foramen Magnum]] → L1/L2
- Children: further down
#### Structure

![[Screenshot 2026-03-19 at 12.30.13 AM.png]]

31 total segments

- Spinal cord segment: portion that gives rise to nerve roots forming 1 pair of spinal nerves
- Cervical enlargement (C4-T1): Creates nerves of [[brachial plexus]]
- Lumbosacral enlargement (T11-S1): Creates nerves of [[lumbar plexus]] and [[sacral plexus]]
- [[Conus medullaris]]: adult spinal cord terminates at L1/L2
- [[Caudal equina]]: Lumbar and sacral nerve roots extend distal to conus medullaris to reach exit 

>[!Note] Clinical
>[[Cauda Equina Syndrome]] -- compression of cauda equina with symptoms below L2
#### Anatomy
- Anterior median fissure -- has anterior spinal a.
- Posterior median sulcus
- Posterior intermediate sulcus 
- Posterior lateral sulcus

![[Screenshot 2026-03-19 at 12.31.30 AM.png]]

- Gray horns (info in/out of spinal cord)
	- Ventral gray horn → somatic (motor) cell bodies
		- Ventral root → motor axons
	- Lateral gray horn (Intermediolateral cell columns (IMLs)) → autonomic cell bodies
		- Axons go through Ventral Root
		- **Only in T1-L2**
	- Dorsal gray horn (sensory dendrites + interneurons)
		- Dorsal root → axons for neurons in dorsal horn
		- Dorsal root ganglia → cell bodies of sensory axons
		
>[!Clinical]
> [[Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis]] (Lou Gehrig’s disease)
> - Progressive degeneration of motor neurons in ventral horn

- White columns (info to and from [[brain]])
	- Funiculus = column (3 per side)
	- Fasciculus = tract (either sensory or motor)
		- Corticospinal tract
		- Spinothalamic tract
	- Anterior white commissure -- crossing myelinated axons anterior to central canal (imp for ascending pain)

>[!Note] Identifying Level
>- Sacral / lumbar have more gray matter than white
>- Thoracic has lateral gray horn
>- Cervical has posterior intermediate sulcus 

![[Screenshot 2026-03-19 at 12.35.20 AM.png]]
	
- [[Spinal Nerve]]: merger of roots → can contain both motor and sensory axons, located proximal to intervertebral foramen
	- Divides into rami
	- Dorsal rami: supply facet (vertebral) joints, deep back muscles
	- Ventral rami: supply body wall and limbs
#### Vasculature
[[Vertebral Artery|vertebral a.]]
- Posterior spinal aa. (paired to posterior 1/3)
- Anterior spinal aa. (unpaired to anterior 2/3)

Segmental feeders (anastamose from medullary a.)
- artery of Adamkiewicz
