*Unattended increase of muscle length causes contraction to shorten smooth muscle*
### Monosynaptic Pathway
- [[Muscle Spindles|Muscle spindle]] sense ↑ length 
- Signal along sensory axon (Ia fiber) -> LMN of [[Ventral Horn|ventral horn]]
- Signal along LMN ($A\alpha$) -> muscle
- Contraction of muscle to original length AND inhibition of antagonistic muscles

>[!Clinical] Tested Clinically
>- Biceps and brachioradialis -- C5-6
>- Triceps -- C7-8
>- Patellar -- L3-4
>- Ankle -- S1-S2
>  
> -  Damage on pathway of the reflex can cause ↓ reflexes [[LMN Lesion]]
>
>- Damage along descending pathway that moderates the reflex can cause ↑ reflexes, [[UMN Lesion]]
