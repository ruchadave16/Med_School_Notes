- Middle cerebellar peduncles (MCP) -- connect tracts to [[cerebellum]]
	- [[Trigeminal Nerve|CN V]] -- exits lateral middle pons in middle cerebellar peduncle
- Basilar pons -- ventral portion of pons
- Pontomedullary junction
	-  [[CN VI]] -- exits midline of pontomedullary junction
- Cerebellopontine angle (CPA) 
	- [[Facial Nerve|CN VII]], [[Vestibulocochlear Nerve|CN VIII]] exit here
- 4th ventricle -- transition from cerebral aqueduct to 4th ventricle

#### Vasculature
- Medial -- basilar paramedian perforators
- Lateral -- anterior inferior cerebellar a. (caudal) and superior cerebellar a. (rostral) 
	- Both come off of [[Basilar A.|basilar a.]]
