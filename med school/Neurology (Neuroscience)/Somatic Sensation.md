### Common Patterns of Loss
- Cortical: C/L sensory loss
	- neglect, extinction, agraphesthesia, astereognosis, visual field defects
- Subcortical: Pure sensory
- Thalamus: C/L sensory loss
	- can have pain ([[Dejerine Roussy Syndrome]])
- Brainstem: I/L sensory loss of face, C/L loss of body
	- Cranial neuropathies
	- I/L ataxia, C/L weakness

##### Other Spinal Cord Conditions
- [[Radiculopathy]]
	- Sensory loss of I/L dermatome
	- Neuropathic pain w radiation
	- Weakness in myotome
	- Areflexia
- Brachial plexus or lumbosacral plexus
	- I/L sensory loss of >1 peripheral nerve
	- Painful, areflexia, weakness
- Dorsal root ganglion
	- Sensation loss in dermatome
	- No weakness
- Peripheral nerve
	- Mononeuropathy: sensory loss of all in distribution of nerve, might have weakness
	- Mononeuritis Multiplex: no sensation in various peripheral nerve distribution
	- [[Polyneuropathy]]: distal symmetric sensory loss
		- Small fiber is pain temp loss, maybe autonomic
		- Large fiber is vibration and proprioception loss
#### Terms
- Numbness – Loss of sensation, not painful, may be result of pathology anywhere along
the neuroaxis (cortex, brainstem, spinal cord, dorsal root ganglion, peripheral nerves)
- Paresthesia- spontaneous, abnormal sensation, “tingling”, “pins and needles”, usually
not painful
- Dysesthesia- evoked unpleasant sensation to innocuous stimuli, can be painful
- Hyperesthesia-increase sensation to stimuli not expected to cause pain
- Hypoesthesia- decreased sensation to stimuli not expected to cause pain
- Neuropathic pain – pain specific to nerve injury, commonly a painful paresthesia,
sometimes characterized as burning, may radiate (radiculopathy)
- Hyperalgesia- exaggerated response to stimuli known to evoke pain (pain out of
proportion to stimulus
- Hypoalgesia- reduced response to stimuli known to evoke pain
- Anesthesia- inability to perceive pain via alteration of consciousness
- Analgesia- inability to feel pain
- Allodynia- painful sensation caused by non-painful stimuli
- Nociception- Detection of noxious stimuli
