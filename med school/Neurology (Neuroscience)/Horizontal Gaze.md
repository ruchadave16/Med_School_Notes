- Conjugated movement
	- To look left, L lateral rectus and R medial rectus so L CN VI and R CN III
	- R FEF (in cortex) -> L PPRF -> L VI nuc. -> R MLF -> R CN III nuc.

- FEF (frontal eye field)
	- In middle [[frontal lobe]] gyrus
	- Conjugated movement of voluntary saccades C/L
	- R FEF for L movement of eyes, L FEF for right movement of both eyes
	- *Stroke: gaze towards side of lesion*
	- *Irritation (damage but not destroyed): gaze away from lesion*

- MLF (medial longitudinal fasciculus) connects CN III, IV, VI, and vestibular nuclei. 
	- Highly myelinated
	- *Lesion*: [[INO]] (Internuclear ophthalmoplegia), affected in [[Multiple Sclerosis|MS]]

- PPRF (paramedian pontine reticular formation) is in pons ventral to CN VI
	- Right does horizontal gaze for R
	- Input from cortex, parieto-occipital cortex, basal ganglia, vestibular nuc.
		- FEF (frontal eye field) does C/L PPRF
	- *Lesion*: I/L horizontal gaze palsy

- [[Abducens Nucl.]]
	- I/L projection to LR and C/L MLF -> C/L CN III

#### Lesions
- R [[CN VI]]: looking left is fine, just cannot have R eye looking R -- [[CN VI Palsy]]
- R PPRF: looking left is fine, neither eye looks R -- [[horizontal gaze palsy]]
- L MLF: looking left is fine, L cannot look R and R has nyastagmus -- [[INO]]
- L MLF and L abducens nuclei: neither eye can look L, L cannot look right, R has nyastagmus looking R -- 1 ½ syndrome (e.g. L. horizontal gaze palsy w/ L. INO). A dorsal pontine lesions
- L [[Oculomotor Nucl.]]: looking left is fine, L can't look R but right can -- [[CN III palsy]]
