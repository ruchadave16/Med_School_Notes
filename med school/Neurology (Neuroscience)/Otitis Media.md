### Etiology
- [[Streptococcus Pneumonia|Strep pneumo]], [[Moraxella Catarrhalis]], [[Hemophilus Influenza|H. influenza]]
	- More common in children 
- (less common) [[Rhinovirus]], [[Adenovirus]], [[Influenza Virus|Influenza]], [[enterovirus]]

### Pathophysiology
- Middle ear infection
- ET tube dysfunction
	- Relative negative pressure in the middle ear
	- ↑ fluid and infection
	- Auditory tube is more horizontal in kids < 3 yrs so ↓ drainage

### Clinical Features
Otalgia, generally u/l
- Commonly preceeded by URIs
- ↓ hearing
- Fever
- Pulling at ears, irritation
- ↓ Hearing
- Dizziness
- **Purulent ear drainage indicates tympanic membrane rupture**

### Treatment
- Antibiotics + [[Decongestants|decongestants]]
	- Amoxicillin or augmentin for 5-10 days
	- [[Ceftriaxone]] if penicillin resistant
- Tympanocentesis if indicated so ↓ pressure before rupture

### Complications
- [[Chronic otitis media]]
- Mastoiditis
