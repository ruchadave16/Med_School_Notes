### Gate Control Theory
*Pain controls entering spinal cord can be reduced or increased based on which sensory fibers are more active*

- Large Fibers ($A\alpha$, $A\beta$) carry touch / pressure
- Small fibers ($A\delta$, C) carry pain

Both meet and synapse in substantia gelatinosa of [[dorsal gray horn]]
- If large fibers more active, close gate and less pain transmitted
- If small fibers dominate, open gate and 2nd order pain fibers activated
