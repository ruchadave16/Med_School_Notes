*Prominent memory loss with other cognitive disturbances but NO delirium*

- [[Alzheimers Disease|Alzheimers]]
	- Median temporal atrophy
	- Neurofibrillary tangles (tau), amyloid b plaques
- [[Lewy Body Dementia]]
	- Fluctuating, pleasant hallucinations, Parkinsonism
	- Normal radiology
	- Lewy bodies build up
- [[Vascular Dementia]]
	- Decrease in function slowly
	- Multiple infarcts
- [[Frontotemporal dementia]]
	- Personality changes
	- [[Kluver-Bucy Syndrome]]
	- Atrophy of frontal and temporal lobe
	- Pick bodies, tau +
- [[Huntingtons Disease]]
	- Atrophy of caudate and putamen
	- Gliosis
- [[Creutzfeldt-Jakob]]
	- Rapid progression
	- T2 hyperintensities of cortex, thalamus, basal ganglia
	- Spongiform
	
### Risk Factors
- Disease states
- Cerebrovascular disease
- Pseudodementia 
- Intracranial processes 
	- CNS infections like HIV
	- [[TBI]]
	- Radiation
	- Tumors
- Seizure disorders
- Metabolic disorders
- Endocrinopathies
- Nutrition
	- [[Beriberi]]
	- [[Pellagra]]
	- [[Pernicious anemia]]
- Toxins
	- Alcohol
	- Inhalants
	- Sedative-hypnotics
	- Anxiolytics
	- Anticonvulsants
	- Antineoplastic meds
	- Heavy metals
	- Insecticides
	- Solvents
### Workup for Reversible
- Labs: CBC, CMP, TSH, RPR-VDRL, HIV, B12, Folate, UA
- EKG
- CXR
