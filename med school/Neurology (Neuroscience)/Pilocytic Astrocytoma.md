### Etiology
- Common in young patients, especially cerebellar fossa
-  [[BRAF]] mutation: V600E
	- Associated with [[Neurofibromatosis 1|NF1]]
### Pathophysiology
- [[Rosenthal Fibers]] in ECF
- Eosinophilic granular bodies 
- Elongated tumor cells w long think cytoplasmic processes
