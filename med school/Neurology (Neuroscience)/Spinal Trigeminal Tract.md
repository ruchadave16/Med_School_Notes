*Pain, temperature, non-discriminative touch from face*

1) Cell bodies of pain fibers in [[trigeminal ganglion]]
	- Axons descend in spinal trigeminal tract and synapse at spinal trigeminal nuclei
		- Caudal [[pons]] -> cervical cord (C3)
		- Continuous fibers with [[Lissauer's tract]]
	- *Lesion*: **I/L** pain and temp loss on face
	- Blood supply = PICA -- lateral [[medulla]] infarct
		- Remember that [[STT]] is C/L pain and temp of body, this is I/L pain and temp of face
	- ![[Screenshot 2026-03-26 at 7.53.33 PM.png]]

2) Cell body in spinal trigeminal nuclei
	- Ascend and cross in trigeminal lemniscus (posterior to medial lemniscus)
	- Same tract as chief sensory trigeminal nuclei
	- *Lesion*: C/L loss of pain and temp from face and C/L fine touch from body
	- Terminates in [[VPM]] of thalamus

3) Cell body in VPM
	- Posterior limb of IC -> [[Somatosensory Cortex|somatosensory cortex]]
