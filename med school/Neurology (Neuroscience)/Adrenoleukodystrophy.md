### Etiology
- 1) X-linked recessive in ABCD1 protein -- peroxide <- Most COMMON
- 2) autosomal recessive from losing multiple peroxidal functions

### Pathophysiology
- Cannot catalyze very-long-chain-fatty-acids
	- Issues w myelination and [[Adrenal System|adrenal system]]
### Clinical Features
- Behavioral changes
- Adrenal insufficiency

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Lorenzo's Oil
	- 4:1 mix of oleic acid and erucic acid, extracted from rapeseed oil and olive oil
	- Can slow progression if started before symptoms show

### Complications
- Death by 10 years
