>[!Warning]
>Order CT without contrast if head trauma to rule out hemorrhage
#### Epidural Hematoma
- Cause: middle meningeal a. rupture
- **Lens shaped biconvex hematoma** on CT
- does NOT cross suture lines
- Blood compresses brain causing ↑ intracerebral pressure, herniation (midline shift), and death
- **Sx**: unconscious, then Lucid interval -> headache and rapid deterioration with ↑ [[ICP]]

#### Subdural Hematoma
- Cause: Rupture of bridging cerebral v. from *shear forces*
- **Crescent shaped hematoma** between dura and arachnoid on CT
- Does cross suture lines, causes midline shift
- Chronic
	- Elderly cuz brain atrophies or from alcohol abuse
	- Slow bleed for days to months
	- Sx: personality change, headache, seizure
- Acute
	- Sudden acceleration and slowing down
	- Density could be different
	- Sx: ICP ↑ within 3 weeks
![[Screenshot 2026-03-20 at 11.52.45 PM.png]]
#### Subarachnoid Hemorrhage
- Cerebral a. in subarachnoid space
- Spontaneous or traumatic
- 50% mortality
- Sx: "worst headache of life" -- thunderclap
	- lethargy
	- fever
	- stiff neck
	
![[Screenshot 2026-03-20 at 11.52.56 PM.png]]
#### Intraparenchymal Hemorrhage
- Cerebral arteries bleed into brain
- Round in shape, no connection to sulci
- Sx: focal deficits at location of hemorrhage
- Cause: 
	- Traumatic -- contusion
	- HTN causing hemorrhagic stroke, amyloid angiopathy, infection, tumor, AVM, blood clotting deficits, reperfusion injury post ischemic stroke
