### Pathway
- I/L with receptors synapsing on CN VII, IX, X
	- Anterior 2/3 = [[Facial Nerve|CN VII]]
	- Posterior 1/3 = [[Glossopharyngeal Nerve|CN IX]]
	- Epiglottis = [[Vagus Nerve|CN X]]
- All 3 synpase on solitary nucl. -> ascends central tegmental tract -> VPM -> gustatory cortex
