*Tone = resistance of muscle to passive strength*

- Muscles under constant stretch mediated by type II fibers through [[Reflexes|reflex]] circuit

**Alpha-gamma-coactivation**
- Ensures [[muscle spindles]] respond to change in length always
- Every alpha MN signal that contracts a muscle also sends gamma MN signal to shorten muscle spindle
#### Gain of Gamma MN
- Dynamic change based on activity
- [[Cerebellum]] sets tone through ↓ pathways like [[reticulospinal tracts]], [[vestibulospinal tracts]], and [[rubrospinal tracts]]
- ↓ gain means ↑ strength to stimulation contraction via reflex
	- When laying down
- ↑ gain means small strain causes ↑ reaction by alpha MN
	- Can go up when maintaining posture
	- Also in emotional states
