*Locations where subarachnoid space is large because of change of brain or brainstem space*

- Cisterna Magna -- dorsal -- cerebellum and medulla
- Superior cerebellar cistern -- dorsal -- cerebellum and midbrain
- Interpeduncular cistern -- ventral -- between cerebral penduncles, has CN III
- Pontine cistern -- ventral -- pons and medulla, has [[basilar a.]] and CN VI
- Ambient cistern -- lateral -- superior cerebellar cistern -> interpeduncular cistern, has CN IV, [[Posterior Cerebral A.|PCA]], SCA, basal v. of Rosenthal
- Cerebellopontine angle cistern -- opening for lateral fontine of Lushka, has CN VII, CN VIII
- Lumbar cistern -- inferior to end of spinal cord
