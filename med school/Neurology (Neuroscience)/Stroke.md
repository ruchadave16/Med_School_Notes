- Lacunar infarct -- noncortical infarct from occlusion of small distal end of deep penetrating branches
- [[Transient ischemic attack]] (TIA) -- short episode that resolves in < 1 hr
### Ischemic Stroke
*Due to lack of oxygenation of brain tissue*
- Penumbra -- part around infarct that is at risk of progressing but can be saved if reperfused
### Hemorrhagic Stroke
*Due to loss of blood from rupture*
### Locations
#### Cortical Stroke
- [[Anterior Cerebral A.|ACA]] stroke
	- C/L leg weakness more than arm or face and sensory impairment
	- Abulia -- ↓ motivation, stops tasks prematurely
	- [[Urinary incontinence]]
	- Judgement and emotion disturbance
	- [[Transcortical Motor Aphasia]]
	- Non-dominant limb [[ataxia]]
- [[Middle Cerebral A.|MCA]] stroke
	- Superior Division
		- C/L arm and lower face weakness and sensory loss
		- [[Dysarthria]]
		- I/L gaze neglect
		- [[Broca's Aphasia]], expressive
		- C/L hemineglect (non-dominant)
	- Inferior Divison
		- C/L hemineglect
		- [[Wernicke's Aphasia]]
		- C/L homonymous superior quadrantanopia
		- Constructional apraxia, behavior disturbance
	- Stem of MCA
		- Global aphasia
		- C/L hemiplegia + hemianesthesia
		- C/L homonymous hemianopia
		- I/L gaze preference
		- Non-dominant neglect
- [[Internal Carotid Artery|ICA]] stroke -- ACA and MCA both
	- C/L lower face, arm, and leg weakness
- [[Posterior Cerebral A.|PCA]] stroke
	- Spares motor cortex but early branches can affect brainstem
	- C/L Homonymous hemianopia
	- Memory loss / behavior change
	- Visual neglect ABSENT
- Subcortical stroke -- [[Lateral Striate A.|Lenticulostriate aa.]]
	- Centrum Semiovale -- anterior 2/3 of posterior limb of IC
	- Lower face, arm, leg equally affected, no cortical symptoms
#### Midbrain Stroke

![[Screenshot 2026-03-28 at 12.25.21 AM.png]]

- [[Weber syndrome]]
	- CNIII fascicle -- I/L [[CN III Palsy|oculomotor palsy]]
	- Cerebral peduncle -- C/L hemiparesis
	- Blood: Paramedian from top of [[Basilar A.|basilar a.]] or proximal [[Posterior Cerebral A.|PCA]]

- [[Claude syndrome]]
	- Dorsal midbrain tegmentum
	- CNIII fascicle -- I/L [[CN III Palsy|oculomotor palsy]]
	- Red nucleus -- C/L ataxia, rubral tremor, choreoathetosis
	- Superior cerebellar peduncle -- C/L ataxia
	- Blood: branches of [[basilar a.]] and proximal [[Posterior Cerebral A.|PCA]]

- [[Benedikt syndrome]]
	- Ventral midbrain tegmentum
	- I/L oculomotor palsy, C/L ataxia, C/L resting tremor and choreoathetosis
	- Same manifestation as Claude syndrome 

- [[Parinaud’s syndrome]]
	- Dorsal midbrain
	- Upward gaze palsy -- damage to posterior commisure
	- Light-near dissociation
		- damage the dorsal [[Edinger-Westphal nucl.|Edinger-Westphal nucleus]] (light reflex) but spare the rostroventral [[Edinger-Westphal nucl.|Edinger-Westphal nucleus]]
		- Pupils respond to light but not stimulus (pseudo Argyll Robertson pupil)
	- Convergence-retraction nystagmus when looking up
	- Lid retraction -- sclera above iris
	- [[Pinealoma]], SCA stroke
	- non-communicating hydrocephalus due to aqueduct compression 
#### Pons Stroke
- [[Locked in syndrome]]
	- Complete pontine stroke
	- Cortex and parts of midbrain preserved
	- Patient conscious
	- CN III preserved but not MLF -- vertical gaze only
	- No facial, tongue movements
	- Ddx: top of the basilar artery stroke, central pontine myelinolysis

- [[Medial Pontine Syndrome]] 
	- CN VI fascicule -- I/L abduction palsy
	- [[CN VI nucleus]] (PPRF) -- I/L [[horizontal gaze palsy]]
	- MLF -- I/L adduction palsy
	- Medial leminscus -- C/L body proprioception and vibration loss
	- Corticobulbar and corticospinal -- C/L UMN body
	- Ddx: Basilar artery (paramedian branches) occlusion, Multiple sclerosis (May be isolated INO)

- [[Lateral Pontine Syndrome]] *AICA occlusion*
	- CN VII nucleus and fascicule -- I/L facial n. palsy
	- CN VIII -- hearing loss
	- Spinothalamic tract -- C/L body sensory pain and temp
	- Trigeminal spinal tract -- I/L face sensory pain and temp
	- Sympathetic tract -- I/L [[Horner’s Syndrome]]
	- Vestibular n. -- ataxia, nystagmus, vertigo, nausea, vomiting
	- Spinocerebellar tract: I/L coordination
#### Medullary Stroke
![[Screenshot 2026-03-28 at 12.46.49 AM.png]]

- [[Medial Medullary Syndrome]]: Dejerine syndrome from *ASA occlusion*
	- [[Hypoglossal Nerve|CN XII]] I/L paralysis of tongue (deviates to lesion)
	- Medial lemniscus: C/L body sensation (vibration, proprioception)
	- [[Corticospinal Tract|CST]]: C/L UMN arm and leg weakness

- [[Lateral Medullary Syndrome]] Wallenberg Syndrome from *PICA occlusion*
	- Nucleus ambiguus: I/L CN IX and CN X motor -- hoarseness, dysphagia
	- Nucleus solitarius: I/L low taste
	- Dorsal motor nucleus X: PSY innervation to abdomen / thorax
	- Spinothalamic tract: C/L body sensory (pain and temp)
	- Trigeminal spinal nucleus: I/L face sensory
	- Sympathetic tract: I/L [[Horner’s Syndrome]]
	- Vestibular nucleus: nystagmus, vertigo, nausea, vomiting
	- Spinocerebellar tract: I/L ataxia
