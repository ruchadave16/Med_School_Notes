- Lacunar infarct -- noncortical infarct from occlusion of small distal end of deep penetrating branches
- [[Transient ischemic attack]] (TIA) -- short episode that resolves in < 1 hr
### Ischemic Stroke
*Due to lack of oxygenation of brain tissue*
- Penumbra -- part around infarct that is at risk of progressing but can be saved if reperfused
### Hemorrhagic Stroke
*Due to loss of blood from rupture*
