*Crest cells from edges of neural plate that migrate to lateral of neural tube*
**Trunk**
- Sympathetic and parasympathetic ganglia
	- Prevertebral (celiac, sup. mesenteric, etc)
	- Ganglia on walls of organs
	- [[Sympathetic trunk]]
- Dorsal root ganglia
- [[Chromaffin cells]] (adrenal neurosecratory)
- [[Melanocytes]]
- [[Schwann cells]]

**Cranial**
- Parasympathetic ganglia of CN III, VII, IX, X
- Sensory ganglion -- CN V, VII, IX, X
- [[Pharyngeal Arches|Pharyngeal arches]] bones and cartilage
- Pia and arachnoid mater
- Neurocranium bones
- Odontoblasts (teeth dentin)
- Aorticopulmonary septum

>[!Clinical] Neurocristopathy
>Conditions from abnormal migration of neural crest cells
>- Hirschsprung syndrome
>- [[Albinism]]
>- [[Pheochromocytoma]]
>- [[Neurofibromatosis 1]]
>- [[Sturge-Weber]]
>- [[Tuberous sclerosis]]
