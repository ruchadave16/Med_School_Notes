### Etiology
- 80% in adults = enterovirus: [[Coxsackievirus|coxsackie virus]] B, echovirus, enterovirus 71
- [[Arbovirus]]
- [[HSV2]] > [[HSV1]]
	- HSV2 when [[encephalitis]]
- VZV
- HIV
- EBV

### Clinical Features
- Fever
- Headache
- Neck stiffness
- Photophobia

### Diagnosis
- [[Lumbar Puncture]] to check [[CSF]]
	- **NORMAL** glucose
	- ↑ protein
	- Clear appearance
	- ↑ [[lymphocytes]]

### Treatment
- Supportive treatment
- IV [[acyclovir]] if encephalitis suspected
