### Etiology
- Creutzfeldt–Jakob disease (most common)
- Mad Cow Disease -- contaminated meat
- Gerstmann–Sträussler–Scheinker disease 
- Fatal familial insomnia
- Kuru

- Incidence = 1 in 1 million
### Pathophysiology
- CNS diseases with abnormal form of a cellular protein ([[Prions|prion]])
	- Contagious
- Causes neuronal death, synapse loss, **microvascularization** 
- Spongiform transformation

### Clinical Features
- Behavior changes
- Rapidly progressing dementia
- Startle myoclonus
### Diagnosis
- EEG
	- Starts diffuse and non-specific
	- Changes to stereotyped high-voltage slow- (1- to 2-Hz) and sharp-wave complexes
- MRI
	- T2 FLAIR hyperintensities near [[basal ganglia]]
- CSF
	- RT-QuIC -- protein amplification
### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- Fatal in abt 7 months
