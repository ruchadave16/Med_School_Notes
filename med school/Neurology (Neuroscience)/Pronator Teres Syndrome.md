### Clinical Features
- Motor loss (wrist flexion, thumb movement, finger flexion)
- Sensory loss (lateral palm, 3.5 fingers)
- Pain over forearm + [[Anterior Interosseous Syndrome]] + [[Carpal Tunnel Syndrome]] + proximal palm
- Papal sign
