### Etiology
- [[JC Virus]] reactivation 
- Onset after immunosuppression
	- [[Leukemia]] or [[lymphoma]]
	- [[HIV]]
	- Natalizumab
	- Rituximab
	- All others.

### Pathophysiology
- Virus is latent in kidneys and becomes active
- Mutates into neurotropic strain
- Infects [[Oligodendrocytes]] -- demyelinating lesions
	- Granular white matter on gross exam
- Large, atypical astrocytes, viral inclusions

### Clinical Features
- Acute onset with cognitive defects, visual symptoms, gait improvement, etc.

### Diagnosis
- Need clinical suspicion for immunosuppressed because it looks like [[Multiple Sclerosis|MS]]
- MRI:
	- White matter lesions hypointense on T1 and hyper on T2/FLAIR
- CSF:
	- PCR for JC virus + (95% sensitivity)
