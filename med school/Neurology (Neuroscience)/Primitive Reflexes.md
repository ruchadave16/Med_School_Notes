*Present at birth -> disappear by 1 year*
- Inhibited by frontal lobe
- Sign of CNS pathology
#### Moro Reflex
- From supine, lift baby's head and let go -> abduct and extends arms, and draws in
- Birth -> 4 months
#### Rooting
- Stroke skin near mouth and baby turns towards it
- Birth -> 3-4 months
#### Suckling
- Touching roof of mouth should elicit suckling response
#### Palmar Grasp
- Fingers should grasp if palm is stroked
- Birth -> 3-4 months
#### [[Plantar Reflex]]
- Upward, spreading of toes  = normal
- + if adult = [[UMN Lesion]]
- Birth -> 6-8 months
#### Galant Reflex
- Prone, stroke one side and swings legs to that side
- Birth -> 3-4 months
