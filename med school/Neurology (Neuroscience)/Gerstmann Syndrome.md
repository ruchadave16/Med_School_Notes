### Etiology
- Stroke
- [[TBI]]
- Tumor

### Pathophysiology
- Lesion around angular gyri in dominant [[parietal lobe]]

### Clinical Features
- Agraphia -- cannot write but motor strength intact
- Acalculia -- can't do basic math
- Finger agnosia -- can't name fingers of hand
- R-L confusion -- can't distinguish R/L on self or others
