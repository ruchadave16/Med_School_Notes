- Ventral carries crude touch 
- Lateral carries fast localized pain + temperature

Spinal Cord
- Blood supply = ASA
- *Lesion* = Loss of P&T **below** lesion C/L
- [[Anterior cord syndrome]] -- loss of pain bilat because only 1 ASA

Medulla
- posterior to olivary nucleus, anterior to inf. cerebellar peduncle
- Blood supply = PICA

Pons/midbrain
- Pons -- [[basilar a.]]
- Midbrain -- [[Posterior Cerebral A.|PCA]]
- *Lesion* of brainstem means C/L loss of P&T of body
### Pathway 
**3 neuron pathway**

1) Cell body in [[dorsal root ganglion]]
	- Enters gray horn and can ascend or descend 1 neuron in [[Lissauer's tract]]
	- Synapse in I/L dorsal gray horn
		- *Referred pain*: visceral nociceptors enter same root as cutaneous neurons
	- *Lesion* of PSA (post. spinal aa.) = loss of pain and temp AT the level on ipsilateral side

2) Cell body in [[dorsal gray horn]]
	- Cross in **anterior white commissure** (ascends 1-3 levels)
		- [[Syringomyelia]] causes bilateral loss of pain and temp. 1-2 segments at the level
	- Ascend towards [[thalamus]] in STT
		- Sacral is lateral, cervical is medial
		- Blood supply = ASA
		- *Lesion* = Loss of P&T **below** lesion C/L
		- [[Anterior cord syndrome]] -- loss of pain bilat. because only 1 ASA
	- Synapse in [[VPL]] of thalamus
	- *Lesion* in midbrain = C/L loss of P&T in body

3) Cell body in [[VPL]] thalmus
	- Fibers pass through posterior limb of IC -> [[somatosensory cortex]]
	- *Lesion* of VPL means C/L loss of all sensation of body
		- [[Central pain syndrome]]: from thalamic stroke (PCA), AVM, HTN
	- *Lesion* of [[internal capsule]]
