### Etiology
- Autosomal recessive defect to Galactocerebroside B-galactosidase

### Pathophysiology
- Accumulation of galactocerebroside <- causes toxicity that [[oligodendrocytes]] are sensitive to
- Severe gross atrophy of brain from extensive myelin and oligodendrocyte loss
- Globoid macrophages filled with PAS + myelin breakdown products
