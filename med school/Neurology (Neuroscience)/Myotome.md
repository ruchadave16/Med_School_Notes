*“group of muscles with common action”*
- C2 -- look at shoe (cervical flexion)
- C3 -- falling tree (neck to side)
- C4 -- I'm not sure (shoulder elevation)
- C5 -- arms out wide (shoulder abduction)
- C6 -- sniff wrist (elbow flexion, wrist extension)
- C7 -- zombies in heaven (elbow extension, wrist flexion)
- C8 -- doing great (thumb extension, finger flexion, ulnar deviation)
- T1 -- all done (finger abduction)

- L2 -- lift shoe (hip flexion)
- L3 -- extend knee (knee extension)
- L4 -- stop the door (dorsiflexion ankle)
- L5 -- toes divide (great toe extension)
- S1 -- lift a ton (plantar flexion)
- S2 -- come back to you (knee flexion)

Split into: 
- Dorsal epimeres:
	- Give rise to **epaxial** muscles – erector spinae
	- Innervated by [[dorsal rami]] 
- Ventral hypomeres
	- Give rise to **hypaxial** muscles – body wall + limbs
	- Innervated by [[ventral rami]]
