#### Inferior Cerebellar Peduncle
- Medulla -> cerebellum
- Mostly input
- [[Spinocerebellar Tracts]], vestibular input, [[Inferior olivary nucleus]]
- Blood Supply: PICA
- *Lesion = I/L ataxia, vertigo, nystagmus*

#### Middle Cerebellar Peduncle
- Pons -> cerebellum
- Input only
- [[Pontocerebellar Tract]] from C/L cerebral cortex
- Blood Supply: AICA
- Motor planning information

#### Superior Cerebellar Peduncle
- Cerebellum -> midbrain
- Mainly output
- [[Deep cerebellar nuclei]] -> VL thalamus and red nucleus, crosses in midbrain
- I/L signs (double crossign)
