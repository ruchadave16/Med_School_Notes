### Etiology
- Generally infectious

### Pathophysiology
- Inflammation or infection of [[Brain Meninges|meninges]], usually due to infection

 **Categories**
1) [[Acute pyogenic meningitis]]
2) [[Aseptic meningitis]]
3) [[Chronic meningitis]]
### Clinical Features
- Fever, headache
- Photophobia
- Nuchal rigidity
### Diagnosis
- Physical Exam
	- Kernig sign, Brudzinski sign
- [[Lumbar puncture]]
	- [[CSF]] testing

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- [[Hydrocephalus]] -> CN III, IV, VI, VII palsys
- Death
- Seizures
