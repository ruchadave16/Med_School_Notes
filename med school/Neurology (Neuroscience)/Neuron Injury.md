#### Acute Neuron Injury
- "Red Neurons"
- Seen after severe CSN hypoxia / ischemia, severe [[hypoglycemia]]
	- Earliest marker of cell death 6-12 hrs after
	
1. Shrinking of cell body
2. Pyknosis of nucleus
3. Nucleolus disappears
4. Nissl substance loss
5. ↑↑ eosinophilia

 ![[Screenshot 2026-03-24 at 9.56.51 PM.png]]
#### Subacute Neuron Injury / Degeneration
- Seen in [[Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis|ALS]] and [[Alzheimers Disease|Alzheimers]]
- Loss of synapses followed by cell death
	- Glial changes help identify
#### Axonal Reaction
- Change to cell body when regeneration of axon
- Seen in [[ventral horn]] when motor neurons are cut
- ↑ [[protein synthesis]]
	- Rounding of cell body
	- Peripheral displacement of nucleus
	- Enlargened nucleolus
	- Central chromatolysis -- Nissl bodies to periphery
- *[[Wallerian degeneration]] = degeneration of axon after disruption of nerve fibers*

#### Inclusions

- Pick bodies -- [[Pick Disease]]
- Cowdry A / B -- [[herpes]]
- Negri bodies -- [[Rabies Virus]]
