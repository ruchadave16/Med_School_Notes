*Withdrawal of [[CSF]] from subarachnoid space.*
- Lay patient on side in flexed position to open up back
- Needle between midline of L3 and L4 spinous processes into lumbar cisterns
- Avoid end of spinal cord at L1 / L2

- Opening pressure normal = 70 - 180 mmH2O
#### Spinal Myelography
- Lumbar pucture, remove 3-6cc of CSF
- Inject iodinated contrast
- Xray / CT

*Use in patients who cannot go under MRI*
