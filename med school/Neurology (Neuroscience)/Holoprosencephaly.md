### Etiology
- SHH mutations
- [[Patau Syndrome|Trisomy 13]], [[Edwards Syndrome|Trisomy 18]], [[Meckel Syndrome]]
- [[Fetal alcohol syndrome]] severe manifestation

### Pathophysiology
- No cleavage of prosencephalon into 2 hemispheres in week 5-6

### Clinical Features
- Lobar holoprosencephaly
	- Narrowing on midline structures and facial features
- Semilobar holoprosencephaly
	- Incomplete separation of L/R cortex
- Alobar holoprosencephaly
	- No separation
