*Type of superficial [[Reflexes|reflex]] that assesses [[Corticospinal Tract|corticospinal tract]]*

Mediated by mechanoceptors on skin

- Stroking lateral sole from heel -> little toe and then sweep medially across ball of foot -> big toe

**Adults**
- Response Normal: Plantar flexion, toe flexes, or silent
- Abnormal: Dorsiflexion, toe dorsiflexes or toes fan 

**Infants**
- Expect dorsiflexion until 6-8 months
