### Etiology
- Autosomal recessive
- ATP7B gene on chromosome 13

### Pathophysiology
- Deficiency of ceruloplasmin so copper cannot be excreted in bile and ↑ copper in **blood and urine**
- Deposits in liver first and then kidney, cornea, brain

### Clinical Features
- Secondary Parkinsonism
	- Wing beating tremor
- Cognitive disturbances
- Brown deposits of copper on outer cornea
- Liver damage signs
