### Etiology
- Autosomal recessive
- Loss of function of ATP7B gene on chromosome 13
- Presentation in early childhood or mid 40s

### Pathophysiology
- Deficiency of ceruloplasmin so copper cannot be excreted in bile and ↑ copper in **blood and urine**
- Deposits in liver first and then kidney, cornea, brain

### Clinical Features
- Secondary Parkinsonism
	- Wing beating tremor
- Cognitive disturbances -> mainly [[basal ganglia]]
- Brown deposits of copper on outer cornea (Kayser-Fleischer) (ring around eye)
- Liver damage signs -- acute or chronic

### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- Low serum ceruloplasmin
	- Increase in hepatic copper content
	- ↑ urinary excretion of copper
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Long term copper chelation therapy has SIGNIFICANT impact
- Liver transplant if cirrhosis 
### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
