### Etiology
- Newborns (< 6 months)
	- [[Group B Streptococcus|Group B streptococcus]]
	- [[E. Coli]]
	- [[Listeria monocytogenes]]
- Young Kids (till 6-8 years)
	- [[Streptococcus Pneumonia|Strep Pneumo]]
	- [[Neisseria Meningitidis]]
	- [[Hemophilus Influenza|H. Influenza]] type B (unvaccinated)
- Most Individuals (8-60 years)
	- [[Streptococcus Pneumonia|Strep Pneumo]]
	- [[Neisseria Meningitidis|N. meningitidis]] <- #1 cause for teenagers
- >60 years
	- [[Streptococcus Pneumonia|Strep Pneumo]]
	- [[Neisseria Meningitidis|N. meningitidis]]
	- [[Hemophilus Influenza|H. influenza]] Type B
	- [[Listeria Monocytogenes]]
	- [[Group B Streptococcus|Group B strep]]
### Pathophysiology
- Located at convexities
	- Purulent material 
- Marked acute inflammatory (PMNs) infiltrate

### Clinical Features
- Rapid onset
- **Fever**
- **Neck stiffness**
- Impaired consciousness
- Headache
- Photophobia
- Irritability w seizures
- Papilledema
- Nausea / vomiting

**Physical Exam**
- Kernig sign -- Supine with hip flexed at 90, extend leg has pain
- Brudzinski sign -- supine and flex head passively to chest
	- + test means flexion of knee or hips
- Rotating head makes headache worse

### Diagnosis
- **CT FIRST**
	- If ↑↑ ICP, LP can cause herniation
- Lumbar Puncture (CSF)
	- ↑ pressure
	- ↑ [[neutrophils]]
	- ↑ protein
	- ↓ glucose

### Treatment
- DO NOT DELAY if suspicious of bacterial meningitis 
	- Start treatment before LP
- Start IV [[Penicillins|Penicillin]] or [[ceftriaxone]] IMMEDIATELY
### Complications
- [[Cerebral Edema|Cerebral edema]]
- [[Hydrocephalus]] (comm. or noncomm)
- ↓ blood supply so infarct common
- Hippocampal infarct
- [[Waterhouse-Friderichsen Syndrome]]
