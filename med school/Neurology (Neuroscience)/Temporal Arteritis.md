*Chronic inflammatory disease affecting medium to large vessels*
### Etiology
- Age > 50

### Pathophysiology
- Granulomatous inflammation most likely [[T Lymphocytes|T cell]] mediated
- Common in temporal but also aorta, vertebral and ophthalmic arteries.

![[Screenshot 2026-05-05 at 10.05.44 PM.png]]
### Clinical Features
- Fever, fatigue, weight loss
- Tenderness over temporal artery and facial pain
	- **Pain upon palpation**
- Visual disturbances if ophthalmic a. 
- Jaw claudification
- Polymyalgia rheumatica
- Amaurosis fugax

### Diagnosis
- Temporal artery biopsy for diagnosis
- Elevated **ESR = early finding**

### Treatment
- Start [[Synthetic Corticosteroids|corticosteroids]]
- Anti [[IL-6]] therapy if resistant to steroids

### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
