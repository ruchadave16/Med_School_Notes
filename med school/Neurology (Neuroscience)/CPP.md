*Net pressure gradient that drives CBF delivery to brain*

>[!Equation] CPP = [[MAP]] - [[ICP]]
>Normal = 60-80 mmHg
>Normal MAP = 70-100 mmHg

If MAP ↓ or ICP ↑, perfusion ↓ 
- Autoregulation through PCO2
	- if MAP ↓, cerebral vasodilation so blood flow ↑ and CPP normal
	- CPP proportional to PCO2 until > 90 mmHg
#### Therapeutic Hyperventilation
↓ PCO2 causes vasoconstriction -> ↓ blood flow -> ↓ ICP
