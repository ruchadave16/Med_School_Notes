#### Cerebellum Formation

#### Medulla
- Caudal medulla has canal closed (same layout as spinal cord)
- Rostral has 4th ventricle open so motor n. medial and sensory lateral
#### Pons
- Open 4th ventricle so same as motor n. medial and sensory lateral
#### Midbrain
- Alar = dorsal and basal = ventral with aqueduct in middle
