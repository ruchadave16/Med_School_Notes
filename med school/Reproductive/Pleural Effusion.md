### Pathophysiology
- Build-up of fluid inside [[Pleura|pleural cavity]] due to either excess formation or reduced removal / blockage
- Transudate vs exudate
	- Transudative = systemic factors that influence the formation and absorption of pleural fluid are altered.
		- Fluid leaks from ↑ [[Hydrostatic Pressure|hydrostatic pressure]] or ↓ [[Oncotic Pressure|oncotic pressure]]
		- Causes: [[Chronic Heart Failure|chronic left heart failure]] or [[liver cirrhosis]], [[nephrotic syndrome]], [[Hypoalbuminemia]], 
	- Exudative = local factors that influence the formation and absorption of pleural fluid are altered
		- Blocked blood / lymph flow
		- Inflammation, injury, infection, or tumors
### Clinical Features
- Chest pain
- Dyspnea
- Dry, non-productive cough

### Diagnosis
- PE:
	- dull to percussion
	- ↓ tactile fremitus
- Labs
	- Pleural fluid analysis
		- Pleural fluid protein/serum protein >0.5
		- Pleural fluid LDH/serum LDH >0.6
		- Pleural fluid LDH is more than two-thirds the normal upper limit for serum
	- IN exudative:
		- Description of the appearance of the fluid
		- Glucose level
		- Differential cell count
		- Microbiologic studies
		- Cytology
- Imaging
	- Chest x-ray
		- Blunting of the Costophrenic Angle
		- Blunting of Cardiophrenic Angle
		- Meniscus Sign:
		- Increased Density/Homogeneous Opacification:
		- Obscuring of the Hemidiaphragm and Heart Border
