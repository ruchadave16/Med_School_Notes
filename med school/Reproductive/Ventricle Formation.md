Happens along with [[brain formation]]

- Cavities in telencephalon -> lateral ventricles
- Diencephalic cavity -> third ventricle
- Mesencephalic cavity -> cerebral aqueduct
- Opening between lateral + 3rd ventricle -> intraventricular foramina
- Rhombencephalic cavity -> 4th ventricle
