##### Day 28 (Week 4)
- Superior neuropore of [[neural tube]] closes
- Neural tube expands -- primary brain vesicles + 2 flexures made
	- Prosencephalon -- becomes cerebrum + diencephalon
	- Mesencephalon -- becomes midbrain
	- Rhombencephalon -- becomes pons, medulla, cerebellum
	- Cervical flexure -- between rhombencephalon + spinal cord
		- Disappears
	- Cephalic flexure -- between prosencephalon and mesencephalon
		- Exists as bend

##### Week 5
- Secondary brain vesicles form making 5 enlargements
- Prosencephalon split by telencephalic flexure
	- Telencephalon  -- turns into cerebrum
	- Diencephalon -- forms thalamus, hypothalamus, epithalamus
- Rhombencephalon split by pontine flexure (eventually disappears)
	- Metencephalon -- pons + cerebellum
	- Myelencephalon -- medulla
