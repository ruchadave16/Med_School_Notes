*Any agent that causes abnormality in fetal development*
- Drugs, toxins, infections, maternal change to body
#### Viral
- [[Rubella Virus]]
- [[Cytomegalovirus|CMV]] -- 1/200 pregnancies affected
	- Most common cause of congenital abnormalities
- [[HSV2]]
- [[HHV3|Varicella-Zoster virus]]
	- First 2 trimester = abortion, neuro defect, eye defect, 
	- Close to term = chickenpox, high risk of shingles
- [[HIV]]
	- Chronic diarrhea
	- Recurrent infections
