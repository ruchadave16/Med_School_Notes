**> 100.4 F (38 C)** rectal
- Viral can be > 40C

**Measurement**
- Birth - 3 months: rectal
- 3 month - 4 years: rectal or temporal artery
- >4 years: oral

>[!Note] Red Flags
>< 28 days
>Lethargy, cyanosis
>Persistent irritability
>Respiratory distress
>Petechiae
>Dehydration
>Altered mental status / seizure

**Occurrence**
0-28 days
- EMERGENCY
- Full evaluation

1-3 months
- Significant
- Labs, inflammatory markers

3-36 months
- Assess based on appearance
#### Mechanism
- ↑ [[PGE2]]
- Hypothalamus raises set-point
