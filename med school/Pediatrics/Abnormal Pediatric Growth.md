#### Caloric Insufficiency
- ↓ intake *most common*
	- weight decelerates first

- malabsorption
	- greasy stools
	- diarrhea
	- nutrient deficiencies
	- weight falters despite intake
	- consider [[Celiac Disease]] and [[Cystic Fibrosis]]
	
- excessive loss
	- vomiting
	- chronic diarrhea
	- protein loss
	- glucosuria
	- weight dips with ongoing losses
	
- ↑ energy requirements
	- tachypnea
	- sweating
	- chronic inflammation
	- hypermetabolism
	- weight falters even though frequent feeds
