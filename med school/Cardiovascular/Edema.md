*Abnormal accumulation of excess fluid within tissues*

- [[Subcutaneous edema]]
	- Dependent → influenced by gravity
	- Pitting
- [[Periorbital edema]]
	- In eyelids, sign of renal disease
	- In children, consider hypoalbuminemia from nephrotic syndrome
- [[Pulmonary edema]]
	- Associated with CHF
- [[Cerebral edema]]

**Inflammatory Edema**
- [[Acute Inflammation]] → vascular permeability → fluid and intravascular proteins lost
- Results in protein-rich fluid mix called exudate

![[Screenshot 2026-01-24 at 5.11.06 PM.png]]

**Non-Inflammatory Edema**
- Vessel wall intact so intravascular proteins maintained but hydrostatic / oncotic pressure causes loss of fluid
- Results in protein-poor fluid called transudate

**Pitting Edema**: Most common, capillary associated
**Non-Pitting Edema**: interstitum or lymphatic causes, does NOT respond to diuretics
#### Mechanisms
- ↑ [[Hydrostatic Pressure|hydrostatic pressure]] in capillaries
	- ↑ venous pressure
		- [[Chronic Heart Failure|Chronic left heart failure]] -> pulmonary edema
		- [[Right heart failure]] -> peripheral edema
		- Incompetent valves in veins
		- Venous obstruction
		- Gravity
	- ↓ arteriolar resistance
		- Physiological (exercise, [[heat edema]])
		- Pharm
	- ↑ blood volume
		-  [[Congestive Heart Failure]]
		- [[Renal failure]]
		- ↑ fluid intake
- ↓ [[Oncotic Pressure|oncotic pressure]] in capillaries (Inadequate [[albumin]] synthesis or loss of it)
	- [[liver disease]] or [[alcoholism]] 
	- [[nephrotic syndrome]] (protein loss in urine)
	- [[malnutrition]] protein
- ↑ capillary permeabiliity
	- Vascular damage
- ↑ oncotic pressure in interstitial -> Impaired reabsorption of interstitial fluid = [[lymphedema]]
	- Lymphatic scarring from [[chemotherapy]]
	- Lymph node surgical excision
	- Lymphatic filariasis
