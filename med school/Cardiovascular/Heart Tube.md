- [[Cardiac Looping]]

- Division of this into 4 chambers + formation of [[Heart Valves|valves]] led by [[endothelial cushion]] cells at 4th week

| Structure           | Developed Structure                                                           |

#### Atria Septation
![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 10.50.52 PM.png]]

- Originally 1 cavity
- Septum primum grows towards endothelial cushion cells
	- Opening = ostium primum
- Tissue is reabsorbed at top forming the ostium secundum (2 holes as seen in (c))
	- Another enfolding called septum secundum forms at the same time (d)
- Complete separation of 2 atria happens when septum II fuses (f)
- If ostium secundum is patent -> [[Atrial Septal Defect|atrial septal defect]] (foramen ovale forms) <- shown in F
#### Ventricular Septation
- 3 components: muscular, inlet, outlet
- Interventricular septum forms from tissue coming upwards to the endothelial cushions from the apex (c)
- Fusion of these two seen in F
#### Outflow Tract Formation

![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 11.08.26 PM.png]]

- [[Neural crest cells]] control development of this by forming aorticopulmonary septum  to make passageways 
- 2 ridges along truncus arteriosus -- trucal and bulbar
	- Truncal divides into proximal ascending aorta and pulmonary trunk
	- Fusion of 2 ensures pulm trunk = right and aorta = left
