### Etiology
- Primary causes
- Risk factors

### Pathophysiology
- Pulmonary artery connects to LV and aorta to RV
- Oxygen poor blood on right side of heart to body without lungs
- Rich blood through left side to lungs
- Ductus / Foramen ovale MUST stay open during early days of life

### Clinical Features
- Baby blue immediately after birth

### Diagnosis
- Chest Xray: "egg on a string” heart shape

### Treatment
- [[PDE1]] to keep ductus arteriosus
- Balloon atrioseptostomy to keep open

Surgery
- Atrial switch: Mustard or Senning procedure (>20 yrs issues = Systemic RV dysfunction, Baffle obstruction, Baffle leak, Atrial arrhythmias)
