*Site of gas, nutrient, and waste exchange in body as well as extravasation of WBCs*

![[Screenshot 2026-04-28 at 10.43.56 AM.png]]
- Fenestrated
	- ↑ rate of exchange because ↑ porosity
	- tissue specialized for high volume exchange, such as the intestinal villi (absorption of nutrients), endocrine glands (production of hormones), and renal glomeruli (maintenance of vascular volume, electrolytes, and pH)
- Discontinued
	- highest exchange out of capillary
	- liver and spleen 
- Continued
	- ↓ exchange
	- lung, brain

![[Screenshot 2026-04-28 at 10.43.29 AM.png]]
### [[Diffusion]]
Passive transport from high to low concentration
### Bulk Flow
water and small lipid-insoluble molecules move through due to **[[Hydrostatic Pressure]]** and **[[Osmosis|Osmotic pressure]]** determine fluid movement
- If water is lost from capillaries, it is taken up by lymphatics 
- this happens around endothelial cells, not through them 

>[!Tip]
>Fluid movement $J_v = K_f ((P_c - P_i) - (\pi_c - \pi_i))$
>Where P = hydrostatic pressure, $\pi$ = [[Osmosis|osmotic pressure]], c = capillary, i = interstitial, $K_f$ = water permeability coefficient

If net pressure > 0, filtration of fluid out of capillary (generally at arterial end)
If net pressure < 0, absorption of fluid into capillary (generally at venous end)

*P ↓ from atrial to venous end*
