### Etiology
- [[Viral Infections|Viral infection]]
- Secondary
	- [[Myocardial Infarction|MI]] -> [[Dressler's Syndrome]]
	- Radiation to mediastinum
	- [[Acute Bacterial Pneumonia|pneumonia]] or [[pleuritis]]
	- Metastasis
	- [[Uremia]]

### Pathophysiology
- Inflammation of [[pericardium]] around the heart
- Causes irritation of epicardium -- sharp pain aggravated by inspiration, relieved by sitting up and leaning forward
- Friction rub

1) Serous pericarditis
	- Noninfectious inflammatory diseases
2) Fibrinous pericarditis
	- ![[Screenshot 2026-05-27 at 4.00.34 PM.png]]
3) Purulent pericarditis
	- Active infection -> can cause [[constrictive pericarditis]]
	- Pus visible
4) Hemorrhagic pericarditis
	- Malignant neoplasm spread
