### Etiology
- #1 congenital heart condition
- 15% have 22q11 deletion syndrome ([[DiGeorge Syndrome]])
### Pathophysiology

1) [[Pulmonary stenosis]] reduces flow of blood to lungs
2) [[Ventricular Septal Defect]] causes mixing of oxygen rich from LV and poor from RV
3) [[Right Ventricular Hypertrophy]] due to increased blood from LV to RV
4) [[Aorta]] shifted to right to be above ventricular septal defect

![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 11.48.46 PM.png]]
### Clinical Features
4 heart defects, **blue/gray skin when crying or feeding** (Tet spells)
- Squatting increases systemic vascular resistance which in turn decreases right-to-left shunt, improves cyanosis

### Diagnosis
- Chest X-ray
	- Boot shaped heart

### Treatment
- Surgical intevention in 1st year

### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
