#### Definition
Heart attack because of lack of [[blood]] and oxygen to heart

#### Etiology
- Blocked [[Coronary Arteries|coronary artery]] → [[Coagulative Necrosis|coagulative necrosis]]
	- Coronary artery stenosis/occlusion
	- Coronary artery spasm (Prinzmetal's)
	- Hypotension / shock
	- Severe anemia (↓ O₂ carrying capacity)
	- Hypoxia (pneumonia, PE, CO poisoning)
	- Coronary steal syndrome
	- Tachycardia (↑ HR = ↑ demand + ↓ diastolic filling time)
	- Hypertension (↑ afterload = ↑ wall stress)
	- LVH (↑ muscle mass = ↑ demand)
	- Fever / thyrotoxicosis
	- Cocaine use (vasospasm + ↑ HR/BP)
- Risk factors
	- low [[tPA]] (increased chance of thrombosis of coronary artery because ↓ plasmin)
#### Pathophysiology
- Oxygen Demand > Oxygen Supply
- Vulnerable subendocardium
	- Highest wall stress here (most oxygen demand)
	- Last to be perfused
- [[STEMI]] vs [[NSTEMI]]
	- STEMI = transmural ischemia (full depth of tissue)
	- NSTEMI = only subendocardial ischemia 

1) ATP depletion -> anaerobic metabolism -- angina
2) 20-40 min = irreversible cell death -- [[troponin]] release starts
3) 1-4 hrs = [[coagulative necrosis]] -- troponin ↑
4) 4-24 hrs = [[Neutrophils|neutrophil]] infiltrate, [[contraction bands]], edema -- peak [[troponin]]
5) 1-3 days = [[macrophage]] infiltrate -- risk of wall rupture
6) 3-7 days = granulation, [[fibroblasts]]
7) Weeks - months = collagen scarring
###### Categories
- Type I -- Spontaneous MI
	- atherosclerotic plaque rupture, erosion, or dissection → coronary thrombosis → myocardial necrosis.
- Type II MI: Imbalance in oxygen demand and supply WITHOUT a rupture of a plaque
	- Higher mortality and more common in women
- Type III MI: MI Resulting in Death Before Biomarkers Available
	- Cardiac death with symptoms suggestive of ischemia and presumed new ischemic ECG 
- Type IVa: MI Related to PCI
	- Troponin elevation >5× the 99th percentile URL within 48 hours of PCI, plus evidence of ischemia
- Type IVb: MI Related to Stent Thrombosis
- Type IVc: MI Related to Stent Restenosis
	- Troponin elevation >99th percentile URL with ≥50% stenosis at the site of prior PCI, without other culprit lesion
- Type V: MI Related to CABG
	- Troponin elevation >10× the 99th percentile URL within 48 hours of CABG,

#### Clinical Features
- Chest pain, referred pain to shoulder, mid-thoracic back, arm
- **Women more likely** atypical symptoms (fatigue, nausea, jaw/back pain, dyspnea) rather than classic crushing chest pain.
	- More likely to have [[NSTEMI]] and [[MINOCA]]
- Diaphoresis, nausea, vomiting, SOB, fatigue

#### Treatment
- [[Percutaneous Coronary Intervention]] (Angioplasty and stent)
	- Catheter with balloon ([[angioplasty]]) and stent position in narrowed artery
	- Balloon expands to push plaque back, balloon deflated

**POST MI** -- ABCDE
A. [[Aspirin]] + [[Antiplatelets]] (DAPT)
- Aspirin indefinitely
- [[P2Y12 Inhibitors]] for 12 months to reduce recurrence or thrombosis

B. [[Beta Blockers|beta blocker]]
- [[Metoprolol]], [[timolol]], and [[atenolol]] proven to increase lifespan
- Continue for 3 yrs min

C. [[Statins|Statin]] / [[Smoking Cessation]]
- High-intensity statin ([[atorvastatin]] 40–80 mg or [[rosuvastatin]] 20–40 mg) for ALL post-ACS patients regardless of LDL
- Goal LDL = < 70 (< 55 for high risk)
- Smoking cessation reduces risk by 50%

D. Diet + Diabetes
- Mediterranean diet
- HbA1c goal individualized (usually <7%)
- [[GLP-1 agonists]] and [[SGLT2 Inhibitors]] have proven CV benefit.

E. Exercise + Echo + [[Eplerenone]]
- Cardiac rehabilitation reduces mortality
- [[Echo]] in 6 weeks for EF
- If EF < 35%, ICD evaluation
- [[Eplerenone]] if EF < 40% and HF or diabetes post-MI

- [[ACE Inhibitors]] or [[ARBs]] in 24 hrs if EF < 40%, HTN, or diabetes
#### Complications
**Mechanical** (3-7 days post MI)
1) [[Free Wall Rupture]]
	- Sudden cardiovascular collapse, PEA (pulseless electrical activity), pericardial tamponade
	- Transmural MI (often anterior/lateral) → wall ruptures → blood in pericardial sac
	- Emergency surgery (commonly futile)
2) [[Ruptured Papillary Muscle]]
	- New harsh holosystolic murmur at apex + sudden pulmonary edema and hemodynamic collapse
	- Urgent surgical valve repair/replacement; IV vasodilators, IABP as bridge
3) [[Ventricular Septal Defect|VSD]]

**Electrical**
- [[Arrhythmias|cardiac arrhythmias]]

- [[pericarditis]] 
	- Day 1-3
	- Direct inflammatory response to adjacent myocardial necrosis

	- Immune-mediated
