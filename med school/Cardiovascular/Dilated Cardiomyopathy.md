### Etiology
- 90% of all cardiomyopathy cases
- Genetic in 20-50% of cases <- loss of function
	- Mostly autosomal dominant
	- 20% mutations of [[titin]]
- Infectious: [[adenovirus]], [[enterovirus]], [[Parvovirus B19]], [[HHV6]]
- **Excessive** [[Alcohol|alcohol]] consumption
- Exposure to [[cobalt]] or [[doxorubicin]]
- Peripartum -- 50% of patients spontaneously recover normal function
- Iron overload -- [[Hemochromatosis|hemochromatosis]]

### Pathophysiology
- Progressive cardiac dilation and contractile (systolic) dysfunction
	- Becomes more flappy with dilation of all chambers
- End stage -- EF < 25%
- Can cause secondary [[Mitral Regurgitation|mitral regurgitation]]
- Signs of slowly progressive [[congestive heart failure ]]
