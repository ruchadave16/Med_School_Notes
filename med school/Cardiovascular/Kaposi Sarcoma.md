*Vascular neoplasm caused by [[HHV8]]*
#### Classic KS
- Worldwide in older men
-  Multiple red-purple skin plaques on distal lower extremities
- Skin excision for treatment
#### Endemic African KS
- sub-Saharan Africa
- <40 HIV negative patients
#### Transplantation-associated KS
- Solid organ transplant recipients if [[T Lymphocytes|T cell]] immunosuppression
- Aggressive course
#### AIDS-associated KS
- A virally encoded G protein induces [[VEGF]] production in infected endothelial cells, stimulating endothelial cell growth
### Progression of Lesions
1) Patch: flat pink, red, or purple lesions (typically on the distal lower extremities)
2) Plaque: violaceous, raised plaques.
3) Nodule: frequent nodal and visceral involvement.
