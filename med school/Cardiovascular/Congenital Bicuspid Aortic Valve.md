### Etiology
- ~2% of world population

### Pathophysiology
- See [[aortic stenosis]]

### Clinical Features
- See [[aortic stenosis]]
- Early systolic ejection click
### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Balloon valvuloplasty in young if ↑ stenosis
	- Will most likely cause aortic regurgitation
- Valve replacement
	- TAVR
	- SAVR
	- Ross procedure: The abnormal bicuspid valve is removed surgically and replaced with the patient’s native pulmonic valve

- Long-term
