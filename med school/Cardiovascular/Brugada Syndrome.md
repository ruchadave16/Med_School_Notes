### Etiology
- Genetic mutation of cardiac sodium channel

### Pathophysiology
- Voltage gradient in the right ventricular outflow tract, creating a substrate for lethal arrhythmias
### Clinical Features
- Symptoms
- Signs
- Variants

### Diagnosis
- [[ECG]]
	- The diagnostic Type 1 Brugada pattern is a coved ST-segment elevation (>2mm) followed by an inverted T-wave in leads V1 and/or V2
### Treatment
- Urgent consult
- Implant of [[ICD]]
### Complications
- Death from [[vfib]] especially in sleep or during fever
