### Etiology
- Primary causes
- Risk factors
	- [[Atherosclerosis]] and [[coronary artery disease]]
	- [[Vasodilators]]
	- Coronary artery fistulas 
	- [[CABG]]

### Pathophysiology
- Endocardium is more susceptible to infarct because of compressive forces
	- Normally compensated by vasodilation during diastole
- Area of myocardium with ↓ flow reserve because of **coronary stenosis** becomes ischemic, causing this condition
	- Because of stenosis, they're already dilated and cannot anymore
	- Vasodilators may dilate non-stenotic arteries, pulling more blood away from area of ischemia

### Clinical Features
- Asymptomatic at rest
- Exertional angina, SOB, dyspnea
- Severe:
	- Hypotension
	- Syncope
	- [[Arrhythmias]]

### Diagnosis
- ECG shows ischemia (ST depression)
- Vasodilator stress imaging
- PET (nuclear imging) 
- Coronary angiogram
- Dopler Ultrasound / MRI perfusion

### Treatment
- Revascularization or CABG
- Coil embolization or ligation if secondary to fistula
- If post-CABG, re-implantation
- Avoid vasodilators
- Beta-blockers or calcium channel blockers so ↓ myocardial O2 demand
- Antiplatelets and [[statins]]
