### Etiology
- 3-4 in 100,000
- Males > females (2:1)

**Risk Factors**
- [[Hypertension]]
- Genetics:
	- [[Marfan Syndrome]] (FBN1)
	- [[Loeys-Dietz syndrome]] (TGFBR1/2)
	- [[Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome|Ehlers-Danlos]] Type IV (COL3A1)
	- FTAAD
- [[Bicuspid aortic valve]]
- Aortic surgery
- High speed deceleration injuries
- [[Aortitis]]

### Pathophysiology
- Tear through intimal layer that allows blood to enter media and make a false lumen
	- ↑ high pressure blood into media
- Medial degeneration and intimal damage
- Accumulation of mucoid material in cystic medial degeneration
	 
	![[Screenshot 2026-05-06 at 8.47.05 PM.png]]

**Classifications**
- Stanford Type A: Involves ascending aorta, may involve descending aorta (~65%)
- Stanford Type B: Involves descending aorta only (~35%)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Debakey Type I: Involves ascending and descending aorta
- Debakey Type II: Involves ascending aorta only
- Debakey Type III: Involves descending only (IIIa – above diaphragm, IIIb – below)

### Clinical Features
- Tearing pain radiating to the back
- Ascending can cause anterior chest pain
- Pain radiating to neck, jaw, intrascapular region
- Asymmetric pulses
- Syncope, shock, neurologic deficits, organ ischemi
- Acute aortic regurgitation or aortic tamponade

### Diagnosis
- **CT Angiogram** = gold standard
	- Key finding: intimal flap with true and false lumens
- X-ray:
	- widened mediastinum
	- pleural effusion
- TEE
- MRA
	- Contraindicated in unstable and can take time
- D-Dimers may be elevated but NOT specific
	- Check for troponin for MI

### Treatment
- Surgical EMERGENCY
	- ICU admission
- [[Beta Blockers|beta blocker]] = **first line** ([[Esmolol]]
- Can give vasodilators

**Type A**
- Emergent surgical repair
- Mortality ↑ 1-2% if untreated
	- ~90% mortality in 1st week
- **HIGH RISK for [[cardiac tamponade]], severe [[aortic regurgitation]], [[Myocardial Infarction|MI]]**

**Type B**
- Medical management till severe, better prognosis
- TEVAR if complicated
### Complications
- Rupture of adventitia can cause
	- [[hemothorax]]
	- [[hemopericardium]]
