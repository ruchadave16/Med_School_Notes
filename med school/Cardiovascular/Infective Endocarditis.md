*Infection of **heart valves** or endocardium causing vegetation along with destruction*

- Acute endocarditis = rapidly progressive, destructive lesions
- Subacute endocarditis = insidious infections
### ### Etiology
**Risk Factors**
- [[Rheumatic heart disease]]
- [[Mitral valve prolapse]] (now the leading preexistent risk factor)
- [[Congenital Bicuspid Aortic Valve|Congenital bicuspid aortic valve]]
- Calcific valvular stenosis.
- Prosthetic heart valves.
- Presence of pacemaker lines or indwelling vascular catheters

**Source**
- Community acquired -- [[S. Viridans]]
	- Commonly after dental procedure
- Heathcare or IV drug users -- [[Staph Aureus|S. aureus]]
	- [[Tricuspid Valve Endocarditis]]
- [[Streptococcus Gallolyticus|S. bovis]] = endocarditis + [[colon cancer]]
- [[Staph Epidermidis|S. epidermidis]] if prosthetic valve
- HACEK
- Culture negative -- 10% cases 
	- [[Coxiella Burnetii|Coxiella]], causes [[Q fever]]
	- [[Bartonella Henselae|Bartonella]]
### Pathophysiology
- Mixture of fibrin, inflammatory cells, and microorganisms in vegetation
- Aortic and mitral valves are most common.

### Clinical Features
- Fever
- Petechiae
- Splenomegaly in subacute
- Murmur in 90% (new **regurg**)

**Embolism**
- Nail bed (splinter) hemorrhages.
- Retinal hemorrhages (Roth spots)
- Painless, erythematous lesions of the palm or sole (Janeway lesion).
- Painful fingertip nodule (Osler node).
