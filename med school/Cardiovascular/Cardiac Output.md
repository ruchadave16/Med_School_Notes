CO = L/min of blood flow
- Increases by ↑ HR and ↑ blood flow, ↑ SV
- ↓ SVR by vasodilation in heart and muscle, cutaneous vasodilation, splanchnic vasoconstriction
- MAP and PP ↑ because CO ↑ is more than SVR ↓ 
#### Ficks Principle

	$CO = \frac{VO_2}{C_a - C_v}$
	 Estimate denominator to $1.34 * [Hgb] * 10 * (SpO2_a - SpO2_v)$
	 
>[!Warning]
>For a given patient at rest with normal arterial oxygen saturation (so that [Hgb] and 𝑽̇ 𝑶𝟐 remain stable), a drop in mixed venous O2 saturation collected from a catheter in the pulmonary artery would indicate declining cardiac output.

#### Increase in CO
- For oxygen consumption to ↑ either CO ↑ or difference in arterial and venous oxygen saturation increases
	- For healthy, arterial = 100% so venous must decrease
- Significant increases in muscular and cutaneous flow and decreasing SVR
- Increased venous return promoted by the abdominothoracic and skeletal muscle pumps maintains central venous pressure (CVP)
