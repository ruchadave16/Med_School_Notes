**EMERGENCY**
### Etiology
- Anything that elongates QT: medications, [[hypomagnesemia]]

### Diagnosis
- [[ECG]]: A very fast, polymorphic V-Tach where the QRS amplitude appears to "twist"around the isoelectric baseline

### Treatment
- IV [[Magnesium Sulfate]]
- Stop all QT elongating agents
- Immediate defibrillation if risk
