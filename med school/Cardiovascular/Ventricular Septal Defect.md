*Wall separating ventricles doesn’t form properly*
### Etiology
- Congenital
- Post [[Myocardial Infarction|MI]]
### Pathophysiology
*In MI, this happens from necrosis causing the shunt to open*
- Higher pressure in LV causes blood to go to RV (pumped back to lungs)
- **Oxygen rich blood mixing with oxygen poor blood**
- ↑ BP in lungs, heart may work harder

### Clinical Features
- Small = asymptomatic
- New holosystolic [[Cardiac Murmurs|murmurs]] at L lower sternal border
- [[Biventricular failure]] if large
- step-up in O₂ saturation from RA→RV on Swan-Ganz

### Diagnosis
- Echo
	- Qp:Qs ratio is greater than 1.5:1 should be considered for closure

### Treatment
- Urgent surgical repair
- [[IABP]]
- [[Vasodilators]]
- May close percutaneously

### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
