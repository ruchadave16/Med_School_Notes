Location, exertion, types of activities, intensity, radiation, symptoms associated, nocturnal occurrence, relieving factors
- Baseline activity
- Atypical (Women >65 → upper back, neck, jaw pain, SOB, nausea, vomiting, PND, fainting)
- Diagnoses LIFE THREATENING (Angina pectoris, MI, dissecting aortic aneurysm, pulmonary embolus)
### Differential
- [[Myocardial Infarction|heart attack]]
- [[Pericarditis]] -- Sharp, pleuritic, positional; friction rub; diffuse ST elevation (concave) + PR depression
- [[Myocarditis]]  -- Chest pain + troponin rise in young patient; often post-viral; ECG diffuse changes 
- [[Cardiac Tamponade]]
- [[Hypertensive Emergency]] -- Severe HTN + end-organ damage; no plaque rupture
- [[Aortic Dissection]] Tearing/ripping pain radiating to back; pulse differential; widened mediastinum on CXR. Give lytic → disaster!
- [[Pulmonary Embolism]] Pleuritic pain + dyspnea + tachycardia; risk factors for DVT; S1Q3T3 on ECG 
- [[Pneumothorax]] Sudden sharp unilateral pain; decreased breath sounds; tracheal deviation
- [[Pleuritis]]/Pneumonia Pleuritic, fever, productive cough; friction rub
- [[GERD]]/Esophageal spasm Burning, often postprandial, relieved by antacids; can mimic angina
- [[Peptic ulcer]] / [[gastritis]] Epigastric tenderness; history of NSAID use
- [[Costochondritis]] Reproducible with palpation at costochondral junctions (Tietze syndrome)
- [[Panic attack]] / anxiety Chest tightness + palpitations + shortness of breath; exclude cardiac first
- [[HHV3|Herpes zoster]] (shingles) -- Dermatome burning
