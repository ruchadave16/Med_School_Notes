### Pathophysiology
- ↓ ventricular compliance -> impaired filling in diastole
- Size of ventricles is normal but myocardium is firm

### Clinical Features
- Presents with signs of [[Right heart failure]]
- Kussmaul sign (a paradoxical rise in JVP during inspiration) 
- S4 heart sound
- Dyspnea

### Diagnosis
**Differential**: Consider [[constrictive pericarditis]]

- ECG
	- Low signal on ECG
- Echocardiogram
- Cardiac MRI
- Cardiac biopsy
