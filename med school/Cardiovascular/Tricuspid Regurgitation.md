### Etiology
- Commonly secondary to [[left sided heart failure]] or [[pulmonary hypertension]]
- Primary
	- [[infective endocarditis]] from IV drug use
	- [[Rheumatic heart disease]]
	- [[Carcinoid Heart Disease]]

### Pathophysiology
- Volume overload of RA and RV

### Clinical Features
- [[Systemic venous congestion]]
- Murmur: Holosystolic, blowing murmur at left lower sternal border; inc. with inspiration.

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Treat condition that caused it
- [[Diuretics]] if fluid overload
- Surgery = rare
### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
