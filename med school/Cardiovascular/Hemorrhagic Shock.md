### Pathophysiology
- Significant blood loss -> ↓ [[Baroreflex]]
- Response initial through neuroendocrine to ensure perfusion
	- Release of [[Epinephrine]] and [[Norepinephrine]]
	- [[Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System|RAAS system]] activation
	- Release of [[Vasopressin]]
- With continuous hypoperfusion, injury worsens and decompensation starts
- Microcirculatory dysfunction and inflammatory cells activate

- Body shifts fluid from extracellular -> intracellular 
	- Normocytic anemia 
	- ↑ [[erythropoietin]] so ↑ RBC production in 5-7 days
