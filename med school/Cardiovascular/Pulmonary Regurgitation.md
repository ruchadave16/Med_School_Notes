### Etiology
- [[Pulmonary hypertension]] -> dilation of pulm. artery
	- Repair of [[Tetralogy of Fallot]]
### Pathophysiology
- Volume overload of RV -> dilation and dysfunction

### Clinical Features
- No symptoms for years
- Eventual [[right sided heart failure]]
- Murmur: High-pitched, early diastolic decrescendo murmur at the left upper sternal border 

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Treat pulm. hypertension
- Pulmonary valve replacement in severe cases

### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
