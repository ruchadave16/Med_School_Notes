### Etiology
- [[Congestive Heart Failure]], [[Hypoalbuminemia]] (serous)
- Trauma, malignancy, a ruptured myocardial infarct or an aortic dissection (blood)
- Lymphatic obstruction (lymph)

### Pathophysiology
- Collection of fluid around fluid in pericardial space around heart
	- Acts as an insulator dampening signal
- If its slow, then pericardium can adapt and stretch but rapid can cause [[Cardiac Tamponade|cardiac tamponade]]

- Symptoms

### Diagnosis
- ECG
	- Electrical Alternans: A beat-to-beat variation in the QRS complex amplitude.
	- Low Voltage: Decreased QRS amplitude in both the limb leads (<5 mm) and precordial leads (<10 mm).
- Echo to confirm size and look for signs of tamponade
