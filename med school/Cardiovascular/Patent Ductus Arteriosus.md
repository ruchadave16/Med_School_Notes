### Etiology
- Primary causes
- Risk factors

### Pathophysiology
- Ductus arteriosus DOESN’T close
- Higher aortic pressure → blood from aorta to pulmonary trunk (continuous murmur)
	- ↑ pulmonary blood flow  
### Clinical Features
- [[Gibson’s murmur]] (throughout systole and into diastole)
- Cyanosis in lower extremity but not upper
- Widened pulse pressure

### Diagnosis
- TTE for diagnosis

### Treatment
- Surgical ligation

### Complications
- [[Pulmonary hypertension]]
- [[Eisenmenger’s Syndrome]]
