### Etiology
- All races, genders, and age
- Predilection for young adults, African Americans, Danes, Swedes, and non-smokers
### Pathophysiology
- Diisordered immune regulation in genetically susceptible persons upon exposure to unidentified environmental antigens
- Non-caseating [[granuloma]] 
![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 11.00.11 AM.png]]
- Macrophages within granuloma express 1-α-hyroxylase
	- Catalyzes Vitamin D -> active form
### Clinical Features
- Bilateral hilar lymphadenopathy
- Iritis
- Erythema nodosum
- Lung, eye, and skin involvement
- 5-10% = [[Hypercalcemia]]

### Diagnosis
- Identification of non-caseating granulomas
- Exclusion of all other options

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- Hypercalcemia related [[renal failure]]
