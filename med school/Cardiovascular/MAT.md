### Etiology
- Multiple irritable ectopic foci in the atria
- Commonly [[COPD exacerbation]], electrolyte disruption, sepsis

### Diagnosis
- ECG:
	1) Rate > 100 bpm, 
	2) At least 3 distinct P wave morphologies
	3) Variable P-P, P-R, and R-R intervals. 
It is an irregularly irregular rhythm with visible P waves (distinguishing it from Afib). 

*Note: A Wandering Pacemaker has similar criteria but has a rate <100 bpm
