### Etiology
- Genetic missense mutations for cardiac [[sarcomere]] <- gain of function
	- Mostly $\beta$-myosin heavy chain is involved -- 70-80%
	- Mostly autosomal dominant
- 1/3 of cases of sudden cardiac death in athletes younger than 35 years of age
- [[Friedreich Ataxia]] associated
- If maternal diabetes, infants can have transient hypertrophic cardiomyopathy
### Pathophysiology
- Myocardial hypertrophy -- thick walled, hard to contract
	- Most common in interventricular septum
- Defective diastolic filling
- Ventricular outflow obstruction

### Clinical Features 
- Young person with syncope
- Exertional syncope
- Harsh systolic ejection murmur

### Diagnosis
**Differential** -- [[Cardiac Amyloidosis|cardiac amyloidosis]], [[Left Ventricular Hypertrophy|left ventricular hypertrophy]]

- ECG
	- The ECG is abnormal in >90% of cases. Look for signs of LVH, often with deep, narrow, "dagger-like" Q waves in the lateral (I, aVL, V5-V6) and/or inferior leads
	- Giant, deep T-wave inversions may also be present.
- Urgent Echo
### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle
