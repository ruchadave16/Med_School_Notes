### Etiology
- [[Aortic stenosis]]
- Chronic [[Hypertension]]

### Pathophysiology
- Thickening of LV muscle from chronic ↑ pressure (afterload)
- Generates larger electrical signals (QRS amplitude)
- Takes longer to repolarize

- Symptoms
- Signs

### Diagnosis
- ECG
	- High voltage in the precordial leads (e.g., S in V1 + R in V5 > 35 mm) with an optional "strain pattern" (ST depression in lateral leads).
