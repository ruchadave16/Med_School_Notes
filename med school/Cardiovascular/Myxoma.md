### Etiology
- Common tumor of adult heart
- [[Carney Complex]]
### Pathophysiology
- 90% = atrial, 80% = LA
- Originate from septum
- Soft, translucent, or gelatinous
- Release of [[IL-6]] from tumor cells

### Clinical Features
- Valvular obstruction
- Embolism
- Fever + malaise

### Diagnosis
- Echo

### Treatment
- Surgical resection = curative

### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
