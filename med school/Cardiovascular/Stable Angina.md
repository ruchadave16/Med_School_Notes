**Mechanism**
- Partial obstruction (generally > 70% occlusion)

**Symptoms**
- Pain upon exertion 
	- Relieved upon rest and delivery of sublingual [[nitroglycerin]] in 5-10 minutes
- ECG normal, might show ST depression
- Troponin negative

**Treatment**
- Smoking cessation
- Diet and weight management
- Regular aerobic exercise
- Control of comorbidities (HTN, HLD, DM)

- *First Line*: [[Beta Blockers]] 
	- Sublingual nitroglycerin for acute pain
- Second Line
	- Long acting [[nitrates]]
	- [[Dihydropyridines|DHPs]] 
	- [[Non-dihydropyridines|Non-DHPs]] -- in patients with angina and supraventricular arrhythmias
	- [[Ranolazine]] if needed
- [[Aspirin]] (**81 mg daily**) for all unless contraindicated
	- [[Clopidogrel]]if intolerant or in combination
- [[Statins]] for risk reduction
	- [[ACE Inhibitors]]if diabetes, CKD, LV dysfunction
