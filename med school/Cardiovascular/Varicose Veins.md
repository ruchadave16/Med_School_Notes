*abnormally dilated [[Vein|veins]] from chronic ↑ intraluminal pressure and ↓ vessel support*

- Common in superficial veins of lower extremities
- Common in [[Prenatal Period|pregnancy]] and [[obesity]]

- Superficial rarely have thrombus while deep veins have **common** [[Thrombus]] formation

#### [[Esophageal Varices]]
- [[Liver cirrhosis]], portal vein obstruction or hepatic vein thrombosis causes [[Hepatic Portal Hypertension|portal hypertension]]
	- Opening of shunts
	- GI junction -> esophageal varices
	- Rectum -> [[hemorrhoids]]
	- Periumbilical -> [[caput medusae]]
- Prone to rupture and hemorrhaging
