**Vasodilation**
- ↑ radius and flow so ↓ resistance
- [[Parasympathetic Nervous System|Parasympathetic]] for thoracic and abdominal organs and brain

**Vasoconstriction**
- ↓ radius, so ↑ flow but ↑ resistance

**MAIN FACTOR: Arterioles**
- Control of arteriole resistance to determine cardiac output distribution in body
- Mechanism = change to arteriole diameter by contracting or relaxing [[Smooth Muscle|smooth muscle]]
	- Flow diverted from ↑↑ resistance arterioles to lower ones
	![[Screenshot 2026-05-03 at 11.04.44 PM.png]]

1) Intrinsic Control
	- Tissue metabolites formation, levels of $CO_2$ change
	- [[Adenosine]] (vasodilator)
2) External Control
	- [[Sympathetic Nervous System|Sympathetic]] nervous system, humoral factors, endothelial factors

#### Factors
Generally autoregulate so that when flow changes, radius changes accordingly
1) [[Myogenic Mechanism|Myogenic reflex]]
2) Metabolic hypothesis
	- Concentration of vasodilating metabolites is what determines 
	- If perfusion pressure ↓, oxygen consumption > supply so metabolite concentrations ↑ 
	- This loop induces dilation
