*Common tumors present often at birth*
- Regress spontaneously
- Localized to head and neck
- **Rarely** malignant
#### Variations
- Capillary hemangioma
	- Most common, found on skin, subcutaneous tissues, mucous membranes, organs
	- Thin walled capillaries with scant stroma
	
	![[Screenshot 2026-05-06 at 3.27.16 PM.png]]
- Infantile hemangioma
	- Common skin lesion
	- Rapid growth for few months, involute by 1-3 years
	- Disappear by 7 yrs
- Pyogenic hemangioma
	- Red pedunculated lesions on the skin or oral mucosa
	- Represent granulomatous tissue
	- Trauma / pregnancy associated
- Cavernous hemangioma
	- Large dilated vessels
	- Infiltrative and don't just go away
	- Component of [[von Hippel Lindau]]
