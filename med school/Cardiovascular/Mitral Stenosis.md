### Etiology
- [[Rheumatic heart disease]]
	- > 95% cases 
- MS has a 2:1 female-to-male predominance

### Pathophysiology
- Narrow mitral valve = ↓ blood flow from LA to LV in diastole
- Chronic inflammation, leading to commissural fusion (leaflets fuse at the edges)
- LA presssure ↑ so LA dilates
	- Pulmonary backlog so [[pulmonary venous hypertension]] = [[pulmonary edema]]
- Secondary increase in pulmonary arterial hypertension
	- RV hypertrophy and [[Right Heart Failure|right sided heart failure]]

### Clinical Features
- Dyspnea
- Palpitations: Often due to the onset of [[AFib|atrial fibrillation]].
- Fatigue: Due to low cardiac output
- Hemoptysis: Coughing up blood, caused by rupture of higher pressured bronchial veins
- Systemic Embolism: Presenting as a stroke or acute limb ischemia
- Hoarseness (Ortner's Syndrome): A classic but uncommon sign where a massively dilated LA compresses the left recurrent laryngeal nerve.
- [[Afib]] -- atrial wall stretching
- Thromboembolism
### Diagnosis
- Physical exam
	- A low-pitched, mid-diastolic "rumble"
	- Opening snap: high-pitched, sharp sound heard shortly after S2 caused by the sudden tensing of stenotic valve leaflets as they open. The closer the snap is to S2, the more severe the stenosis.
	- Loud S1
	- Mitral Facies: Rosy or purplish patches on the cheeks due to chronic low cardiac output and cutaneous vasoconstriction (rare)
- TTE
	- "hockey stick" appearance of the anterior leaflet
	- Mitral Valve Area (MVA). Also evaluates LA size, pulmonary artery pressure, and RV function
- ECG = "P mitrale" (broad, notched P waves in lead II)
### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
	- Diuretics: To reduce pulmonary congestion and relieve dyspnea.
	- HR Control: [[beta blockers]] or [[CCBs|Calcium Channel Blockers]]
	- [[Anticoagulants]]
		- If Afib, [[warfarin]]
- Surgical
	- Percutaneous Mitral Balloon Commissurotomy (PMBC):
	- Surgical Mitral Valve Replacement or Repair 
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- Embolic stroke
- [[Afib]]
- [[Right Heart Failure|Right sided heart failure]]
