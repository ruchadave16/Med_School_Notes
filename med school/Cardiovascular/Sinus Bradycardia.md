### Etiology
- Athelete
- Pathological
	- Drug effect, ischemia

### Pathophysiology
- ↑ vagal tone so SA node fires more slowly

### Clinical Features
- Assess for dizziness / syncope
### Diagnosis
- ECG
	- A normal P wave for every QRS, with a regular rhythm and rate <60 bpm.

### Treatment
- No acute if no dizziness
- [[Pacemaker]] implant if symptomatic

### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
