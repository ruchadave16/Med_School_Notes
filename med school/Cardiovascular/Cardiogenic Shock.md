### Etiology
- Complicates ~5–8% of [[STEMI]]; mortality ~40–50% even with treatment
- Most common cause = [[Myocardial Infarction|MI]] -- Mortality 50-80% here
- Blunt cardiac injury

### Pathophysiology
- Adequate volume but ↓ forward flow

**Classification**
Stages A (at risk) → B (beginning) → C (classic) → D (deteriorating) → E (extremis)
### Clinical Features
- Symptoms
- Signs
- Variants

### Diagnosis
- TEE
- Serum troponin

**Criteria**
- BP 90 for >30 min (or vasopressors required)
- Signs of end-organ hypoperfusion 
- CI 2.2 L/min/m

**Differential**
- Exclude [[Hemorrhagic Shock]]
### Treatment
- Emergency revascularization (Class I)
- Mechanical circulatory support (IABP, Impella, ECMO) as bridge
- [[Vasopressors]] ([[norepinephrine]] preferred over dopamine if hypotensive)
	- Else [[Dobutamine]] or [[Epinephrine]]
