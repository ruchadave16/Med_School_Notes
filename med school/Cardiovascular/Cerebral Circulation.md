>[!Note] See [[brain]] for Circle of Willis and veins

>[!Info] 
>13-15% of cardiac output goes to brain, and this is the most susceptible organ to lack of oxygen

#### Autoregulation
- Prevents pressure change and metabolic response to local activity
- Response more sensitive to CO2 than O2
	- Small change in CO2 causes rapid increase in blood flow through dilation
	![[Screenshot 2026-05-03 at 11.35.27 PM.png]]

>[!Warning] 
>Significant [[hypotension]]
>- MAP < 60 mmHg can cause [[cerebral hypoxia]]
>  
>  

- Cerebral arteries can remodel to chronic ↑ pressure
	- Maintains normal blood flow for higher MAPs
