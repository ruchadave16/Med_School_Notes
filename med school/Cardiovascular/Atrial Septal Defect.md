#### Mechanism
Because of higher pressure in LA, blood to RA through “hole” in interatrial wall 
- Foramen Ovale DIDN’T CLOSE
- RV works harder so grows larger + weaker
- BP in pulmonary arteries may increase → pulmonary hypertension

- Left to right shunt
	- Longstanding RA dilation
#### Variations
1) The region of the fossa ovalis (ostium secundum)
2) Superior portion of the atrial septum near the junction with the superior vena cava (sinus venosus ASDs)
3) Inferior portion of the atrial septum near the tricuspid valve annulus (ostium primum ASDs)
#### Symptoms
- Mostly asymptomatic till adulthood
- Symptoms of [[right heart failure]]
- Can have atrial arrhythmias
- Peripheral edema
- **wide and fixed splitting of the second heart sound**
#### Diagnosis
- Echo for size
#### Treatment
- Can undergo treatment for closure
