*Malignant endothelial cell neoplasms*
### Etiology
- Older adults more commonly affected
- Aggressive with **poor** survival
- Can arise in setting of [[lymphedema]]
	- Lymph node resection for breast cancer
- Also after [[radiation therapy]]
- [[PVC]] = risk for hepatic angiosarcoma

- Mechanism of disease

### Clinical Features
- In setting of [[lymphedema]] -> I/L upper extremity after many years
- Skin, soft tissue, breast and liver are common sites
- Lesions start as small, sharply demarcated red nodules -> large fleshy masses
