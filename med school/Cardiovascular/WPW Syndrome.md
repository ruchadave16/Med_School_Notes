### Etiology
- Congenital bundle of Kent

### Pathophysiology
- Congenital accessory pathway (Bundle of Kent) that **bypasses** the AV node
- Provides an electrical "shortcut" from the atria to the ventricles
	- Early excitation (delta wave)
- Can get tachycardia (AVRT)
### Clinical Features
- Mostly asymptomatic

### Diagnosis
- [[ECG]]
	- The classic triad is a short PR interval (<0.12s), a delta wave (slurred upstroke of the QRS), and a widened QRS complex.

### Treatment
- Ablation of extra pathway

### Complications
- High risk of [[afib]], which can convert to [[vfib]]
	- Very very fast heart rate (300-500/min)
	- **Normal therapies are contraindicated**

- Classic buzzwords
