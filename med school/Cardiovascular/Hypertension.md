### Etiology
- Diastole > 80 or systole > 120
- Mostly idiopathic
	- [[Secondary Hypertension]]
- Genetic factors
- Stress, obesity, smoking, physical inactivity, and high levels of salt consumption

### Pathophysiology
- BP = [[cardiac output]] * SVR
- ↑ Blood Volume
	- ↓ renal sodium excretion
- ↑ Peripheral Resistance

### Clinical Features
- Often asymptomatic

### Diagnosis
**BP Classification (ACC/AHA 2017):**
• Normal: <120/80 mm Hg
• Elevated: 120–129/<80 mm Hg
• Hypertension (Stage 1): 130–139/80–89 mm Hg
• Hypertension (Stage 2): ≥140/90 mm Hg
### Treatment
- [[Antihypertensives]]
- DASH diet (↑ fruits/veg, ↓ saturated fat) 8–14 mm Hg
- Weight loss (ideal BMI ~25) 5–20 mm Hg per 10 kg lost
- Reduced sodium intake (<1.5–2.3 g/day) 2–8 mm Hg
- Physical activity (150 min/week aerobic) 4–9 mm Hg
- Moderate alcohol intake 2–4 mm Hg
### Complications
- ↑ risk of [[Stroke]] and [[coronary heart disease]]
- lead to cardiac hypertrophy and heart failure, [[aortic dissection]], multi-infarct dementia, and renal failure
- 5% have rapidly increasing hypertension -> [[malignant hypertension]] risk
