**Systolic Blood Pressure:** peak blood pressure produced by cardiac contraction

**Diastolic Blood Pressure:** lowest blood pressure occurring between cardiac contractions
#### Perfusion Pressure (Pulse Pressure)
- Difference in arterial and venous pressure
- Since systole = 1/3 of cardiac cycle, MAP = 1/3 P(sys) + 2/3 P(dia)
	- MAP = diastolic pressure + 1/3 pulse pressure 
	- If pulse pressure (PP) = systolic - diastolic
- This is the driving force for flow through systemic circulation

![[Screenshot 2026-05-03 at 11.53.17 PM.png]]
>[!Equation]
>$MAP - CVP = CO * SVR$
>$CO = HR * SV$
>$MAP = HR * SV * SVR + CVP$
#### Hydrostatic Pressure
- Gravity induced part of total pressure due to weight of fluid above that level
- P = $\rho gh$  -- position dependent
#### Transmural Pressure
- Intravascular vs extravascular pressure
- If healthy organ, extravascular pressure is abt 0 
### Circulation Systems
Pressure HIGHEST in [[aorta]] and lowest in vena cava
- Highest drop in arterioles which brings P down to be enough for capillaries

![[Screenshot 2026-04-27 at 10.42.19 AM.png]]
### Regulation
[[Baroreflex]]

- If elastic walls, deformation of walls reduces pressure compared to non-elastic
	- These elastic vessels = high pressure reservoir 
	- Stroke volume is stored in arterioles and during diastole, the recoil provides blood flow
- Anything that increases flow into the vessel will increase pressure
![[Screenshot 2026-05-03 at 11.58.13 PM.png]]

- Compliance = $\Delta$V / $\Delta$P 
	- Veins are more compliant than arteries at low pressures
	- Compliance ↓ as vessel dialates
		- Stiffer [[collagen]] especially in adventitia
	- Certain veins but arteries and arterioles have vasoconstriction through smooth muscle contraction -> ↓ vascular compliance
![[Screenshot 2026-05-04 at 12.01.07 AM.png]]
### Determinants
1) [[Stroke volume]]: ↑ by exercise and cardiac contractility, ↓ with SVR, ↓ as HR ↑ 
2) [[Vascular compliance]]:
	- Inversely related to SBP
	- Can also decrease diastolic if systemic resistance same
3) SVR:
	- Directly related to DBP
4) [[Vascular elasticity]]:
	- Inversely related to SBP
	- Directly related to DBP
5) [[Heart rate]]
	- Inversely related to DBP

**Conditions**

>1) Endurance Training
>- Reduces resting SVR and strengthens myocardium
>- ↑ SV (can increase SBP potentially) and ↓ resting HR
>- ↓ DBP so ↑ PP
>2) [[Hypertension]]
>- ↑ SVR and ↓ vascular compliance
>- ↑ SBP, DBP, PP
>3) [[Aortic Regurgitation]]
>- ↓ DBP so Cardiac output and SV ↑ to balance this, SBP ↑ 
>- ↑↑ PP
>4) [[Hyperthyroidism]]
>- T3 dilates arterioles and ↓ SVR so ↓ DBP
>- ↑ SV and cardiac output so ↑ SBP and PP
>5) [[AV Fistula]]
>- ↓ SVR (↑ SV and CO) causes ↑ SBP
