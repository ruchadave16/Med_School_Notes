*Disease of elastic arteries hardening*
### Etiology
- Vascular response to endothelial cell injury
- Very common in high income and developed nations, associated with Western diets
### Predisposing Factors
- Genetics: Most important
	- [[Familial Hypercholesterolemia|familial hypercholesterolemia]]
-  [[Hyperlipidemia|Dyslipidemia]] (> 3:1 LDL:HDL ratio)
	- LDL makes riskier, HDL makes safer
	- [[Omega-3-fatty acids]] beneficial
	- Exercise and ethanol consumption can increase HDL levels
- Hyperglycemia or [[Diabetes Mellitus|diabetes mellitus]]
	- 100x ↑ risk of [[Gangrenous Necrosis|gangrene]]
- [[Hypertension|Hypertension]]
- Toxins from [[smoking]]

**Other Risk Factors**
- Inflammation
- [[Homocystinuria]]
- [[Metabolic syndrome]]
- Lipoprotein(a) levels
### Pathophysiology
- Damaged or dysfunctional endothelial cells
- **Causes**
	- Turbulent blood flow
	- [[Hypercholesterolemia]] -- can increase free radical production and cause accumulation of LDLs
#### Mechanism
- [[Smooth Muscle]] cells migrate to intima from media and proliferate
	- They form [[Extracellular Matrix|ECM]] normally
![[Screenshot 2026-05-05 at 8.34.31 PM.png]]
- In this pathology, they oxidize LDLs in [[Blood Vessels|tunica intima]]
	1)  Endothelial damage causes ↑ penetration
	2) This causes adhesion and entry of monocytes -> [[Macrophage|macrophages]] to eliminate [[LDL]]
	3) Lipid-filled macrophages (foam cells) accumulate forming fatty streaks (reversible)
	4) **[[PDGF]] = growth factor for smooth muscles**
	5) When worsening, form fibro-fatty plaques (atheromas)
- Commonly see [[dystrophic calcification]]

![[Screenshot 2026-05-05 at 8.28.01 PM.png]]

**Vulnerable Plaques**
- Thin fibrous cap, large lipid core, inflammatory infiltration
#### Outcomes Pathologic
Most common in large elastic arteries or medium muscular arteries
- Atherosclerotic stenosis
	- **Critical** when 70-75% occlusion
- Acute plaque change
	- Rupture
		- Subendothelial collagen exposed -> platelets aggregate -> thrombus -> [[ACS]]
	- Hemorrhage
	- Atheroembolism

- This can destroy walls and cause bulging, eventually causing [[aneurysm]]
- In muscular arteries, can occlude blood flow causing [[ischemic heart disease]]

### Clinical Features
- Manifests as [[Coronary artery disease]]
