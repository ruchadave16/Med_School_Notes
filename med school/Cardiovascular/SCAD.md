*Non-atherosclerotic cause of [[ACS]]*
### Etiology
- Most common in young women < 45
- Leading cause of MI in women < 50 years and pregnancy-associated MI
- Associated with fibromuscular dysplasia ([[FMD]]), connective tissue disorders, peripartum state, extreme physical/emotional stress
