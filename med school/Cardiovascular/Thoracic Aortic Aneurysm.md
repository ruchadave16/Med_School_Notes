### Etiology
- 5-10 cases / 100k people
- Common in adults > 60 yrs
- Men > women (2:1)
- Risk factors
	- [[Atherosclerosis|atherosclerosis]]
	- hypertension
	- [[hyperlipidemia]]
	- smoking
- Genetic:
	- [[Marfan Syndrome]]
	- [[Loeys-Dietz syndrome]]
	- [[Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome|Ehlers-Danlos]] -- vascular
- [[Bicuspid aortic valve]]
- [[Temporal Arteritis|Giant cell arteritis]]
- [[Takayasu Arteritis]]
- [[Syphilitic aortitis]]

### Pathophysiology
- Localized dilation of thoracic aorta > 150%
	-  Ascending (~60%), arch (~10%), descending (~35%);
- True aneurysm including all 3 layers
- Elastolysis, inflammatory infiltrates (variable depending on cause), and potential cystic medial degeneration
- ↑ Wall stress and remodeling from HTN
### Clinical Features
- Asymptomatic until rupture
- Chest pain
- Hoarseness ([[Recurrent Laryngeal Nerve|Recurrent laryngeal]])
- Cough, dyspnea 
- Aortic regurgitation
- [[SVC Syndrome]]

### Diagnosis
- Can be seen on imaging
- Echo (TEE or TTE)
- CTA

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
	- BP control through [[Beta Blockers]]
	- Smoking cessation
	- Serial imaging
- Surgical
	- 5.5 cm diameter in sporadic TAAs
	- 5.0 cm in Marfan, Loeys-Dietz, or BAV
	- Rapid expansion (>0.5 cm/yr)
	- Symptomatic or dissection
- Open Surgical Repair with synthetic graft
- TEVAR

**Good post-operative survival when electively repaired; requires lifelong surveillance**
### Complications
- Associated with [[aortic dissection]]
