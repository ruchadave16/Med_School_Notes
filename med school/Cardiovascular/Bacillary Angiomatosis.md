*Vascular proliferation that is caused by [[Bartonella Henselae|Bartonella]] in immunocompromised patients*

![[Screenshot 2026-05-06 at 3.49.16 PM.png]]

- Produces hypoxia-inducible factor-1α (HIF-1α) -> [[VEGF]] ↑  and vascular proliferation ↑
- Lesions bleed easily
#### Treatment
- [[Antibiotics]]
