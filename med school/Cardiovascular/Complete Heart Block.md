### Etiology
- Electrical "bridge" is completely out
	- Degenerative fibrosis in older adults or ischemia from an MI

### Pathophysiology
- Complete dissociation between the atria (P waves) and ventricles (QRS complexes) due to a failed AV node

### Clinical Features
- Symptoms
- Signs
- Variants

### Diagnosis
- [[ECG]]: P and QRS present but have no relationship 
	- P's and QRS's each march out at their own regular, but different, rates.

### Treatment
- Assess for hemodynamic instability
- [[Atropine]] acutely
	- Prep for transcutaneous [[pacing]] then [[dopamine]] or [[Epinephrine|epinephrine]] infusion
