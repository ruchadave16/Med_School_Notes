*Load against which heart is pumping during systole*

- Main factors = vascular resistance and arterial pressure outside valves
	- Pulmonary artery pressure
	- Aortic/pulmonary valve stenosis
- ↑ afterload means ↑ ESV and ↓ SV
	- No change to EDV idealistically
	- In real patients, SBP, DBP, and ESP all increase
