### Etiology
- Primary causes

### Pathophysiology
- A very fast rhythm originating from the ventricles that can be pulseless (an arrest) or with a pulse 
- A re-entrant circuit forms around scar tissue (e.g., from a prior MI) or an irritable focus in the ventricles fires automatically

### Clinical Features
- Symptoms
- Signs
- Variants

### Diagnosis
- [[ECG]]: A regular, fast (>100 bpm) rhythm with wide QRS complexes (>0.12s).

### Treatment
- Acute: [[DCC]] 
	- If structural heart disease, [[Amiodarone]] [[procainamide]], or [[sotalol]]
	- If none, consider [[Beta Blockers|beta blocker]] or [[verapamil]]
- Sustained VT
	- [[ICD]]
- [[Lidocaine]] if acute ischemia
