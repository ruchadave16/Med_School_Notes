*Receptors in adventitia of arteries in [[carotid sinus]] and [[aortic arch]]*
- Respond to **stretch**
#### Carotid Sinus
- Afferent nerves join carotid sinus n. -> joins with [[Glossopharyngeal Nerve|glossopharyngeal n.]]
- Continue to [[nucleus solitarius]]
#### Aortic
- Ascend in [[Vagus Nerve|vagus]] -> nodose ganglion
- Terminate in nucleus solitarius of brainstem
