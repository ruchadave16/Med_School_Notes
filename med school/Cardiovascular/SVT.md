### Etiology
- Commonly AV Nodal Re-entrant Tachycardia ([[AVNRT]]), a small re-entry circuit within the AV node itself

### Pathophysiology
- Mechanism of disease
- Relevant pathways (e.g. immune, hormonal)

### Diagnosis
- [[ECG]]
	-  regular, narrow-complex (<0.12s) tachycardia, typically with a rate >150 bpm where P waves are often hidden.

### Treatment
- Attempt [[Valsalva]]
- If not, administer [[adenosine]] acutely
	- If not, then [[Beta Blockers|beta blocker]] or [[CCBs]]

- Chronic prevention (if frequent or symptomatic):
	- Class Ic: [[flecainide]], [[propafenone]] for pregnant pts
	- Class II: [[Beta Blockers|Beta blocker]]
	- Class IV: [[Non-dihydropyridines|Non-DHPs]]
	- [[Digoxin]] -- first line for chronic SVT in pregnancy
