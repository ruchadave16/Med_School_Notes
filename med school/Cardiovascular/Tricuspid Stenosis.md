### Etiology
- Most likely [[Rheumatic heart disease]]

### Pathophysiology
- See almost always with **[[Mitral Stenosis|mitral stenosis]]
- Obstruction of flow from RA to RV -> RA dilation and pressure

### Clinical Features
- Looks similar to [[Right Heart Failure|right sided heart failure]]
- Peripheral [[Edema]]
- [[Ascites]]
- [[Hepatomegaly]]
- Murmur: Mid-diastolic "rumble" at left lower sternal border

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
	- [[Diuretics]] for fluid overload
- Surgical
	- Surgery (valve replacement) if severe
