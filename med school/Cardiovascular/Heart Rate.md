### Regulation
- [[Parasympathetic Nervous System|Parasympathetic]] and [[Sympathetic Nervous System|sympathetic]] constantly (tonically) active, but parasympathetic dominates at rest
	- Sympathetic is slower onset than parasympathetic
- Rapid changes to HR are based on parasympathetic
#### Sympathetic
*↑ HR, contractility, and relaxation rate*
- Norepi binds to $\beta 1$ on cardiac membrane
- Epi also activates $\beta 1$
	- ↑ HR (chronotropic effect)
	- ↑ AV velocity (dromotropic effect)
	- ↓ action potential length
	- ↑ contractile force (inotropic effect)
	- ↑ relaxation rate (lusitropic effect)
