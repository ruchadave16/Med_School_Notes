*Varicose dilations of venous plexus at anorectal junction*

- Prolonged pelvic vascular constriction in [[Prenatal Period|pregnancy]] or [[constipation]]

![[Screenshot 2026-05-06 at 5.27.25 PM.png]]

[[Internal hemorrhoids]] – PAINLESS, dilation of internal rectal venous plexus (drains superior rectal v. into portal vein)

[[External hemorrhoids]] – PAINFUL, [[Pudendal Nerve|pudendal n.]]., dilation of external rectal venous plexus (drains inferior rectal v. → IVC)
