*Varicose dilations of venous plexus at anorectal junction*
### Etiology
Cause: Prolonged pelvic vascular constriction in [[Prenatal Period|pregnancy]] or [[constipation]]

![[Screenshot 2026-05-06 at 5.27.25 PM.png]]

- Risk:
	- Advancing age
	- Pregnancy
	- Prolonged sitting and straining at stool
	- Chronic constipation
	- Portal hypertension
	- Diarrhea
	- Pelvic tumors
	- Patients on anticoagulation and antiplatelet therapy
### Pathophysiology
[[Internal hemorrhoids]] – PAINLESS, dilation of internal rectal venous plexus (drains superior rectal v. into portal vein)
- Within dentate line 

[[External hemorrhoids]] – PAINFUL, [[Pudendal Nerve|pudendal n.]]., dilation of external rectal venous plexus (drains inferior rectal v. → IVC)
- Past dentate line
### Clinical Features
- Pain 
- Rectal bleeding (bright)
