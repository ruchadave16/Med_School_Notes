### Diagnosis
- [[ECG]]
	- A classic "sawtooth" pattern of flutter waves, often with a regular ventricular response (e.g., 2:1 block)

### Treatment
- Assess stability, control the rate, and consider anticoagulation
	- Start with [[Factor Xa Inhibitors]] if score > 2
	- [[Aspirin]] if score < = 1 
- [[Metoprolol]] to manage and hten if not, provide cardiovension
