- Acute [[Myocardial Infarction|MI]] -- [[STEMI]]
- Coronary spasm
- [[Pericarditis]]: associated with PR depression; T waves usually upright
- [[LBBB]] (increased QRS duration, STE discordant from QRS complex)
- LVH (increased QRS amplitude with repolarization abnormality)
- [[Brugada syndrome]] (rSR', downsloping STE V1–V2; Na channelopathy associated with sudden cardiac death)
- [[Hyperkalemia]]
- [[Hypothermia]]: Osborn waves (positive deflection at J point, proportional to degree of
hypothermia)
- Early repolarization: Most often V2–V5 in young adults; 1–4 mm elevation of J point; ratio of STE/T wave 25%; may disappear with exercise
- aVR: STE >1 mm associated with increased mortality in STEMI; STE in aVR > V1 associated with left main disease
