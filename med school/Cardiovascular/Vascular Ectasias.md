*Vascular dilation*
- Telangiectasia = permanent dilation that forms a discrete red lesion
#### Categories
- Nevus simplex (Salmon patches)
	- Most common -- birthmarks
	- Light pink to deep purple flat lesion on head or neck
	- **Regress spontaneously**
- Port Wine Stain: 
	- [[Sturge-Weber]]
	- Grow in childhood
	- Lesions along [[Trigeminal Nerve|trigeminal n.]]
- Spider telangiectasia
	- Lesions of pulsatile arrays of dilated subcutaneous arteries
	- Face, neck, chest
	- **Hyperestrogenic states**
	![[Screenshot 2026-05-06 at 3.02.39 PM.png]]
- Hereditary hemorrhagic telangiectasia ([[Osler-Weber-Rendu disease]])
