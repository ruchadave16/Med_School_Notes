- Stenosis: valve fails to open completely, creating a narrowed, stiff, or obstructed opening that restricts forward blood flow
	- Chamber before valve has pressure overload and concentric hypertrophy
- Regurgitation: valve fails to close completely, allowing blood to leak backwards
	- Volume overload and eccentric hypertrophy
### Staging
- Stage A (At Risk)
- Stage B (Progressive)
- Stage C (Asymptomatic Severe; C1 = compensated, C2 = decompensated)
- Stage D (Symptomatic Severe)
#### Right Heart
- Lower pressure system - valvular lesions are often better tolerated over longer periods of time
	- Associated with systemic venous conjestion
