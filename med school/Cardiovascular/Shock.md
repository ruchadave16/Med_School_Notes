*State of circulatory failure that impairs tissue perfusion causing [[hypoxia]]*
#### Mechanism
-  Delivery of oxygen ↓ and removal of metabolites ↓

1) Compensated: Neuroendocrine and CV response maintains perfusion
	- [[Sympathetic Nervous System|sympathetic]] stimulation of $\beta1$ cause HR ↑ and contractility ↑ 
	- Also causes myocardial O2 usage ↑ 
	- $\alpha1$  causes vasoconstriction and ↑ BP
	- [[Hypothalamus]] releases [[ACTH]] -> [[cortisol]]
	- Pituitary releases [[Vasopressin]]
	- ↓ perfusion of renal artery and ↑ Na+ concentration means [[renin]] released

**Inflammatory** Response
- TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6, IL-8, Complement, Neutrophil -> ROS

**Cellular** Response
- Anaerobic glycolysis -> Metabolic acidosis because ↑ lactate
- Endothelial Glycocalyx shedding

2) Decompensated: Worsening cellular injury, microcirculatory failure
3) Irreversible: Profound ATP depletion, MODS, death

#### Treatment
1) ABCs
	- [[IV fluids]] first
2) [[Vasopressors]]
	- Goal: restore perfusion to organs: improved mental status, urine output ≥0.5 mL/kg/hr, warm extremities
3) Maintain MAP ≥65 mmHg
	- If not reached, add [[Vasopressin]]
	- Consider [[angiotensin II]]
