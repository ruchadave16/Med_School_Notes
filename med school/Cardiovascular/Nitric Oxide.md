#### Synthesis
- endothelial nitric oxide synthase: conversion of oxygen and L-arginine -> NO and L-citrulline
- Activity regulated by Ca2+ dependent and independent mechanisms
	- Shear stress -> PKA activation (flow induced vasodilation)
	- ↑ intracellular Ca2+ -> binds [[calmodulin]] -> activates eNOS
- Once produced, NO diffuses to smooth muscle to activate guanylyl cyclase: cGTP -> cGMP
	- cGMP-dependent [[smooth muscle]] relaxation
