### Etiology
- MPO-ANCA auto-antibody (ANCA)
- Older adults

### Pathophysiology
- Type of [[ANCA Associated Vasculitis]] but unknown pathogenesis
- Histology:
	- segmental [[fibrinoid necrosis]]
	- focal transmural necrotizing lesions 
	- **NO granulomatous inflammation**

### Clinical Features
- Lesions of skin, mucous membranes, lungs, brain, heart, GI tract, kidneys, or muscles
	- Similar to [[Polyarteritis Nodosa|PAN]] but all lesions of **same** age
- [[Necrotizing glomerulonephritis]] in 90%
- [[Pulmonary capillaritis]] common
