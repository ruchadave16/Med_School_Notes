### Etiology
- ↑ vagal tone
- Meds like [[beta blockers]]
- Intrinsic [[Sick Sinus Syndrome]]

### Pathophysiology
- Transient failure of SA node to fire -- misses a beat

### Clinical Features
- Symptoms
- Signs
- Variants

### Diagnosis
- ECG:
	- A sudden pause in the rhythm that is not a multiple of the normal P-P interval, with no P wave visible during the pause

### Treatment
- If symptomatic or > 3sec pauses
	- Holter monitoring
	- Cardiac consult for pacemaker

### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
