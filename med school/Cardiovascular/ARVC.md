### Etiology
- Genetic: autosomal dominant
- 1 in 2000 to 1 in 5000
- 10% death in atheletes

### Pathophysiology
- RV muscle replaced by fibrofatty tissue
	- Causes thinning of RV

![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 11.04.31 AM.png]]

- Leads to [[vtach]] and RV failure
	- [[right heart failure]]

### Clinical Features
- Syncope or ventricular arrhythmias

### Diagnosis
- [[ECG]]
	- The pathognomonic finding is an **Epsilon wave**, a small positive deflection (a "blip") at the very end of the QRS complex, best seen in V1.
	- T-wave inversions in the right precordial leads (V1-V3)
	
![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 11.05.20 AM.png]]
### Treatment
- Consult and consider [[ICD]]
