- SA node generally determines [[heart rate]] because fastest frequency of firing
	- A gradient of intrinsic pacemaker frequencies along the cardiac electrical system
#### Electrical Activity
![[Screenshot 2026-04-30 at 10.01.25 PM.png]]

- Phase 4
	- Initial potential at -50 to -70 mV (fewer K+ channels)
	- Spontaneous decay over time <- pacemaker potential
		- Rate of decay determines time needed to meet threshold
	- *funny current* <- channel that is activated by hyperpolarization
		- Carried mainly by Na+ ions
		- Cyclic nucleotide-regulated ion channel (HCN channel)
		- Activated by [[cAMP]] <- sympathetic acts on this
		
	![[Screenshot 2026-04-30 at 10.06.07 PM.png]]
- Phase 0: 
	- Threshold = -40 to -55 mV is met
	- Heartbeat is sparked
	- *Volt-gated Ca2+ channels (T-type Ca2+ channels)*
		- Past -55mV, Ca2+ enters cell through these
	- When further decayed, *L-type Ca2+ channels* open
		- These activate at **lower** potential than T-type
		- Ca2+ influx increases significantly once here
		- These are activated by **PKA**

- Phase 3
	- Repolarization because Ca2+ channels close 
	- Outward current through *delayed rectifier K+ channels*
