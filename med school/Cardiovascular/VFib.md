### Etiology
- Multiple, rapid, chaotic electrical wavelets propagate randomly through the ventricles
- It is most often triggered by severe ischemia, electrolyte disturbances, or structural heart disease.

### Pathophysiology
- Chaotic ventricular electrical activity resulting in no cardiac output; a pulseless arrest rhythm

### Clinical Features
- Symptoms
- Signs
- Variants

### Diagnosis
- [[ECG]]: A chaotic, wavy line with no discernible P waves, QRS complexes, or T waves.

### Treatment
-  Code blue and perform immediate **defibrillation**
