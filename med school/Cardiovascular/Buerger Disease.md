### Etiology
- Heavy [[Tobacco]] use
- < 35 age

### Pathophysiology
- Segmental acute and chronic inflammation of medium / small arteries (tibial and radial mostly)

### Clinical Features
- Cold-induced [[Raynaud phenomenon]] (change of color)
- Instep foot pain induced by exercise
- Chronic extremity ulcerations -> [[Gangrenous Necrosis|gangrene]]
