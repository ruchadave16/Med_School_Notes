Matches with stages from [[cardiac cycle]]
- a) Atrial systole:
	- Depolarization of the atrium, **p-wave on ECG**
- b) Isovolumetric contraction
	- Depolarization of ventricle, **QRS wave**
- c) Early ejection
	- Rapid decline in LV volume, **end of QRS**
- d) Reduced ejection
	- **t wave**
- e) Isovolumetric relaxation
	- dicrotic notch
- f) LV filling (rapid) 
- g) LV filling (slow)
