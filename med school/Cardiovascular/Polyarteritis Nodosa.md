*Systemic vasculitis of small / medium **muscular** arteries, commonly renal or visceral vessels but **NOT** pulmonary*
### Etiology
- NO association with ANCAs
	- Many patients have [[Hepatitis B Virus]]
- Commonly of young adults

### Pathophysiology
- Segmental transmural necrotizing inflammation of medium-sized vessels with superimposed thrombosis
- Acute [[fibrinoid necrosis]] and luminal thrombosis

![[Screenshot 2026-05-05 at 10.31.18 PM.png]]
### Clinical Features
- Episodic with long interval-free periods
- Rapidly accelerating [[hypertension]]
- Abdominal pain
- Bloody stools
- Muscle aches
- [[Peripheral neuritis]]

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Immunosuppression

### Complications
- Renal involvement -> death
- Untreated = fatal
