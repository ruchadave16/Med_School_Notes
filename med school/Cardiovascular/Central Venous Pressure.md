*Pressure of thoracic vena cava near RA which determines filling of RV*]

![[Screenshot 2026-05-04 at 12.20.38 AM.png]]
### Conditions
- Skeletal muscle pump
	- Walking upright can reduce venous pressure in legs significantly -> 1 way pumps
- Respiratory activity
	- ↑ return with increase in difference between abdominal vena cava and RA
	- ↑ with increase in rate and depth of respiration
- Valsalva
### Wave (changes to RA pressure)
- a-wave: right Atrial contraction
- c-wave: protrusion of triCuspid valve at start of RV contraction
- x-descent: right atrial relaXation and downward movement of RV during contraction
- v-wave: back pressure wave during right atrial filling 
- y-descent: opening of tricuspid valve during earlY ventricular diastole
