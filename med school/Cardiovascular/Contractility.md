*Intrinsic strength and velocity of myocardium to contract for sarcomere length independent of preload*

- Isolated increase will mean ↓ ESV and ↑ SV with NO change to pressure
- Independent of length of sarcomere
- **+ Factors**
	- Autonomic regulation and [[catecholamines]] = #1
	-  [[Afterload]] and [[heart rate]]
	- [[Sympathomimetic Drugs|Sympathomimetics]]
	- Cardiac glycosides ([[digoxin]])
	- [[Phosphodiesterase inhibitors]] ([[amrinone]])
	- Ca2+ sensitizers([[levosimendan]])
- **- Factors**
	- [[beta blockers]]
	- [[calcium channel blockers]] ([[nifedipine]])
	- Class 1A and 1C anti-arrhythmics

#### Mechanism
- Related to ↑ $[Ca^{2+}]$ in cell -> ↑  # actin-myosin bridges because ↑ [[Troponin]] C activation
- ↑ phosphorylation of [[myosin light chain kinase]]
#### Anrep Effect
- Contractility ↑ if increased [[afterload]] to maintain SV
#### Bowditch Effect
- Contractility ↑ to increase in HR to increase velocity of ejection
