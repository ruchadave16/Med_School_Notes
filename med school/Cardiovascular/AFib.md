### Etiology
- Most common sustained arrhythmia
-  Triggered by atrial stretching from hypertension, heart failure, or valvular disease

### Pathophysiology
- Mechanism of disease
- Relevant pathways (e.g. immune, hormonal)

### Diagnosis
- [[ECG]]
	- An "irregularly irregular" rhythm with no discernible P waves.

### Treatment
- Assess patient stability
- Focus on controlling the heart rate and 

**ABCs**
- [[Anticoagulants]] to lower stroke risk
	- Evaluate the need for anticoagulation (e.g., using the CHA₂DS₂-VASc score)
	- 0 (men) or 1 (women) = no need
	- 1 (men) or 2 (women) = consider anticoagulants
	- >2 (men) or >3 (women) = recommended

- Better symptom control (rate vs rhythm)
	- Rate Control
		- [[Beta Blockers]] <- first line
		-  [[Non-dihydropyridines|Non-DHPs]]
		- [[Digoxin]] if HF or sedentary patient
		- [[Amiodarone]] if refractory to other 
	- Rhythm Control 
		- [[DCC]] (Direct Current Cardioversion) = 80-90% success
		- No structural heart condition = [[flecainide]], [[propafenone]], [[dofetilide]], [[dronedarone]], [[sotalol]]
		- Heart failure = [[amiodarone]] or [[dofetilide]]
		- [[coronary artery disease]] = [[dofetilide]], [[dronedarone]], [[sotalol]] > amiodarone
- CV comorbidity optimization (HTN, obesity, diabetes, etc.)

**If [[WPW Syndrome]]: most are contraindicated**
- Do either cardiovension or [[Antiarrhythmics]] ([[Ibutilide]] or [[Procainamide]])
