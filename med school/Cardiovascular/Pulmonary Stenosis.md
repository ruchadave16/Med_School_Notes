### Etiology
- Congenital, often part of [[Tetralogy of Fallot]]
- Associated with [[Noonan syndrome]]
### Pathophysiology
- Overload of RV -> [[Right Ventricular Hypertrophy|RV hypertrophy]]
- Commonly malformation of valve
	- Dilation of pulmonary artery

![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 11.37.26 PM.png]]
### Clinical Features
- Generally asymptomatic 
- Severe cases = exertional dyspnea and fatigue, syncope
- Murmur: systolic crescendo-decrescendo murmur through the narrowed valve, preceded by a pulmonic ejection click
	- Click can decrease intensity on inspiration
	- Earlier the click is, the **more** the stenosis
	- Palpable thrill
