### Etiology
- Underlying heart disease

### Pathophysiology
- Significant conduction delay through left bundle branch
- Impulse must travel through right branch and spread cell by cell -> LV

### Clinical Features
- Symptoms
- Signs
- Variants

### Diagnosis
- [[ECG]]: A wide QRS (>0.12s) with a deep S wave in V1 ("W") and a broad, notched R wave in V6 ("M").

### Treatment
- If new LBBB + chest pain, treat like [[Myocardial Infarction|MI]]

- Short-term
- Long-term
