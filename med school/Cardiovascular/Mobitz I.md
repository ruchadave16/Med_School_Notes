### Etiology
- Often caused by high vagal tone (e.g., in athletes) or medications.

### Pathophysiology
- A functional block within the AV node itself. The AV nodal cells "fatigue" with each beat.

- Symptoms
- Signs
- Variants

### Diagnosis
- [[ECG]]
	- Progressive lengthening of the PR interval until a QRS complex is dropped.

### Treatment
- Benign often, so observe
- Look for reversible causes like [[beta blockers]]
