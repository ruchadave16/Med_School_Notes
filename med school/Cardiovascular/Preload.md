*passive wall stress to LV at end of diastole before contraction*

- Increases if venous return increases
- Effect on contraction force depends on [[sarcomere]] length before systole
- Normal length is below this point so normally increasing will increase contraction force
	- **Frank-Starling relationship**
	- Any factors that increase EDV or EDP will increase preload and thus SV / CO
	- ↑ V so ↑ muscle fiber length so ↑ tension so ↑ stroke volume –
	- [[Hill 3-Element Muscle Model]] says this only happens till max length is passed, then decreases

![[Screenshot 2025-08-25 at 7.21.14 PM.png]]

- Normally this causes ↑ EDV and SV but normal ESV
- No contractility changes 
- Realistically, arterial pressure ↑ so [[afterload]] ↑ as well
#### What Changes?
Any change to  LV pressure and stroke volume
- Increase Reasons
	- Total blood volume
	- Skeletal muscle contraction increases venous return
	- Inspiration effects to ↓ intra-thoracic pressure dilates thoracic veins and diaphragm contracts to compress veins
- Decrease Reasons
	- ↓ Blood Volume
	- Exhalation lowers venous return
