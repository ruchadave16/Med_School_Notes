*Modified cardiac cells that generate waves of depolarization to stimulate contractions*
Found in subendocardial layer and adjacent [[myocardium]]
#### Nodes
1) Sinoatrial (SA) Node: At [[Right Atrium|RA]] near [[SVC]] entrance 
	- [[Pacemaker cells]]
	- Fire [[Cardiac Ventricular Action Potential|cardiac action potential]] spontaneously to excite adjacent myocytes

2) Atrioventricular (AV) Node: [[Right Atrium|RA]] beneath [[endocardium]] at junction of [[Right Ventricle|RV]]
	- Impulse goes through fibrous skeleton to ventricles through AV Bundle (of His)
	- Start of the only electrical connection across the annulus fibrosus
	- Delays impulse by 0.1 s and **allows atria to contract** before ventricles
	
3) Atrioventricular Bundle (of His)
	- Tissue conducting from AV to IV septum
	- AV bundle splits into **[[Purkinje Fibers]]** (L and R)
	- Purkinje Fibers = widest cells in heart, highest conduction velocity

**Pathway**
- Heart contraction of ventricles (systole) initiated by intrinsic bio-electric signal from SA node of RA (P wave)
- Signal relayed through atrioventricular node after delay -> bundle of His -> Purkinje fibers -> this depolarizes ventricles (QRS complex)
- Fire off [[Cardiac Ventricular Action Potential|cardiac ventricular action potential]] -> ↑ intracellular Ca2+
- Myocyte contraction
