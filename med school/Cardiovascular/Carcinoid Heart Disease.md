### Etiology
- Metastases of primary neuroendocrine tumor

### Pathophysiology
- ↑ [[Serotonin]] and other vasoactive substances secreted into hepatic veins of liver
- Lung deactivates these, sparing **left heart**
### Clinical Features
- Severe [[Tricuspid Regurgitation|tricuspid regurgitation]]
- Moderate [[pulmonary stenosis]]

**Triad**
- [[Right Heart Failure|right sided heart failure]]
- [[carcinoid syndrome]] 
	- flushing, diarrhea, bronchospasm
- Valve lesions
