### Etiology
- MPO-ANCAs in 1/2 cases (auto-antibodies)
- Type of [[ANCA Associated Vasculitis]]

### Pathophysiology
- Extravascular necrotizing granulomas
- Peripheral [[eosinophilia]]
- Infiltration of vessels with [[eosinophils]]

### Clinical Features
- **Palpable [[purpura]]**
- GI bleeding
- Renal disease (focal and segmental [[glomerulosclerosis]])
- Asthma
- Allergic rhinitis
- Lung infiltrates
