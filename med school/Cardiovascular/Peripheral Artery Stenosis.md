*Narrowing of larger artery from [[Atherosclerosis]]*
### Etiology
- ~8.2 million in US
- Common in males > 50 
- Risk factors
	- [[Smoking]]
	- [[Diabetes Mellitus|Diabetes mellitus]]
	- Hypertension
	- [[Atherosclerosis|atherosclerosis]] family history
	- [[Hyperlipidemia]]

### Pathophysiology
- [[Atherosclerosis]]: Fatty streaks -> plaques -> calcified lesions -> thrombosis / stenosis
- Downstream vasodilation means it is critical past 50-60% radius loss
- 

### Clinical Features
- Lower limb stenosis in multiple locations
- Intermittent claudication -- cramping pain that resolves with rest
	- Advanced can have rest pain
- Distal pulses reduced
- Cool extremities
- Atrophic skin, hair loss, delayed capillary refill
- Bruits over stenotic artery

### Diagnosis
- Ankle-Brachial Index (ABI): supine systolic of ankle : arm
	- Normal: 1.0-1.4 
	- Moderate PAD: <0.7 
	- Mild PAD: <0.9 
	- Severe PAD: <0.4
- Treadmill exercise test
- Duplex ultrasound to visualize stenosis
- CTA or MRA to map arteries
### Treatment
- Lifestyle changes
- [[Statins]] for pre-emptive
	- ACEi/ARB for BP control
	- [[Antiplatelets]] ([[aspirin]] or [[cloridogrel]])
	- [[PDE-3 inhibitors]] for ↑ walking distance
- Exercise therapy
- Revascularization
- Amputation

### Complications
- Key finding for [[peripheral artery disease]]
