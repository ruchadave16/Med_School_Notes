### Normal Heart Sounds
- **S1** (Mitral Valve and Tricuspid Valve closes)
	- Ventricular pressure > aortic pressure
- **S2** (AV and PV Close)
	- Aortic pressure > ventricular pressure
	- During inspiration, AV can close before PV -- physiological splitting
		- ↓ intrathoracic pressure during inspiration from ↑ venous return 
	- Fixed splitting -- split similar regardless of inspiration or expiration
	- Paradoxical splitting -- greater on expiration than inspiration

### Additional Sounds
- Systolic Sounds
	- Ejection Clicks: sharp, high pitched early systolic during opening of aortic and pulmonary valve
		- Associated with [[aortic stenosis]] or pulmonary stenosis
- Diastolic Sounds
	- Opening Snap: Mitral or tricuspid stenosis (high pitched sound)
	- S3 Sound: dull, low pitched sound heard from rapid ventricular filling
		- Normal in healthy adults / children
	- [[S4]] Sound: dull, low pitched sound from atrial contraction due to ejection of blood into a stiff ventricle
	- Quadruple Rhythm: S3 and S4 are present along with S1 and S2
	- Pericardial Knock: high pitched sound in early diastole after usual opening snap but before S3
