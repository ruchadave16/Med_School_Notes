Come from [[Subclavian Artery|subclavian a.]], ascend in transverse foramen of cervical [[vertebrae]] C1-6
- Comes through foramen magnum
- L and R combine to make [[basilar a.]]

Supplies: [[spinal cord]], brainstem, [[cerebellum]], thalamus, occipital and inf. temporal lobes

**Branches**
- Posterior inferior cerebellar a. (PICA)
	- Posterio-lateral medulla, posterior inferior cerebellum, inferior cerebellar peduncle, choroid plexus of 4th ventricle
	- Posterior spinal a. (PSA)
		- Posterior 1/3 of spinal cord
- Anterior spinal a. on medulla (unpaired) (ASA)
	- Anterior 2/3 of spinal cord
