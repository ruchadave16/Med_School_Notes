### Etiology
- **# 1 cause of death worldwide**, ~9 million deaths yearly
- Men > Women before menopause; gap narrows post-menopause
	- [[Estrogen]] is cardioprotective
- Higher burden in African Americans, South Asian, higher in rural and lower income areas

**Risk Factors**
- [[Hypertension]]
- [[Hyperlipidemia|Dyslipidemia]]
- [[Diabetes Mellitus]]
- [[Smoking]]
- [[Obesity]] (BMI > 30)
- Sedentary lifestyle
- [[Obstructive sleep apnea]]
- [[Chronic kidney disease]]
- Stress, bad diet

>[!Note] Non-modifyable Factors
>Age, male sex, family history of early CAD, Ethnicity **South Asia, African American**

### Diagnosis
- HEART Score
	- H = History (0–2): Slightly/moderately/highly suspicious
	- E = ECG (0–2): Normal/nonspecific/significant ST deviation
	- A = Age (0–2): 45 / 45–64 / ≥65
	- R = Risk factors (0–2): None / 1–2 / ≥3 or known atherosclerotic disease
	- T = Troponin (0–2): Normal / 1–3× normal / >3× normal
		- Total: 0–10
		- Low risk (0–3): 30-day MACE rate ~1.7% → consider early discharge
		- Moderate risk (4–6): Observation, serial troponins, further workup
		- High risk (7–10): Admit, early invasive strategy
		- Meta-analysis of 30 studies (n=44,202): HEART score ≥4 has 95.9% sensitivity for MACE
			- ER chest pain — predicts 30-day MACE; score ≥4 warrants admission
- TIMI (UA/NSTEMI)
	- 7 variables incl. age, CAD risk factors, ST changes, troponin, ASA use
	- Risk stratification of NSTEMI — guides invasive vs. conservative strategy
- GRACE Score 
	- Age, HR, SBP, creatinine, Killip class, cardiac arrest, ST deviation, troponin
	- Most validated for in-hospital & 6- month mortality in ACS
