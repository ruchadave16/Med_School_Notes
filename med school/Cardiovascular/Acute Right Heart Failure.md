### Etiology
- [[Pulmonary Embolism|Pulmonary embolism]]
- RV [[Myocardial Infarction|MI]]

### Pathophysiology
- Blocks pulmonary artery and increases RV preload
- RV dilates acutely causing [[Tricuspid Regurgitation|tricuspid regurgitation]] and ↑ CVP
- This means ↓ blood to LV, which goes into [[cardiogenic shock]]
- Systemic hypotension
### Clinical Features
- Hypotension
- ↑ [[JVD]]
- Clear lungs
