### Etiology
- Primary: Can be isolated anomaly or a result of [[Milroy disease]] from agenesis of lymphatic vessels
- Secondary: accumulation of interstitial fluid in a obstructed lymphatic
	- Tumors
	• Surgical procedures (e.g.. axillary lymph node dissections associated with mastectomy)
	• Post-radiation fibrosis
	• Filariasis
	• Post-inflammatory thrombosis and scarring.
### Pathophysiology
- ↑ hydrostatic pressure in lymphatics causing [[edema]]
