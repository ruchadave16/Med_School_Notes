*[[Myocardial Infarction|MI]] with 50% stenosis*
- 5-15% of all MIs

**Causes**
-  Coronary spasm
- Coronary microvascular dysfunction
- Plaque erosion/disruption not visible on angiography
- Spontaneous coronary artery dissection ([[SCAD]])
- [[Takotsubo]]
- Myocarditis
