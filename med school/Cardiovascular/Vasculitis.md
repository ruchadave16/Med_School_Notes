*Vessel wall inflammation*

- Non-infectious vasculitis: immune-mediated 
- Infectious vasculitis: vascular invasion by infectious pathogens

**Categories**
- Large vessel
	- [[Takayasu arteritis]]
	- [[Temporal Arteritis|Giant cell arteritis]]
- Medium vessel
	- [[Polyarteritis nodosa]]
	- [[Kawasaki disease]]
	- [[Buerger disease]]
- Small vessel
	- [[ANCA associated vasculitis]]
		- Microscopic polyangiitis
		- Granulomatosis with polyangiitis -- Wegener
		- Eosinophilic granulomatosis with polyangiitis -- Churg-Straus
	- [[Immune complex vasculitis]]

#### Finding
- [[Purpura]] that are palpable
	- Raised and non-blanched
