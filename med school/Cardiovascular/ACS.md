*Group of conditions that stop or reduce blood flow to heart*
- Includes [[Myocardial Infarction|MI]] (damage or death to heart tissue)
#### Physical Exam
- Tachycardia -- pain/sympathetic activation
- Bradycardia (inferior [[Myocardial Infarction|MI]] → vagal)
- Hypotension (cardiogenic shock or RV infarction)
- Hypertension (common, pain-driven)

**Cardiac**
- New [[S4]]
- New [[S3]]
- New [[Cardiac Murmurs|murmurs]]
- [[JVD]]

**Pulmonary**
- B/L crackles/rales (LV failure -> pulmonary edema)
- ↓ breath sounds

**Skin**
- Cool, clammy skin

**Abdomen**
- Epigastric tenderness in inferior MI
#### Management (MONA-BAH)
- [[Morphine]] -- can block pain though so be careful
- [[Oxygen]] -- only if SpO2 < 90% (supplemental can be harmful)
- [[Nitrates]] -- sublingual or IV NTG for pain relief
	- Avoid in [[RV MI]] (need preload) and if [[PDE Inhibitors]] (PDE-5) used within 24–48 hrs
- DAPT
	- [[Aspirin]] -- 325 mg non-enteric-coated, chewed, ASAP (irreversibly inhibits COX-1 → ↓ TXA₂ → ↓ platelet aggregation)
		- **Give to ALL**
		- Continued indefinitely
	- [[Ticagrelor]] or [[prasugrel]] recommended over [[clopidogrel]]
- [[Statins]]
	- If at maximum statin and LDL > 70 (or if statin intolerant) adding a nonstatin lipid-lowering agent is recommended to further reduce the risk of MACE
- 
- [[Beta Blockers]] -- reduce myocardial O₂ demand
	- Caution in [[acute decompensated HF]], bradycardia, hypotension, active bronchospasm
- [[Anticoagulants]] -- [[heparin]], [[bivalirudin]], [[fondaparinux]]
- Cardiology Consult + ECG (12 lead) + Monitoring + IV access + Continuous SpO₂
