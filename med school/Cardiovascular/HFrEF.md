### Etiology
- Primary causes
- Risk factors

### Pathophysiology
- Weak, damaged ventricle
- [[PV Diagrams]] shifts to **right**, isovolumetric contraction phase reduced and SV ↓
- Frank-Starling curve shifts to **down and right**
- Low CO -> [[Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System|RAAS system]] causing sympathetic activation
	- [[Norepinephrine]] and [[Angiotensin II]] causes maladaptive compensation

### Clinical Features
- Dyspnea
- Orthopnea
- Paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea (PND)
- Edema
- Fatigue and weakness

### Diagnosis
**Differential**: Evaluate for [[coronary artery disease]]
- Chest xray
	- Cardiomegaly
	- pulmonary venous congestion 
- ECG
- TTE (first line)
	- LVEF ≤ 40%
	- eccentric hypertrophy
	- Global Longitudinal Strain
- Cardiac MRI
	- Late Gadolinium Enhancement (LGE) is a key differentiator of etiology
- Coronary CT angiogram
	- Rule out obstructive CAD as cause
- PET -- to guide revascularization

- Labs
	- [[BNP]] / NT-proBNP massively increases
	- highly likely for BNP > 400 pg/mL
	- mildly elevated [[troponins]]
- Left heart catheterization
- Right heart catheterization
	- Low Cardiac Output / Cardiac Index (CO/CI).
	- High Pulmonary Capillary Wedge Pressure (PCWP > 18 mmHg).
	- High Systemic Vascular Resistance (SVR).

### Treatment
- ARNI: [[Sacubitril]] or [[Valsartan]] or else ACE inhibitors
- [[Beta Blockers|beta blocker]]: [[Metoprolol]], [[Carvedilol]], [[Bisoprolol]]
- [[Spironolactone]] or [[Eplerenone]] to block aldosterone receptors
- [[SGLT2 inhibitors]]
- Addon of [[Hydralazine]] and [[Nitrates]] in black patients 

- [[Loop Diuretics]] for symptomatic treatment

- [[ICD]] or [[CRT]]

**End Stage**: LVAD vs heart transplant
### Complications
- [[right heart failure]]
- Secondary [[Mitral Regurgitation|mitral regurgitation]]
- Risk of sudden cardiac death from arrhythmia
