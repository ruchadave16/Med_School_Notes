### Etiology
- Infancy and childhood (most < 5 yrs)
- Asian descent = more common
- Viral agents identified
### Pathophysiology
- Arteritis of large and medium vessels
	- [[Coronary Arteries|coronary aa.]] involvement -> [[Myocardial Infarction|MI]]
- Transmural inflammatory infiltrate
	- 
### Clinical Features
- Acute fever (> 5 days)
- Red, dry cracked lips and inflammed tongue
- Peeling skin
- Red eyes
- Swollen lymph nodes
- Swelling of palms and soles
- Widespread rash
- Coronary artery aneurysms
