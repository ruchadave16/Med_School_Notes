*Partial occlusion -> subendothelium necrosis*
- Similar presentation to unstable angina
### **ECG**
- ST depression or T wave inversion
- Positive troponin
#### Treatment
- Obtain cardiac troponins
- Start [[Antiplatelets]] and anti-ischemic therapy

Risk Stratify First — TIMI/GRACE/HEART
- EARLY INVASIVE (Cath within 24–48h):
	- Refractory ischemia, hemodynamic instability
	- High TIMI/GRACE score
	- New ST changes or elevated troponin
	- Prior PCI or CABG
- CONSERVATIVE (Medical management ± stress test)
