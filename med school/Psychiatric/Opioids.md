*Broad class of drugs that bind to opioid receptors and have morphine-like effect*

| Drug              | Receptor Activity                            | Potency         | Clinical Notes                                        |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| [[Morphine]]      | $\mu$ agonist                                | Standard        | Prototype, renal impairment                           |
| [[Hyrdomorphone]] | $\mu$ agonist                                | High            | Used when morphine not tolerated                      |
| [[Oxycodone]]     | $\mu$ agonist                                | Moderate - high | Oral for moderate - severe pain                       |
| [[Fentanyl]]      | $\mu$ agonist                                | Very high       | Used in ICU, transdermal patch for chronic pain       |
| [[Methadone]]     | $\mu$ agonist, [[NMDA Receptors]] antagonist | High            | ↑ life, used for chronic pain and opioid use disorder |
| [[Codeine]]       | Weak $\mu$ agonist                           | Low             | Converted to morphine by [[CYP2D6]]                   |
| [[Hydrocodone]]   | $\mu$ agonist                                | Moderate        | Used with [[Acetaminophen\|acetaminophen]]            |
| [[Buprenorphine]] | partial $\mu$ agonist                        | High            | Used to treat [[opioid use disorder]]                 |
| [[Butorphanol]]   | $\kappa$ agonist, $\mu$ antagonist           | Moderate        |                                                       |
| [[Naloxone]]      | ANTAGONIST                                   |                 | OPIOID OVERDOSE REVERSAL                              |
#### Action
- [[Opioid Receptors|Opioid receptors]], mainly $\mu$ (**Gi**) in PAG, raphe nuclei, or dorsal horn
	- ↓ cAMP, ↓ Ca2+ influx, ↑ K+ efflux
	- Neuronal **inhibition** of pain
	- Reduce Substance P release from C and $A\delta$ fibers
- Act on [[mesolimbic system]] by increasing [[dopamine]] release
- Medulla respiratory centers suppressed 
- Medulla cough centers suppressed by codeine and dextromethorphan
- Parasympathetic activation -- miosis
- ↓ GI motility by activating $\mu$ in [[enteric system]]
	- ↑ smooth muscle tone in biliary tract
	- Urinary retention
#### Signs
- Analgesia
	- Both sensory and emotional aspects of pain
	- Nociceptive highly responsive, neuropathic less
- Euphoria
- Pruritus
- **Respiratory depression**
	- Medulla respiratory centers suppressed
- Cough suppression
- Sedation / coma
- **Miosis**
	- **IF** hypoxia, then could be mydriasis
- Constipation <- HUGE issue
- Nausea / vomiting
	- Can cause biliary colic
- Temperature regulation 
- Hypotension in hypovolemic pts
- Urinary retention

**Consequences**
- [[Opioid-Induced Hyperalgesia]] 
#### Uses
Codeine and dextromethorphan -- cough suppressors

Pain Management
- Nociceptive pain 
- Combo with NSAIDs or acetaminophen

[[Diarrhea]] Treatment
- [[Loperamide]]
	- Does NOT cross BBB so ↓↓ CNS effects
- Diphenoxylate + atropine
	- Can cross BBB
	- Atropine to reduce abuse

[[Anesthesia]]
#### Tolerance
1) Upregulation of cAMP pathway
	- Normally opioids inhibit adenylyl cyclase so ↓ cAMP
	- Chronic usage means upregulation of adenylyl cyclase 
2) Receptor Desensitization
	- Receptors can get phosphorylated and become less sensitive
3) [[NMDA Receptors]]Activation
#### Administration
- Use opioid switching
	- Reduces dose by **20-25%** 
#### [[Opioid Overdose]]
- **Miosis**
- **Respiratory depression**
	- ↓↓ RR
	- Shallow breathing
- **Low consciousness**
- Truncal rigidity
- Hypotension, bradycardia

>[!Warning]
>Give [[Naloxone]]
#### Withdrawal
Flu-like symptoms
- Mydriasis
- Diarrhea
- Lacrimation
- Piloerection
- Muscle aches
- Stomach pain
- Rhinorrhea

**Management**
- Gradual taper
- Long-acting agonists for maintenance therapy ([[methadone]])
- [[Buprenorphine]] = partial agonist
- [[Clonidine]] for ↓ autonomic symptoms
#### Contraindication
**High risk for respiratory depression**
- Pulmonary disease
- CNS depressants
