1) Hypoventilation
	- Reduced PaO2 and an increased PaCO2
	- No increase in A-a gradient
2) Decreased Inspired O2
	- Common at high gradients
	- PB decreased
	- Reduction in Pao2 due to the decrease in PAO2
	- No increase in A-a gradient
3) Poor [[gas exchange]]
	- Failure of PO2 in the pulmonary capillary blood to equilibrate with alveolar gas
	- A-a gradient is **increased**
	- Normally this is not common
		- O2 equilibrates early along the length of the pulmonary capillary 
	- Can measure diffusion capacity through DLCO (carbon monoxide) because of high capacity of hemoglobin for CO
	- ↓ DLCO when surface area for exchange lost
	- ↑ DLCO when ↑ blood volume
4) [[Ventilation-Perfusion]] Mismatch
5) **Shunts** – blood flow that *may not* be in contact with [[alveoli]]
	- Right-to-left: Not oxygenated by alveoli
		- Can be cardiac shunt because of defect in wall between ventricles 
		- Allows right ventricular blood to pass through left ventricle and bypass lungs
		- [[Hypoxemia]] by having patient breath high $O_2$ gas
		- Could also be blood flow in lungs to area not ventilated
	- Physiological Shunt: Normal
		- Bronchial blood flow and small amount of coronary blood flow into left ventricle bypassing alveoli
	- Left-to-right: Doesn’t bypass alveoli
		- Shunted from left to right side (not delivered to tissues)
		- ↑ PaO2
		- **No hypoxemia**
