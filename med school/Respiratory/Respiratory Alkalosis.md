*CO2 elimination due to hyperventilation causes ↑ pH*

**Causes**: excitement, anxiety, high altitude, overdose of stimulants
- ↑ alveolar ventilation and ↓ CO2

- [[Buffers]]: [[Intracellular Buffers|Intracellular buffering]]
- [[Respiratory Regulation of Acid-Base|Lungs]] (minutes): CANNOT compensate because cause
- [[Renal Regulation of Acid-Base|Kidneys]] (hours/days): ONLY method
	- ↓ PCO2 and ↑ cell pH 
	- ↓ secretion of $H^+$ and NH4 and  $HCO_3^-$ reabsorption 
		- ↑ HCO3- excretion
	- pH ↓  to normal but ↓↓ HCO3-
	
![[Screenshot 2026-08-31 at 11.47.35 PM.png]]

**NORMAL Compensation**: 
- Acute: ↓ HCO3- 2 mEq/L for every 10 mmHg PCO2
- Chronic: ↓ HCO3- 4 mEq/L for every 10 mmHg PCO2
