### Etiology
- Inhalation of crystalline silica 
- Most prevalent industrial disease worldwide

### Pathophysiology
- Hyalinized collagen fibers concentrically arranged around an amorphous center (imparting a “whorled” appearance)
- Birefringent silica particles may be identified, typically toward the center of the nodule
