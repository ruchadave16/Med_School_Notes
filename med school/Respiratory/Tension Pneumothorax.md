### Etiology
- Mechanical ventilation
- Resusitation

### Pathophysiology
- Pleural pressure becomes equal to atmospheric pressure -- + throughout cycle
- Ventilation is severely compromised 
- Positive pressure is transmitted to the mediastinum -> ↓ venous return and cardiac

- One-way valve lets air enter but not leave

### Clinical Features
- ↓ breath in one hemithorax
- Hyperresonance on percussion
- JVD
- Mediastinum shift to normal side
- Hypotension
- Distended neck veins

### Diagnosis
- Xray WASTES time
### Treatment
- Bilateral chest tubes
-  Large bore needle should be inserted into the pleural space through the 2nd anterior intercostal space.

- EMERGENCY
