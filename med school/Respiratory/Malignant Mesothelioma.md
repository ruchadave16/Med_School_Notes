- [[Asbestos]] exposure = 90% of cases

### Pathophysiology
- Lesion from pleura that spreads in pleural space
- 60% = epithelioid 
	- + for keratin proteins, calretinin, Wilms tumor 1 (WT-1), cytokeratin 5/6, and podoplanin
	- Negative for TTF1 and p40
- 10% = sarcomatoid
- 10-15% = biphasic
