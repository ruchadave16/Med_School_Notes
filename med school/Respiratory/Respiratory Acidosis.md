*CO2 retention due to hypoventilation causes ↓ pH*

**Causes**: trauma to respiratory control center, pulm. obstruction, respiratory muscle weakness, overdose of [[Opioids|opiates]], sedatives, [[Guillain Barre Syndrome]], [[Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis|ALS]], [[Multiple Sclerosis|MS]]
- ↓ ventilation

- [[Buffers]]: [[Intracellular Buffers|Intracellular buffering]]
- [[Respiratory Regulation of Acid-Base|Lungs]] (minutes): **CANNOT compensate** because cause
- [[Renal Regulation of Acid-Base|Kidneys]] (hours/days): ONLY method
	- ↑ PO2 and ↓ renal cell pH
	- ↑↑ secretion of $H^+$ and $HCO_3^-$ formation
	- More acidic urine
	- pH ↑ brought back to normal 
	- BUT HCO3- ↑↑ 

![[Screenshot 2026-08-31 at 11.46.28 PM.png]]

**NORMAL Compensation**: 
- Acute: HCO3- 1 mEq/L for every ↑ 10 mmHg PCO2
- Chronic: HCO3- 3 mEq/L for every ↑ 10 mmHg PCO2
