### Etiology
- Infectious
- Other = gastric reflux, allergy, chronic smoking
- 50% cases occur among individuals aged 5-24 years
- 25-40% cases = viral
	- [[Adenovirus]]
	- [[EBV]]
	- [[Rhinovirus]]
	- [[Enterovirus]]
	- [[Coxsackievirus]]
	- [[Influenza Virus]]
	- [[Parainfluenza Virus]]
- 10-15% = bacterial
	- [[Strep Pyogenes|group A strep]]
	- [[Neisseria Gonorrhoea]]
	- Arcanobacterium haemolyticum
	- Fusobacterium necrophorum
	- [[Mycoplasma Pneumoniae]]
- Fungal = [[Candida Albicans]]
### Pathophysiology
- Primary disease of the [[pharynx]], invasion of mucosa
- Triggers inflammation
	- S. pneumoniae and H. influenzae colonize the oropharynx, but do not cause pharyngitis.

### Clinical Features
- Sore throat
- Viral URI symptoms
- Strep Throat: pharyngal erythema, exudate, white/grayish patches
	- Tonsillar exudates
	- Fever
	- Absence of cough and URI symptoms.

- Throat culture

### Treatment
- [[Penicillins|Penicillin]] if Group A
- [[ceftriaxone]] if [[Neisseria Gonorrhoea]] 

### Complications
- Acute [[Rheumatic Fever]]
