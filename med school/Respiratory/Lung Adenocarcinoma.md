### Etiology
- EGFR – especially in never smokers
- ALK
- KRAS – very rare in never smokers
- More common in women

### Pathophysiology
- Located on periphery, smaller
- Can be 1 or many nodules

- Invasive **EPITHELIAL** malignant tumor -- glandular differentiation or **mucin** production
	- Microinvasive Adenocarcinoma = <5 mm of invasion
	- Adenocarcinoma in situ = < 3 cm in size and only dysplastic cells
- **TTF-1 POSITIVE** cells, p63, p40, synaptophysin, chromogranin negative
