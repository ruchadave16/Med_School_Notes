### Etiology
- Children and adults, especially in fall / winter
- Risk factors
	- Acute bronchitis: Viral infection, exposure to irritants, weakened immune system
	- Chronic bronchitis: Cigarette smoking, prolonged exposure to pollutants, advanced age, and underlying lung diseases such as asthma or COPD
- Mainly: [[Viral Infections|viral infection]], else atypical bacteria
### Pathophysiology
- Inflammation of bronchi and/or lower trachea from damage to respiratory epithelium
	- Loss of ciliary function
	- Bronchial wall thickening

### Clinical Features
- Persistent cough
- No evidence of COPD or pneumonia
- URI symptoms -> expiratory wheeze

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Supportive care
- Antibiotics are not routinely indicated

### Complications
- Evaluate for pneumonia if lasts > 5-7 days
