### Pathophysiology
- Exudative [[pleural effusion]] with [[pneumonia]]

1) Uncomplicated parapneumonic effusion:
	- Sterile fluid
	- pH > 7.2
	- small and free flowing
	- no drainage, just antibiotics
2) Complicated pleural effusion
	-  Bacteria present
	- Low glucose
	- pH < 7.2
	- Drainage and antibiotics
3) [[Empyema]]
