### Etiology
- M > F
- Disease of aging
- Germline mutations leading to a loss of [[telomerase]]
- Genetic variant of MUC5B gene
- Smoking and other resp. risk factors

### Pathophysiology
- Progressive, bilateral interstitial fibrosis from result of repetitive injury and subsequent defective repair of the alveolar epithelium
	- **Temporally and spatially heterogeneous**

- Cobblestone change to pleura

### Clinical Features
- Progressive cough and dyspnea

### Diagnosis
- Dry crackles

### Treatment
- Lung transplant

- Poor prognosis (3-5 years)
