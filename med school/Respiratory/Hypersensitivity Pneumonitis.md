### Etiology
- Yeast/fungal antigens = wood, humidifiers, heating ducts.
- Bacterial antigens = dairy barns, hay. [Referred to as farmer’s lung]
- Mycobacterial antigens = hot tubs and saunas.
- Avian antigens = pigeons, doves, ducks, parakeets.
- Chemical antigens = automotive paints, zinc, dye

### Pathophysiology
- Loose non-caseating granulomas in peribronchiolar distribution

### Clinical Features
- Acute or chronic after exposure
	- **Temporal relationship** is important clinically
- Impaired lung compliance, decreased lung volumes, and decreased diffusion capacity
