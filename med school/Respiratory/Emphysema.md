#### Etiology
- Cigarette smoking

#### Pathophysiology
- **Nonfibrotic degradation** of elastase and other proteins in alveolar septa due to enzymes from [[Alveolar Macrophages|macrophages]] and [[neutrophils]]
- Protease activity causes loss of [[alveoli]], alveolar capillaries, and parts distal to terminal bronchiole → loss of gas exchange area
	- Permanent ↑ air spaces distal to terminal bronchioles
- Loss of elastic tissue + recoil → lungs and chest walls to expand 
	- Airways collapse during expiration
- Development of bullae can happen
	-

**4 classifications**
- Centriacinar -- **MOST common**
	- Associated w cigarettes
	- Presents alongside chronic bronchitis -> [[COPD]]
- Panacinar
	- [[Alpha-1-Antitrypsin Deficiency|AAT deficiency]] -- this is an inhibitor of neutrophil proteases
	- ↑ tissue destruction by neutrophil proteases
- Distal acinar
	- Adjacent to regions of pulmonary fibrosis, scarring, or atelectasis
- Irregular
#### Clinical Features
- Difficulty exhaling
- Pursed lip breathing → increases airway pressure + opens airways
- Dyspnea
- Trouble eating -- weight loss
- Barrel chest

#### Diagnosis
- Radiograph: hyperlucent lung fields and a flattened diaphragm

#### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

#### Complications
- [[Pulmonary hypertension]]
- [[Right Heart Failure|Right sided heart failure]]
