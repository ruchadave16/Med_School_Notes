### Etiology
- Young adult male smokers

### Pathophysiology
- Autoimmune condition with lung and kidney involvement
- Autoantibodies against type IV [[collagen]]
- Causes necrotizing hemorrhagic interstitial pneumonitis and rapid glomerulonephritis
	- If no pulm involvement, then called just anti-GBM disease

### Clinical Features
- Lung issues with elevated creatinine and proteinuria

### Diagnosis
- Anti-GBM antibodies in blood

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
