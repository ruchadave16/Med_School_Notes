### Etiology
- Median sternotomy post incidence is 0.4–5.0%
- Risk
	- Nasal colonization of Staph aureus, Diabetes, Obesity, Smoking 
	- Urgent surgery
	- Prolonged ventilation
- Commonly [[Staph Aureus]]

### Pathophysiology
- Inflammation / infection of mediastinum
- Acute -- esophageal perforation or infection
- Chronic -- histoplasmosis or tuberculosis

### Clinical Features
- Signs of compression of a mediastinal structure
- Fever, [[leukocytosis]], sepsis signs
- Chest pain
- Wound infection signs
