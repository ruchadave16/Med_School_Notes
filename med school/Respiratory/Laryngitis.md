### Etiology
- [[Parainfluenza Virus]] and [[Rhinovirus]]
- S. pyogenes, M catarrhalis & H. influenzae
- Vocal strain, Reflux, overuse

### Pathophysiology
- Inflammation of vocal folds of the [[larynx]]

### Clinical Features
- Hoarseness and loss of voice or inability to speak
- Preceded by an upper respiratory infection
### Diagnosis
- Inflamed, swollen (edematous) vocal cords

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
