### Etiology
- Primary causes
- Risk factors

### Pathophysiology
- Inability to catabolize [[Pulmonary Surfactant|pulmonary surfactant]]
	- Accumulation in intra-alveolar spaces
- ↓ [[GM-CSF]] signalling so ↓ [[Alveolar Macrophages|alveolar macrophage]] function

1) Autoimmune
	- Autoantibodies that neutralize [[GM-CSF]]
	- 90% of all cases
2) Secondary
	- Uncommon
3) Hereditary
	- Extremely rare
	- Loss-of-function mutation

### Clinical Features
- Cough + sputum
	- Chunks of gelatinous material
- Recurrent pulm infections
### Diagnosis
- B/L asymmetric pulmonary opacifications

### Treatment
- Whole lung lavage
- [[GM-CSF]] therapy
- Allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation 

### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
