Minimal as dissolved oxygen, mainly oxygenated or oxyhemoglobin

*[[Hemoglobin]] binds to up to 4 $O_2$ molecules through cooperative binding*

![[Screenshot 2026-07-08 at 9.37.32 AM.png]]

		Red: Loading region (hemoglobin binds tightly to O2)
		Blue: Unloading region (decrease in saturation)

- Curve can also shift to change affinity of Hb – Right shift causes it to release more easily and left makes it hold more tightly

![[Screenshot 2026-08-02 at 5.38.16 PM.png]]

- O2-binding capacity -- Maximum amount of O2 that can be bound to hemoglobin per volume of blood
- Although the minority of O2 is in the dissolved form, it is this form of oxygen which generates the partial pressure of O2 
	 ![[Screenshot 2026-08-02 at 5.39.52 PM.png]]
#### Right Shift
- Increase in $P_{CO2}$
- Decrease in pH
- ↑ Temperature
- ↑ 2,3-DPG (during low $O_2$ conditions)
	- Normally found in [[Erythrocytes|RBCs]] and decreases affinity for O2 so higher delivery
#### Left Shift
Opposites of above + [[CO poisoning]]

#### Notes
- At 70 mmHg, hemoglobin is essentially 100% saturated.
	- Arterial blood = 100 mmHg
- At 40, 70% saturated -> venous blood
- At 25, 50% saturated
