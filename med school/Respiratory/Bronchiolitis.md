### Etiology
- [[RSV]]
- Primarily affects Infants < 2 years

### Pathophysiology
- Acute infection of small airways (< 2 mm in diameter)
- Inflammation, edema, and mucous plugging of the bronchioles
	- Infants don't have mature protective antibodies

### Clinical Features
- URI symptoms (nasal congestion, rhinorrhea, low-grade fever)

PROGRESSES
- Cough, wheezing, tachypnea, nasal flaring, chest retraction, grunting
	- Air trapping = long, forced expiration
	- Hyperinflation
	- [[Atelectasis]]
- Apnea

### Diagnosis
- Pulse oximetry
- Chest X-ray if pneumonia is suspected

### Treatment
- Maintain O2 > 90%
- Aerosolized [[ribavirin]] in severe infections

### Complications
- Rapidly life threatening
