### Etiology
- Primary causes
	- [[Staph aureus]] & [[Pseudomonas aeruginosa]]
	- Polymicrobial
- Risk factors
	- Prolonged hospitalization (≥5 days)
	- Recent IV antibiotics within the preceding 90-days.
	- Colonization or prior infection with MDR bacteria
	- Immunosuppression

- Mechanism of disease

### Clinical Features
- New onset of fever
- Purulent sputum
- Leukocytosis
- Decline in oxygenation

### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- Culture
- Imaging
	- New infiltrate on Chest X-ray
- Criteria
	- ≥48 hours after hospital admission and was not incubating at the time of admission
	- VAP: ≥48 hours (about 2 days) after endotracheal intubation

### Treatment
7 day treatment 

Low Risk:
*No recent IV antibiotics, no hospitalization within 90 days, no high resistance in the facility*
-  [[Cefepime]] or [[Piperacillin-tazobactam]] or [[Levofloxacin]]

High Risk:
*Add MRSA coverage + double coverage for Pseudomonas*
- ONE OF: [[Cefepime]], [[ceftazidime]], [[piperacillin-tazobactam]], [[meropenem]]
- [[Levofloxacin]] or [[Aminoglycosides]] 
- [[Vancomycin]] or [[linezolid]]

 **VAP**
 1) Antipseudomonal beta-lactam:
	 - [[Piperacillin-tazobactam]]
	 - [[Cefepime]]
	 - [[Ceftazidime]]
	 - [[Meropenem]]
2) Second antipseudomonal agent
	- [[Levofloxacin]] or [[ciprofloxacin]]
	- OR an [[Aminoglycosides]]
3) MRSA coverage
	- [[Vancomycin]] or [[linezolid]]

Elevate head of bed (30–45°)
Daily sedation interruption & readiness-to-extubate checks
Oral care with [[chlorhexidine]]
Subglottic suctioning if available
Minimize unnecessary ventilator days
