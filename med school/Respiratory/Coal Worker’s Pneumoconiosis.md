### Etiology
- Coal dust exposure

### Pathophysiology
- Asymptomatic anthracosis: small pigmented collections
- Simple CWP: presence of coal macules/coal nodules
- Complicated = large depositions

### Clinical Features
- Can be asymptomatic
- Simple CWP -- no to mild dysfunctin
	- <10% -> complicated
- Complicated CWP
	- Significant pulmonary compromise
