- Primary causes

### Pathophysiology
- Persistent middle ear infection or inflammation (> 6 months)
	- Chronic Suppurative otitis media (CSOM):
		- ear discharge with infected fluid due to TM rupture
	- Chronic Otitis Media with Effusion (COME)
		- Only persistent fluid in middle year more than 3 months
		- No active infection
	- Cholesteatoma
		- Abnormal skin growth in middle ears behind TM.

### Clinical Features
- Persistent ear drainage >6 months
- Conductive deafness
- Perforated tympanic membrane
- Minimal ear pain
