### Etiology
- Smoking
- Acquired mutations  -- TP53 mutations leading to p53 over expression, CDKN2A, FGFR1
- 20% of all lung cancers

### Pathophysiology
- Parathyroid Hormone-related Peptide -> [[Hypercalcemia|hypercalcemia]]
- More commonly central (hilar)
- Can obstruct bronchus and cause lobular collapse

![[Screenshot 2026-08-16 at 5.25.20 PM.png]]

- **Precursor** = squamous metaplasia
- Epithelial tumor characterized by the presence of keratinization (keratin pearls) and/or intercellular bridges
	- + for **p63 and/or p40**
	- Negative for TTF-1, Synaptophysin, and Chromogranin
