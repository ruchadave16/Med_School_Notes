### Etiology
- Primary causes
- Risk factors

### Pathophysiology
- Mass of lung tissue with no communication with bronchial tree
	- Abnormal blood supply from systemic arterial circulation
- Extralobar: separate/external to the normal lung 
	- Congenital
	- Commonly presents in infants
- Intralobar: within the normal lung and is most often acquired
	- Older children
![[Screenshot 2026-08-16 at 9.22.34 PM.png]]
### Clinical Features
- Asymptomatic at birth
- Respiratory distress
- Recurrent lower resp. tract infections
	- Common with intralobar
- Mass effect
	- Common with extralobar

### Diagnosis
- Chest x-ray
- CT scan
- Angiography

### Treatment
- Surgical if large
### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
