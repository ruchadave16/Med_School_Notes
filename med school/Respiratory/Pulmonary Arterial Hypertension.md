### Etiology
- Idiopathic
- Hereditary: BMPR2 mutation
- Drug induced: appetite suppression ([[fenfluramine]]), [[methamphetamines]]
- Connective tissue problems, portal hypertension, HIV, Schistosomiasis
### Pathophysiology
- Elevated pressure in pulm. arterioles from remodeling of arteries
	- Endothelial dysfunction so ↓ nitric oxide, ↓ prostacyclin, ↑ TXA2
	- Smooth muscle hypertrophy
	- Vasoconstriction

**WHO criteria**
- Class I: No symptoms with activity
- Class II: Mild symptoms with ordinary activity
- Class III: Marked limitation of activity
- Class IV: Symptoms at rest
### Clinical Features
- Progressive dyspnea, fatigue
- Syncope or chest pain from low CO
- [[Right Heart Failure|right heart failure]]
### Diagnosis
- Criteria
	- Mean pulmonary arterial pressure (mPAP) > 20 mmHg
	- Pulmonary capillary wedge pressure (PCWP) ≤ 15 mmHg
	- Pulmonary vascular resistance (PVR) ≥ 3 Wood units
- 6 minute walk test
	- 440 meters suggests low risk
	- <165 meters indicates high risk and poor prognosis
- Vasoreactivity Test in pulm catheterization
	- If positive, [[CCBs]] high dose
	- Else start targeted PAH therapy
### Treatment
- Immunizations -- [[Influenza Virus|Influenza]] and [[Streptococcus Pneumonia|Pneumococcus]]
- Pulmonary rehab
- O2 therapy
- [[Diuretics]]

**DRUGS**
1) [[Endothelin Receptor Antagonists]] (ERAs) -- block [[endothelin 1]]
2) [[PDE5 Inhibitors]] -- nitric oxide mediated vasodilation
3) [[sGC Stimulators]] -- ↑ cGMP production
	- do NOT combine with [[PDE5 Inhibitors|PDE5i]]
4) Prostaglandin Analogs
5) Prostaglandin Agonists

>[!Note]
>Low Risk: Oral monotherapy with ERA or PDE5i
>Class III or IV: ERA + PDE5i + prostaglandin drug
>Severe: IV prostacyclin

- Atrial septostomy: R -> L shunt creation
- Lung transplantation
### Complications
- [[Eisenmenger’s Syndrome]] in individuals with congenital heart disease and L->R shunt
- HIGH MATERNAL DEATH in pregnancy
- Teratogenicity
