### Etiology
- Right lung most affected
- [[Obligate Anaerobes]], [[Gram Negative]] rods

### Pathophysiology
- [[Pneumonia]] after aspiration of food or gastric content

### Clinical Features
- Recent aspiration
- Impaired consciousness

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
	- RLL consolidation
- Criteria

### Treatment
Outpatient:
- [[Amoxicillin]]-[[clavulanate]] or [[clindamycin]]

Inpatient
- [[ampicillin]]-[[sulbactam]] or [[Piperacillin-tazobactam]]

PCN allergy
- [[Clindamycin]] or [[moxifloxacin]]

>[!Note] Metronidazole alone is not sufficient for aspiration pneumonia
### Complications
- Lung abscess
- Empyema
- Bronchiectasis
