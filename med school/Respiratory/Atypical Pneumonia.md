### Etiology
- [[Legionella Pneumophilia]]
- [[Mycoplasma Pneumoniae]] -- most common cause of pneumonia in healthy adolescents and college students
- [[Chlamydia]] Pneumoniae
- Chlamydia Psittaci

- Mechanism of disease

### Clinical Features
- Systemic symptoms
- Dry cough
- GI symptoms (especially Legionella)
- Rash, anemia, encephalitis

### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- Cold agglutins -- [[Mycoplasma Pneumoniae]]
	- Hyponatremia -- [[Legionella Pneumophilia]]
	- PCR and urine antigen
- Imaging
	- Diffuse interstitial infiltrate
- Criteria
### Treatment
Outpatient
- [[doxycycline]] or [[Azithromycin]]

Inpatient
- Add [[azithromycin]] or [[fluoroquinolones]] to [[Beta lactams]]
