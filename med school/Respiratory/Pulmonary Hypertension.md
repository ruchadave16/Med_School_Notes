### Etiology
- Idiopathic: women 20-40 years
- Familial: **BMPR2** mutation

### Pathophysiology
- Pulmonary mean arterial pressure > 25 mmHg
- Caused by ↑ blood flow or ↑ resistance
	- Associated with endothelial injury -> causes vascular remodeling
	- Intimal fibrosis and medial hypertrophy

- **Classification**
	- Pulmonary arterial hypertension: 
		- Idiopathic
		- Familial
		- Collage vascular disease, HIV infection, various drugs.
	- [[Chronic Heart Failure|chronic left heart failure]]
	- Lung disease / [[Hypoxemia]]
		- [[COPD]]
		- [[interstitial lung disease]]
		- [[obstructive sleep apnea]]
		- [[Bronchopulmonary dysplasia]]
	- Chronic thromboembolic pulmonary hypertension
