#### Ventilation
- Minute Ventilation: Tidal Volume * breath / min
- Alveolar Ventilation: (Vt - Vd) * breath / min (remove dead space)
	- Va = VCO2 / PaCO2
	- VCO2 = total CO2 production in body 
	- PaCO2 = partial pressure of CO2 in arterial blood
- Airflow = Δ P / R (pressure gradient / [[Pulmonary Resistance|pulmonary resistance]])
