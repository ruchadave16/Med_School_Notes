### Etiology
- Acute sinusitis: Less than 4 weeks
- Chronic sinusitis: 12 weeks and more
- Subacute sinusitis: Between 4-12 weeks.
- Recurrent sinusitis: > 4 episodes/year lasting for 7-10 days

- Commonly seasonal -- allergies in spring
	- [[Rhinovirus]]
### Pathophysiology
- Impaired mucociliary clearance due to viral infection 
- Blocking of sinus openings so fluid accumulation within the paranasal sinuses -> bacterial
	- [[Streptococcus Pneumonia|S. pneumonia]], [[Hemophilus Influenza|H. influenza]], and [[Moraxella Catarrhalis]]
- Fungal: [[Aspergillus Fumigatus|aspergillus]] or [[mucor]]

### Clinical Features
- Experience nasal congestion, postnasal drip, throat irritation, sneezing, and a decreased sense of smell
- Facial pressure & pain, sinus fullness & tenderness.
- Headache
- Malodorous breath

- CT shows bilat opaque![[Screenshot 2026-08-18 at 6.26.25 PM.png]]

### Treatment
- Mild = supportive
	- Over-the-counter analgesics, saline nasal irrigation
	- [[Decongestants]] but only 2-3 days
- Antibiotics if >10 days
	- [[Amoxicillin]] or [[Augmentin]]
- Nasal steroids if severe inflammation with allergic components

### Complications
- Orbital cellulitis
- Meningitis
- Brain abscess
- Septic cavernous thrombosis
