### Etiology
- Primary causes
- Risk factors

### Pathophysiology
- Inflammatory condition affecting the bronchioles and surrounding lung tissue
	- Alveolar exudate persists and eventually transforms into fibrous tissue within the lung alveoli.

### Clinical Features
- Fever, chills, night sweats, fatigue
- Dyspnea & cough.
- Crackles on auscultation

### Diagnosis
- CT scan
	- Air space consolidation with air bronchograms
- Pulmonary function test
	- Restrictive defect

### Treatment
- [[Synthetic Corticosteroids|Corticosteroids]]

- Short-term
- Long-term
