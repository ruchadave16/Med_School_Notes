### Etiology
- A leading cause of death in US
- Associated with cigarette smoking -> 1/2 smokers get COPD

### Pathophysiology
- Mechanism of disease
- Relevant pathways (e.g. immune, hormonal)

### Clinical Features
- Dyspnea

### Diagnosis
- Pulmonary function tests: ↑ lung volumes with ↓ FEV1/FVC (<0.7)

### Treatment
- [[Smoking cessation]]
- [[Vaccines]] -- [[Influenza Virus|Influenza]], [[Streptococcus Pneumonia|pneumococcus]], [[Covid]], Tdap
- Bronchodilators
	- [[Beta Agonist|beta agonists]] (long acting): [[Salmeterol]], [[Formoterol]], Arformoterol, Olodaterol, Vilanterol as monotherapy allowed
	- Ultra-LABA (e.g., vilanterol) has 24-hour action
	- [[Muscarinic Antagonists|antimuscarinic]] (long): [[Tiotropium]] = **first line**
	- Combination of LABA and LAMA used if many exacerbations
- Inhaled [[Synthetic Corticosteroids|corticosteroids]]
	- Do NOT use as monotherapy -- pneumonia
- [[Theophylline]]-- not commonly recommended
- [[Roflumilast]] -- LAST if triple acute exacerbations does not work
- [[Prednisone]] -- oral only if acute exacerbations
- Oxygen therapy
- Surgical

**Exacerbation Management**
- Acute symptom = [[albuterol]], [[Ipratropium]]
