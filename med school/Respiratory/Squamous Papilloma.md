### Etiology
- Multiple papillomas in children -- Juvenile Laryngeal Papillomatosis
	- Associated with [[HPV]] 6 and 11

### Pathophysiology
- Benign neoplasm on true vocal cords
- Multiple slender, fingerlike projections supported by central fibrovascular cores and covered by an orderly stratified squamous epithelium
