1) Atmosphere and Alveoli exchange of gas → facilitated by [[respiratory system]]
2) [[Gas exchange]] between alveoli and blood → depends on blood gases pressure
3) Transport of [[Oxygen Transport|Oxygen]] and $CO_2$ in body → [[cardiovascular system]]
4) Exchange of gases into [[capillaries]] and cells 
5) [[Cellular Respiration]]

**Forces**
- Inward recoil of lungs: created by elastic tissue
- Outward recoil of chest wall: created by chest wall's tendency to expand
- Intrapleural pressure
- Intra-alveolar pressure
- Transpulmonary pressure: Intra-alveolar pressure - intrapleural pressure

### Cycle
**Rest**
- Gas volume in lungs = FRC
- Inward and outward recoil are equal
- In [[Emphysema|emphysema]], ↓ elasticity so ↓ lung collapse
	- higher FRC so barrel chested
- In [[lung fibrosis]], ↑ tendency of lung to collapse
	- lower FRC

### Controls
- Central chemoreceptors in [[medulla]] respond to pH change of [[CSF]]
	- CO2 mixes with H2O to make H+ and HCO3-
- Peripheral carotid and aortic body chemoreceptors
	- increased PaCO2 or decreased pH or PaO2.
	- PaO2 must reach low levels (< 60 mm Hg) before breathing is stimulated
- Lung stretch receptors
	- ↑ stretching = inhibition of dorsal respiratory center
- Irritant receptors
	- between airway epithelial cells and are stimulated by noxious substances
- Juxtacapillary receptors
	- close to the capillaries in the alveolar walls
