### Etiology
- [[Parainfluenza Virus]]
- 6 month -> 3 years

### Pathophysiology
- Upper airway infection -> inflammation and edema of larynx, trachea, and bronchi (subglottic)
- 

### Clinical Features
- Barking cough
- Inspiratory stridor
- Hoarseness
- Worse at night

### Diagnosis
- Steeple sign
![[Screenshot 2026-08-18 at 7.09.16 PM.png]]
### Treatment
- Mild
	- Supportive at home
- Moderate to severe
	1) Nebulized [[Epinephrine|epinephrine]] -- vasoconstriction so ↓ edema
	2) [[Glucocorticoids]] ([[Dexamethasone]]) -- anti inflammatory
- Life threatening
	- Intubation + ventilation
### Complications
- AVOID THROAT EXAM
