### Etiology
- Neoplasms may arise in the thymus
- Adults older than 40 years, M=F

### Pathophysiology
- Tumors of thymic epithelial cells
- Commonly in anterior superior mediastinum
- Lobulated, firm, gray-white masses of up to 15 to 20 cm in size

### Clinical Features
- 40% of thymomas present with symptoms stemming from impingement on mediastinal structures
- 30% to 45% are detected in the course of evaluating patients with myasthenia gravis

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- 20% to 25% of tumors penetrate the capsule and infiltrate perithymic tissues and structures
