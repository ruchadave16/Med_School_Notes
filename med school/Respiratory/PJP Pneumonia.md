### Etiology
- AIDS patients (CD4 < 200 = highest risk)
- Transplant patients
- People on immunosuppressive drugs

### Pathophysiology
- Interstitial pneumonia 
	- Thick-walled cyst with 8 intra-cystic bodies
- Attachment of organisms to alveolar septal walls
	- cyst—trophozoite—cyst cycle within the definitive host.
![[Screenshot 2026-08-19 at 12.23.50 AM.png]]
### Clinical Features
- Interstitial thickening 
- Dry cough and exertional dyspnea

### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- Supportive lab work: elevated Beta-D-Glucan
	- BAL finding of PJP and positive PCR test
- Imaging
	- Classic ground-glass opacities on imaging study
	- Supportive radiological signs: B/L diffuse alveolar infiltrate
- Criteria
	- CD4 count <200 cells per cubic millimeter.

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
	- [[Trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole]]
	- [[Synthetic Corticosteroids|Corticosteroids]] -- moderate-to-severe PJP in HIV-infected patients
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

**Prevention**
- [[Sulfamethoxazole]]: indicated in HIV patients with CD4 <200 cells per cubic millimeter
