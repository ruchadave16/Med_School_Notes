**Laplace's Law:** P = 2T / r
- P = collapsing pressure
- T = surface tension
- r = alveolar radius

Normally, alveoli are likely to collapse during expiration when r ↓ (P needed to keep open is greater)
- This process = atelectasis
- [[Pulmonary Surfactant]] reduces surface tension so this pressure decreases
