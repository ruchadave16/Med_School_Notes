Active process
- Creates negative intrathoracic pressure
- Draws air into lungs

#### Pressures
- Intrapleural: -5 -> -8 cm H2O
- Intra-alveolar: <0
- Transpulmonary is increasing
### Physiology

Uses [[diaphragm]] and [[external intercostal muscles]]
- Diaphragm increases vertical dimension -- contracts to increase space
- External intercostal increase side to side and front to back dimensions
	- Pump-handle movement – anterior posterior of thorax increase, Ribs 2-6 at costovertebral
	- Bucket-handle movement – transverse, Ribs 6-10
- Continue until inward pressure balances out the outward pressure from muscles of inspiration (+8 cm H2O)
