### Etiology
- F>M
- U/L more than B/L
- [[CHARGE syndrome]]

### Pathophysiology
- Congenital narrowing of posterior nasal aperture -> nasal cavity does NOT connect with nasopharynx


### Clinical Features
- Acute respiratory distress
- B/L has cyclic cyanosis that is better with crying and worse with feeding
