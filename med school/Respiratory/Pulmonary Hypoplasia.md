### Etiology
- [[oligohydramnios]]
- [[Congenital Diaphragmatic Hernia|congenital diaphragmatic hernia]]

### Pathophysiology
- ↓ development of [[lungs]] due to abnormalities that compress lung

### Clinical Features
- Respiratory distress
- ↓ breath sounds
- Cough
- Asymptomatic in adults -- healthy lung compensates
	- Chronic cough
	- Recurrent pneumonia
	- 

### Diagnosis
- Imaging
	- Hyperexpanded healthy lung with a mediastinal shift toward the affected side
	- Chest CT to distinguish aplasia, hypoplasia, agenesis

### Treatment
- Supportive care

### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
