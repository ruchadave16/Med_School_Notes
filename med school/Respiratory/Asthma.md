#### Definition
[[Chronic inflammation]] of [[bronchioles]] tree in lungs

#### Etiology
- Allergens
- Irritants
- Resp. tract infections
- Exercise

#### Pathophysiology
- Bronchioles become **hypersensitive** because of allergic reactions ([[Type I Hypersensitivity]])
	- Driven by [[Th2]] cells 
		- IL4 -> B cells make IgE
		- IL5 -> [[Eosinophils|eosinophil]] activation
		- IL-13 -> mucus production
	- [[Leukotrienes]]
		- LTC₄ and LTD₄  -> bronchoconstriction and mucosal edema
- [[Mast Cells|Mast cells]] degranulation triggered by dust, dander, pollen
- Increased, sudden constrictions of [[Smooth Muscle|smooth muscle]] → [[bronchoconstriction]]
- Lungs hyper-inflated with mucus plugs
	- Eosinophil infiltrates
	- Smooth muscle hypertrophy in bronchioles
	- Charcot-Leyden crystals and Curschmann spirals

**Categories**
- Extrinsic asthma: Common in children
	- Family history of asthma
	- Onset = common triggers
	- IgE mediated 
- Intrinsic asthma: more common in adults
	- Viral and air pollutant associated
	- No atopy (IgE rxn)
- Drug-induced with [[Aspirin]]
- Occupational asthma

#### Clinical Features
- Dyspnea
- Wheezing
- Chest tightness
- Coughing
- Attacks at night or early morning

#### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Test
	- Bronchial challenge testing through [[Methacholine]]
- Imaging
- Criteria

#### Treatment
1) Assess risk
2) Step up therapy if not controlled well
	- Check for medication adherence
	- Check inhaler usage
	- Unaddressed comorbidities
3) Step down if possible
	- If controlled for > 3 months
	- the last drug added is the first to be reduced or withdrawn

**RAPID RELIEF**
- [[Beta2 Agonists]]: used for immediate relief **First Line** 
	- SABA: [[albuterol]]
	- LABA: [[salmeterol]],[[ ]][[formoterol]] (more rapid)
- [[Muscarinic Antagonists|Antimuscarinic]] (M3): add-on therapy
	- [[tiotropium]]
- [[Theophylline]]: Non-selective phosphodiesterase inhibitor 
	- Narrow therapeutic window, not used commonl

**MAINTANENCE THERAPY**
- Inhaled [[Synthetic Corticosteroids|corticosteroids]]: [[Fluticasone]] or [[Budesonide]]
	- Alone or combine with LABA
	- Beclomethasone, Budesonide, Ciclesonide, Fluticasone, Mometasone, Triamcinolone
- [[Leukotriene antagonists]] (LTRAs) -- [[Montelukast]]
	- CysLT₁ receptor
	- Add-on if allergic asthma, AERD
	- Neuropsychiatric events
- [[Zileuton]] -- 5-lipoxygenase inhibitor -> no [[Leukotrienes|leukotriene]] synthesis
	- Hepatotoxicity
- [[Monoclonal Antibodies]]
	- [[Omalizumab]] = anti IgE
	- Mepolizumab, Benralizumab = anti IL-5
	- Dupilumab = Anti IL-4Ra
	- Tezepelumab = Anti TSLP
- Cromones = prevent degranulation of mast cells, rare today
