### Etiology
- Viral -- [[Rhinovirus|rhinovirus]], [[Covid]], [[RSV]]
- Common in children
### Pathophysiology
- Inflammation of the nasal cavity
- Virus binds with surface receptor leading to hypersecretion of mucus to trap the virus
- Elicit inflammatory response

### Clinical Features
- Coryz = congestion nasal
- Stuffiness
- Rhinorrhea
- Sneezing

### Diagnosis
- Mainly clinical
- RT-PCF

### Treatment
- Use of nasal decongestant, antihistaminic, saline nasal irrigation etc.
- Zinc acetate helps to shorten illness

### Complications
- Secondary bacterial pneumonia
- [[Otitis Media]]
