### Etiology
- **Secondary** condition after obstruction or chronic infection
	- Bronchial obstruction
	- [[Cystic fibrosis]]
	- [[Tuberculosis|TB]]
	- [[Staph Aureus]] pneumonia or [[Klebsiella Pneumonia]]
	- Immunodeficiency
	- [[Primary ciliary dyskinesia]]

### Pathophysiology
- Permanent dilation of bronchi and bronchioles
	- Destruction of smooth muscle and elastic tissue 
	- Common in lower lobes
- Traction bronchiectasis
	- Bronchiectasis where the airways in the lungs become widened / dilated due to “pulling” forces of surrounding fibrotic lung tissue
	- 

### Clinical Features
- Cough and purulent sputum

### Diagnosis
- Abnormally widened airways
