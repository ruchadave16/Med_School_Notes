#### Structure
*Tetrametric globular protein made of 2 heterodimers each made of alpha and beta chain*
- Chains held together by hydrophobic bond
- Heterodimers held by ionic and hydrogen bonds

![[Screenshot 2026-07-08 at 9.15.11 AM.png]]

Heme molecule in center that binds to $O_2$
- When deoxygenated, molecules are NOT planar (**Tense State**)
- When oxygenated, planar (**Relaxed State**)
- Cannot bind to Fe3+ 
#### [[Oxygen Transport]]

- Oxyhemoglobin (O2)
- Carbaminohemoglobin (CO2)
- Carboxyhemoglobin (CO) -- [[CO Poisoning|Carbon monoxide poisoning]]
- Methemoglobin (oxidized iron) -- [[Methemoglobinuria]]
#### Fetal Hemoglobin (HbF)
- Two alpha (α) chains
- Two gamma (γ) chains

HbF has a higher affinity for O2 than adult hemoglobin
#### Adult Hemoglobin
- HbA1 = 90% of adult (2 $\alpha$ and 2 $\beta$)
- HbA2 = 2-3% (2 $\alpha$ and 2 $\delta$)
- HbS = pathological (valine for glutamine in $\beta$ chain)
	- [[Sickle Cell Anemia]]
- HbC = pathological (lysine for glutamine in $\beta$ chain)
#### Degradation
Removed by [[Macrophage|macrophages]] of [[reticuloendothelial system]], broken into [[heme]] and globins
- Globin -> [[amino acids]]
- Heme oxidized -> [[biliverdin]] -> [[bilirubin]]
