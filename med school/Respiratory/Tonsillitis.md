### Etiology
- 5-15 years most common
	- [[EBV]]
	- [[Strep Pyogenes|group A strep]]
- **HIV testing** if in adults

### Pathophysiology
- [[Tonsils]] inflammation

### Clinical Features
- Sore throat
- Fever
- Enlarged tonsils
- Exudate and white patches

### Diagnosis
- Rapid strep test
- Ultrasound or CT scan may be used when fluid collection or abscess is suspected

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle
