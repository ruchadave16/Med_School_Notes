Positive end-expiratory pressure (PEEP): Pressure does not fall to 0 at the end of respiration
- Maintains airway patency during [[Expiration]]
- Useful in [[ARDS]] and other acute hypoxemic states

Continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP): Patient initiates all breaths -- pressure remains positive
- Used in [[obstructive sleep apnea]]
