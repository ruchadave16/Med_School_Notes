 Resistance = (8hl)/(pr4)
- R is resistance, h is the viscosity, l is airway length, and r is airway radius
- Main source of bronchi = medium-sized bronchi
#### Factors
- [[Sympathetic Nervous System|Sympathetic]]: Dilate through $\beta2$ 
	- Common agonist = [[Albuterol]]
- [[Parasympathetic Nervous System|Parasympathetic]]: M3 causes contraction
	- [[Ipratropium]] = anticholinergic
