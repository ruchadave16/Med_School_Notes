### Etiology
- Leading infectious cause of death in the USA
- ~30% hospitalization / year in the USA
- All-cause mortality in patients with CAP is roughly 28% in 1 year

**MOST COMMON: [[Streptococcus Pneumonia|Strep Pneumo]]**

![[Screenshot 2026-08-18 at 8.28.27 PM.png]]

### Pathophysiology
- Pathogen reaches lung and normally, mucociliary elevator gets rid of it
	- Once it reaches [[alveoli]], pathogen ↑ from virulence factors 
	- Macrophages kill pathogen and release cytokines
	- Local inflammatory response
	- ↑ vascular permeability
	- ↑ neutrophils, lymphocytes, and antibodies
- Alveolar spaces FILL with inflammatory fluid
### Clinical Features
- Cough
- Purulent, colored sputum production
- Fever & chills
- Shortness of breath
- Pleuritic chest pain
- Tachypnoea
- Tachycardia

### Diagnosis
- X-ray has infiltrate (new)
- Sputum growth in hospitalized
- Respiratory specimen -- antibiotics
- Multiplex PCR assay
- Urine antigen tests -- legionella and pneumococcus

**Scoring:**
- Pneumonia Severity Index (PSI)
- CURB-65 (C=Confusion, U=Urea >7 mmol/L (≈ BUN > 20 mg/dL). R= Respiratory rate ≥ 30/min, B – Blood pressure low (Systolic < 90 mmHg or Diastolic ≤ 60 mmHg), 65=Age ≥ 65 years)

### Treatment
- Outpatient therapy 
	- [[Amoxicillin]] (high dose)
	- [[Doxycycline]] -- typical and atypical 
	- [[Macrolides]] (only if local resistance < 25%)
	
>[!Note] 
>If comorbidities present (chronic heart, lung, liver, or renal disease; diabetes; alcoholism; malignancy; or immunosuppression)
> -  [[Amoxicillin]]/[[clavulanate]] plus [[Macrolides|macrolides]] or [[doxycycline]]
> - Respiratory [[Fluoroquinolones|fluoroquinolone]] monotherapy
> 	- Avoid unless needed
- Inpatient
	- [[Ceftriaxone]] + [[Azithromycin|azithromycin]]
	- [[Levofloxacin]] or [[moxifloxacin]] monotherapy
- Hospitalized
	- Broad empiric antibiotic coverage with a plan to de-escalate once culture and susceptibility results 
	- Treatment = 5-7 days + clinical stability
	- [[Ceftriaxone]] + [[Macrolides]] 
		- [[Cefepime]] if more sick patient 
	- OR [[ceftriaxone]] + [[Fluoroquinolones|fluoroquinolone]]
	- Add [[Vancomycin]] or [[linezolid]] if MRSA risk
	- [[Piperacillin]] + [[Tazobactam]], [[Cefepime]], or [[Meropenem]] if [[Pseudomonas Aeruginosa]]
	- [[Clindamycin]] or [[ampimycin]] + [[sulbactam]] if aspiration
	- [[Azithromycin]] or [[Fluoroquinolones|fluoroquinolone]] if water (legionella) risk
### Prevention
- Vaccination
	- Adults ≥ 65 years
	- Immunocompromised
	- Chronic disease
	- Smokers
	- Cochlear implants or CSF leaks

**Conjugate vaccines (PCV13, PCV15, PCV20)**
- T-cell–dependent response
- Preferred if no vaccination before

**Polysaccharide vaccine (PPSV23 / PCV23)**
- No memory
