- Biopsy: small tissue sample collected with needle commonly
	- Needle biopsy (also called core needle biopsy; prostate or breast)
	- Endoscopic biopsy (colon)
	- Ultrasound-guided biopsy (thyroid)
	- Punch or shave biopsies (skin)
	- Excisional biopsies (lymph nodes)
- Resection: larger tissue sample recovered
- Cytology = study of individual cells
