- At muscle-tendon junction
- Slow-adapting mechanoceptors that run in **series** in muscle fibers
- Senses tension of tendon
#### Mechanism
- Muscle contracts -> pulls [[collagen]] on [[tendon]] -> GTO is compressed -> Ib fibers stimulated -> signal of ↑ tension
