*smooth [[Endoplasmic Reticulum|ER]] of muscles, site of $Ca^{2+}$ storage and release for [[excitation-contraction coupling]]

- Forms interconnected meshwork of tubules around myofibrils
- Terminal cisternae is region near [[T Tubules]] which is part of a triad
- Membrane contains $Ca^{2+}$-ATPase (calcium pump) which transfers calcium from intracellular fluid to inside of SR
- Contains $Ca^{2+}$ bound loosely to [[calsequestrin]]
- Contains $Ca^{2+}$ release channel called [[ryanodine receptor]]
#### Method of Release
- Feet from sarcoplasmic reticulum extend -> [[T tubules]]
- Have $Ca^{2+}$ channel called [[ryanodine receptor]] or [[CICR channel]]
- SR takes in sarcoplasmic $Ca^{2+}$ through Ca-ATP ([[SERCA pumps]])

>[!Warning] SERCA inhibited by [[phospholamban]]
>- This brakeing effect is inhibited by [[Epinephrine]] and [[Norepinephrine]]
#### Stores
Depends on
- Extracellular Ca2+ concentration 
- Size of L-type Ca2+ current -> ↑ by $\beta 1$ and ↓ by [[Calcium channel blockers]]
- [[Heart rate]] -> changes duration of systole as compared to diastole
	- Bowditch effect
- [[Digoxin]] increases Ca2+ store
- [[Caffeine]]
#### Reuptake
- [[Calsequestrin]] stores in SR are rebuilt
