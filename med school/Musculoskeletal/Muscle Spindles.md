*Mechanoreceptors that detect change in length of muscle*
- Run in parallel to muscle fibers
- Made of [[Connective Tissue|connective tissue]]
	- Ia -- rapidly adapting, senses velocity and direction of movement
	- II -- tonic, always firing, detect static position
- Intrinsic muscle fiber = contractile region in spindle innervated by gamma motor neuron
#### Mechanism
- Change to muscle length -> spindle to fire signal to spinal cord
