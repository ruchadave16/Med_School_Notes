#### Mechanism
- Inhibits [[Cytochrome C]] Oxidase -> cells can't use O2 even though it's being delivered
#### Sources
- Combustion of polyurethane
- Insecticides
- Gold extraction
- Jewelry cleaners
- CN-Salt consumption
- HOUSE FIRE
#### Toxicity
- Symptoms: headache, confusion, seizures, lactic acidosis
#### Findings
- PaO2 normal
- Bright red venous blood
- [[Lactic acidosis]]
#### Antidotes
- [[Hydroxocobalamin]]
- Sodium thiosulfate
- [[Nitrites]]
