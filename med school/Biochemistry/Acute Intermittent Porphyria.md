### Etiology
- Enzyme: Porphobilinogen deaminase

### Pathophysiology
- Acute attacks (GI, psych, motor) associated with photosensitivity
	- Precipitated by alcohol and/or oral contraceptives.
- High demand for synthesis of cytochrome P450

### Clinical Features
- Neurovisceral complaints
	- severe abdominal pain, polyneuropathy, and psychiatric disturbances
- Signs
	- ALA and porphobilinogen accumulate in urine
- Variants

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Hemin treatment
- Avoid triggers
