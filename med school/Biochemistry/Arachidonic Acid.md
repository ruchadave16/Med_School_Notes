### Metabolism

![[Screenshot 2026-03-31 at 8.29.15 AM.png]]

1) [[COX]] Pathway
	- Makes [[prostaglandins]], [[PGI2|prostacyclin]], and [[TXA2|thromboxane]]
	- COX 1 and COX 2 enzymes
	- Targeted by [[NSAIDS]]
2) LOX Pathway
	- Makes [[leukotrienes]] and [[lipoxins]]
### COX Pathway
- COX-1
	- Always expressed
	- Found in most tissues
	- Gastric mucosal protection, platelet aggregation, renal blood flow
	- Produces [[TXA2]], [[PGE2]], and [[PGI2]]
- COX-2
	- Induced during inflammation, causes pain and fever
	- Found on inflammatory cells only
	- [[PGE2]] and [[PGI2]] during just inflammation
