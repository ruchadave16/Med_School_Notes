*Prostaglandin type*
- Made by [[kidneys]] and inflammatory sites
- Receptor = EP
- Function
	- Fever, vasodilation, pain sensitization, gastric mucosal protection
	- Peripherally increases nociceptive pain and lowers activation threshold so NSAIDs inhibit production
	- Centrally increases excitability of neurons in dorsal horn
