*Inability to remove [[bilirubin]] from blood*

- Unconjugated bilirubin is insoluble and tightly bound to albumin
	- Cannot be removed by urine
- If ↑↑ production of bilirubin -> unconjugated hyperbilirubinemia 
- Bile duct injury or obstruction -> conjugated hyperbilirubinemia
#### Adults
- Damage to liver, cirrhosis, hepatic injury causes
#### Neonatal 
- [[Neonatal jaundice]]
- Liver can’t handle breakdown of erythrocytes
- Bilirubin is not glucuronidated and accumulates
