### Etiology
- **Autosomal recessive** disease due to [[Mutations|mutation]] of gene for [[Cystic Fibrosis Transmembrane Regulator]]
	- Could in some cases be de novo
- Most common mutation = deletion of F508 (CFTR gene)
	- 1 in 2000–2500 live births
### Pathophysiology
- Chloride secretion lowers, depletion of water from cell surface
- Thick mucus created that clogs pancreatic ducts and stops pancreatic enzymes from reaching intestine
	- Low fat absorption
	-

### Clinical Features
- Symptoms
	- Progressive degenerative pulmonary disease -- recurrent cough
		- [[Pseudomonas aeruginosa ]]
		- 
	- [[Exocrine Pancreas|Exocrine pancreas]] deficiency
	- Obstructive Azoospermia
- Signs
	- Elevated sweat chloride
	- Growth failure  
- Variants
	- 6 different CFTR mutations

### Diagnosis
- Newborn screening
- Chloride sweat test

### Treatment
**Acute**
- [[Tobramycin]]

- Pharmacologic
	- CFTR modulators
	- Replacement of pancreatic enzymes
	- Supplementation through fat-soluble vitamins
- Surgical
	- Double lung transplant
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- Chronic lung infections with progressive pulmonary disease
	- [[Bronchiectasis]]
- Male infertility
