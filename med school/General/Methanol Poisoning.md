### Etiology
- Windshield wiper fluid, antifreeze, paints, varnishes, adhesives, moonshine
- Suicide
- Accidental (kids)
- Alcoholism

### Pathophysiology
- Bilat. necrosis of **putamen**
![[Screenshot 2026-04-14 at 9.20.09 PM.png]]
### Clinical Features
- Nausea / vomiting
- [[Metabolic acidosis]]
	- CNS depression
	- Hyperventilation
- **Retinal toxicity**
	- Scotomas -> total blindness

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Sodium bicarbonate
- Dialysis
- Fomepizole or ethanol to compete with methanol

### Complications
- Blindness
- Parkinsonism or dystonia
