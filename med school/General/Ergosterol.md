- Found on [[Fungi|fungal]] cell membranes
### Synthesis
- [[Acetyl CoA]] -> squalene by squalene synthase
- Squalene -> squalene epoxide by squalene epoxidase
- Squalene epoxide -> lanosterol by lanosterol synthase
- lanosterol -> ergosterol by lanosterol 14-alfa demethylase
