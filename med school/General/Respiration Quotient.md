*RQ = VCO 2 (L) / VO 2 (L)*

- Used to measure amount of energy expended while performing activity
- In healthy adult, this should be 0.85
	- Ketogenic diet: ~0.7 because not much glucose
	- Underfeeding: ~0.7 because fat stores are being used
	- Overfeeding: ~1.0

Weir Equation: 
RMR (resting metabolic rate) (kcal/day) = [3.9 × VO ₂(L) + 1.1 × VCO ₂(L))] × 1440 (min)
