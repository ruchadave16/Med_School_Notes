- Absorbed in [[duodenum]] and [[jejunum]]
- Similar metabolism to [[iron]]
	- Cupric ion (Cu2+) is reduced to Cuprous ion (Cu+) 
	- Copper Transporter 1 (CTR1) carries Cu+ into the cytosol of the enterocyte -> golgi apparatus
	- Cu+ is then exported out of the cell through the ATP7A transporter
	- Once in the blood, Cu+ is oxidized by O2 to Cu2+
### [[Copper Poisoning]]
- green or blue-colored stool
- common GI symptoms are present: vomiting, jaundice, diarrhea.
