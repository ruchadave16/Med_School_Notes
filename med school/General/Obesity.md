*An excess accumulation of body fat and a BMI > 30 kg/m^2.* 
### Etiology
- High intake of energy -dense foods , sweetened beverages, alcohol
- Sedentary lifestyle
- Heavy marketing of energy-dense foods

**[[Nutrition]] Workup Example**
1) calculate BMI
2) calculate RMR from weight, height, and age to figure out how much total intake per day
3) ask about living, exercise etc to figure out PAL 

**General advice**
- Cut down on drinking: explain how many extra calories come from 1 drink a day
