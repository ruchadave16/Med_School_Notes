### Malnutrition Disorders
 - [[Chronic Pancreatitis]]: 
	 - ↓ amylase so cannot absorb di and monosaccharides
	 - ↓ lipase so fats undigested 
 - [[Crohn's Disease|Crohns disease]]: 
	 - Terminal ileum damage so no bile acid absorption and thus fat undigested
	 - Brush border damage
	 - Protein losing enteropathy
- [[Celiac Disease]]:
	- Villious atrophy so ↓ disaccharide digestion

### Overnutrition
- Protein overload:
	- kidney strain
	- constipation
	- weight gain
- Carbs:
	- Insulin resistance
	- [[Diabetes Mellitus]]
- Fat:
	- Gastrointestinal distress: nausea, indigestion, diarrhea
	- ↑ [[Atherosclerosis]] risk
