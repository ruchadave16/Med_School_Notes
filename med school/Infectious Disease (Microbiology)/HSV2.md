#### Mechanism
- STD
- Birth canal to child -- 4% cases

- Entry through mucosal surfaces and replicate in epithelial cells
- Spread to sensory nerve endings -- retrograde to sacral dorsal root
- Latency in sensory neurons
- Reactivation due to stress, trauma, immune issues
	- Anterograde back to skin
#### Symptoms
- Herpes genitalis
- Aseptic meningitis
- Neonatal infection
	- Meningoencephalitis
	- Pneumonitis
	- Vesicular rash
#### Diagnosis
- Distinguish from [[HSV1]] by monoclonal antibody against **glycoprotein G**
	- HSV1 = gG-1
	- HSV2 = gG-2
- PCR

>[!Note]
>Consider in any infant < 1 month with fever, seizures, lethargy, poor feeding, and neuro symptoms
>- Any infant with vesicular rash up to 8 weeks
#### Treatment
- [[Acyclovir]] IV in kid
- C section for woman in pregnancy with active infection
#### Prevention
- No vaccine
- Protection during sex
- C-section during birth if mother has active lesions
