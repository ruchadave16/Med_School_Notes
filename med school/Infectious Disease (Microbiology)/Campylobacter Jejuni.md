*[[Gram negative]] seagull shaped curved rod*
- Oxidase positive
- Grows at 42 C on Campy blood agar
- Cross contamination in food handling
#### Mechanism
- Flagella motility
- Cholera toxin 
#### Symptoms
- Watery diarrhea, bloody
- Fever
- Abdominal pain (RLQ)

**Complications**
- [[Guillain Barre Syndrome]]
- [[IBS]]
- [[Reactive arthritis]]
- [[Hemolytic uremic syndrome]]
- Erythema nodosum
#### Diagnosis
- Stool cultures
- Biopsies show only non-specific neutrophilic colitis with variable epithelial damage
#### Treatment
- Self limited
- Can give [[Azithromycin|azithromycin]]
