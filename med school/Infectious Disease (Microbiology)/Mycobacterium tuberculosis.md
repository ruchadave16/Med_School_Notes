*Slow growing non-spore intracellular aerobe*
- Acid fast staining bacteria -- mycolic acid
- LJ media
#### Mechanism
- Transmission = airborne, highly contagious
- Cord factor = promotes granuloma formation
- Mycolic acid -> triggers [[IL-12]] release ->  [[Th1]] response
- Lipoarabinomannan (LAM) -> blocks phagolysosome fusion → chronic infection

**Cell Wall**
- Mycolic acids (long-chain fatty acids)
- Arabinogalactan (a sugar polymer)
- Peptidoglycan
- Lipoarabinomannan
#### Symptoms
- Latent tuberculosis -- not contageous
	- Caseating [[Granuloma]]
	- Risk factors for reactivation
		- [[HIV]]
		- [[infliximab]]
		- Immunosuppressive
		- Diabetes, smoking
- Active TB
	- Cough
	- low grade fever
	- blood in sputum
	- night sweats
	- ↓ appetite
	- weight loss
#### Diagnosis
- 6-8 weeks to grow on lab media
- [[Lowenstein-Jensen Agar]] -- selective

- Acid fast stain
- Fluorescent stain
- QunatiFERON Gold test / TB spot test
- IGRA blood test
	- [[IFN-y]] measurement from sensitized T lymphocytes
	- Not affected by prior BCG vaccination
- PPD test ([[Type IV Hypersensitivity]])
	- Skin test small amount of PPD (purified protein derivative) antigen
	- Induration (not the redness) at the injection site is measured 48-72 hours after
	- False positive reactions may occur: Prior BCG vaccine
- TB PCR
