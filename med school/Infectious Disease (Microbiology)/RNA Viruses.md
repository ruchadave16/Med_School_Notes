#### Characteristics
- Most are ssRNA (ex: reovirus)
	- [[Rota virus]] = ds and segmented RNA
	- All dsRNA are non-enveloped
- Positive Sense
	- Like mRNA
	- Immediately translated
	- Icosahedron mainly
	- Either enveloped or not 
	- No virion polymerase in +ssRNA -- use host ribosomes
- Negative Sense
	- Translated to mRNA first
	- Have their own RNA dependent RNA polymerase (RdRP)
	- Helical mainly (all helical are enveloped)
	- Virion polymerase for -ssRNA and dsRNA
- Ambisense have both
