- + sense RNA virus
- No envelope
- Transmission through fecal oral path
	- Exception = [[rhinovirus]]
- Uses cells machinery to make RNA but cleaved through proteases
	- Replication in cytoplasm

**Types**
- Enterovirus -- #1 cause of [[aseptic meningitis]] -> glucose in CSF normal, no bacteria, elevated protein
	- [[Poliovirus]]
	- Echovirus
	- Coxsackievirus
- [[Hepatitis A virus]]
- [[Rhinovirus]]
