- + RNA virus
- [[Picornavirus]]
- Naked -- no envelope
- Fecal oral transmission -- acid stable
#### Mechanism
- Initial replication in lymphoid in GI like [[Peyer's Patches|Peyer's patches]]
	- 2-3 weeks
- Spreads to **anterior horn** of spinal cord

- Cleaves TATA-binding subunit, stopping initiation complex
#### Symptoms
- Asymmetric paralysis in lower limbs
- Respiratory insufficiency -- diaphragm paralysis
- Aseptic meningitis
#### Diagnosis
#### Treatment
None
#### Prevention
- Inactivated -- Salk vaccine
	- Only IgG antibodies
- Live attenuated oral -- Sabin ("saving a life")
	- IgA and IgG
	- Rare chance of getting disease
