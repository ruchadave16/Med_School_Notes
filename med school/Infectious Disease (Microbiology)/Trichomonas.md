- Sexual transmission 
- Humans = reservoir
#### Mechanism
#### Symptoms
- Vaginitis -- burning, itching, yellow-green malodorous discharge
- Cervicitis -- red, inflamed cervix
- Trichomoniasis
#### Diagnosis
- Wet mount KOH -- motile trophozoites, no cysts
- pH 4.5 and up (similar to [[Gardnerella Vaginalis]] but candida is low pH)
#### Treatment
- [[Metronidazole]] -- treat woman and partner both
#### Prevention
- Condoms
