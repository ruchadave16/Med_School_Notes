- Source = fecal oral transmission, commonly in poorly purified water
#### Mechanism
- Ingestion of cysts that form trophozoites in duodenum
- Attach to wall but NO invasion
#### Symptoms
- Foul smelling diarrhea (fatty diarrhea)
	- Significant weight loss
#### Diagnosis
- Trophozoite shape -- attaches but does not invade intestinal wall
	- Presence in stool = diagnostic (Stool O&P)
	-![[Screenshot 2026-03-05 at 1.32.12 PM.png]]
	
- ELISA stool antigen
#### Treatment
- [[Metronidazole]]
#### Prevention
- Water purification and handwashing
