*Weak gram staining rods*
- Grow on [[Buffered Charcoal Yeast Extract|BCYE]]
#### Mechanism
- Transmission through inhalation of contaminated water
	- Air conditioners, hot tubs, cooling towers, swimming pool, hospital ventilators
- Survives in [[Macrophage|macrophages]]
#### Symptoms
- [[Atypical pneumonia]]
- Pontiac fever
- [[Legionnaires' disease]]
	- diarrhea, nausea
	- [[hyponatremia]], [[hypophosphatemia]]
	- confusion
#### Diagnosis
- Urine antigen testing for serogroup 1
- Sputum culture
#### Treatment
- [[Azithromycin]]
- [[Doxycycline]]
- [[Levofloxacin]]

**Beta lactams INEFFECTIVE**
#### Prevention
