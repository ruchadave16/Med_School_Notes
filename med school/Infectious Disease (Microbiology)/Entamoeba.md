- Cyst form = infectious from contaminated water, also anal-oral transmission
	- Cysts **resistant** to chlorination
#### Mechanism
- Ingestion of cysts -> form trophozoites in small intestine
- Pass to colon and multiply there, pass in feces
- Invade colonic epithelium -- flask shaped ulcers
#### Symptoms
- Liver abscess in right lobe -- RUQ pain
	- Pus like anchovy paste
	![[Screenshot 2026-03-05 at 1.27.45 PM.png]]
- Intestinal amebiasis -- ulcerations that look like flasks
	- Bloody diarrhea
#### Diagnosis
- Stool O&P to see trophozoites with **endocytosed RBCs**
- ELISA antigen on stool and serum

![[Screenshot 2026-03-05 at 1.29.16 PM.png]]
#### Treatment
- [[Metronidazole]]
- Luminal: [[paramycin]] (aminoglycoside), [[iodoquinol]]
#### Prevention
