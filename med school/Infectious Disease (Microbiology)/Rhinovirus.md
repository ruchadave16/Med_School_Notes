- Transmission through respiratory droplets + fomites -- acid labile
- Positive-sense, single-stranded RNA (+ sense RNA), non-enveloped.
#### Mechanism
- Attaches to ICAM-1 to enter host cell
	- Symptoms by inflammatory response
	- [[IL-8]], cholinergic, 
- Replicate in nasopharynx
#### Symptoms
- [[Rhinitis|common cold]] -- upper respiratory tract infection
