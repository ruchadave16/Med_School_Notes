- - RNA virus
- Enveloped
	- Bullet shaped
	- Helical capsid
- Carried by bats, squirrels, rodents
#### Mechanism
- Glycoprotein that binds to [[Nicotinic Receptors|nicotinic receptors]] in post synaptic motor endplate
- Incubation of weeks - months
- Retrograde travel along peripheral nerves
	- Replicates in motor neuron
	- Travels to dorsal root ganglion
	- Spreads to brain
#### Symptoms
1) Incubation
	- 10 days - 2 years
	- Shorter in children
2) Prodrome
	- 0-10 days
	- Nonspecific symptoms (fever, malaise)
3) Acute neurologic period
	- 2-7 days
	- [[Encephalitis]]
		- Hyperactivity and agitation
		- Altered mental status, Confusion
		- Hallucinations
		- Pharyngeal muscle spasms (leading to hydrophobia, aerophobia, coughing, and choking)
		- Nuchal rigidity
		- Muscle fasciculations and contractions (e.g. Facial grimacing; back and neck hyperextension)
		- Autonomic instability (e.g. Hypersalivation, Sweating, Lacrimation, pupil dilation, fever)
	- Paralytic form
		- Ascending flaccid paralysis
		- Headache
		- Fever
		- Muscle fasciculations
4) Coma
5) Death (2-14 days after symptoms start)
#### Diagnosis
- Negri bodies (eosinophilic)
	- Hippocampus cytoplasm -- pyramidal cells
	- Purkinje cells
- Bullet shaped cells

*These are intracytoplasmic eosinophilic inclusions with basophilic stippling surrounded by clear halo*

#### Treatment
Antidote before symptom onset

- Passive immunization after exposure
#### Prevention
- Vaccinate cats and dogs
- Preexposure prophylaxis
- Postexposure prophylaxis
	- Human rabies immune globulin (HRIG) if not vaccinated
