- Species
	- Plasmodium malariae -- Day 1, Day 4 fever
	- Plasmodium vivax + ovale -- Dormant hypnozoites in hepatocytes
		- Day 1, Day 3 fever
	- Plasmodium flaciparum -- Irregular fevers, neuro symptoms, kidney and lung occlusions
		- Banana shaped in blood smear
![[Screenshot 2026-07-08 at 10.51.32 PM.png]]
#### Mechanism
- Transmitted by **Anopheles** mosquito with spores in saliva
- Sporozoites (infectious) -> trophozoites (ring forms on blood smear) in liver
	- Rupture of [[hepatocytes]] and infection -> schizont -> merozoites (asexual) in RBCs
	- Continues cycle in RBCs
	- Can also form gametocytes (sexual form)
- Falciparum
	- Can infect RBCs of any age
	- Causes them to clump together (rosette) and block blood flow -> ischemia
	
![[Screenshot 2026-07-08 at 10.41.33 PM.png]]
#### Symptoms
- Fever, chills, headache, sweat, fatigue, nausea, vomiting
- 1) cold stage, 2) hot stage, 3) sweats
	- Septic shock -- flaciparum
	- knowlesi = 24 hr fever cycle
	- falciparum, vivax, and ovale = 48 hr cycle
	- malariae = 72 hr cycle
- **Falciparum** = highest virulence and death 
#### Diagnosis
- Blood smear with [[Giemsa Stain]]
#### Treatment
- Chloroquine -- blocks heme polymerase 
- Primaquine -- stops hypnozoites
	- Check for [[G6PD deficiency]]
- [[Mefloquine]] -- stronger, also used for travelers
- Atovaquone
- Severe infections:
	- Artamisins
	- Atovaquone + proguanil
- Backup
	- IV artesunate
	- IV quinidine
#### Prevention
Travellers:
- Mefloquine
- Atovaquone + proguanil

**Genetic**
- Mutations in globin genes – [[Sickle Cell Anemia|Sickle cell]]; [[HbC disease]]
- Mutations causing globin deficiencies – [[thalassemias]]
- Mutations affecting RBC enzymes – [[G6PD deficiency]]
