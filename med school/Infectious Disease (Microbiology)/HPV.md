- Oncogenic strains (16, 18, 31, 33)
- Direct contact -- sexual
#### Mechanism
- Entry of hrHPV into immature basal epithelial cells at transformation zone of cervix or area of trauma
- Persistent infection in maturing epithelium
- E6 and E7 proteins 
	- E6 binds [[p53]] 
		- Degrades
		- ↓ G1 stopping of damaged DNA
		- ↓ apoptosis
	- E7 binds to [[Rb protein]]
		- Degradation of protein
		- Release of E2F transcription factor
		- Uncontrolled proliferation
#### Symptoms
- Common papilloma -- warts (1-4)
	- Non-painful
- Condyloma acuminata (6, 11)
- Cervix carcinoma -- squamous cell carcinoma
	- Can cause oropharyngeal lesions
#### Diagnosis
- Koilocyte detection -- squamous epithelial cells (keratinocytes)
![[Screenshot 2026-03-03 at 11.07.24 PM.png]]
- Biopsy of lesions
- PCR along with cytology
#### Treatment
- Liquid nitrogen for skin lesions
- Podophyllin for genital lesions
- Salicylic acid for plantar lesions
#### Prevention
