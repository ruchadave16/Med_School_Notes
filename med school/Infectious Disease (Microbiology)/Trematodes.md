#### Schistosoma
- Swimmers at risk (aquatic environment)
- Into blood -> liver (mature to adults), eggs excreted to water
	- Migrate against portal flow
	- *S. Mansoni* -- large lateral spine
	- *S. japonicum* -- small spine
- Snails = intermediate host

- Swimmer's itch 
	- Mansoni + japonicum = portal hypertension causing cirrhosis, jaundice
	
*S. haematobium* -- large terminal spine
- Against portal flow
- Hematuria, bladder cancer

Treatment:
- [[Praziquantel]]
#### Chinese Liver Flu (Clonorchis sinensis)
- Snails = intermediate host
- Uncooked fish -> resides in biliary system
	- Biliary fibrosis
	- Cholangiocarcinoma
	- Pigmented gallstones
- Eggs: look operculated

Treatment:
- [[Praziquantel]]
#### Paragonimus Westermani
- Chronic cough with bloody sputum
- Snail = intermediate host
- Undercooked crab meat with larvae
	- Mature to adults -> lung
- Operculated eggs on O&P

Treatment:
- [[Praziquantel]]
