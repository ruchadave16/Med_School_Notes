- Transmission: sexual contact, body fluids, trans-placental, breast milk
	- NOT through saliva, contact, insect bites
#### Characteristics
- Reovirus
	- [[Reverse transcriptase]]
	- Enveloped, 2 copies of ssRNA -- + sense RNA
	- 9 genes: gag (Gp17, p24), pol (enzymes), env (for Gp120, Gp41), rev, nef, tat, vif, vpu, vpr in HIV1
		- vpx instead of vpu in HIV2
	- Lipid envelope with spike proteins
		- Gp120 (env) -- CD4 and chemokine binding
		- Gp41 (env) -- fusion and viral entry
		- Gp17 (gag) -- matrix for structure
		- Capsid protein p24 (gag) -- specific antigen
- Enzymes:
	- Reverse transcriptase (pol) -- viral RNA -> DNA
	- Integrase -- viral DNA into host DNA
	- Protease -- cleaves polyproteins to functional proteins

#### Mechanism
- Destroys [[Helper T Cells|CD4]] cells (also macrophages, dendritic, astrocytes)
	- Initially infects [[Macrophage|macrophages]]
	1) Gp120 binds to CD4 and chemokine coreceptors **CCR5** (early stage) or **CXCR4** T cells (late stage)
	2) Conformational change post binding exposes Gp41 for fusion of membrane
	3) Shell of capsid disintegrates inside cell
	4) *Reverse transcriptase* converts RNA -> DNA in cytoplasm
	5) Viral DNA -> nucleus
	6) Integrates with host DNA through *integrase*, called proviral DNA
	7) Either stays dormant OR replicates into viral progeny
		- Transcribes into viral RNA and mRNA, out to cytoplasm
		- Translated into HIV proteins that are cleaved to be functional by *protease*
		- New immature HIV virions form
 
![[Screenshot 2026-03-03 at 12.03.28 PM.png]]

- Move to lymph nodes and new virions in 3-4 days
- Faster replication in blood -> high viral load
- Acute phase = 2-12 weeks

- Rapid infection of CCR5 memory CD4 especially in mucosal tissues
	- Death of CD4 through lysis
	- Non-infected also undergo [[apoptosis]] because of chemokines
- Pyroptosis = main mechanism
#### Symptoms
- Asymptomatic (60%) -> Initially like mild flu 
	- Lymphadenopathy
	- Fever
	- Aseptic meningitis
- NON-painful vesicles
- Acute retroviral syndrome (rare)

**Chronic Stage**
Latency up to 10 years
- [[HIV Subacute Encephalitis]]
- No AIDS (CD4 > 200)
	- Generally no symptoms,
	- Sometimes lymphadenopathy
	- Opportunistic infections
	- Lasts years, eventually development of AIDS
- [[AIDS]] (CD4 < 200 or defining condition)
#### Diagnosis
**Screening** (4th gen antigen)
- All 13-64 once
- Annual for high risk 
- Pregnant
- ELISA test
	- Confirm with Western blot
	
**Diagnosis**
- First positive = PCR (3-4 days) -> not routinely done
	- Gap = window period
- P24 antigen is positive in 10 days
- Antibody detected 3-4 weeks -- generally negative
- If CD4 count < **200** -> [[AIDS]]
#### Treatment
[[ART|ARTs]]
#### Prevention
Prophylaxis
- PrEP for high risk individuals
	- Daily pill with 2 [[NRTIs]]
- PEP for exposure within 72 hours
	- 28 day course of 2 [[NRTIs]] and integrase inhibitor
- HIV+ pregnancy
	- IV Zidovudine if RNA load unknown or > 1000 during delivery

- Mutation of CCR5 (delta 32 deletion) = natural resistance to HIV
