[[Paramyxovirus]]
- HA, NA, F protein (fusion) and M protein (matrix)
- Large, enveloped with helical nucleocapsid, single-stranded, negative-sense RNA.
#### Mechanism
- HA and NA on same spikes
- HA attaches to epithelium then F protein does fusion
	- Nucleocapsid released into cell -> viral assembly
	- 
#### Symptoms
- [[Laryngotracheobronchitis|Croup]]
	- "seal like" barking cough
	- Inspiratory stridor
- [[Bronchiolitis]] and [[pneumonia]]
#### Diagnosis
- Steeple sign on X-ray
- Multiplex PCR-based NAATs
#### Treatment
- Nasal decongestants are **not indicated** for children < 5 years
- [[Synthetic Corticosteroids|Corticosteroids]] if needed (1 dose of [[Dextromethorphan|dextromethorphan]])
#### Prevention
