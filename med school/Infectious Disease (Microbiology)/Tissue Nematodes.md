#### Dracunculus
- Contaminated water with copepods with larvae
- Penetrate stomach when copepods die
- Migrate to skin surface -- skin ulcer

Treatment
- Slowly extract worm with a stick
#### Onchocerca Volvulus
- Black fly, larvae in skin
- Scattered papules that can become hyper / hypopigmented
- Can float into eye -- #2 cause of eye blindness from microb
- Microfilariae under microscope

Treatment
- [[Thiabendazole|Ivermectin]]
#### Wuchereria Bancrofti
- Elephantiasis (long standing lymphedema)
- Cough from microfilariae in lungs
- Source = mosquito -- larvae from skin -> lymphatics
- Thick blood smear for diagnosis

Treatment:
- Diethylcarbamazine
#### Toxocara canis and catis
- Food with dog / cat feces
- Larvae stay and circulate in blood, can cause blindness in eye

Treatment: [[Albendazole]]
#### Loa Loa
- Transient angioedema 
- Can be seen migrating across conjunctiva
	- Blood smear shows micofilariae
- Deer flys 

Treatment:
- Diethylcarbamazine
- [[Albendazole]]
