[[Paramyxovirus]]
- No NA but do have HA and surface F (fusion) protein
#### Symptoms
Measles (rubeola)
- Prodromal fever with cough, coryza, conjunctivitis 
- Koplik spots (bright red spots with blue white center on buccal mucosa)
- 1-2 later maculopapular rash head -> face to downwards
	- Confluent so spots blend together
	- Itchy

**Pneumonia** = #1 cause of death related to measles

Rare Consequences
- SSPE
- Encephalitis
- Giant cell pneumonia
#### Diagnosis
- Lymphadenitis with Warthin-Finkeldey giant cells with paracortical hyperplasia
	- Eosinophilic
#### Treatment
- [[Vitamin A]] supplements to reduce mortality
#### Prevention
- [[MMR vaccine]] -- live attenuated
