#### Mechanism
- Respiratory droplet transmission and transplacental
- Infect [[erythroblasts]]  in [[bone marrow]] -> aplastic anemia

#### Symptoms
- "slapped face" rash in children (Fifth disease)
- Can cause [[aplastic anemia]]
- Acute, self-limited arthritis
- Pregnancy infection -> [[hydrops fetalis ]]
- Can cause [[Dilated Cardiomyopathy|dilated cardiomyopathy]]
