#### Mechanism
- Spike (S) glycoproteins -- receptor binding and fusion with membrane
	- Bind to ACE-2 on respiratory epithelioid cells
- Large, enveloped, single-stranded, positive-sense RNA virus 
- Encodes polyproteins
#### Symptoms
- URI symptoms
- Fever, chills, fatigue, loss of taste and smell
- GI symptoms: Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea
- Many usual symptoms: cardiac symptoms, deep vein thrombosis.
- Severe cases: Respiratory failure, sepsis like presentation with multiorgan failure.
#### Diagnosis
- PCR -- viral RNA from respiratory specimens
#### Treatment
- [[Paxlovid]] -- use for medium Covid in high risk patients
	- Nirmatrelvir: inhibits SARS-CoV-2 3CL protease.
	- Ritonavir: CYP3A4 booster
- [[Remdesivir]] = IV antiviral
	- RNA-dependent RNA polymerase inhibitor
	- survival benefit in hospitalized patients requiring oxygen
	- Give to hospitalized and high risk
- [[Molnupiravir]]
	- Nucleoside analog;
	- RNA mutagenesis, not first line
	- AVOID in pregnancy
- [[Dexamethasone]] for patients who need o2
- [[Tocilizumab]]
	- IL-6 inhibitor
#### Prevention
- mRNA vaccines for Spike protein
	- Pfizer
	- Moderna
- Protein subunit
	- Novavax
