- Protozoa
- Found in South America / Central America
#### Mechanism
- Kissing bug (reduviid) bites around mouth and deposits feces
	- Scratching causes it to go in
- Burrows into blood and then into endocardium
#### Symptoms
- 10-20 years later, might cause [[Chagas disease]]
	- Megacolon
	- Dilated cardiomyopathy
	- Mega-esophagus
#### Diagnosis
- Blood smear in active infection
	- Might be seen in cardiac cells in heart biopsy
- Chronic through serology + symptoms
#### Treatment
- Nifurtimox -- acute
#### Prevention
