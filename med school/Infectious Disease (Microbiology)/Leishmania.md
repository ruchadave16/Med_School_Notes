- Donovani in Middle East and Africa
- Braziliates -- host = vertebrates, vector = sandfly
#### Mechanism
- Amastagote in macrophage 
#### Symptoms
- Cutaneous leishmaniasis -- brazili
- Visceral leishmaniasis = black fever -- donovani
	- Pancytopenia when bone marrow affected
	- Liver, spleen affected -- hepatosplenomegaly
	- Fever
#### Diagnosis
#### Treatment
- [[Stibugluconate]] for cutaneous
- [[Amphotericin B]] for visceral
#### Prevention
