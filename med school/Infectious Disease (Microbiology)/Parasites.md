#### Characteristics
- Eukaryotic organism, all multicellular except protozoa
- Most have flexible cell membranes 
	- Protozoa have tough cell walls
- Reproduction through [[binary fission]]
	- Sporozoan = sporogony + schizogony
	- Helmiths can have genders, some are hermaphrodites
- Most are motile
	- Sporozoans are non-motile
- Protozoa
	- Generally facultative anaerobe
	- Diffusion nutrition
- Adult helminths 
	- Anaerobic
	- Ingestion for nutrition
#### Classification
- Ectoparasites
	- Pediculus humanus = head lice
	- Phthirus pubis = pubic lice
	- Sarcoptes scabiei = scabies
	- Dermatobia hominis = blot fly infection
- Protozoa (unicellular)
	- Intestinal
		- [[Entamoeba]]
		- [[Giardia]]
		- [[Cryptosporidium]]
		- Cyclospora, Cystoisospora
	- Tissue / Blood
		- [[Trichomonas]]
		- [[Toxoplasmosis|Toxoplasma]]
		-  [[Plasmodium|Malaria]]
		- [[Babesia]]
		- [[Leishmania]]
		- [[Trypanosoma]]
		- [[Naegleria fowleri]]
- Helminths (multicellular)
	- Nematodes
		- [[Intestinal Nematodes]]
			- Ascaris -- roundworm
			- Trichuris -- whipworm
			- Enterobius -- pinworm
			- Strongyloides
			- Trichinella
			- Hookworms
		- [[Tissue Nematodes]]
			- Microfilaria
			- Toxocara canis and catis
			- Zoonotic hookworm
	- [[Cestodes]]
		- Tinea (tapeworms)
		- [[Cysticercosis]]
	- [[Trematodes]]
		- Intestinal fluke
		- Lung flukes
#### Pathogenesis
- Inflammatory processes
	- Some can cause [[hyperplasia]]
	- Some can cause [[neoplasia]]
- Humoral response -- [[IgE]] fights helminths, [[IgM]] and [[IgG]] against protozoa
- [[T Lymphocytes|T cells]]
#### Life Cycle
- Definitive host = where adult parasite lives
- Intermediate host = where live during development (asexual)
- Reservoir host = essential for survival of parasite in order to spread
