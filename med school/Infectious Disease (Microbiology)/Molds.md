#### Structure
- Hyphae -- long tubular filaments that grow anywhere with oxygen and nutrients
- Mycelium -- body of fungi from network of hypahe
	- Arial mycelium = part that grows vertically for oxygen
- Septate 
	- [[Aspergillus]]
	- [[Fusarium]]
	- [[Dermatophytes]]
- Non-septate (continous cytoplasm w no septa)
	- [[Mucor]]
	- [[Rhizomucor]]
	- [[Absidia]]
- Cell wall
- Cytoplasm
- Spores -- give fuzzy appearance
### Reproduction
1) Spore germination -- hyphae grow
2) Branching forms mycelium
3) Asexual spore production 
4) Sexual reproduction (optional)
#### Asexual Reproduction
- Fast for colonization, low variations
- Sporangiospores -- inside sporangia ([[Rhizopus]])
- Conidia -- chains of spores externally found ([[Aspergillus]], [[Penicillium]])
	- Ex: Arthroconidia in Coccidioides immitis
	- ![[Screenshot 2026-03-04 at 5.32.21 PM.png]]
- Fragmentation -- hyphae break into segments and each can grow into new fungi (Coccidioides immitis)
- Budding -- rare 
	- Chlamydospores for survival
	- Pseudohyphae presence = infective fungal infection
#### Sexual Reproduction
- Fusion of specialized hyphae, forms resistant spores
- Zygospores -- Mucor/Rhizopus/Rhizomucor/Absidia
