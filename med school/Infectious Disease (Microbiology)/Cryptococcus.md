Transmission through inhalation, found in pigeon droppings
- High risk in [[HIV]] or immunocompromised -- opportunistic
- **Heavily encapsulated**
	- Capsule has repeating polysaccharide capsular antigen
- [[Urease Test]] positive
#### Mechanism
#### Symptoms
- Cough, dyspnea -- pneumonia
- [[Meningitis]] by spreading to CSF 
- Fever
- Umbilicated lesions on face and trunk
#### Diagnosis
- Bronchopulmonary washings
	- Mucicarmine (red stain)
	- Methanamine ([[Silver Stain|silver stain]] )
- Lumbar puncture - [[India Ink]] -- clear halo
	- ![[Screenshot 2026-03-04 at 9.22.26 PM.png]]
- Latex agglutination test -- detect the capsular antigen
- "Soap bubble lesions" on brain
#### Treatment
- [[Amphotericin B]] + flucytosine followed by fluconazole
#### Prevention
