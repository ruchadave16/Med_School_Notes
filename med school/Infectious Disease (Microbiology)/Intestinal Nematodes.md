### Generic
- Treatment overall = [[Albendazole]]
#### Ascaris -- roundworm
- blood stream -> lungs -> GI
- malnutrition, respiratory symptoms
- intestinal obstruction 

Diagnosis
- Eggs in stool and eosinophil count ↑ 
#### Enterobius -- pinworm
- Female lays eggs at anus
- Fecal oral transmission
- Scotch tape test
Treatment: pyrantel pamoate
#### Strongyloides
- Penetrate through skin of soles
- Blood -> lungs -> GI
- Autoinfection by laying eggs in intestinal wall

Diagnosis: larvae in stool + eosinophilia

Treatment: ivermectin
#### Trichinella
- Fever, vomiting, periorbital edema
- Severe myalgias
- Undercooked meat -> larve in blood stream -> striated muscle
	- Form cysts there
	- Muscle inflammation
#### Hookworms
- Anylostoma and Necator
- Infection through walking barefoot -- blood -> go to lungs, swallowed into intestine
- [[Iron deficiency anemia]]

Diagnose -- eggs in stool
- ↑ eosinophil count

Treatment: pyrantel pamoate
