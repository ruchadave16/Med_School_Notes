- Azole -- inhibit lanosterol 14-α demethylase (fungal CYP450) that converts lanosterol -> ergosterol
- Terbinafine -- dermatophyte infections
	- Inhibits squalene epoxidase (no conversion so can't make ergosterol)
- [[Amphotericin B]] and Nystatin -- bind to ergosterol and create holes in cell membrane
	- Ions leak out = cell death
