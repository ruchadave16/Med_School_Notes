- High risk to pregnant and immunocompromised

Transmission
- Cysts in raw meat
- Oocytes in animal feces (kitty liter) -- cats common
- Transplacental
#### Mechanism
#### Symptoms
- Immunocompromised -- ring enhancing lesions on CT (brain abscesses)
	- Encephalitis
- Healthy ppl -- flu like symptoms
- Congenital
	- Brain calcifications
	- Hydrocephalus
	- Chorioretinitis
	- Deafness
#### Diagnosis
- Brain biopsy to separate from CNS lymphoma
- Serology doesn't indicate active infection
#### Treatment
- [[Sulfadiazine]]
- [[Pyrimethamine]]
#### Prevention
Prophylaxis if CD4 < 100 and positive for IgG against toxo
- [[TMP-SMX]]
