#### Replication
- 2 copies of genome and has **reverse transcriptase**
- RNA dependent DNA polymerase

*Steps*
- RNA -> DNA in cytoplasm
- circular DNA -> nucleus integrates with cell DNA
- Transcription within cell to mRNA
- Full RNA used as viral genome and mRNA made through splicing
