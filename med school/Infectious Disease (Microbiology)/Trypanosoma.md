- Gambiense, Rhodesiense ()
- Transmission from tsetse fly
- Motile with flagella
#### Mechanism
- Move from blood to lymph nodes
- Variable surface glycoproteins that have antigenic variation
#### Symptoms
- Sleeping sickness -- coma
- Cervical and axillary lymphadenopathy
- Recurrent fevers
#### Diagnosis
- Trypomastigotes on blood smear
#### Treatment
- [[Melarsoprol]] -- CNS
- [[Suramin]] -- Peripheral blood
#### Prevention
