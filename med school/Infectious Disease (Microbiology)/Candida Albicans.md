#### Symptoms
- Diaper rash
- [[Oropharyngeal Candidiasis]] 
- Candidal esophagitis -- [[AIDS]] defining illness (CD <100)
- Vaginal candidasis

	- Antibiotic use
	- **NO** change to pH (only < 4 pH)

- Endocarditis
	- Tricuspid valve
	- IV drug users

#### Treatment
