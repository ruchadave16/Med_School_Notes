#### Structures
- Cell Wall
	- [[Chitin]] (long chains of N-ag)
	- Other polysaccharides: B-1,6 glucan and B-1,3 glucan ([[Echinocandin]] blocks synthase of these)
	
	![[Screenshot 2026-03-04 at 5.10.52 PM.png]]

- Cell Membrane
	- **Sterol** ([[ergosterol]]) for transport of ions
	- Target for [[antifungals]]
### Classification
1) Yeasts
	- Unicellular
	- Round / oval
	- Asexual budding
2) [[Molds]] (Filamentous)
3) Dimorphic Fungus
	- Molds at ambient temp
	- Yeast in human body
### Pathogenesis
- [[Granuloma]] formation
- Active suppuration = [[Neutrophils]] in exudate
- Mycotoxins may be produced
- Delayed hypersensitivity -- skin test

>[!Example] [[Dermatophytes]]
>Generelly keratin blocks fungus but these use [[keratin]] for growth

**Inhibition**
- Normal skin
- Fatty acids inhibit dermatophytes
- Mucosal membranes stop fungi
- In respiratory, mucosal membrane of nasopharynx blocks spores and alveolar macrophages
### Lab Detection
- 10% KOH mount
	- Dissolves keratin to see fungus clearly
- Lactophenol cotton blue wet mount
	- Stains chitin
- India ink wet mount
	- Cryptococcus neoformans has thick capsule that can be seen through this
- Mucicarmine for [[cryptococcus]]
