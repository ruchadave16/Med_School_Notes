- Southwest US location
- Transmission through inhalation of spores (dust)
- Earthquakes = risk
- Dimorphic -- endospores in body
#### Mechanism
- Enter lungs through inhalation
- Transform into spherules and make endospores
- Rupture to release and make new spherules
	- Wall resistant to phagocytosis
	- 
#### Symptoms
- Asymptomatic, can appear like fever, pneumonia, arthalgias in normal ppl
	- Erythema nodosum on shins

Systemic in Immunocompromised
- Skin and lung infection
- Bone infection
- Meningitis
#### Diagnosis
- See endospores in body, larger than RBCs
	-![[Screenshot 2026-03-04 at 10.29.10 PM.png]]
- May seem cavities or nodules in Xray
- KOH stain
#### Treatment
Itraconazole for local

#### Prevention
