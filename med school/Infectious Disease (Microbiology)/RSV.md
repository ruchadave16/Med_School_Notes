[[Paramyxovirus]]
- F protein  -- syncytia formation (multinucleated giant cells)
	- Antibodies target this
- Enveloped, non-segmented, single-stranded, negative-sense RNA virus
#### Mechanism
- Attaches to respiratory epithelium through G protein
	- Initial replication in nasopharyngeal 
- Spreads to lower resp. tract
	- Infects ciliated cells and causes necrosis
- NO NA or HA
#### Symptoms
- [[Pneumonia]] (#1 cause in infants)
- [[Bronchiolitis]] (#1 cause in infants)
#### Diagnosis
#### Treatment
- [[Ribavirin]] for adults
#### Prevention
High risk = premature birth, congenital heart syndrome, Down syndrome

- [[Nirsevimab]]
	- Single-dose monoclonal antibody.
	- First line
	- ~80% reduction in RSV hospitalization; ~90% reduction in ICU admission.
-  [[Palivizumab]] = immunoglobulin
	- No proven survival benefit
	- Reserved for high-risk infants
- RSV Vaccine
	- Approved for pregnant mothers and not indicated for infants

- [[Ribavirin]]: rarely used, in case of severe RSV for immunocompromised
