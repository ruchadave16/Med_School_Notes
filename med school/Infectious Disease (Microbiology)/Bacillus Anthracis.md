*[[Gram positive]] rod found in chains*

- **Spore** forming
- Forms b-hemolytic colonies on [[blood agar]]
- Capsule of D-glutamic acid
#### Mechanism
- Together, 3 toxins cause toxicity
	- Protective antigen (PA) -> enables entry of other 2 into cells
	- Lethal factor (LF) -> destroys MAPK signalling
	- Edema factor (EF) -> ↑ cAMP so ↑ edema
- Spores taken up by [[Macrophage|Macrophages]]
- Edema toxin = EF + PA
- Mimics adenylate cyclase
	- Acts on skin, lungs, GI
#### Symptoms
- Lungs -- [[necrotizing pneumonia]]
	- Inhalation of spores
	- Hemorrhagic [[pleural effusion]]
	- Mediastinal lymphadenopathy -- widening of mediastinum
- GI -- ulcers

Cutaneous anthrax
- Skin lesions with edematous borders
- Painless
#### Diagnosis
- Chest X-ray/CT chest:
	- Widening of mediastinum and hilar lymphadenopathy.
- Blood cultures
#### Treatment
- [[Ciprofloxacin]] or  [[doxycycline]] + protein synthesis inhibitor for 60 days
#### Prevention
