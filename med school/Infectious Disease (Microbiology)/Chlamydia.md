- Obligate [[Intracellular Bacteria|intracellular bacteria]]
- Biphasic replicative cycle
	- Elementary body -- infectious, inactive metabolically
	- Reticulate body -- intracellular, active metabolically, can replicate
#### Mechanism
- Chlamydia trachomatis
	- Serotypes A, B, C
		- Trachoma
		- Endemic to developing regions
	- D-K
		- Nongonococcal urethritis
		- Pelvic inflammatory disease, ectopic pregnancy, neonatal conjunctivitis
	- L1-3
		- Lymphogranuloma venereum
- Chlamydophila pneumoniae -- [[atypical pneumonia]]
	- [[Laryngitis]], [[pharyngitis]], [[bronchitis]]
	- Worsening asthma, encephalitis, or myocarditis
- Chlamydophila psittaci -- psittacosis
	- From birds
	- Abrupt fever, headache, dry cough
	- Hepatitis, renal failure, endocarditis
	- Multilobar involvement
	- [[Doxycycline]]

- PCR for pneumoniae

- [[Doxycycline]] or [[azithromycin]]
