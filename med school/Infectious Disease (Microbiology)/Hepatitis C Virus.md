- + enveloped RNA virus -- [[flavivirus]]
- Transmission through blood exposure, IV drug use, placental, sexual
#### Mechanism
- Antigenic variation in envelope -- hard to make vaccine
	- No proofreading from no 3'->5' exonuclease
- Proteins:
	- NS5A -- RNA replication
	- 
#### Symptoms
- Liver inflammation -- jaundice, RUQ pain, hepatomegaly, liver enzymes increase
- 60-80% become chronic
	- Cirrhosis
	- Hepatocellular carcinoma
#### Diagnosis
Acute -- ALT rise and fall in 6 months
- Cryoglobulins (serum proteins that precipitate in cold temp)
#### Treatment
Acute cases untreated

Chronic:
- NS3/4A -- viral protease inhibitors (PIs)
- NS5B -- nucleoside polymerase inhibitors (NPIs)
- NS5B -- non-nucleoside polymerase inhibitors (NNPIs)
- NS5A inhibitors -- stop RNA replication
#### Prevention
