- dsRNA virus, 11 **segmented** (BOAR)
- Non-enveloped
- NSP4 -- toxin for symptoms 
- Most common cause of severe childhood diarrhea
#### Mechanism
- NSP4 increases Cl- permeability
- Selective destruction of mature small intestine enterocytes
#### Symptoms
- Watery diarrhea in kids (winter) -- #1 cause
	- Osmotic

#### Prevention

- WHO recommends that the first dose be given at 6 weeks of age
