#### Taenia Genus
- Intermediate host = cattle (saginata), pigs (Solium)
	- Eating  meat
- Hooks on proglottid heads of T. solium
- Can cause [[neurocysticercosis]] (eat **eggs** transmitted by water contaminated)
	- Seizures
	- Can also cause lesions anywhere else in the body
	
Treatment
- [[Praziquantel]]
- Neuro: [[Albendazole]]
#### Fish Egg Tapeworm
- Undercooked fish
- Stays in intestine, causes diarrhea
- Also causes B12 deficiency -- megaloblastic anemia
- **Longest** tapeworm
- Proglottid segments on stool O&P

Treatment
- [[Praziquantel]]
- [[Niclosamide]]
#### Echinococcus Granulosus
- Dogs = definitive host, sheep dog = intermediate
- Dog feces transmission
- CT with egg shell calcifications in liver
	- Hydatid cyst in liver
	- Rupture can cause anaphylaxis and acute abdomen
