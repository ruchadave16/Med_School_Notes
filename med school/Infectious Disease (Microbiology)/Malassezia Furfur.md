- Thrives in heat
- Lipophilic
- Found in [[stratum corneum]] only
**Risk**
- Catheters -- especially neonates
- Immunocompromised
#### Mechanism
- Lipid degradation in [[Melanocytes|melanocytes]]
#### Symptoms
- Hypo/hyperpigmented patches in healthy patients -- [[Pityriasis versicolor]] (tinea versicolor)
	- Resolve spontaneously
#### Diagnosis
"Spagetti and meatball appearance" -- KOH prep
