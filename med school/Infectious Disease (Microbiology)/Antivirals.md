#### Mechanisms
1) Block attachment and stop penetration
	- Ex: [[Maraviroc]] and [[enfuvirtide]] to block CCR5 in HIV, stopping entry
	- Ex: [[Docosanol]] for cold sore from herpes -- stops entry into epithelial
2) Stop uncoating of virus
	- [[Amantadine]], [[Rimantadine]]
3) Competitive inhibition of nucleic acid synthesis
	- Guanosine analog -- stop lengthening of DNA strand, reducing viral proliferation
		- Also affect mitochondrial DNA though
		- [[Acyclovir]], [[ganciclovir]]
		- [[Ribavirin]]
	- DNA polymerase inhibitors like [[foscarnet]]
4) Stop late viral protein synthesis and assembly
	- Protease inhibitors in HIV and Hep C
	- These stop large polypeptide from being cut
5) Stop packaging
	- Neuraminidase (NA) inhibitors like oseltamivir for influenza
6) Stop protein synthesis and stimulate inflammatory
	- IFN-a
