*[[Gram negative]] rod*
- Non-lactose fermenting colonies
- Produces H2S -- black colonies on TSI agar
#### Mechanism
- Type III secretion system transfers proteins into M cells
#### Symptoms
- Salmonella typhi
	- [[Typhoid]] fever
	- [[Osteomyelitis]] in patients with [[Sickle Cell Anemia|sickle cell disease]]
	- Reaches liver through blood
- Non-typhoid
	- Gastroenteritis
	- Uncooked food transmission
#### Diagnosis
- Stool culture
#### Treatment
- Salmonella typhi
	- [[Fluoroquinolones]] like ciprofloxacin
- Antibiotics NOT recommended for non-typhoid
#### Prevention
