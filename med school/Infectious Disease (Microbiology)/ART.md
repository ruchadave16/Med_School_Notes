### Classes
- Entry inhibitors: Maraviroc, Enfuvirtide
- NRTI (nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitors): Zidovudine, Abacavir, Lamivudine,
Emtricitabine, Tenofovir
- NNRTI (non-nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitors): Efavirenz, Rilpivirine
- INSTI (integrase strand transfer inhibitor): Raltegravir, Elvitegravir, Dolutegravir, Bictegravir
- PI (protease inhibitor): Atazanavir, Darunavir
- Post attachment blocker: monoclonal antibody: Ibalizumab
### Mechanisms
1) Attachment and Fusion
	- Gp120 -> CD4
	- Cp41 (Enfuvirtide binds) -> CCR5 (Maraviroc blocks) / CXCR4
2) viral RNA -> DNA by reverse transcriptase
	- NRTIs
	- NNRTIs
3) Integration of viral DNA with host DNA through integrase
	- INSTIs -- **first line drugs**
4) Replication or latency 
	- No drugs
5) Assembly of virion
	- Protease inhibitors -- darunavir preferred
6) Budding and release
#### **Protocol**
**First Line**: INSTI (bictegravir) + 2 NRTIs (emtricitabine and tenofovir)
Protease based: Darunavir and 2 NRTIs (emtricitabine and tenofovir)
- Boost with ritonavir and cobicistat to reduce toxicity
NNRTI based: Efavirenz + 2 NRTIs (emtricitabine and tenofovir)
