### Etiology
- Genetic (autosomal recessive): GAA (intron region) -> ↓ generation of protein
	- Normal = 6-32
	- Abnormal = 200-1700
- FXN gene on Chromosome 9 -- frataxin coding
- Onset: Childhood (10-15 yrs)
### Pathophysiology
- ↓ Frataxin means impairment of [[Mitochondria]] function
- Degeneration of
	- [[Corticospinal Tract]] -- spastic paralysis
	- [[Spinocerebellar Tracts]] -- appendicular ataxia
	- [[DCML|dorsal column medial lemniscus system]] -- ↓ vibration, proprioception
	- [[dorsal root ganglion]] -- ↓ [[Deep Tendon Reflexes]]

### Clinical Features
- Symptoms
	- Progressive limb ataxia
	- Spastic paralysis
	- ↓ vibration, proprioception
	- ↓ deep tendon reflexes
	- Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy (75%)
	- Diabetes (1/3)
	- Pyramidal weakness in legs
	- Nystagmus, dysarthria
	- Kyphoscoliosis
	- Might have pes cavus
