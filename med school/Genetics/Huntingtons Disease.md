### Etiology
- Genetic: CAG (polyglutamine) codon expanded to sequence in HTT gene Chromosome 4
	- Normal = 6-34 
	- Abnormal = 36–121 times 
- More common through father
- Autosomal **dominant**
	- Dominant negative so mutants stops WT from doing function
- Shows **anticipation**
- Commonly on male transmitted alleles
- Onset: 30-50 age
### Pathophysiology
- Production of Huntington protein (expanded polyglutamines at amino terminus)
	- Clump together
- Loss of motor neurons ([[basal ganglia]] -- caudate nucleus and putamen)
	- ↓ thalamic inhibition of undesired movement
- See prominent [[Gliosis]]
- Inclusions + for [[Ubiquitin]]
### Clinical Features
- Starts as mood disorder (annoyed, irritable)
- Loss of motor control (**bilat**.)
	- Involuntary ‘choreic’ movements
	- Progressive
- Dementia
- Hallucinations
- Affective disorder
