- Vomiting center in [[Medulla]] coordinates reflex
- Afferent (vestibular system)

*no gastric slow wave activity -> normal peristalsis decreases -> reverse peristalsis -> stomach relaxation -> forced expulsion of contents*

#### Treatments
- [[5-HT3 antagonists]] -> [[Ondansetron]]
- [[D2 antagonists]] -> [[metoclopramide]], [[prochlorperazine]]
- [[NK1 antagonists]] -> [[aprepitant]]
- [[H1 antagonist]] -> [[diphenhydramine]], [[meclizine]], [[dimenhydrinate]]
- [[M1 antagonists]] -> [[scopolamine]]

**[[Prenatal Period|Pregnancy]]** -> [[Vitamin B6|pyridoxine]], [[doxylamine]], ginger, acupuncture
