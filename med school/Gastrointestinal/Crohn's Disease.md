### Definition
Inflammatory bowel disease ([[IBD]]) that causes swelling of digestive track (mainly [[small intestine]])

### Etiology
- Primary causes
- Risk factors

### Pathophysiology
- Noncaseating granulomas → [[Th1]] mediated
- Transmural inflammation
	- Fibrosis
	- Serositis
- Ulcers are deep, knife-like
- Skip lesions -- Areas of normal bowel in between areas of affected bowel (Cobblestone appearance)
- Location: anywhere mouth -> colon, common in ileocecal region

### Clinical Features
- [[Diarrhea]]
- Bowel obstruction
- Fever
- Fatigue
- Abdominal pain + cramping
- Strictures common
- [[Iron Deficiency Anemia]] -> blood loss
	- [[B12 Deficiency]] from malabsorption

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- [[Glucocorticoids|Glucocorticoids]], azathioprine, antibiotics, infliximab, adalimumab
- Surgical
	- Surgeon can remove damaged part of digestive tract and connect healthy parts
	- AVOID surgery cuz healing can trigger attacks
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- ↑ risk of [[colonic adenocarcinoma]] but less than UC
