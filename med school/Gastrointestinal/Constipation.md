*↓ in bowel movements*

### Etiology
- ↓ fiber diet, dehydration, immobility
- Metabolic disorders
- Medications (opioids, anticholinergics, CCBs)
- Structural lesions
- Colon cancer

### Pathophysiology
- Could be from alterations of motility
- Could be changes in secretion and absorption in colon

### Clinical Features
- Abdominal discomfort

**RULE OUT**
- Rectal bleeding
- Weight loss
- Anemia
- Colon cancer history
- Onset past 50 yrs

### Diagnosis
- Colonoscopy if any of the RULE OUT conditions

### Treatment
1st: lifestyle changes
- ↑ fiber in diet, hydration, exercise

Bulk-forming agents: [[psyllium]], [[methylcellulose]] -> similar to fiber
Osmotic agents: PEG 3350, lactulose, magnesium salts, glycerin -> draw water into stool
Stool softeners: [[docusate]] -> ↓ surface tension
Laxatives: short term relieve only
Enemas
 [[lubiprostone]] -> ↑ Cl- 

**CASE**
- [[Opioids|opioid]] induced: Stimulant + stool softener OR [[methylnaltrexone]]
- Pregnancy or post MI: [[docusate]] to reduce straining
