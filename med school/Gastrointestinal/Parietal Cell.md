*Produces HCl and [[intrinsic factor]]*

- Neck + deeper parts of gastric glands
- Large cells (round / pyramidal) w 1-2 round nuclei
- ↑↑ eosinophilic cytoplasm because ↑ mitochondria
	- Drive the apical H + , K+ -ATPase
	- Cells have carbonic anhydrase that catalyzes CO2 + H 2 O -> H + + HCO3
	- K+ channels and Cl- channels to supply for pump and HCl secretion

#### Agonists
On basolateral membrane
- [[Parasympathetic Nervous System|parasympathetic]] -- [[Ach]]
	- ↑ IP3 and Ca2+
- [[Histamine]]
	- ↑ cAMP
- [[gastrin]] 
	- ↑ IP3 and Ca2+
