### Etiology
- Diets high in carbohydrates with little micronutrient content

### Pathophysiology
- [[Vitamin B1|thiamine]] deficiency

### Clinical Features
- Wet:
	- Adults
	- Edema, tachycardia, cardiomegaly
	- [[Congestive Heart Failure|CHF]]
	- Death from heart failure
- Dry:
	- Adults
	- Peripheral neuropathy
	- Bilat. loss of sensory and motor conduction
	- Severe weight loss

- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Parental [[Vitamin B1|thiamine]]
