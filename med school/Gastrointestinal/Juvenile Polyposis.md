### Etiology
- Rectum of kids < 5 yrs
- SMAD4 and BMPR1A mutation
- Autosomal dominant

### Pathophysiology
- pedunculated, smooth-surfaced, reddish lesions with characteristic cystic spaces
- Dilated glands with normal colonic epithelial lining filled with mucin

![[Screenshot 2026-09-30 at 3.34.00 PM.png]]
### Clinical Features
- Rectal bleeding
- Intussusception, obstruction, or prolapse
- Pulmonary AV malformation
- Clubbing

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Colectomy to stop bleeding

### Complications
- Risk of gastric, small intestine, colonic, and pancreatic adenocarcinoma
