### Etiology
- Autosomal recessive -> mutation causing misfolding of a1AT -> loss of function

### Pathophysiology
- $\alpha1AT$ synthesized by [[hepatocytes]] to inhibit proteases, mainly neutrophil elastase
	- Deficiency means ↑↑ [[Neutrophils|neutrophil]] mediated damage to elastic fibers in lungs
- Liver disease from misfolded accumulates in liver

- Histology: round accumulations PAS positive in hepatocytes

### Clinical Features
- Adolescent hepatitis or cirrhosis
- [[Emphysema]] at young age (panacinar)
