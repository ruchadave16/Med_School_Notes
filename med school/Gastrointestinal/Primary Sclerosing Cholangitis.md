### Etiology
- [[IBD]] in 70% of patients co-exists
- Male > F, 30s to 50s

### Pathophysiology
- Inflammation and obliterative fibrosis of intra- AND extrahepatic bile ducts
	- The ones left dilate
- Large duct inflammation shows neutrophilic infiltrates
- Small shows onion skin like fibrosis
### Clinical Features
- Fatigue
- Jaundice
- Pruritus

### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- ↑ alkaline phosphate
	- 75% of patients have antismooth muscle antibodies and antinuclear antibodies (p-ANCA)
- Imaging
	- "Beading" appearance of biliary tree 
	
	-![[Screenshot 2026-10-12 at 9.36.23 PM.png]]
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- 10-12 yr survival without transplant
- [[cholangiocarcinoma]] progression
