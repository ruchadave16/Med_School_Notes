### Etiology
- [[Smoking]]
- [[Chronic Pancreatitis]]
- Visceral obesity and high body mass index
- [[Diabetes]]
- 80% > 60 yrs

- [[KRAS]] mutation in >90%
- [[TP53]] in 70-75%

### Pathophysiology
- Precursor lesions
	- Cystic lesions
	- Pancreatic intraepithelial neoplasm (PanIN)
	
![[Screenshot 2026-10-13 at 9.57.36 PM.png]]

- Hard, stellate, gray-white, poorly defined masses 
- Desmoplastic response -> deposition of dense collagen
- Grow along nerves and invade into blood vessels and the retroperitoneum
### Clinical Features
- Silent until obstruction
	- By this time its beyond cure and metastatic
- Pain radiating to back
- Weight loss
- Jaundice
- Courvoisier Sign = palpable, non-tender gallbladder
- Trousseau Syndrome = Migratory thrombophlebitis with redness and tenderness on palpation of extremities

### Diagnosis
- Associated with CA19-9 tumor marker 

### Treatment
- Whipple procedure
	- Pancreaticoduodenectomy
	- 

### Complications
- 5-year survival rate is a dismal 10%
