*Diverticula (sacs) in intestinal wall (mainly sigmoid colon) presence*

### Etiology
- Risk factors
	- Low-fiber diet → constipation
	- [[Obesity]]
	- Constipation

### Pathophysiology
- Continuous pressure in digestive track with straining during bowel movements
- False diverticuli = Blind pouch composed of only mucosa and submucosa
	- Common in sigmoid colon
	- Colon does not have a fully circumferential longitudinal layer of the muscularis externa -> tinea coli

### Clinical Features
- Symptoms
	- Constipation
- General asymptomatic
- Converts to [[diverticulitis]]
