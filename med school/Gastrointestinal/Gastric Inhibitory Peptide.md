*Released by K cells in duodenum and jejunum*

#### Actions
- Reduces gastric churning (mobility)
- Increases [[insulin]] release at physiological values
- Inhibits gastric acid secretion

#### Stimuli
- ↑ by glucose and fat in [[duodenum]]
