### Etiology
- 20% of population 
- Risk
	- Age -> 40s, older = higher risk
	- W > M by 2x
	- Hereditary
	- Estrogens increases uptake of cholesterol
	- Gallbladder hypomobility

### Pathophysiology
- Cholesterol stones -> 80%, contain crystalline cholesterol monohydrate
	- ↑ if hypomobility of gallbladder
	- NOT visible on x-ray
- Pigment stones = 20%, made of bilirubin calcium salts
	- Visible on x-ray

### Clinical Features
- 80% have no symptoms
- RUQ pain after fatty meal
	- Can radiate to R shoulder
