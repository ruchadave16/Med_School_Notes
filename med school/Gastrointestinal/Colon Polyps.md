### Non-Neoplastic
- Hamartomatous polyps -- Solitary, no risk of transformation
	- Associated with [[juvenile polyposis]] and [[Peutz-Jeghers Syndrome]]
- Hyperplastic -- Small, most common, can become serrated
- Inflammatory pseudopolyps -- [[IBD]] erosion
- Mucosal -- < 5 mm, similar to mucosa, no importance
- Submucosal

### Potentially Malignant
- Adenomatous -- Neoplastic through APC -> KRAS pathway of [[Familial Adenomatous Polypsis|FAP]]
	- Tubular is less malignant than villous 
	- Asymptomatic
- Serrated -- CpG island methylator phenotype
	- Can silence mismatch repair gene (MHL1)
	- Can cause microsatellite instability and BRAF mutations
	- "Saw-tooth pattern" of crypts -> [[Hereditary Non-Polypsis Colorectal Cancer|HNPCC]]

These have epithelial dysplasia
