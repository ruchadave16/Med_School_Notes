### Etiology
- Insulin resistance
- [[Metabolic syndrome]]
	- [[Diabetes Mellitus]]
	- Obesity
	- [[Hyperlipidemia|Dyslipidemia]]
	- [[Hypertension|HTN]]
- Most COMMON cause of ↑ serum transaminases

### Pathophysiology
- Steatosis -- intracellular
- Steatohepatitis -- hepatocyte ballooning
	- Mallory hyaline and neutrophil infiltrates (tangled skeins of intermediate filaments visible as eosinophilic cytoplasmic inclusions)
- Fibrosis -> micronodular cirrhosis
