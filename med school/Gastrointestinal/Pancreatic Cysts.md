### Non-Neoplastic
- Congenital
	- lined by simple cuboidal epithelium
	- associated with [[Autosomal Dominant Polycystic Kidney Disease]] and [[von Hippel Lindau]]
- Pseudocysts
	- [[Pancreatitis|acute pancreatitis]]
### Neoplastic
- Serous Cystic Neoplasm
	- Benign cysts lined by glycogen-rich cuboidal cells, and contain clear, thin, straw-colored fluid
	- Not precancerous
	- More common in tail of pancreas
- Mucinous Cystic Neoplasm
	- **Precancerous**
	- columnar mucin-producing epithelium associated with a dense stroma similar to ovarian stroma
	- 95% in women
- Intraductal Papillary Mucinous Neoplasm (IPMN)
	- Precancerous mucin-producing neoplasms that involve the larger ducts of the pancreas
	- More common in men
	- KRAS mutation in 80% and loss-of-function RNF43 mutations in up to 50%
