### Etiology
- Most common GI malignancy -> 10% of cancer deaths worldwide, 15% in US
- Peak incidence at 60-70 years
- ↓ fiber, smoking, [[IBD]], moderate to high alcohol risk

### Pathophysiology
- APC pathway -> [[Familial Adenomatous Polypsis|FAP]]
- Microsatellite instability from DNA mismatch repair errors ([[Hereditary Non-Polypsis Colorectal Cancer]])
- K-RAS and p53 mutations
- SMAD mutations 
- Telomerase re-activation 

![[Screenshot 2026-09-30 at 3.45.53 PM.png]]

- Distributed across whole colon 
	- Distal are generally annular with luminal narrowing
	- Proximal commonly turn into polypoid masses along one wall of colon
### Clinical Features
- Fatigue -> [[Iron Deficiency Anemia]]

### Complications
- Most common metastasis to liver, lung, bone
- 5 yr survival = 65%
