	- Outer circular in stomach
	- submucosal in colon

#### Migrating Motor Complex
- During fasting, cycles of activity go from stomach -> distal ileum
- Quiescent period (phase I), irregular electrical and mechanical activity (phase II), and burst of regular activity (phase III)
- Initiated by [[Motilin]]
- ↑ secretion, bile flow, pancreatic secretion
