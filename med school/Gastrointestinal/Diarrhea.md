*↑ stool mass, frequency, or fluidity, > 200g daily*

- [[Dysentry]] = painful, bloody diarrhea
- 
### Causes
1) Osmotic Diarrhea
	- "Stool volume ↓↓ when fasting" and ↑ osmotic gap
	- Meds: [[antacids]], [[lactulose]], [[sorbitol]]
	- [[Lactose intolerance]]
	- [[Magnesium]], [[laxatives]]
2) Secretory Diarrhea
	- ↑↑ volume (> 1L daily), no change with fasting, no osmotic gap
	- [[VIPoma]], carcinoid, medullary carcinoma of thyroid (calcitonin), [[Zollinger-Ellison Syndrome]] (gastrin)
	- [[Phenolphthalein]], cascara, senna
	- [[Villous adenoma]]
	- Bile salt malabsorption
	- Medications
3) Inflammatory
	- Fever, abdominal pain, hematochezia
	- [[Ulcerative Colitis]]
	- [[Crohn's Disease|crohn disease]]
	- [[Microscopic colitis]]
	- Lymphoma, adenocarcinoma
	- [[Radiation enteritis]]
4) Medications
	- [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors|SSRIs]], [[Cholinesterase Inhibitors]], [[NSAIDs]], [[PPIs]], [[ARBs|Angiotensin II Receptor Blockers]], [[Metformin]], [[Allopurinol]]
5) Malabsorption Syndromes
	- Weight loss, abnormal labs, fecal fat > 10g/24h
	- Small bowel mucosal disorder: [[celiac disease]], tropical sprue, [[Whipple disease]], [[Eosinophilic gastroenteritis]], [[Short bowel syndrome]], [[Crohn's Disease|crohn disease]]
	- Lymphatic obstruction: lymphoma, carcinoid, TB, MAI, [[Kaposi Sarcoma]], [[Sarcoidosis]], [[retroperitoneal fibrosis]]
	- Pancreatic: [[chronic pancreatitis]], [[pancreatic carcinoma]]
	- Bacterial overgrowth
	- Mobility issues
	- Diverticula
6) Motility Issues
	- Systemic disorder or prior surgery
	- Surgery: [[Vagotomy]], [[Gastrectomy]]
	- [[Scleroderma]], [[Diabetes Mellitus]], [[Hyperthyroidism]]
	- [[IBS]]
7) Chronic Infections
	- *Treatment = rehydration first, [[Azithromycin]] if severe*
	- [[Giardia]], [[Entamoeba]]
	- AIDS: [[Cytomegalovirus|CMV]]
	- [[C. Diff]], [[MAC]]
	- [[Cryptosporidium]]

### Treatments
- [[Loperamide]] can reduce diarrhea
	- **CONTRAINDICATED in bloody stools or fever**
- [[Bismuth]](peptobismol)
