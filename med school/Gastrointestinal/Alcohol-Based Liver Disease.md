### Etiology
- >60% of liver disease in western World
- Women > susceptibility
### Pathophysiology
- Short term ingestion of ethanol produces mild reversible changes, such as steatosis
- Generation of [[acetaldehyde]] is maximal in the centrilobular region, this region is most susceptible to alcohol-induced injury

### Clinical Features
- Symptoms
- Signs
- Variants

### Diagnosis
- AST > ALT in ratio 2:1

### Treatment
- With complete abstinence, at least partial regression of the fibrosis may occur.

### Complications
- 8-20% = cirrhosis
