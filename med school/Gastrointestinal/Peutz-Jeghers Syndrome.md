### Etiology
- Age = 10-15 yrs
- STK11 mutation
- [[Autosomal dominant]]

### Pathophysiology
- Polyps in small intestine, can also be stomach / colon
- Large, pedunculated with a lobulated contour
- Arborizing network of connective tissue lined by normal-appearing intestinal epithelium
- Smooth muscle admixed with lamina propria

![[Screenshot 2026-09-30 at 3.32.27 PM.png]]
### Clinical Features
- Arborizing polyps
- Small intestine > colon > stomach
- Skin macules
- Mucocutaneous hyperpigmentation

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- ↑ risk of thyroid, breast, lung, pancreas, gonadal, and bladder cancers (40% lifetime risk)
