### Etiology
- [[Gallstones]]
- [[Alcohol]]
- Scorpion stings
	- VERY low chance its idiopathic, trauma, steroids, mumps, autoimmune, Hypercalcemia/Hypertriglyceridemia (> 1000 mg/dL), ERCP, drugs
- ↑ risk for [[cystic fibrosis]]

### Pathophysiology
- Reversible pancreatic parenchymal injury and inflammation leading to auto-digestion of the organ by pancreatic enzymes
- Inappropriate release and activation of pancreatic enzymes -> destroys pancreatic tissue
- Duct obstruction
	- ↑ pressure means enzyme rich fluid builds up
- Alcohol
	- Related to direct acinar injury or secretion of protein-rich pancreatic fluid

![[Screenshot 2026-10-13 at 9.21.38 PM.png]]

- Microvascular leak and edema
- [[Fat necrosis]] -> saponification
- Acute inflammation (neutrophils)
### Clinical Features
- 2 of 3:
	- Acute epigastric pain often radiating to the back
	- Serum amylase or lipase > 3x upper limit
	- Imging findings

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- FLUIDS -> IV hydration
- Pain control
	- [[Opioids]]
	- Avoid morphine since it can increase the pressure of the sphincter
- NO oral intake, supplement nutrition

### Complications
- Pancreatic pseudocyst
- Infection
- Organ failure
- Hypocalcemia -> saponification uses Ca
