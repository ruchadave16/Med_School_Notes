### Etiology
- Second most common primary malignancy of the [[liver]] after [[Hepatocellular Carcinoma|HCC]]
- [[Liver fluke]] infestation 
- Chronic inflammation of bile ducts -> [[Primary Sclerosing Cholangitis]]
### Pathophysiology
- Tumor arises from intra- and extra-hepatic bile ducts
- Infiltrating neoplastic ducts associated with a desmoplastic stroma
