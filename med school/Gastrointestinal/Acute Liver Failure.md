### Etiology
- [[Acetaminophen]] = 50% cases in US

### Pathophysiology
- Hepatic necrosis with minimal scarring
	- Drug induced is perivenular -> centrilobar
	![[Screenshot 2026-10-12 at 12.20.12 AM.png]]

### Clinical Features
- Jaundice -- bilirubin retention
- Nausea and vomiting
- Coagulopathy -- liver makes coag factors that decline
- [[Hepatic Portal Hypertension|Portal hypertension]] -- less common
- [[Hepatorenal syndrome]]

### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- ↑ serum transaminase levels
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- [[Hepatic Encephalopathy]] within 6 months -> ↑ ammonia
