### Pathophysiology
- ↓ intestinal blood flow from small vessel occlusion or hypoperfusion
- Watershed zones are most vulnerable, esp: Splenic flexure between superior and inferior mesenteric artery circulations

### Clinical Features
- Acute severe pain -- much worse than physical findings
- Bloody diarrhea or gross melena -- red current jelly stools
- Abdominal rigidity
- Vomiting / nausea
