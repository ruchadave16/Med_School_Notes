### Etiology
- 1/3 of cases of neonatal cholestasis
- **MOST COMMON cause of death from liver disease**

### Pathophysiology
- Complete or partial obstruction of the extrahepatic biliary tree within first 3 months
	- Perinatal form: biliary tree develops normally but is injured 
	- Fetal: other anomalies
- Fibrosis stricture of hepatic or common bile ducts
