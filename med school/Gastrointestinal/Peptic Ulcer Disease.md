### Etiology
- [[H. Pylori]] -- 70% cases
- [[Aspirin]] and other [[NSAIDs|NSAID]]
- [[Zollinger-Ellison Syndrome]]

### Pathophysiology
- GI and duodenal ulceration from breakdown of barrier
	- HYPERacidity
	- Ulcers are round and sharply punched out
- Most common in antrum of stomach and first part of duodenum

![[Screenshot 2026-09-29 at 11.57.34 AM.png]]
### Clinical Features
- Epigastric burning 
- 3-5 hours AFTER last meal
	- Relieved by eating in duodenal ulcers
	- Worsens by eating in gastric ulcers
- Bleeding -- melena or hematemesis
### Diagnosis
- Biopsy to rule out malignancy

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
	- [[PPIs|Omeprazole]]
	- [[H2 Blockers|H2 antagonists]]
	- Antibiotics for H. pylori
	- [[Misoprostol]]
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- Gastric ulcers associated with [[gastric adenocarcinoma]]
