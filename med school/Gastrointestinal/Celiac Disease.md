*Immune reaction to eating gluten in [[small intestine]]*
### Etiology
- Association: HLA-DQ2, HLA-DQ8 haplotypes and northern European descent

### Pathophysiology
- Gluten-mediated, delayed-type hypersensitivity -> against 33 AA α-gliadin polypeptide resistant to digestive enzymes
- Gliadin binds to MHC -> CD4 activation -> epithelial damage
- Eventually damage to small intestine lining preventing it from absorbing nutrients
	- Chronic inflammation of lamina propria
- Reduced capacity because of lack of villi and microvilli

### Clinical Features
- Diarrhea
- Flatulance
- Fatigue
- Weight loss
- Bloating
- Anemia
- Aphthous ulcers
- 10% have [[dermatitis herpetiformis]]
### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- ⊕ IgA anti-tissue transglutaminase(IgA tTG) 
	- Anti-endomysial, and anti-deamidated gliadin peptide antibodies
	- Biopsy specimen with complete loss of villi (flat mucosal surface)
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Avoid gluten

### Complications
- [[Malnutrition]]
- ↑ risk of T cell lymphoma and [[small intestinal adenocarcinoma]]
