### Pathophysiology
- Secondary bacterial infection of biliary tree, commonly from obstruction
- Influx of periductular [[neutrophils]] into the bile duct epithelium and lumen

### Clinical Features
- Charcot’s Triad
	- Fever
	- Jaundice
	- RUQ pain
- Often chills too
- Charcot’s triad + sepsis and altered mental state = Reynolds’ pentad
