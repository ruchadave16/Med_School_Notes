*Substance that gives bile yellow-green color*

Byproduct of [[hemoglobin metabolism]] by [[reticuloendothelial system]] where RBCs are broken down

- Produced by [[spleen]]
- Free is hydrophobic, attaches to [[albumin]] to travel to [[liver]] (unconjugated)
	- Liver removes bilirubin from albumin and conjugates it by **uridine diphosphate (UDP) glucuronyl transferase**
- Excreted by liver in [[bile]]

#### Bilirubin Metabolism
Some converted to others that make color of feces

- Bilirubin binds to [[albumin]] -> unconjugated
- Taken up by hepatocytes
- Conjugated by UDP-glucuronosyltransferase, secreted in [[bile]]
- Deconjugated in gut -> converted to [[urobilinogen]] by gut bacteria 
	- 20% reabsorbed and taken back to liver -> bile
