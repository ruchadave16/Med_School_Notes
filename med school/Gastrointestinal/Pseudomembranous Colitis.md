### Etiology
- [[C. Diff]]
- 30% hospitalized patients can be colonized
- Frequency of antibiotics 
	- [[PPIs]]

### Pathophysiology
- Adherent inflammatory pseudomembranes over injury sites
- Toxins cause ribosylation of GTPases -> cytoskeletal disruption 
### Clinical Features
- Fever
- Leukocytosis
- Watery diarrhea
- Abdominal pain

### Diagnosis
- Biopsy shows plaque-like adhesion of yellow gray mucus, necrotic 

### Treatment
- Oral [[Vancomycin]]
- [[Metronidazole]]
- Fecal microbial transplantation to prevent re-infection
### Complications
- [[Toxic megacolon]]
- 40% recurrence
