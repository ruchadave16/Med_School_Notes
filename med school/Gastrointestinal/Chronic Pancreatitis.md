### Etiology
- [[Alcoholism]]
- Long-standing obstruction of the pancreatic duct by calculi or neoplasms
- Autoimmune
- Genetic predisposition (eg, cystic fibrosis, SPINK1 mutations)

### Pathophysiology
- **irreversible** destruction of exocrine parenchyma, fibrosis, and, in the late stages, loss of endocrine parenchyma
- Autoimmune pancreatitis
	- Type 1: Immunoglobulin G4 -> secreting [[plasma cells]] in pancreas, systemic 
	- Type 2: restricted to pancreas except for [[Ulcerative Colitis]] patients
- Acinar cell dropout → pancreatic insufficiency →
### Clinical Features
- During an attack there may be mild fever and mild-to-moderate elevations of serum amylase
- Steatorrhea
- Fat-soluble vitamin insufficiency
- [[Diabetes Mellitus|Diabetes mellitus]] 
- Weight loss
- Edema

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
	- Calcifications within the pancreas by CT and ultrasonography
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Cessation of alcohol and tobacco
- Low fat diet, high protein, small meals
- Enzyme replacement
- Pain control
- [[Synthetic Corticosteroids|Steroids]] -> autoimmune

### Complications
- 20- to 25-year mortality rate of 50%
- Pancreatic exocrine insufficiency, chronic malabsorption, and diabetes mellitus
- Chronic pain
- Pancreatic pseudocysts develop in about 10% of patients
- [[Pancreatic cancer]]
