*Coordinated contraction behind object and relaxation ahead of*

1) Primary: begins with swallowing, innervated by brainstem->vagal

2) Secondary: triggered by esophageal distention
#### Innervation
- Local stretch -> [[Serotonin]] that activates sensory neurons
- Cholinergic in retrogade cause release of substance P and Ach -> contraction behind
- Anterograde -> [[Nitric Oxide]] and [[VIP]] for relaxation ahead
