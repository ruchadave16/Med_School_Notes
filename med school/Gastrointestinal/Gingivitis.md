### Etiology
- Common from bad oral hygiene

### Pathophysiology
- Inflammation of squamous mucosa and soft tissue around teeth
- Buildup of plaque that becomes mineralized as tartar

### Clinical Features
- Erythema, edema, and bleeding

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Reverse with brushing and flossing
