### Etiology
- <10% of [[chronic gastritis]] cases
- Diagnosis at ~60 yrs

### Pathophysiology
- SPARES antrum 
- Auto-antibodies to parietal cells and intrinsic factor
- ↓ acid secretion
- [[B12 Deficiency]]
- Lymphocyte and magrophage infiltrate

### Clinical Features
