### Etiology
- Most common cancer of stomach -- 90%
- Intestinal:
	- [[H. Pylori]]
	- Tobacco
	- Nitrosamines
	- [[EBV]] = risk factor

### Pathophysiology
- Intestinal type:
	- Discrete mass / ulcer with malignant glands, raised margins
	- Type A blood type increases risk 
- Diffuse type
	- Signet ring cells 
	- Infiltrative growth
	- “leather bottle” appearance to the stomach termed linitis plastica

### Complications
- Virchow node: L supraclavicular LN involvement from metastasis
- Krukenberg tumor: metastasis to ovaries bilat. from diffuse type
- Sister Mary Joseph nodule: metastasis to subcutaneous periumbilical region
- Blumer shelf: palpable mass on rectal exam -- metastasis to [[pouch of Douglas]]
