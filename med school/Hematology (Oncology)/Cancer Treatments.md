### Drug Classes
- Antimetabolites -- mimic molecules normally used in the creation of DNA and interfere with normal ability to replicate
	- Act at S phase
	- Folic acid analogs: [[Methotrexate]]
	- Purine Analogs: [[6-MP]], [[Azathioprine]]
	- Pyrimidine analogs: [[5-FU]], [[Leucovorin]], [[Cytarabine]]
- Topoisomerase inhibitors
	- [[Camptothecin Derivatives]]: Irinotecan & Topotecan
	- [[Doxorubicin]]
	- [[Etoposide]]
- Microtubule inhibitors
	- Act at [[Mitosis]] stage
	- [[Vinca Alkaloids]]
	- [[Paclitaxel]]
- [[Alkylating Agents]] -- DNA cross-linkage
- Platinum agents -- DNA cross-linkage
	- Cell cycle–independent
	- Nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity, peripheral neuropathy
- Anthracyclines
- [[Hydroxyurea]]

#### Supportive

- [[Dexamethasone]] -- antiemetic regimens
- [[Diphenhydramine]] -- H1-antihistamine prevention or treatment of mild allergic/infusion reactions
