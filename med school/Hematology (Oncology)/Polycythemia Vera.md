### Etiology
- JAK2 mutations
### Pathophysiology
- ↑ production of red cells, granulocytes, and platelets (red cells -> symptoms)
	- ↑ viscosity 
- Hypercellular marrow
- Peripheral blood contains increased numbers of basophils and abnormally large platelets

### Clinical Features
- Thrombosis and hemorrhage risk
	- [[Deep Vein Thrombosis|DVT]], [[hepatic vein thrombosis]], MI, stroke
- Ruddy complexion
- Headache, dizziness, hypertension, or GI complaints
- Pruritis (itchy skin)

### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- ↓ [[Erythropoietin|EPO]]
	- Hemoglobin concentration is typically > 16 g/dL
	- Platelets > 500,000
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Phlebotomy to maintain hematocrit can extend life past 10 yrs
### Complications
- Marrow fibrosis (after 10 yrs)
