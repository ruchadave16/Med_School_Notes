### Etiology
- **Most common bleeding disorder in humans**

### Pathophysiology
- [[vWF]] normally interacts with GpIb to allow adhesion of platelet to subendothelial collagen
- Its made by both [[Megakaryocytes]] and endothelial cells and secreted into circulation
- Binds to Factor VIII

**Categories**
- Type I: mild - moderate (70%)
- Type II: qualitative defects in vWF
- Type III: severe, prolonged PTT, ↑↑ bleeding
### Clinical Features
- Symptoms
- Signs
- Variants

### Diagnosis
- Ristocetin cofactor activity measures plasma levels of active vWF
- Platelet count **normal** but function low so PTT down

### Treatment
Type I/II
- [[Desmopressin]]
- Plasma infusions
- Recombinant vWF

Type III
