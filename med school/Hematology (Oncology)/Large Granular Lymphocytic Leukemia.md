### Etiology
- Mutation in STAT3

### Pathophysiology
- T cell and NK cell variants
- T-cell variants express CD3, NK = CD56
- Large cells with abundant blue cytoplasm and course azurophilic granules

### Clinical Features
- Mild to moderate lymphocytosis
- Splenomegaly
- Neutropenia and anemia
- [[Felty syndrome]]: rheumatoid arthritis, splenomegaly, and neutropenia
