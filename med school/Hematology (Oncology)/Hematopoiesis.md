*production of [[blood]] by differentiation of multipotent cells into myeloid and lymphoid*

#### Embryology
Embryonic [[Yolk Sac]] (3-8 weeks) -> fetal [[Liver]] (6 weeks -> birth) + spleen (10-28 weeks) +  [[bone marrow]] (18 weeks - adult)

**Infancy**
- All bones have active marrow early on
- Liver = minimal to no hematopoeisis (abnormal if present)

**Adulthood**
- Restricted to axial skeleton + localized foci in long bones
- Pathologically can have [[extramedullary hematopoiesis]]
#### Method
Depends on [[stem cells]] (pluripotent) -> myeloid or lymphoid
