### Etiology
- Associated with [[EBV]]
- Rare in the U.S., but more common in Asia
### Pathophysiology
- Destructive nasopharyngeal mass with necrosis
- CD3- and lack T-cell

### Clinical Features
- Present with a nasal mass or epistaxis
