*Newly formed [[Erythrocytes|RBC]]*
- Larger than mature RBCs
- Slightly basophilic -> ↑ cytoplasmic ribosomes (RNA)

High amount is not normal (↑ # of reticulocytes = ↑ marrow [[erythropoiesis]])
- **Normal = 1%**

![[Screenshot 2026-07-08 at 9.07.14 AM.png]]

#### [[RPI]] (Reticulocyte Production Index)
- Given as % of total RBCs -- Correct based on baseline RBC (can be falsely elevated)

![[Screenshot 2026-07-08 at 9.10.14 AM.png]]

- Anemic pt with low RPI (<2%): inappropriate marrow response to anemia
	- Problem with marrow production of RBCs
- Anemic pt with high RPI (>3%): appropriate marrow response to anemia
