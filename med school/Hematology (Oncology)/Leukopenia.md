*↓ total [[Leukocytes|WBCs]]*
- ↓ [[neutrophils]] or less commonly [[lymphopenia]]
	- [[Agranulocytosis]] = ↓↓ neutrophils
#### Neutropenia
- Inadequate or ineffective granulopoiesis
	- [[Aplastic anemia]]
	- Marrow infiltration (e.g., metastatic cancer, granulomatous disease)
	- Drug effect **Most common cause**
	- [[Megaloblastic anemia]]
	- Myelodysplastic syndromes
- Increased destruction or sequestration of neutrophils within the circulation
	- Immune-mediated neutrophil injury
	- Splenomegaly (sequestration and destruction of neutrophils)
	- Increased peripheral utilization
