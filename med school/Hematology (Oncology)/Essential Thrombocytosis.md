### Etiology
- Mutation of JAK2 (activating) in 50-60% cases
- >60 yrs age

### Pathophysiology
- Proliferation of [[Megakaryocytes]]
- NO polycythemia

### Clinical Features
- ↑ risk of bleeding and thrombosis
- Erythromelalgia -- pain in hands and feet from occlusions

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Gentle chemotherapy 

### Complications
- Prognosis great (20+ years)

### High-Yield Associations
- Classic buzzwords
- Boards clues
