### Pathophysiology
- High grade prostatic intraepithelial neoplasia = precursor
- Normal prostate: glandular cells with layer of basal cells in periphery
- KEY: Loss of basal cells, smaller glands

**Gleason Grading System**
*Calculate by determining most common pattern (major and minor) and summing*
- Pattern 3: small, round glands
- Pattern 4: cribriform glands (Swiss cheese)
- Pattern 5: individual cells
![[Screenshot 2026-07-22 at 4.57.49 PM.png]]

Gleason Score 6 = 3 + 3 -- most common form of [[pancreatic adenocarcinoma]]
Gleason Score 7 = 4 + 3 -- intermediate biological behavior
Gleason Score 8, 9, 10 -- biologically aggressive
