### Etiology
- Most common lower GI malignancy
	- 300,000 new cases/year
- 2nd leading cause of cancer related death in men
- Age: 67 yrs median
- 70% higher chance in black men than white
- Father or brother with CaP >2x risk
- Family history of breast cancer

### Pathophysiology
- High grade prostatic intraepithelial neoplasia = precursor (HGPIN)
	- Prominent nucleoli, no invasion
- Normal prostate: glandular cells with layer of basal cells in periphery
- KEY: Loss of basal cells, smaller glands

**Gleason Grading System**
*Calculate by determining most common pattern (major and minor) and summing*
- Pattern 3: small, round glands
- Pattern 4: cribriform glands (Swiss cheese)
- Pattern 5: individual cells
![[Screenshot 2026-07-22 at 4.57.49 PM.png]]

Gleason Score 6 = 3 + 3 -- most common form of [[pancreatic adenocarcinoma]]
Gleason Score 7 = 4 + 3 -- intermediate biological behavior
Gleason Score 8, 9, 10 -- biologically aggressive

**Differential:**
- Malignancies are at the peripheral zone while [[BPH]] is at the transitional zone

### Diagnosis
- SCREENING men 55-69 years
	- Digital rectal exam
		- Nodules – firm and hard
		- [[BPH]] feels like thenar eminence
		- Asymmetry
		- Size
	- PSA test: Normal = <1ng/mL per year and the PSA trend has greatest diagnostic value, trend has most value
		- NOTE: low specificity
- Biopsy to make diagnosis
