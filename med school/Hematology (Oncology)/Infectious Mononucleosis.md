### Etiology
- Incidence = teenagers
- Person to person spread

### Pathophysiology
- [[EBV]] infection
- [[Lymphocytosis]]
- Production of heterophile antibodies

### Clinical Features
- Fever
- Sore throat
- Cervical lymphadenopathy

### Diagnosis
- Monospot test
- Peripheral blood smear = activated (reactive) lymphocytes
