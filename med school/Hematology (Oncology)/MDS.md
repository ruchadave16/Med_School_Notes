### Etiology
- Idiopathic -- older adults
- Secondary to drug or radiation therapy

### Pathophysiology
- ↑ cellularity in bone marrow but ↓ in peripheral blood
- Disordered (dysplastic) differentiation affecting erythroid, granulocytic, monocytic, and megakaryocytic lineages to varying degrees
	- Erythroid: ringed sideroblasts
	- Granulocytic: Pseudo-Pelger-Huet cells
	- Megakaryocytic: pawn ball megakaryocytes

### Clinical Features
- Weakness, infections, and hemorrhage

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Hematopoietic stem cell transplantation

### Complications
- Can transform to [[Acute Myeloid Leukemia|AML]]
