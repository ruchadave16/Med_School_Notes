### Pathophysiology
- Severity of disease depends on how many of the α globin genes are affected
	- Newborns, unpaired excess γ globin chains form γ4 tetramers
	- Older children / adults, unpaired excess β globin chains form β4 tetramers (HbH)
- Silent Carrier: single α globin gene deletion
- Two α globin genes deletion
	- a single chromosome (α/α, -/-) deletion more common in Asian populations
	- each chromosome (α/-, α/-) more common in regions of Africa
	- Microcytosis
- [[HgH disease]]
- [[Hydrops fetalis]]
