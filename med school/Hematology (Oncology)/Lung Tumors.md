- **#1 cause of mortality in US**
- Lung = **most common metastasis site**

![[Screenshot 2026-08-16 at 4.27.32 PM.png]]

Risk Factors
- [[Smoking]]
	- ~80% of lung cancers occur in current smokers or recently quit smokers
- Industrial Hazards
	- [[Asbestos]]
	- [[Arsenic]]
	- Chromium
	- Uranium
	- Nickel
	- Vinyl chloride
	- Mustard gas
	- [[Ionizing radiation]]
- Air Pollution
#### Neuroendocrine Features
**Positive for Synaptophysin and/or Chromogranin**

Growth patterns
- Organoid 
- Trabecular
- Rosette formation
- Nested
- Zellballen

Morphology
- Low grade = uniform with round nuclei and salt and pepper chromatin
	- < 2 mitosis per 2 mm2
- Intermediate = same but with necrosis
	- 2-10 mitosis per 2 mm2
- [[Small cell carcinoma]] = round blue cells minimal cytoplasm, molding, smudging
	- >10 mitosis per 2 mm2
- [[Large Cell Neuroendocrine Carcinoma]] = large cells, abundant cytoplasm, prominent nucleoli
	- >10 mitosis per 2mm2
	
![[Screenshot 2026-08-16 at 5.29.50 PM.png]]

#### Screening
Low-dose CT
- Have a 20 pack-year or more smoking history, **and**
- Smoke now or have quit within the past 15 years, **and**
- Are between 50 and 80 years old.

#### Immunotherapy
- Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors
- Adoptive Cellular Therapy
- Vaccines
- Monoclonal Antibodies
