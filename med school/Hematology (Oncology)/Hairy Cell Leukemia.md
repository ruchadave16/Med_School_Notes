### Etiology
- Point mutation in BRAF
- Middle aged white males

### Pathophysiology
- Cells with hairy cytoplasmic extensions
![[Screenshot 2026-07-23 at 11.07.14 PM.png]]
- B cell markers (CD10, CD20,CD79a)+, TRAP+ (tartrate resistant acid phosphatase), CD103, and annexin A1

### Clinical Features
- Splenomegaly
- Marrow fibrosis; pancytopenia

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Excellent response to 2-CDA ([[cladribine]]) and [[BRAF inhibitors]]

### Complications
- Excellent prognosis

- Classic buzzwords
