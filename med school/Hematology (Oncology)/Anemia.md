*Reduction in the oxygen-transporting capacity of the blood because of ↓ [[Erythrocytes|RBC]]*

**Causes**
- Hemorrhage
- ↑ RBC destruction (hemolysis)
- ↓ RBC production

**Mechanism**
- Generally: anemia -> ↑ [[Erythropoietin]] production by [[kidneys]] -> EPO causes hyperplasia of erythroid precursors in [[bone marrow]] -> ↑ [[reticulocytes]]

**General Labs**
- ↓ hemoglobin and hematocrit
- ↓ RBC count
- MCV: discriminator for normo-, micro- or macrocytic anemias
- MCHC: measure of appropriate hemoglobin synthesis
- RPI
- Kidney function
### Classification
#### Blood loss
- Acute (hypovolemia): trauma
- Chronic: GI lesions, gynecologic conditions
#### [[Hemolysis]]
*Normocytic and normochromatic*
Short RBC < 120 days
↑ EPO with increase in erythropoiesis w reticulocytosis (high RPI, >3)
Hemoglobin degradation products accumulation

- Intrinsic
	- Hereditary
		- Membrane abnormalities: [[Spherocytosis]], [[elliptocytosis]]
		- Enzyme deficiencies: [[G6PD deficiency]]
		- HgB synthesis issues: [[Sickle Cell Anemia]], [[Thalassemias]]
	- Acquired
		- Membrane defects: [[Paroxysmal Nocturnal Hemoglobinuria|PNH]]
- Extrinsic
	- Antibody mediated
		- Transfusion reactions
		- Immune hydrops
		- Drug associated
		- [[Systemic Lupus Erythematosus|Lupus]]
	- Mechanical trauma
		- Microangiopathic hemolytic anemias
		- Defective cardiac valves
	- Infections
		- [[Plasmodium|Malaria]]
#### Decreased Production
- Stem cell disorders
	- [[Aplastic anemia]]
	- [[Pure red cell aplasia]]
- Erythroblast disorders
	- Defective DNA synthesis
		- [[Megaloblastic anemia]]
	- Erythropoietin deficiency
		- [[Renal failure]]
	- Iron sequestration
		- Chronic disease
	- Defective HgB synthesis
		- Heme: [[iron deficiency anemia]], [[sideroblastic anemia]]
		- Globin: [[thalassemias]]
- Marrow diseases
	- Marrow replacement: hematopoietic neoplasms, myelodysplastic syndromes
	- Marrow infiltration: granulomas / metastasis

#### How to Categorize
*RPI = measure of marrow functionality*
- High RPI (>3) = hemolysis or blood loss
- Low RPI (<2) = ↓ RBC production

*Measure of RBC size; abn*
- MCV (80-100) = normal, anemia from blood loss or ↓ RBC production (hypoproliferative)
- <80 or >100 = anemia from ↓ RBC production (maturation disorders)

![[Screenshot 2026-07-09 at 2.58.01 PM.png]]

1) Hypoproliferative 
	- Low RPI (<2) with normal RBC morphology
	- [[CKD]]
2) Ineffective erythropoiesis 
	- Low RPI (<2) with abnormal RBC morphology 
3) ↓ RBC survival
	- High RPI (>3)
#### Treatments
- Red Cell Factors
	- [[Iron]]
	- Vitamins (Vitamin B₁₂ and folate)
	- [[Erythropoietin]] (EPO)
- Granulocyte Factors
	- [[G-CSF]] (Filgrastim)
	- [[GM-CSF]] (Sargramostim)
- Megakaryocyte Factors
