### Etiology
- Common > 65-70 years, M>F
- IGH locus on chromosome 14 rearrangements

### Pathophysiology
- Most common malignancy in bone
- Proliferation based on [[IL-6]] -- levels determine prognosis
- ↑↑ [[Plasma Cells]] (>30% cellularity)
	- Release factors that cause bone destruction (punched out defects)
	- Release extra light chains -- excreted in urine (cause renal damage)
- Cells + for **CD138**

### Clinical Features
- Tumor masses across skeletal system 

**CRAB**
- [[HyperCalcemia]]
- Renal failure
- [[Anemia]]
- Bone lesions
### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- ↑↑ M protein (a monoclonal Ig) in blood
	- RBCs stick together in one line because of this
	- Urine has extra light chains (Bence Jones proteins)
- 99% urine has ↑ Ig (55% = IgG)

### Complications
- **Infection** = most common cause of death
- Survival ~4-7 years
