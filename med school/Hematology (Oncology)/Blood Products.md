- Leukoreduction: Most blood products in the U.S. are leukoreduced to reduce chance of CMV, HLA alloimmunization, and [[FNHTR]]
- Washing: prevents allergic rxn especially if [[Selective IgA Deficiency]]
- Irradation: to prevent [[GVHD|graft versus host disease]]
#### Whole Blood
- RBCs, WBCs, platelets, plasma
- Treat significant blood loss
#### [[Erythrocytes|RBCs]]
- Treat anemia without increasing blood volume
- Treat blood disorders
#### [[Thrombocytes|Platelets]]
- Treat [[thrombocytopenia]] and functional platelet disorders
#### [[Plasma]]
- Trauma and severe burn and shock patients
- Severe liver disease
- Clotting factor deficiencies
#### Cryoprecipitate
- Plasma + clotting factors
- Hemophilia and [[von Willebrand Disease|vWD]]
#### White Cells & Granulocytes
- Rarely used for infections related to [[leukopenia]]
