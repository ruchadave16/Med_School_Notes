#### Agents
**Storage:** 
- [[ECL cells]] in lamina propria of stomach produce it
- [[Mast cells]] release it in response to [[IgE]]
- [[Basophils]] -- IgE
- [[Heparin]] granules
#### About It
- Formed by decarboxylation of L-histamine
- **Receptors**
	- H1
		- Smooth muscle, endothelium, CNS
		- Allergic responses, bronchoconstriction
	- H2 
		- [[Parietal Cell|Parietal cells]] -- [[gastric acid]] secretion
	- H3
		- Presynaptic neurons -- regulate release
	- H4
		- Eosinophils, mast cells, T cells for [[chemotaxis]]
#### Actions
- Draws [[eosinophils]] to site of action
- Binds to H2 receptors in parietal cells to increase HCl secretion
	- Blocked by [[cimetidine]]
	- Decreased by [[somatostatin]] and [[prostaglandins]]
	- Increased by [[gastrin]] and [[Ach]]
- Increases small intestine secretions
- Vasodilation (warmth)
	- ↓ BP
	- Reflex tachycardia
- ↑ permeability venules  -- [[edema]]
- Smooth cell contraction (bronchospasm)
