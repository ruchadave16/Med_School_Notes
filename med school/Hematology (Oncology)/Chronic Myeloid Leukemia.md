### Etiology
- Frequency: 4500 cases in US, 2400 deaths per year

### Pathophysiology
- 95% patients have Philadelphia Chromosome
	- Reciprocal translocation of Chromosome 9 and 22
	- Fuses ABL tyrosine kinase from Chr 9 to BCR gene on Chr 22
	- **BCR-ABL fusion protein made**
- This is an active (unregulated) ABL tyrosine kinase that causes ↑↑ cell division, stops DNA repair
	- Always on since BCR is always on promotor
	- Proliferation of granulocytic and megakaryocytic 
- Hypercellular bone marrow and ↑↑ macrophages with wrinkled cytoplasm (sea blue)

### Clinical Features
- Splenomegaly
- 50% stable for 3 years and then accelerated phase
	- Increasing anemia and thrombocytopenia
	- Then in 1 yr "blast crisis"
- 50% stable -> blast crisis

### Diagnosis
- Leukocytosis often exceeds 100,000 cells/mm3, to include neutrophils, band forms, eosinophils, and basophils

### Treatment
- BCR-ABL inhibitors -- [[imatinib]]

### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
