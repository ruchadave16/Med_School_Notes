### Etiology
- Rh- mother and Rh+ baby (2nd pregnancy)

### Pathophysiology
- Commonly due to anti-D antibodies
- Mother is exposed to fetal RBCs during first pregnancy (75% of all first pregnancies)

### Clinical Features
- Anemia
- Jaundice
- Hepatosplenomegaly
- Hydrops

### Prevention
- RhoGAM = Rh IG shot between 26-28 weeks pregancy and also within 72 hrs of birth to prevent Anti-D production
- Give in FIRST pregnancy
### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Phototherapy (jaundice)
- IV IG (to neutralize antibodies)
- Exchange transfusions

### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
