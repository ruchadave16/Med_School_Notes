### Etiology
- Causes
	- [[Systemic Lupus Erythematosus|lupus]]
	- [[HIV]]
	- B cell neoplasms
- Classically woman < 40

### Pathophysiology
- Auto-immune destruction of [[Thrombocytes|platelets]] through splenic macrophages

### Clinical Features
- Skin and mucosal bleeding, petechial hemorrhages
- Easy bruising, nosebleeds, or gingival bleeding

### Diagnosis
- Diagnosis of exclusion
### Treatment
- Splenectomy
- Steroids
- Immunomodulatory agents
- Romiplostim (Nplate): Small peptide that binds and activates the thrombopoietin receptor
- Eltrombopag

### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
