### Etiology
- t(14;18) translocation → overexpression of [[BCL-2]] → ignore apoptosis
- M = F, middle age

### Pathophysiology
- ↑ lymph node follicles of EVEN size and shape
- Germinal centers have NO apoptotic cells or macrophages
- Cells + CD10, CD19, CD20, **BCL2** and BCL6
- Cells are **negative for CD5**
### Clinical Features
- Waxing and waning lymphadenopathy
- Bone marrow involvement occurs in a majority of cases (85%) -- paratrabecular lymphoid **aggregates**

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Palliative therapy
- Low-dose chemotherapy or immunotherapy -- [[Rituximab]]
- BCL2 inhibitors are also used

### Complications
- Survival <1 year after conversion to [[Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma]]
