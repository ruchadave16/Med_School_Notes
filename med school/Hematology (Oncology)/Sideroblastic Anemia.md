### Etiology
- X linked or autosomal recessive
- Radiation therapy
- [[Vitamin B6]] deficiency
- [[Copper Deficiency]]
- [[Lead Poisoning]]
- [[Chloramphenicol]] or [[Isoniazid]]
- Alcohol excess
### Pathophysiology
- Ringed sideroblasts in the bone marrow -- abnormal erythroblasts with pathologic iron deposits distributed in a ring-like fashion around the nucleus
