### Etiology
- 85% are Children disease (2-5 yo)
- Risk: [[Down Syndrome]] and radiation exposure
- t(12;21) = good prognosis
- t(9;22) = poor prognosis

### Pathophysiology
- Precursor B cells
	- Normal cells squeezed out by neoplastic lymphoblasts
- TdT + B cell markers = CD10, CD19, PAX5
