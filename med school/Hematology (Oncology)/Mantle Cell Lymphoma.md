### Etiology
- ~50-60 y/o, M>F
- t(11;14) -- cyclin D1 gene -> Ig heavy chain
	- Overexpression of cyclin D1

### Pathophysiology
- Aggressive proliferation of naïve B cells that expand the mantle zone of a lymphoid follicle
- Cyclin D1, CD19, and CD20 +
- CD5 positive but CD23 negative
### Clinical Features
- Painless lymphadenopathy
- Lymphomatoid polyposis (intestinal polyps)

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- Aggressive, incurable
- Survival 8-10 years
