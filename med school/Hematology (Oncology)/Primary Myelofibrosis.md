### Etiology
- JAK2 activating mutations
- Age > 60

### Pathophysiology
- Obliterative marrow fibrosis -- non-neoplastic [[fibroblasts]] deposit collagen
- Hypocellular with atypical megakaryocytes (cloud like)
- Teardrop red cells

![[Screenshot 2026-07-24 at 6.51.10 PM.png]]
### Clinical Features
- Progressive anemia
- Splenomegaly

### Diagnosis
- Moderate to severe anemia with leukoerythroblastosis
	- WBC and platelets normal
- Bone marrow biopsy

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle

### Complications
- Survival 3-5 years

### High-Yield Associations
- Classic buzzwords
- Boards clues
