*Procedure to collect and examine [[Bone Marrow|bone marrow]]*

- Commonly done at [[iliac crest]]

![[Screenshot 2026-07-08 at 8.50.01 AM.png]]
#### Evaluation
- Cellularity (**Normal = 100 - age**)
	- Normocellular, hypocellular, hypercellular
- Trilineal development
	- RBC, WBC, Platelet
- Myeloid:erythroid
	- Myeloid = all [[Leukocytes|WBCs]]
	- Erythroid = [[Erythrocytes|RBCs]]
	- Normal = 3:1
