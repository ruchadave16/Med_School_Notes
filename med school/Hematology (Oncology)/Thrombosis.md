*Formation of blood clot ([[thrombus]])*

![[Drawing 2026-01-24 17.57.15.excalidraw]]
#### Endothelial Injury
- Causes [[Platelet Activation|platelet activation]]
- Common pathway for arterial clots (platelet rich)
- Key to avoiding is aspirin
#### Abnormal Blood Flow
- Turbulences cause endothelial injury → clot formation by mechanism above
- Form countercurrents that cause stasis
	- Major reason for venous clots
