*Auto-antibodies that bind to RBCs and destroy them*
#### Warm Antibody Type (IgG at 37C)
- 80% of cases
- Mostly extravascular, idiopathic
- Can also be from [[Systemic Lupus Erythematosus|lupus]], drugs, lymphoid neoplasms
- **spherocytes** seen in smear -> IgG cleared by macrophages so RBCs lose part of membrane
#### Cold Antibody Type (IgM at <37C)
- Agglutinated RBCs can cause obstruction
- Acute -- [[Infectious Mononucleosis|infectious mononucleosis]], [[Mycoplasma Pneumoniae]]
- Idiopathic too
#### Other Causes (Drugs)
-  [[Penicillins|Penicillin]], [[cephalosporins]], [[methyldopa]]
### Testing
**Direct Coombs Antiglobin Test (DAT)**
- Presence of antibodies on the surface of patient RBCs
- Mix RBCs with antibodies specific to human Ig or complement

**Indirect Coombs Antiglobin Test**
- Presence of antibodies in a patient’s serum
