### Etiology
- Mutation in [[mismatch repair]] gene, commonly MSH1 and MLH1
- Results in microsatellite instability

### Pathophysiology
- 1 defective copy inherited, 2nd = mutation

### Clinical Features
- Cancer develops generally on R side of colon
	- Endometrium
	- Stomach
	- Ovary
	- Ureters
	- Brain
	- Hepatobiliary tract
	- Pancreas
	- Skin
