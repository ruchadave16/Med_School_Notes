**Active Forms**
- Retinal
- Retinoic Acid
#### Sources
- Liver, kidney
- Butter, egg yolk, leafy vegetables
- **DESTROYED by heating**
#### Function
- Vision (retinal pigments)
- Antioxidant
- Gene transcription
- Cell differentiation and specialization

>[!Note] Can help with [[Measles Virus]]
#### Deficiency
- **Night blindness** (Nyctalopia)
- Xerosis cutis
- Squamous cell [[metaplasia]] 
- Xerophthalmia -> Bitot spots are foamy, keratinized lesions on conjunctiva
- Keratomalacia 
- Immunosuppression
#### Toxicity
- Acute: nausea, vomiting, blurry vision, dizziness
- Chronic: 
	- Alopecia (hair loss)
	- Arthalgias
	- Hepatotoxicity
	- **Pseudotumor cerebri**
- [[Teratogen]]
