Components of a CBC include:
- White blood cell (WBC) count
- Red blood cell (RBC) count
- Hemoglobin
- Hematocrit
- MCV 
- MCH
- MCHC
- RDW
- Platelet count
- MPV

#### Abnormal Values
- [[Anemia]]
	- low [[Erythrocytes|RBCs]]
	- low hemoglobin / hematocrit
- [[Polycythemia]]
	- ↑ [[Erythrocytes|RBCs]]
	- ↑ hemoglobin / hematocrit
- [[Leukopenia]]
	- ↓ [[Leukocytes|WBCs]]
- [[Leukocytosis]]
	- ↑ [[Leukocytes|WBCs]]
- [[Thrombocytopenia]]
	- ↓ [[Thrombocytes|platelets]]
- [[Thrombocythemia]]
	- ↑ [[Thrombocytes|platelets]]
- [[Microcytosis]]
	- Low MCV (<80) -- small RBC size
- [[Macrocytosis]]
	- High MCV (>100) -- high RBC size
- [[Anisocytosis]]
	- High variation in cell size (↑ RDW)
- [[Poikilocytosis]]
	- High variation in cell shape
