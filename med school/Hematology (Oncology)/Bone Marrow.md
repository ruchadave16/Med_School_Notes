*spongy, soft tissue filling spaces of spongy [[bone]]*

Made of [[stromal cells]] and [[stem cells]]
- Pronormoblast = an erythrocyte precursor cell
- Myeloblast = a granulocyte precursor cell
- Monoblast = a monocyte precursor cell
- Lymphoblast = a lymphocyte precursor cell

>[!Warning] These cells should NOT be found outside the bone marrow -> need to evaluate for [[leukemia]] if found on [[peripheral blood smear]]
#### Red Marrow
- Flat bones
- [[Metaphysis]] and [[epiphysis]] of long bones
- [[Hematopoiesis]]
#### Yellow Marrow
- Long bones
- Fatty tissue
- NOT hematopoietic
#### Functions
- [[B Lymphocytes|B cells]] and [[T Lymphocytes|T cells]]
