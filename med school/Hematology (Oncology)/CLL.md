### Etiology
- Most common leukemia in adults in western world
- ~60 years
- gene deletions (e.g., 11q, 13q, 17p) or trisomy 12

### Pathophysiology
- SLL: lymph nodes with small lymphocytes ("soccer balls")
- B-cell markers like CD19 and CD20, but are also CD23 and CD5 positive.
![[Screenshot 2026-07-23 at 10.58.46 PM.png]]
### Clinical Features
- Asymptomatic
- Generalized lymphadenopathy and hepatosplenomegaly are only present in ~50% of patients
- Immune dysruption
- Hypogammaglobulinemia
- Small % = [[hemolytic anemia]]

### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- Lymphocyte count > 5000/mm3 = CLL
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical
- Lifestyle
