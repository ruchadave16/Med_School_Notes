### Etiology
- X recessive
- Common in middle east
### Pathophysiology
- Normally G6PD helps create NADPH to produce [[glutathione]]
	- Instead ↑ oxidants so hemoglobin denatured
- Deficiency causes [[Intravascular Hemolysis|intravascular hemolysis]] (older cells)
- [[Heinz Bodies]] formed
	- Removed by splenic macrophages
	- Produce [[bite cells]]
- 
### Clinical Features
- Episodic hemolysis after exposures that generate oxidant stress:
	- Infections (most common)
	- Drugs ([[antimalarials]] – primaquine or chloroquine; sulfonamides; nitrofurantoins)
	- Foods – fava beans
