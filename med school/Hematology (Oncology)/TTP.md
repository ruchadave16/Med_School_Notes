### Etiology
- Deficiency in ADAMTS13 (plasma enzyme)
	- Hereditary (rare)
	- Acquired through autoantibodies

### Pathophysiology
- Normally this degrades very high molecular weight multimers of vWF
- Spontaneous platelet activation and aggregation
### Clinical Features
- Thrombocytopenia
- Fever
- [[Microangiopathic Anemia]]
- Neurologic defects
- Renal failure

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Plasma exchange

- Long-term
