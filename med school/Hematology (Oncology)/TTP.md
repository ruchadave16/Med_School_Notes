### Etiology
- Deficiency in ADAMTS13 (plasma enzyme that cleaves vWF)
	- Hereditary (rare)
	- Acquired through autoantibodies

### Pathophysiology
- Normally this degrades very high molecular weight multimers of vWF
- Spontaneous platelet activation and aggregation
### Clinical Features
- [[Thrombocytopenia]]
- Fever
- [[Microangiopathic Anemia]]
- Neurologic defects
- Renal failure

### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- SEVERE thrombocytopenia: < 20,000
	- Anemia: MAHA with high LDH, low haptoglobin, schistocytes
	- Normal coag studies
	- ADAMTS13 activity -- low or absent
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- EMERGENCY: Plasma exchange
- [ ] [[Glucocorticoids|Glucocorticoid]] to suppressg
