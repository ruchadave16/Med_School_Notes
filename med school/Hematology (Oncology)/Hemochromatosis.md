### Etiology
- Primarily genetic in white population
- Missense [[Mutations|mutation]] in HFE gene (C282Y) or H63D
- Secondary
	- ↑↑ blood transfusions
	- [[Thalassemias|thalassemia]] patients
- Symptoms between 50s and 60s in men, later in women
### Pathophysiology
- Uninhibited iron reabsorption -> deposits of [[hemosiderin]] in liver, pancreas, heart, joints, endocrine organs
- [[Hepcidin]] normally is negative regulator for intestinal iron absorption
	- Mutation in HFE gene means that ↓ hepcidin and ↑ iron absorption
- Pancreatic fibrosis

### Clinical Features
- Liver
	- Abdominal pain
	- Hepatomegaly
	- **Cirrhosis, jaundice**
- Pancreas
	- Signs of [[Diabetes Mellitus|diabetes mellitus]]
	- ↑ glucose, hyperglycemia
- Skin: hyperpigmentation, **bronze skin**
- Cardiac dysfunction
- Arthritis
- Gonadal dysfunction

### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- Liver biopsy will show iron
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Regular phlebotomy
- [[Iron chelation]]

### Complications
- High risk of hepatocellular carcinoma – common cause of death, 200x increased risk
- Can mainly cause [[Dilated Cardiomyopathy|dilated cardiomyopathy]] but sometimes [[Restrictive Cardiomyopathy|restrictive cardiomyopathy]]
