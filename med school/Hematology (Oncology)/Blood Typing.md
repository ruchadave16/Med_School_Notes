*[[Glycoproteins]] (H-substance) found on lipids and proteins of [[Erythrocytes|erythrocyte]] surface*

- **R-GalNAc-GlcNAc-Gal-Fucose.**

Terminal Gal has a distinct connection either to GalNAc, Gal, or None
#### Presence of A and B antigens (IgM, naturally occurring) 
- Nothing = Type O
- N-acetylgalactosamine = Type A
- Galactose terminated = Type B

#### Antigens
- Protein antigens (respond against IgG, acquired after first exposure)
	- Rh -- D antigen
	- Kidd
	- Kell
	- Duffy
- Carbohydrate antigens (after IgM) -- natural
#### Blood Typing
1) Type: ABO and (Rh)D type 
	- Forward typing: Anti-A and anti-B antibodies added to blood to see for + rxn (clumping)
		- If blood reacts to Anti-A, then blood has A antigen and so is type A
	- Reverse typing: plasma is mixed with known ABO type
		- If blood reacts with B-type RCS, the blood has anti-b antibodies and is type A
2) Screening: Testing for unexpected, clinically significant RBC antibodies (non-ABO; e.g. anti-Rh, anti-Kidd, -Kell, or -Duffy antibodies)
	- Indirect Antiglobulin Test (IAT): plasma mixed with known blood to check clumping
	- ![[Screenshot 2026-07-15 at 12.51.33 PM.png]]
3) Crossmatch: plasma is mixed with the anticipated donor RBCs to check for any reactions

#### [[Transfusions]]
**Indications**
- [[Erythrocytes|RBCs]] (Anemia)
	- One RBC unit is estimated to raise a patient’s Hg by 1 g/dL 
	- **Hg < 6 almost always** requires a transfusion (not indicated unless < 7 in hospitalized)
	- Hg > 10 almost never requires a transfusion
- Platelets
	- No bleeding scheduled for surgery:
		- Neurosurgery: transfuse at < 100,000 platelets/μL
		- Normal surgery: transfuse at < 50,000 platelets/μL
	- Adult with no bleeding
		- Transfuse **prophylactically at < 10,000 platelets/μL** to reduce the risk of spontaneous bleeding
- Plasma
	- [[Warfarin]] reversal
	- Factor deficiency and bleeding 
- Cyroprecipitate
	- [[Fibrinogen deficiency]]
	- Each unit raises fibrinogen levels by 5-10 mg/dL
	
**Types**
- RBC transfusion: use O- until ABO type confirmed
- Plasma transfusion: ensure ABO compatibile but use AB until determined
