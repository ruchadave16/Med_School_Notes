### Etiology
- Common in hospitalized patients
- Chronic microbial infections
- Chronic immune disorders
- Neoplasms

### Pathophysiology
- Impairment of RBC production from chronic diseases that cause systemic inflammation
- Bone marrow macrophages **will contain** stainable iron particles (in iron deficient anemia, they do NOT contain these)

### Clinical Features
- Symptoms
- Signs
- Variants

### Diagnosis
- Labs: high serum ferritin level; and a reduced total iron-binding capacity

### Treatment
- Treat chronic disease
- Erythropoiesis-stimulating agents ([[Erythropoiesis-Stimulating Agents|ESAs]]) (e.g., EPO) 
### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
