80% of functional iron in the body is in hemoglobin
- Iron bound to [[ferritin]] in the liver, spleen, bone marrow, and muscles
- Serum ferritin from this pool (reflective of body iron stores)
- Iron is transported in plasma bound to transferrin
	- 33% saturated normally (100 ug/dL)

**Absorption** in [[duodenum]] regulated by [[hepcidin]] from liver
- Inflammation causes hepcidin ↑ so ↓ absorption of iron
### Iron Sequestration
*Shunting of iron away into liver*
- [[IL-6]] stimulates production of [[hepcidin]] so ↓ absorption of iron
- Iron trapped in macrophages 
- EPO production also ↓ 
### Iron Study
*essential role in the differentiation of the microcytic anemias*
- Iron
- [[Transferrin]] / Total Iron Binding Capacity
- Transferrin Saturation: Normal = 1/3
- [[Ferritin]]: protein that stores iron

>[!Note] **[[Iron deficiency anemia]] = low serum ferritin & low serum iron, with high TIBC**
