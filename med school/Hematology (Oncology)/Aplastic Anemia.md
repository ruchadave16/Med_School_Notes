### Etiology
- Any age, any sex

**Causes**
- [[Fanconi anemia]] -- autosomal recessive disorder
- Telomerase defects (5-10%)
- Immune mediated
- [[Benzene]]
- [[Chloramphenicol]]
- [[Carbamazepine]]
- Gold salts
- Alkylating agents
- Viral infections -- viral hepatitis, [[EBV]], [[Cytomegalovirus|CMV]], HIV
- Radiation therapy
### Pathophysiology
- Suppression of multipotent myeloid stem cells -> bone marrow failure and pancytopenia
	- Cells express neoantigens that cause autoimmune rxn
- Bone marrow biopsy: markedly hypocellular and largely devoid of hematopoietic cells

### Clinical Features
- Anemia = progressive weakness, pallor, and dyspnea
- Thrombocytopenia = petechiae and ecchymoses
- Neutropenia = fever, chills, and frequent/persistent infections
	- *No splenomegaly*
