### Etiology
- t(8;21) or inv(16) -- chimeric genes encoding fusion proteins that block maturation
- t(15;17) -- fusion protein that interferes with terminal differentiation
- Post chemotherapy
	- [[Alkylating Agents]]
	- Topoisomerase II inhibitors

- Peak age > 60 -- most common leukemia in adults
### Pathophysiology
- Hematopoietic stem cell tumor -> mutations that stop differentiation
	- ↑↑ immature myeloid blasts in bone marrow

- CD34, CD117, HLADR
![[Screenshot 2026-07-24 at 5.53.29 PM.png]]

**Classification**
- AML with genetic aberrations
	- AML with t(8;21): Auer rods (needle-like azurophilic granules) in cytoplasm , [[APL]]
	- AML with inv(16): abnormal eosinophilic precursors
	- AML with t(15;17): **best** prognosis,  numerous Auer rods
	- AML with t(11q23): poor prognosis, monocytic differentiation
- AML with [[MDS]]
- AML, therapy related
	- Respond poorly to therapy
- AML, not otherwise specified
### Clinical Features
- Anemia -- fatigue
- Thrombocytopenia
- Neutropenia

### Diagnosis
- 20% myeloid blasts in [[Bone Marrow]] -- [[bone marrow biopsy]] needed

### Treatment
- t(15;17) variant -- Treat with all-trans-retinoic acid and arsenic trioxide (curable)
- Chemotherpay

### Complications
- 60% of patient achieve a complete remission
	- Only 15-30% of patients remain free of disease for 5 years
