**Active Form**: [[Tetrahydrofolate]] (THF)
#### Source
- Green leafy vegetables
- Dried legumes
#### Function
- Coenzyme for 1-C transfer methylation
- Synthesis of N-containing bases for DNA and RNA
	- Absorbed in [[jejunum]]
- **dTMP** synthesis
#### [[Folate Deficiency]]

- Non-pregnant adults: 400 mcg/day
- Pregnant women: at least 400 mcg/day, beginning one month prior to conception and continuing through the first trimester to prevent neural tube defects
- >600 mcg/day required during pregnancy due to increased demand
