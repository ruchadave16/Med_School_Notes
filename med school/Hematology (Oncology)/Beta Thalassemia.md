### Etiology
- 100+ mutations
	- β0 mutations = no β globin synthesis
	- β+ mutations = some but decreased β globin synthesis
- Italian, Greek
### Pathophysiology
- Deficits in HbA1 synthesis -> production of microcytic RBCs 
	- ↓ survival times of RBCs
	- unpaired α chains precipitate and form insoluble inclusions, which cause membrane damage producing hemolysis
- Marrow with massive erythroid hyperplasia and extramedullary hematopoiesis
	- Expanding mass of red cell precursors erodes the bone cortex, producing bone abnormalities
	- Excessive absorption of dietary iron

 Major
-  6-9 months after birth
- Transfusions -> cardiac disease due to progressive iron overload and secondary hemochromatosis
- Transfusions + chelation therapy -> survival into the third decade
- Red cells completely lack HbA, mainly just HbF
	- Peripheral smears = anisocytosis, poikilocytosis, microcytosis, hypochromia, target cells, basophilic stippling, and fragmented RBCs are all common
	- “Crewcut” appearance of the skull on radiograph (hair on end sign)
	
Minor
- Asymptomatic
