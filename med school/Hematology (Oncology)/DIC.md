*acute, subacute, or chronic thombo-hemorrhagic disorder characterized by the excessive activation of coagulation and the formation of thrombi in the microvasculature*
### Etiology
- Results from
	- Major trauma
	- Sepsis
	- Obstetric complications
	- Malignancies (pancreatic)

### Pathophysiology
1) Release of [[tissue factor]]
	- Tissue injury
	- Placenta has tissue factor
	- Mucus produced and released by carcinomas acts as a procoagulant
	- Sepsis = endotoxins that can activate XII (coagulation cascade)
2) Endothelial cell injury
	- Platelet activation
	- ↑ TNF from sepsis -> ↑ TF expression on cells so prothrombotic

**Widespread** deposition of [[fibrin]] and consumption of platelets and clotting factors

### Clinical Features
- Signs of [[thrombosis]] 
- Signs of hemorrhage
- [[Microangiopathic Anemia]]
- Widespread fibrin deposits
	- Dyspnea and resp. failure 
	- Oliguria and renal failure
- Convulsions and coma
- Progressive circulatory failure and shock
### Diagnosis
- Thrombocytopenia
- PT and PTT prolonged
- ↓ [[fibrinogen]] concentration
- ↑ [[D dimers]]
- [[Schistocytes]]

### Treatment
- Treat underlying cause
- Blood product support: platelets, FFP, cryoprecipitate
