### Etiology
- Autosomal dominant
- Mutation in APC gene – [[Tumor Suppressor Genes|tumor suppressor gene]]
- 100% untreated patients develop by 30 yrs

### Pathophysiology
- Classic 
- Attenuated presents at 40-50
- Gardner syndrome: osteomas, skin cysts
- Turcot syndrome: CNS tumors, medulloblastoma

### Clinical Features
- Symptoms
	- Thousands of colorectal adenomas as teenagers
	- Mostly before 30, always before 50 yrs
- Signs
- Variants

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria
	- Minimum of 100 polyps

### Treatment
- Prophylactic colectomy

- Long-term
