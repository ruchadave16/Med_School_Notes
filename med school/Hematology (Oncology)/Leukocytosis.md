*Increase in [[Leukocytes|WBCs]]*
- Increase marrow production (growth factor dependent)
- Increased release from marrow stores
- Decreased intravascular margination
- Decreased extravasation into tissues

Changes: toxic granules + Dohle bodies
#### Categories
- Neutrophilic -- (G-CSF) from acute [[Bacteria|bacteria]] infection or sterile inflammation
- Eosinophilic -- [[IL-5]] from [[allergic rxn]], drug rxn, or [[Parasites|parasite]]
- Basophilic -- myeloproliferative disorders
- Monocytosis -- Chronic infections
- Lymphocytosis -- [[Viral Infections|viral infection]]
