*Group of genetically heterogenous disorders from mutations that decrease either α or β globin*
- Endemic to Middle East, Mediterranean, Africa, India, and Asia
#### Mechanism 
1) [[Hemolysis]]
2) Ineffective [[erythropoiesis]] 

**$\alpha$ chain**
- pair of genes on chromosome 16
- [[alpha thalassemia]]

**$\beta$ chain**
- single gene on chromosome 11
- [[beta thalassemia]]
