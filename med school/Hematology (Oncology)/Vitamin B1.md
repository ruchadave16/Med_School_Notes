**Active Form**: Thiamine pyrophosphate
#### Sources
- Whole grain cereals
- Yeast, pork, legumes
#### Function
- Coenzyme for dehydrogenase enzymes
	- pyruvate dehydrogenase
	- a-ketogluterate dehydrogenase
	- transketolase
	- branched a-ketoacid dehydrogenase
#### Deficiency
- [[Wernickes Encephalopathy]]
- [[Korsakoff Amnesia]]
- [[Beriberi]]

#### Dosage
- Oral 300mg/day max
