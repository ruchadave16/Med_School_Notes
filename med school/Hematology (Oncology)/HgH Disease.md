### Etiology
- Deletion of three α globin genes
	- Type of [[Alpha Thalassemia|alpha thalassemia]]
- Common in Asian populations

### Pathophysiology
- HbH has a high 02 affinity, and thus is not useful for oxygen delivery
- Precipitates and forms intracellular inclusions
	- RBC phagocytosis in spleen
	
### Clinical Features
- Moderately severe anemia
