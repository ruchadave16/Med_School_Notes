### Etiology
- Spontaneous or transform from low grade
- Common in older adults but can be any age

### Pathophysiology
- Clinically aggressive (high-grade)
- Large cells that grow diffuse with prominent nucleoli
- B cell markers: CD10, CD19, CD20, CD79a, and BCL6
- Common in Waldeyer ring

- Rapidly enlargening mass

### Treatment
- Chemo: cure rates can reach 40-50%
- Anti CD20 antibody improves
