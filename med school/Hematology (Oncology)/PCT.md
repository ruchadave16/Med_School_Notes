### Etiology
- Most common porphyria
- Acquired or autosomal dominant 
	- Associated with [[Hepatitis C Virus]]

### Pathophysiology
- Uroporphyrinogen decarboxylase

### Clinical Features
- Skin symptoms (photosensitive, blistering skin lesions)

### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Repeated phlebotomies, sun avoidance

### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
