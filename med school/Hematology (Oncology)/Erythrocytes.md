6-8 microns
#### Morphology
NO organelles
ATP through [[glycolysis]]
**Biconcave disk**
- Large surface → area ratio
- Flexible shape
- Adhere in stacks

![[Screenshot 2026-07-08 at 9.43.33 AM.png]]
#### Function
- $O_2$ to tissues, $CO_2$ to lungs
- Has [[hemoglobin]]
- Buffers blood for [[Acid-Base Balance|acid-base balance]]
#### Formation
- [[Bone marrow]]
- [[Erythropoietin]] (EPO) stimulates [[erythropoiesis]] in bone ← made by endothelial cells in peritubular capillaries of kidney and hepatocytes
- Life = **120 days**
- Old removed by [[Macrophage|macrophages]] in [[spleen]] and [[bone marrow]]

**[[Reticulocytes]]** – Immature RBCs 
- Have cytoplasmic [[rRNA]], seen through methylene blue
- Increase due to increased [[erythropoiesis]], decrease for opposite
