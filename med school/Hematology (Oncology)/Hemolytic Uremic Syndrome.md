### Etiology
- Common in children (Shiga toxin)
- Atypical from genetic or acquired defect in complement regulation

### Pathophysiology
- Normal level of ADAMTS13
- Triggered commonly by infectious gastroenteritis from [[E. Coli|E coli]] strain O157:H7

### Clinical Features
- Absence of fever, absent or mild neurologic symptoms
- Prevalent renal failure, and frequent occurrence in children
	- Bloody diarrhea followed with signs of kidney failure

### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- MAHA with high LDH, low haptoglobin, and schistocytes
	- [[Thrombocytopenia]]
	- Renal failure: ↑ BUN and creatinine
	- Coagulation studies NORMAL
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Hydration, blood transfusions
	- Dialysis if needed
- NO antibiotics
- [[Eculizumab]] IF genetic (atypical)

### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
