*Higher chance of forming blood clot (generally venous clots)*
#### Causes
Common
- [[Factor V Leiden Mutation]]: **Most common genetic cause**
	- Autosomal dominant
	- 15% caucasians
	- Factor V is **resistant to cleavage by Protein C** 
	- Recurrent DVTs
- Prothrombin Gene Mutation
	- 1-2% of population
	- 3x increased risk for venous thrombosis
	- ↑ prothrombin

Rare
- [[Antithrombin III Deficiency]]
- Protein C Deficiency
- Protein S Deficiency
- [[Homocystinuria]] / [[Homocysteinemia]]

Acquired
- [[Heparin Induced Thrombocytopenia]]
- [[DIC]]
- [[Antiphospholipid Antibody Syndrome|APS]]
- Prolonged immobilization
- MI
- Afib
- Tissue injury
- Cancer
- Prosthetic cardiac valves
- Hyperestrogenic states
- Oral contraceptive use
- Smoking
- Nephrotic Syndrome
