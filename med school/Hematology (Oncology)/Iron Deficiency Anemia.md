### Etiology
- #1 **nutritional deficiency in the world**, most common cause of anemia
- **Chronic blood loss**
	- Older male -- [[colon cancer]]
	- menstrual irregularities
- ↓ dietary intake
- ↓ absorption ([[Celiac Disease|celiac disease]])

### Clinical Features
- Pallor, fatigue, exercise intolerance, shortness of breath
- [[Glossitis]]
- Esophageal webs
- Angular chelitis
- Koilonychia
- [[PICA]]

### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- Low serum ferritin & low serum iron, with high TIBC
- Peripheral blood smear: microcytic and hypochromatic
	- Pencil-shaped cells
- Bone marrow biopsy: absence of stainable iron particles in marrow macrophages
### Treatment
- Correction of cause
- Iron supplementation
### Complications
- [[Plummer-Vinson Syndrome]]
