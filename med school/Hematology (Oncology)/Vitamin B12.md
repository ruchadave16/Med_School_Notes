**Active Form**: Cobamamide, Methylcobalamin
#### Source
- Meat and dairy
#### Function
- Cofactor for methylmalonyl-CoA and methionine synthase
- Needed to make [[myelin]]
- Binds to [[Intrinsic Factor|intrinsic factor]] to be absorbed in terminal ileum
#### Deficiency
- [[Macrocytic megaloblastic anemia]]
- Neurological symptoms
	-  [[Subacute Combined Degeneration]]
- Hypersegmented polymorphonuclear cells
##### Causes of Deficiency
- Atrophic gastritis
- Gastrectomy
- Ileocecal resection
- Diet deficiency

Findings:
- Elevated methylmalonic acid levels in serum
