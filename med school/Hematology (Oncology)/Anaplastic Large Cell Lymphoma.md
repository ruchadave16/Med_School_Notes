### Etiology
- Presence of rearrangements in the ALK gene on chromosome 2p23
	- Commonly t(2;5) -> activates tyrosine kinase
- Common in children and YA
### Pathophysiology
- Horseshoe nuclei and large cytoplasm (hallmark cells)
	- Common in soft tissues
- ALK protein deletion, CD30+
