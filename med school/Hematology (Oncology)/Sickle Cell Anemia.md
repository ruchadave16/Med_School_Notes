### Etiology
Genetic: Missense [[Mutations|mutation]] in both alleles of [[beta globin]] gene
- GTG replaces GAG in DNA
- 6th AA glutamate → valine
- Makes **HgS**
	- HgF is protective (interferes with HbS polymerization)
	- HbC promotes HbS polymerization

10% African Americans in US = heterozygous
### Pathophysiology
- When deoxygenated, V forms hydrophobic bond with other deoxygenated
	- Fibers formed in [[Erythrocytes|erythrocytes]] that distort cells
	- Distorted, sickle shape
- Erythrocytes block blood vessels 
	- Chronic hemolysis 
	- Microvascular occlusions
	- Ischemic tissue damage
- Hyperplastic bone marrow
	- Extramedullary hematopoiesis
### Clinical Features
- Hypoxia
- Enlarged spleen in childhood which shrinks as more and more episodes occur -> functional asplenism
- Low hemoglobin
- Prominent cheekbones + radiographic “crewcut” changes in the skull 
- Pain crisis
	- Episodes of hypoxic injury and tissue infarction
	- In kids, painful bone crises are common (hand-foot syndrome)
	- Acute chest syndrome
- Sequestration
	- Children with enlarged spleens
	- Massive entrapment of sickled RBCs leading to rapid splenic enlargement, hypovolemia, and shock
- Aplastic Crisis

### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- [[Allele Specific Oligo Hybridization|ASO]]
	- Peripheral blood smear
		- Irreversibly sickled RBCs
		- [[Howell-Jolly bodies]] (due to functional asplenism)
		- Reticulocytosis
		- Target cells (resulting from RBC dehydration)
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- [[Hydroxyurea]]: DNA synthesis inhibitor
- Hematopoietic stem cell transplantation
