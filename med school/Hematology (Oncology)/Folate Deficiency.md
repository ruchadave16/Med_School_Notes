### Etiology
- ↓ Intake
	- Nutritionally inadequate diet 
	- Malabsorption syndromes
	- Intestinal lymphoma
	- [[Phenytoin]]
	- Oral contraceptives
- ↑ Requirement
	- [[Prenatal Period|pregnancy]]
- [[Alcoholism]]
- Folic acid antagonists ([[methotrexate]])
- [[Phenytoin]]
- [[Sulfonamides]]
### Pathophysiology
- Macrocytic, [[megaloblastic anemia]] 
- Hypersegmented polymorphonuclear cells

### Clinical Features
-  Fetal [[neural tube defects]] 
- **No neuro symptoms**
### Diagnosis
- ↑ homocysteine with normal methylmalonic acid

### Treatment
- Folate replacement
	- **Folate supplementation can improve the anemia of B12 deficiency while allowing neurologic injury to progress.**

- Short-term
- Long-term
