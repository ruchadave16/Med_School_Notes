### Etiology
- Autosomal dominant

### Pathophysiology
- Intrinsic defect in [[Erythrocytes|RBCs]] skeleton proteins that cause it to be spheroid and so less deformable
	- Ankyrin
	- Spectrin
	- Band 3
	- Band 4.2
- Get trapped within splenic sinusoids -> ↑ phagocytosis by macrophages ([[extravascular hemolysis]])
- RBC survival from 120 days to 10-20 days
	- Normally fine but when aplastic crisis happens, ([[Parvovirus B19|parvovirus B19]] infection)
	- Sensitive to osmotic lysis when incubaed in hypotonic
- Spherocytes
	- Small, rounded, no central pallor
	- [[Howell-Jolly bodies]] (red arrows) -> DNA remnants normally removed by spleen

### Clinical Features
**Can be asymptomatic**

- Splenomegaly
- Jaundice

### Diagnosis
- ↑ MCHC

### Treatment
- Splenectomy
