### Etiology
- Endemic: associated with [[Plasmodium|malaria]] regions of Africa in children
	- [[EBV]] infected 90% cells
- Sporadic: children and young adults, only 15% cells EBV+
- [[HIV]] associated

TRANSLOCATION: MYC gene on chromosome 8

### Pathophysiology
(1) Endemic Burkitt lymphoma
(2) Sporadic Burkitt lymphoma
(3) HIV-associated Burkitt lymphoma

- Fastest-growing human tumors
- ↑ mitotic index, numerous apoptotic cells
- Characteristic “starry sky” pattern because apoptotic cells eaten by macrophages
- Positive for CD10, CD19, CD20, and BCL6

### Clinical Features
- Endemic: jaw (mandible), orbit, facial bone, or CNS.
- Sporadic: pelvis or abdomen, specifically the ileocecum or peritoneum
### Diagnosis
- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Chemotherapy + IV hydration intense and electrolyte management

### Complications
- Responds well to chemotherapy
- ↑↑ chance of [[Tumor Lysis Syndrome]]
