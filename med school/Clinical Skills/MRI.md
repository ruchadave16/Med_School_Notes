### When To Use
- Posterior fossa
- Brainstem
- Spinal cord lesions
- Small lesions
- Myelination issues (like MS)
- Post-operative back pain

>[!Warning] Contraindications
>Cerebral aneurysm clips
>Pregnancy
>Cardiac pacemakers
>Cochlear implants
>Metal in eyes -- welders
