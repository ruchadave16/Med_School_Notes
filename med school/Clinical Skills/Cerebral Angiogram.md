**Use carotid ultrasound first**
- MRA as alternative to angiogram

Angiogram = well tolerated
- Potential for stroke
### Uses
- Quantify and locate stenosis of carotids
	- After carotid Doppler is abnormal
	- No need for surgery unless stenosis >80%
- Search for [[arteriovenous malformation]] or intracranial aneurysm
- Rule out brain death
