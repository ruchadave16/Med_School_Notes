#### Radiation Exposure
- Avg radiation dose/yr from natural sources in the US: 3 mSv
- Avg dose from a coast to coast round trip plane flight: 0.03 mSv
- Avg dose from a chest xray: 0.1 mSv
- Avg dose from a mammogram: 0.4 mSv
- Avg dose from CT brain: 2 mSv
- Avg dose from CT spine: 6 mSv
- Avg dose from cardiac catheterization: 7 -10 mSv
- Avg dose from CT abdomen/pelvis: 15 mSv
