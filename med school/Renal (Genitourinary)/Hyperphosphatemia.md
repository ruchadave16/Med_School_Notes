*Phosphate level > 4.5 mg/dL*
#### Causes
- ↓ excretion of phosphate
	- [[AKI]]
	- Chronic kidney disease
	- End stage renal disease
	- Bisphosphonate use
	- Hypoparathyroidism
	- Vitamin D intoxication
- ↑ phosphate administration
- ↑ release into ECF
	- [[Rhabdomyolysis]]
	- [[Tumor Lysis Syndrome]]
	- [[Acidosis]]

#### Symptoms
- Metastatic calcification
#### Diagnosis
- serum calcium x serum phosphorus
- If >70, calcification likely
