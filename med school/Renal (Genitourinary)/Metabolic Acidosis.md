*Not due to retention of $P_{CO2}$ – non-respiratory*

**Causes** – acid addition or alkaline loss: diarrhea, ingestion of acid, inability to break down acid, inability to excrete acid
- [[Renal tubular acidosis]]
#### Body Changes
- [[Buffers]]: ECF $HCO_3^-$ ↓ because of buffering $H^+$ or loss of $HCO_3^-$ 
- [[Respiratory Regulation of Acid-Base|Lungs]] (minutes): ↓ pH → ventilation → ↓ $P_{CO2}$ → pH rise, ↓ $HCO_3^-$
- [[Renal Regulation of Acid-Base|Kidneys]] (hours/days):
	- ↓ filtration of HCO3- so all is reabsorbed (as normal)
	-  ↓ renal cell pH because of ↑ H+ means ↑  excretion of $H^+$ and ↑ formation of NH4+
	- As pH increases, hyperventilation ↓ so PCO2 to normal and
