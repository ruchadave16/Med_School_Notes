### Etiology
- [[Central diabetes insipidus]]
- [[Gestational diabetes insipidus]]
- [[Nephrogenic diabetes insipidus]]

### Pathophysiology
**Central**

**Nephrogenic**
- Cells in collecting duct don’t respond to ADH
	- ADH high

Water is NOT reabsorbed
- Urine not concentrated
- Serum osmolarity ↑ 
### Clinical Features
- High thirst
- Excess urination

### Diagnosis
- Water deprivation test to determine central vs nephrogenic DI
	- Water deprive then give DDAVP at time 0 
	- Check osmolarity over 8 hrs
	- If Uosm increases, then [[Central Diabetes Insipidus]]

### Treatment
Central
- ADH Analog
- dDAVP

Nephrogenic
- Thiazide Diuretic
### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
