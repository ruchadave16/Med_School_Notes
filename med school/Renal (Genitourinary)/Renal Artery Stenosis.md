### Etiology
- Atherosclerotic RAS -> 70% cases, older individuals
	- Smoking, HTN, diabetes, hyperlipidemia 
- Fibromuscular dysplasia (<30 yrs, women)

### Pathophysiology
- Plaque formation at origin or proximal 1/3 of renal artery
- Fibromuscular dysplasia = string of beads
	- Distal 2/3 of renal artery

### Clinical Features
- Severe, resistant HTN (uncontrolled with 3 drugs)
	- Sudden >30% increase in creatinine after starting ACE inhibitor or ARB
- Sudden worsening of HTN
- Onset after 50 yrs in pt of risk for atherosclerosis
- Asymmetric kidney size
- Audible bruit

### Diagnosis
- Duplex doppler ultrasound
- CTA or MRA
- Renal angiography

### Treatment
- ACE inhibitors or ARBS can precipitate AKI
- Revascularization
	- PTRA with Stenting 
	- Surgical bypass in complex cases

### Complications
- Commonly causes secondary [[Hypertension|HTN]]
- Can cause renal failure if not reversed
