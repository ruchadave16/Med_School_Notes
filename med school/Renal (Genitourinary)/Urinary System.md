#### Components
Upper
- [[Kidneys]]
- [[Ureters]]
Lower
- [[Urinary Bladder]]
- [[Urethra]]
	- [[Prostate]] around proximal urethra in males
#### Functions
- Metabolic
	- Remove waste from blood + excrete
	- Regulate ion, salt, water balance
- Endocrine
	- [[Renin]] – ↑ BP by producing [[angiotensin II]] and [[aldosterone]]
	- [[Erythropoietin]] – [[Erythrocytes|Erythrocyte]] production and differentiation
	- [[Vitamin D]] – Conversion of 25-OH vitamin D3 to active 1,25(OH)2 D3
#### Protection
- Hydrodynamic flushing so ↓ bacterial dwell time
- Urine chemistry: physicochemical environment that can restrict bacterial growth
	- ↓ pH, ↑ urea, organic acids
- Ureterovesical competence -- ensures uni-directional flow
- [[Uromodulin]]
- Mucosal defense
	- Secretion of defensins, PRRs that draw in neutrophils
- Microbiota
