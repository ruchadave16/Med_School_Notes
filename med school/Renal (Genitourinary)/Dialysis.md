### Peritoneal Dialysis
- Vascular surgeon places catheter, can do at home, done daily while sleeping
### Hemodialysis
- AV fistula made, go in 3x weekly
#### Complications
- #1 cause of death = [[Congestive Heart Failure|CHF]] (WATCH fluid overload)

### Nutrition
**Renal Diet prioritized**

Consult Stage 3b-5 [[Chronic Kidney Disease|CKD]]
- Outpatient covered by most insurances and Medicare
- Up to 4 units per year
- Protein low

Dialysis
- Dialysis facility RD (payment for outpatient will be denied due to bundled care)
- Protein: 1.0 to 1.2
- Maintain normal K, Na, P, Ca
