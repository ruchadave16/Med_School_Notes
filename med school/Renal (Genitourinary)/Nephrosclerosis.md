### Etiology
- Long standing [[Hypertension|HTN]]

### Pathophysiology
- Renal sclerosis from HTN -> damage to small arteries and arterioles leading to ischemia and fibrosis of renal parenchyma
- Slow progressive process
	- Diffusely finely granular 
- [[Hyaline Arteriolosclerosis]]
- Ischemic glomerulosclerosis 
- Tubular atrophy and interstitial fibrosis

### Clinical Features
- Symmetric atropy of kidneys
- Asymptomatic for many years, mild proteinuria and slow rise in creatinine

- Labs
- Imaging
- Criteria

### Treatment
- Management of [[Hypertension|HTN]] -- [[ACE Inhibitors]] or [[ARBs]]
