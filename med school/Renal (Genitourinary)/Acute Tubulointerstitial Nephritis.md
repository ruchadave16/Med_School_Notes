### Etiology
- Non-infectious cause = Drug induced hypersensitivity (70-75% cases)
	- [[Antibiotics]], [[NSAIDs]], [[proton pump inhibitors]], [[Antiepileptic Drugs|antiepileptic]], [[chemotherapy]], 
- Infections: [[Pyelonephritis|pyelonephritis]], systemic w virus or fungi
- Systemic conditions: [[Sarcoidosis]], [[Sjogren’s Syndrome]], [[Systemic Lupus Erythematosus|lupus]]

### Pathophysiology
- Rapid inflammation and edema in interstitium of kidney, causes [[AKI]]
- T cell mediated [[Type IV Hypersensitivity|type IV hypersensitivity]] rxn
	- Drug acts as hapten, bidning to protein causing rxn
- ↑↑ Eosinophils in interstitium

### Clinical Features
- Fever, rash, eosinophilia
- Acute renal failure within 1-3 weeks of starting a medicine

### Diagnosis
- Urinalysis: eosinophiluria and WBC casts

### Treatment
- Stop causative drug. if severe AKI, treat with [[Prednisone]]

### Complications
- [[AKI]]
- [[Chronic Tubulointerstitial Nephritis]] -> CKD
