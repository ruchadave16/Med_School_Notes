*Retaining more urine than normal in bladder after voiding*: (PVR >50mL)
#### Causes
- Obstruction
- Neurogenic
	- Autonomic neuropathy from [[diabetes]]or [[spinal cord injury]]
- Pharmacologic
	- [[Anticholinergic Drugs|anticholinergics]]
	- [[Antihistamines]]
	- [[Tricyclic Antidepressants|TCAs]]
	- [[Opioids]]
	- [[Benzodiazephines|Benzos]]
