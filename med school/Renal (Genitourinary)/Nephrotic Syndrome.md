### Etiology
- [[Minimal change disease]] = children
- [[Focal Segmental Glomerulosclerosis|focal segmental glomerulosclerosis]] = adults
- [[Membranous nephropathy]]
- [[Type I MPGN]]

### Pathophysiology
- Damage to capillary walls of [[Renal Corpuscle|glomeruli]] causing ↑ permeability of proteins

### Clinical Features
- Proteinuria: 3.5g or more in adults
- [[Hypoalbuminemia]]: < 3 g/dL
- General edema: commonly periorbital
- [[Hyperlipidemia]]
	- oval fat bodies in the urine
