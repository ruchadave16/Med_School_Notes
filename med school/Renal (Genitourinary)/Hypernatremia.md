#### Causes
- [[Burns]]
- [[Hypodipsia]] (low thirst) = [[diabetes insipidus]]
	- Central vs nephrogenic
- Hypertonic intake
	- Bicarbonate infusion
	- Hypertonic feeds
	- NaCl ingestion
	- Sea water ingestion
	- Intrauterine injection of hypertonic saline
	- 3% NaCl infusion
	- Hypertonic dialysate
	-  [[Conn Syndrome|Primary hyperaldosteronism]]
	- [[Cushing Syndrome]]
- Free water loss
	- [[Diuretics]]
	- [[Osmotic Diuresis]]
	- Polyuria 2/2 acute tubular necrosis
	- Postobstructive diuresis
#### Treatment
- Water deprivation test to determine central vs nephrogenic DI
