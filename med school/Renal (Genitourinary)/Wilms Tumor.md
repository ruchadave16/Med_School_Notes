### Etiology
- Most common pediatric renal tumor
- 90% diagnosis before 6 yrs

### Pathophysiology
- Large, well circumscribed mass
	- 10% = bilateral
- Triphasic: Blastemal (small, round, blue cells), Stromal (spindled cells), and epithelial (forming gland-like structures)

![[Screenshot 2026-09-15 at 12.28.23 AM.png]]

- 10% with WT have associated malformation syndrome
	- [[Beckwith Wiedemann syndrome]]
	- [[Perlman syndrome]]
	- [[Sotos syndrome]]
	- [[Simpson Golabi Behmel Syndrome]]
