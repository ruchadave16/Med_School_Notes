### Etiology
- Post [[Strep Pyogenes|group A strep]] (1-4 weeks) Child
	- [[Streptococcus Pneumonia|pneumococcus]]
	- Viral

### Pathophysiology
- Deposition of immune complexes causing damage to [[Renal Corpuscle|glomeruli]] and ↑ [[Neutrophils]]
	- Activation of [[Complement System|complement]] through classic pathway
- Pathology
	- diffuse glomerular proliferations
	- ↑ mesangial cells
	- ↑ [[Neutrophils]] through [[C5a]]
	- Immune deposits appear as subepithelial humps
	![[Screenshot 2026-09-15 at 11.47.58 PM.png]]
### Clinical Features
- Acute [[Nephritic Syndrome]]
	- Edema and hypertension
	- Gross hematuria (smoky brown)
- Serum anti-streptolysin O antibody titers are elevated in poststreptococcal cases
- ↓ **serum complement**
