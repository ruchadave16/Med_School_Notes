### Etiology
- Affects 1 in 7 US adults
- Most commonly from [[diabetes]] or [[Hypertension|HTN]]

### Pathophysiology
- Impaired [[erythropoietin]] production from underling [[diabetes mellitus]] -> hypoproliferative [[anemia]]
