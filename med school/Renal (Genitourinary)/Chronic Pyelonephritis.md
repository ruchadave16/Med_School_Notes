### Etiology
- Reflux nephropathy is common cause in children

### Pathophysiology
- Chronic inflammation and scarring of kidney from multiple instances of [[pyelonephritis]]
- [[VUR]] and obstructive uropathy association
- Blunting and dilation of calyces, patchy and focal scarring
- Chronic inflammation with lymphocytes, macrophates
	- Thyroidization of kidney

### Clinical Features
- Asymptomatic for many years, turns to progessive [[Chronic Kidney Disease|CKD]]
- May have hypertension

### Diagnosis
- ↑↑ WBCs in urine but no bacterial growth

- Pharmacologic
