### Pathophysiology
- Acute, uncomplicated lower UTI

### Clinical Features
- 1+ bladder symptoms (urgency, frequency, dysuria, stranguria, hesistancy)
- Suprapubic pain
- Hematuria
- Can also present with no symptoms
- **In elderly, only sign may be new onset confusion**

### Diagnosis
- Urine dipstick
	- Pyuria + positive dipstick is diagnostic
	- The absence of pyuria does not rule out UTI in women
- Urine culture NOT recommended given high-pretest probability of infection, predictable microbiology, prompt clinical response with empirical antimicrobial therapy
	- Only if failure to respond or early recurrence
### Treatment
- TMP-SMX is gaining resistance in community E. Coli
