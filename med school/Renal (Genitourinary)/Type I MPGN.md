### Etiology
- Primary or
	- SLE, hepatitis B or C, chronic liver disease, and chronic bacterial infections

### Pathophysiology
- Deposition of immune complexes or in situ formation with planted antigen
- Glomeruli are large with proliferation of mesangial and endothelial cells, double contour (split), or “tram track” appearance.
- Discrete **subendothelial** deposits

- 1/2 have [[Nephrotic Syndrome|nephrotic syndrome]], other half with [[nephritis]] or proteinuria
