### Etiology
- 80-85% of renal primary neoplasms
- 30,000 cases per year
- 60s to 80s, M 2x rate of F
- Risk
	- Smoking
	- Obesity
	- HTN
	- Occupational exposure 

### Pathophysiology
- From tubular epithelial, located mostly in the cortex

**Subtypes**
- Clear Cell RCC (most common), from PCT
	- Well-circumscribed, solid and cystic
	- Yellow, hemorrhagic
	- "chicken wire" vasculature (cleared out cells)
	- Inactivation of VHL, upregulation of hypoxia inducible factor
		- Associated with [[von Hippel Lindau]]
		- Associated with [[TSC]]
		![[Screenshot 2026-09-15 at 12.24.24 AM.png]]
- Papillary RCC (2nd most common), from PCT
	- better prognosis
	- Prominent pseudocapsule, in renal cortex
	- Characterized by papillae with central fibrovascular core (true papillae) containing **foamy macrophages** ![[Screenshot 2026-09-15 at 12.25.59 AM.png]]
	
- Chromophobe RCC, from collecting ducts
	- better prognosis than both above
	- Solid growth, nests or broad alveoli trabeculae
	-![[Screenshot 2026-09-15 at 12.26.36 AM.png]]

	- “Flocculent” cytoplasm - pale or reticulated, condenses around borders
	- Associated with [[Birt Hogg Dube syndrome]]
- Collecting Duct – Rare, aggressive, bad prognosis
- Medullary – Rare, most aggressive

### Clinical Features
- Asymptomatic
	- "Silent Killer"
- Hematuria
- Flank pain
- [[Paraneoplastic syndromes]]
	- PTHrP -> [[hypercalcemia]]
	- EPO -> [[Polycythemia Vera|polycythemia vera]]
	- ACTH -> [[Cushing Syndrome]]
	- Renin -> Hypertension and hypokalemia
