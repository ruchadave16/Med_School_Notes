### Pathophysiology
1) Pre-renal
	- ↓ blood delivery to kidneys from hypovolemia, hemorrhage, shock, sepsis
	- ↑ renin and ↑ aldosterone, so ↑ sodium in serum, ↑ ADH so ↑ osmolarity of urine
	- **IF HF or cirrhosis pt, same situation (low sodium and high concetration of urine) but do NOT give them fluids**
2) Intra-renal
	- Glomerular damage
		- [[Immune complex glomerulonephritis]]
		- [[Pauci immune glomerulonephritis]]
		- [[Goodpasture Syndrome|anti-GBM disease]]
		- [[Monoclonal Immunoglobulin GN|Monoclonal immunoglobulin glomerulonephritis]]
		- [[C3 Glomerulopathy]]
	- Interstitial damage
	- Tubular damage
	- Blood vessel damage
3) Post-renal
	- Renal pelvis obstruction
		- Tumor
		- Staghorn calculus
	- Ureter obstruction
		- [[Kidney Stones]]
		- Stricture / narrowing
		- Urothelial cancer of ureter
		- External compression
	- Bladder outlet obstruction
		- Stone
		- [[BPH]]
		- Cancer at bladder outlet
		- [[Prostate Cancer]]
		- [[Urinary Retention]]
	- Urethral obstruction
		- [[Urethral Stricture]]
		- [[Urothelial cancer]]
		-

### Diagnosis
- Labs
	- Get urinalysis and bladder scan on everyone
	- Kidney ultrasound if suspicion of [[hydronephrosis]]
	- CT abdomen WITHOUT contrast if stone suspicion
	- Pre-renal: ↓ urine Na and ↑ osmolarity 
		-  give 500-1000 mL bolus of IV fluids if suspected
- Imaging
- Criteria
	- ↑ serum creatinine by > 0.3 mg/dL within 48 hrs
	- ↑ serum creatinine to > 1.5x baseline within 7 days
	- urine < 0.5 mL/kg/hr for 6 hrs

### Treatment
- [[Dialysis]]
	- A: acidemia (pH < 7.1)
	- E: [[HYPERKALEMIA]], [[Hypercalcemia]], [[hypermagnesiumia]], [[Hyperphosphatemia]]
	- I: Intoxications
	- O: fluid overload ([[Pulmonary Edema]] causing resp. failure and diuretics not helping)
	- U: uremia symptoms
