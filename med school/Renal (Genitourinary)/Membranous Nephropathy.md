### Etiology
- Adults (30-60 yrs)
- Secondary to infections, neoplasms, autoimmune disease (SLE), or exposure to drugs

### Pathophysiology
- Subepithelial Ig deposits along basement membrane
- Autoantibodies to podocyte antigens
	- Commonly to PLA2R
- Complement activation so injury to podocytes

**Histology**
- Normal, then thickening of capillary walls
