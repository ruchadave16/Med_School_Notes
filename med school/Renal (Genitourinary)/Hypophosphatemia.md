*Phosphate level < 3.0 mg/dL*
#### Causes
- ↓ absorption
	- Alcohol abuse
	- Vitamin D deficiency
	- Malabsorption of phosphate
	- Excessive use of phosphate-binding antacid
	- Hyperalimentation (during total parenteral nutrition use)
	- Starvation
- ↑ renal excretion
	- Excess PTH states
	- Hyperglycemia
	- Acute tubular necrosis
	- [[Hypokalemia]]
	- [[Hypomagnesemia]]
- [[Respiratory Alkalosis]]
- Anabolic steroids
- [[DKA]]
- Hungry bone syndrome 

#### Symptoms
when severe:
- Confusion
- Weakness
- Hemolysis
- Platelet dysfunction
- Cardiomyopathy leading to cardiac arrest

#### Treatment
- If >1 mg/dL, oral supplements 
- If severe, IV sodium phosphate
