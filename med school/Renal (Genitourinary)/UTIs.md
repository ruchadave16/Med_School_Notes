#### Predisposing Factors
- Anatomical
	- short female urethra
	- urinary obstruction
	- incomplete bladder emptying
	- neurogenic bladder
	- vesicoureteral reflux
	- pregnancy-related urinary stasis
- Behavioral
	- sexual activity
	- urinary instrumentation
	- indwelling catheters
	- stones/foreign bodies
	- altered vaginal microbiota
- Susceptibility
	- genetic
	- variation in innate immune responses
#### Source of Infection
- Ascending infection: intestinal flora 
	- → Periurethral colonization (sexual intercourse, loss of protective microbiota)
	- → urethral entry → bladder colonization/adherence (Catheterization, instruments)
	- → bladder infection ± symptomatic cystitis → ureteral ascent → renal pelvis/parenchyma → [[pyelonephritis]]
		- Urinary tract obstruction (BPH, tumors, calculi)
		- Vesicoureteral reflux: 
		- Intrarenal reflux
	- OR can be blood based

#### Categorization
- Uncomplicated
	- Confined to bladder
	- Local symptoms (dysuria, urgency, frequency, and suprapubic pain)
	- NO signs of systemic illness
- Complicated
	- Signs and symptoms suggesting infection extending beyond the bladder
	- Fever, chills, rigors, fatigue
