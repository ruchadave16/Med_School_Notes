### Etiology
- Autosomal Dominant
	- Mutation in polycystin1 (PKD1) – Chromosome 16
	- More early onset with > complications
- Minority have PKD2 mutation (Women preserve renal function longer than men)
- Late onset (40s to 60s)

### Pathophysiology
- 2 Hit theory
	- Germline mutations were observed in cysts
	- n the cysts there was a second mutation in the wild type allele

### Clinical Features
- Symptoms
	- [[Hypertension]]
	- Kidney pain
	- eGFR loss and CKD progression
- Bilat. cysts in kidneys
- Liver cysts
- [[Berry aneurysms]]
- Pancreatic cysts
- Seminal vesicle cysts
- Arachnoid cysts
- [[Aortic Dissection]]
- Abdominal wall hernias
- [[Mitral valve prolapse]]
