#### Type 1
- [[Amphotericin B]], congenital, autoimmune
- Problem in distal nephron
- H+ secretion into tubule impaired
	- H+ K+ or H+ ATPase pump issue
	- ↓ secretion of H+ into urine
![[Screenshot 2026-08-31 at 11.52.18 PM.png]]
#### Type 2
- [[Fanconi Syndrome]], [[Multiple Myeloma]], [[Acetazolamide]] (Carbonic anhydrase inhibitor use)
- Proximal tubule HCO3- reabsorption issue
![[Screenshot 2026-08-31 at 11.52.31 PM.png]]

#### Type 3
- Combination of Type 1 and Type 2
#### Type 4
- Tubular transport disorder caused by [[hypoaldosteronism]]
- ↓ H+ secretion into tubule so ↓ H+ excretion bound to NH3
