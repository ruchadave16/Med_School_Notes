*Final part of [[Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System|renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system]]*

- ↑ angiotensin II = ↑ [[aldosterone]]
- Goal: maintain and increase vascular volume and pressure and minimize sodium loss 
#### Functions
- Stimulate [[adrenal cortex]] to make [[aldosterone]]
- Kidney to reduce fluid and sodium diuresis
- ↑ [[thirst]] in hypothalamus
- [[Vasopressin|ADH]] release from post. pituitary
- ↑ sympathetic activation
- ↑ cardiac hypertrophy

#### Uses
- Can help rapidly increase BP in [[vasodilatory shock]] with no response to other vasopressors
