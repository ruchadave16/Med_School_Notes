### Etiology
- Autosomal recessive
	- Much more rare
- Mutation of PKHD1 gene (chr 6)
	- Fibrocystin or polyductin 
### Pathophysiology
- Mechanism of disease
- Relevant pathways (e.g. immune, hormonal)

### Clinical Features
- Prenatal identification
	- [[Oligohydramnios]]
	- Nephromegaly
	- Echogenic kidney parenchyma
	- Small microcysts
	- Loss of cortical differentiation
- [[Potter Syndrome]] 
	- Flattened nasal bridge and tip
	- Low set flattened ears with less creasing
	- Micrognathia
	- Talipes equinovarus (club feet)
	- Respiratory insufficiency
	- Pulmonary hypoplasia

### Treatment
- Pharmacologic
- Surgical

### Complications
- Neonatal mortality = 20-50% -- resp. insufficiency
- Death from [[ESRD]] or liver issue after yr 1
	- 20% have ESRD in first decade
- Systemic [[Hypertension|HTN]]
- Liver disease
- [[Esophageal varices]]
