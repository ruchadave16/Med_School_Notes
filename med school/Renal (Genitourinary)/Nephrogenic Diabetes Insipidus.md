### Etiology
- Congenital: mutation in AQP2 gene or AVPR2 gene
- Meds
	- [[Lithium]]
	- [[Amphotericin B]]
	- [[Foscarnet]] and [[cidofovir]]
	- [[tolvaptan]] -- V2 antagonists
	- [[Democlocycline]], [[ofloxacin]], [[orlistat]], [[didanosine]], [[methotrexate]]
- Electrolyte abnormalities
	- [[Hypokalemia]]
	- [[Hypercalcemia]]
- Destruction of renal medulla
	- [[Sickle Cell Anemia|sickle cell]]
	- [[Sarcoidosis]]
	- [[Amyloidosis]]
	- [[Sjogren’s Syndrome]]
	- [[Autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease]]
### Pathophysiology
- Impaired urinary concentration; ADH is being released, but is not taking effect
