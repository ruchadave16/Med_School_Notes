### Etiology
- Most are sporadic
- Middle aged adults, younger if associated with [[Tuberous Sclerosis]]
	- Mutations of TSC2 or TSC1 tumor suppressor genes

### Pathophysiology
- Benign neoplasm of blood vessels, smooth muscle, and fat
- Can **spontaneously hemorrhage**, associated with tuberous sclerosis complex
- Triphasic: myoid spindle cells, mature adipose tissue, dysmorphic thick walled blood, hyalinized vessels
