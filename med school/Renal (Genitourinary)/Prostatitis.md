### Etiology
- [[E. Coli]] in 70%

### Pathophysiology
- Mechanism of disease
- Relevant pathways (e.g. immune, hormonal)

### Clinical Features
- Dysuria, frequency, pain
- Fever, rigors

### Diagnosis
COMMONLY OVER-DIAGNOSED
- **rectal exam could cause bacteremia**
- Urinalysis and culture 
- Ultrasound and MRI IF prostatic abscess

### Treatment
- **EMERGENCY**
- β-lactam and an aminoglycoside
- Oral therapy 2-4 weeks
	- Surgery if abscess
