[[Smooth muscle]] of afferent arteriole of [[Renal Corpuscle|glomerulus]] contracts or relaxes to change in transmural pressure
- Keeps blood flow and [[Glomerular Filtration|glomerular filtration]] constant

#### Mechanism
- Acute ↑ in local arteriole pressure -> ↑ local blood flow -> stretching of arteriole diameter mechanically
- This stretch opens L-type Ca2+ channels and increases intracellular Ca2+ levels
	- Contraction of smooth muscle cells
- Contraction ↓ diameter and flow rate to normal
