### Etiology
- Mutation of Type IV [[Collagen]]
	- Most often X-linked

### Pathophysiology
- Basketweave pattern of basement membrane

### Clinical Features
- Nephritis 
- Sensorineural deafness + eye abnormalities
- Microscopic hematuria

- Imaging
