*Limited sensitivity and specificity, but can tell urine content and pathology*
- Use as a general test when renal pathology suspected or systemic condition is present that can alter kidney function
#### Method
- Random or simple catch specimen has contaminants
- Clean-catch midstream specimen has lower number
- Catheterized specimen can be an even “cleaner” specimen
#### Macroscopic Evaluation
- Volume -- for timed collections
- Color 
- Clarity (should be clear)
- Odor
#### Chemical Evaluation
- “Dipsticks”/reagent strips:
- Specific gravity: Higher concentration of “the stuff” in urine → High specific gravity
	- <1.007 = hyposthenuric
		- Think of [[Diabetes Insipidus]]
	- 1.012 – 1.030 = hypersthenuric
		- after a 12 hour overnight fast (food or water)
		- excess water loss, dehydration, adrenal insufficiency
	- Plasma Range s.g. of 1.010 normal
	
>[!Note] EXCEPTION
>if FIXED: multiple 1.010 values, then urine is only filtrate and severly damaged kidney with poor tubular function or ADH issue

- pH (generally 6.0)
- Protein -- can indicate glomerular damage
- Glucose
- Ketones
	- [[DKA]]
	- Febrile illness
	- Vomiting
	- Diarrhea
	- Starvation + cachexia
- Blood
	- Hematuria = RBCs in urine
	- Hemoglobinuria =  free hemoglobin in urine ([[Intravascular Hemolysis|intravascular hemolysis]])
	- Myoglobinuria = myoglobin in urine ([[rhabdomyolysis]])
	- ** Ascorbic acid can cause false positive
- Bilirubin
	- Excess conjugated bilirubin in blood: obstruction of bile flow or hepatocellular disease
- Urobilinogen
	- Urobilinogen should be present normally
	- Excess when liver cannot remove urobilinogen from blood = hepatocellular damage
	- Complete absence = bile obstruction
- Nitrite
	- + could be [[UTIs]]
- Leukocyte Esterase
	- Commonly high in bacterial infections
#### Microscopic Evaluation
*Performed whenever a renal or urinary tract problem is suspected*
- Epithelial Content
	- Urothelial (transitional) cells:![[Screenshot 2026-08-30 at 11.45.46 PM.png]]
	- Squamous epithelial cells
	- **ABNORMAL** = Renal tubular epithelial cells (tubular injury) and fat bodies and neoplastic cells
- RBCs
- Inflammatory cells
	- ↑ = infection or inflammation
- Organisms (bacteria, yeast, parasites)
- Casts
	- *cylindrical gel-like protein precipitates that may contain other elements*
	- Tamm-Horsfall protein = main component
	- secreted in ascending loop and distal convoluted tubule
	- Hyaline casts
		- 0-2 = normal
		- ↑↑ = renal disease
	- Granular casts
		- Increased protein leakage, inflammation or other injury
	- Fatty casts
		- [[nephrotic syndrome]]
	- RBC casts
		- bleeding in nephron
	- WBC casts
		- [[pyelonephritis]]
		- Infection from upper tract
	- RTE (renal tubular epithelial cells) cast
		- Tubular injury
