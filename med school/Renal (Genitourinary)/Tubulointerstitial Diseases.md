#### Symptoms
- Mild proteinuria from tubular damage, not moderate to high like glomerular
- Mild hematuria, RBC casts ABSENT
- WBC casts present, muddy brown = [[ATN]]
- Low specific gravity and low osmolarity because cannot concentrate urine in tubules
	- Polyuria and nocturia
- ↑ Na excretion
