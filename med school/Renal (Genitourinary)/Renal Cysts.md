#### Renal Ciliopathies
*Disorders characterized by nephronophthisis, cystic kidneys or renal cystic
dysplasia whose underlying disease pathogenesis is related to abnormal structure or
function of the primary cilia complex.*

- Tubular epithelial cells have single nonmotile primary cilium
