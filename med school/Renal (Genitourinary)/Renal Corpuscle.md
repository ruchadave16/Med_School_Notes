#### Filtration Apparatus
*Separates capillary lumen from urinary space*
- Glomerular endothelium – capillary fenestrations
- Glomerular [[basement membrane]] – fused basal lamina of endothelial + [[Podocyte|podocytes]]
	- Structural barrier
	- Electron dense core = lamina densa
	- Electron lucent peripheral layers = lamina rara interna and externa
- Mesangial cells provide support (contractile) and make [[collagen]] matrix
- Visceral layer of [[Bowman's Capsule|Bowman’s Capsule]] – filtration slits between pedicels

[[Glomerular Filtration]] – method by which [[plasma]] is filtered in glomerular capillaries

Arterial pressure pushes fluid from blood through filtration barrier into urinary space
SIZE LIMIT - 70 kDa
#### Disease
- Presents with ↑ [[proteinuria]] or ↑ hematuria

**Nomenclature**
- Focal: <50% glomeruli
- Diffuse: >50% glomeruli involved
- Segmental: part of a glomerulus
- Global: entire glomerulus
- Proliferative: hypercellular
- Membranous: basement membranes are thickened
- Primary vs secondary

**Pathology**
1) Deposition of circulating antigen-antibody complexes in capillary wall
	- [[C5a]] attracts [[Neutrophils]] to location
2) Antibodies react in situ within glomerulus
