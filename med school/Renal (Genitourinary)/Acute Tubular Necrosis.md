### Etiology
- Ischemic: severe hypotension causes ischemia
- Nephrotoxic: injury by drugs or endogenous substances
	- [[Aminoglycosides]], contrast agents, [[cisplatin]], [[myoglobin]] from [[rhabdomyolysis]], hemoglobin from [[Hemolysis|hemolysis]], uric acid from [[Tumor Lysis Syndrome]]
	- [[Amphotericin B|amphotericin]]

### Pathophysiology
- Injury and necrosis of tubular epithelial cells caused DIRECTLY by agent, NOT inflammatory response
	- Ischemic: Patchy necrosis of cells in proximal and thick ascending -> most susceptible to hypoxia
	- Nephrotoxic: more diffuse necrosis

**Phases**
- Initiating Phase: 
	- 36 hrs after initial injury, only slight ↓ urine
- Maintenance Phase: 1-3 weeks
	- sustained oliguria, fluid overload, ↑ BUN, electrolyte issues
- Recovery Phase
	- Diuresis as function increases

### Clinical Features
- Decline of GFR -> 
	- Uremia
	- Electrolyte imbalances
	- Fluid overload

### Diagnosis
- "Muddy brown" casts 
- Urinalysis: Low fractional excretion of sodium (FENa) in pre-renal azotemia (<1%) but high FENa in established ATN (>2%)

### Treatment
- If urate based: [[rasburicase]] and dialysis
- Rhabdo: IV fluids + dialysis
