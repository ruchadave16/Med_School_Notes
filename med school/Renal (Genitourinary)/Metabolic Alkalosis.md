*Alkalosis NOT due to change in $CO_2$ elimination*

**Causes** – alkali addition or acid loss: vomiting of HCl, overdose of alkaline drugs, higher renal excretion of $H^+$

1) [[Buffers]]: ECF $HCO_3^-$ ↑ because of $H^+$ loss or $HCO_3^-$ gain
2) [[Respiratory Regulation of Acid-Base|Lungs]]: pH ↑ decreases ventilation → $P_{CO2}$ ↑ → pH ↓ (limited response) and ↑ HCO3-
3) [[Renal Regulation of Acid-Base|Kidneys]]: 
	- ↑ excretion of $HCO_3^-$  (can't reabsorb all of it) 
	- ↑ renal cell pH so lower secretion of $H^+$ so less acidic urine
	- ↓ NH4+ production
	- ECF pH ↓ to normal so ventilation increases to normal

**NORMAL Compensation**: pCO2 = 0.7 mmHg increase for every 1 mEq/L ↑ of HCO3-
