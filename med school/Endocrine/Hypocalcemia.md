*Low [[blood calcium]] -- Ca < 8.5 mg/dL or < 2.1 mmol/L*
Correct for [[Albumin]]: increase by 0.8 mg/dL for every 1 g/dL that albumin is below 4
#### Potential Causes
- ↓ [[PTH]]
	- Recent surgery
	- Autoimmune (rare) -- [[APS-1]]
	- [[DiGeorge Syndrome]]
- ↓ [[Vitamin D]]
	- Nutritional deficiency
	- Malabsorption -- celiac, Crohns, 
	- Kidney disease (here PTH ↑↑)
- [[hypomagnesemia]]
	- Chronic causes PTH resistance -- downregulatin
- Calcium deposits
	- Acute necrotizing pancreatitis
	- Osteoblastic metastases
	- ↑ Phosphate levels
	- Blood transfusion
- [[Alkalosis]]: Dissociation of hydrogen ions from albumin so Ca2+ binds instead
- [[Pseudohypoparathyroidism]]
#### Symptoms
- Hyperreflexia
- Paresthesias around mouth and fingers
- Twitching
- Muscle cramps and spasms
- Mood changes
- Numbness
#### Signs
- **Chvostek sign** – twitching of face muscles by tapping on [[facial nerve]]
- **Trousseau sign** – carpopedal spasm when BP cuff inflated
- Rickets
- EKG: Prolonged QT interval
#### Diagnosis
- Serum calcium, phosphate, creatinine, Vitamin D, and albumin
- Check magnesium
	- Check PTH if needed
#### Treatment
- Acute and symptomatic
	- IV calcium, calcium gluconate (peripheral venous cath), calcium chloride (central venous cath)
	- Correct hypomagnesemia with IV magnesium sulfate
- Chronic
	- Oral calcium citrate and vitamin D supplements
	- Active vitamin D3 (calcitriol) repletion if need to bypass kidneys
