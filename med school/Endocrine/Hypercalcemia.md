*High [[blood calcium]] -- Ca > 10.5 mg/dL or >2.62 mmol/L*
#### Causes
- [[Acidosis]]
- ↑ PTH
	- parathyroid adenoma
	- [[Addison Disease]]
	- [[Tertiary Hyperparathyroidism]]
- Malignancy
	- ↑ PTHrP ([[Squamous cell carcinoma]] of the lung, squamous cell cancer of the head and neck)
	- ↑ PTH (lung and ovarian tumors)
	- ↑ Vit D ([[lymphoma]])
- Bony invasion from malignancy
	- Bone cancer, multiple myeloma, or metastases from other malignancies
- [[Paget disease of bone]]
- ↑ Vit D intake
- Granulomatous disease
- Familial hypocalciuric hypercalcemia
#### Symptoms
*Stones, bones, groans, psych overtones*
- Kidney stones
- Bone pain, fractures, or osteopenia/ osteoporosis (↑ reabsorption)
- Abdominal pain (constipation, Polyuria and polydipsia, nausea, vomiting, gout, peptic ulcer)
- Neuro / psych (Hyporeflexia, lethargy, memory loss, depression)
#### Treatment
- Ca < 12 mg/dL
	- Avoid calcium
	- Avoid Vitamin D supplementation
	- Stop thiazide diuretics and lithium
	- Encourage oral hydration
	- Treat the underlying cause\
- 12 mg/dL (3 mmol/L) and 14 mg/dL (3.5 mmol/L)
	- IV saline
	- Subcutaneous calcitonin
	- Bisphosphonate
