### Etiology
- Overdose of [[insulin]]
- Insulin-secreting tumor of pancreas 
- [[Alcoholism]] causing depletion of glycogen stores

### Pathophysiology
- Damage to brain

### Clinical Features
- Flushed face, sweating, headache, trembling
- Spasms, decerebrate rigidity
- Deep coma, shallow respirations, slow pulse

### Diagnosis
- Significant lowering of glucose
	- Signs at 30 mg/dL
	- Coma at 10 mg/dL

### Treatment
- Glucose replacement therapy

### Complications
- Short-term
- Long-term
