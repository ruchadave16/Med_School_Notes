*Monophasic demyelinating disease*
### Etiology
- Rapid correction of [[hyponatremia]]
	- Skin burns, SIADH, polydipsia
- Risk: Severe chronic hypoNa < 110, Alcoholism, liver failure or history of orthotopic liver transplant, Malnutrition

### Pathophysiology
- Excessive loss of myelin in pons, reactive astrocytes and lipid laden macrophages
- Brain shrinkage
![[Screenshot 2026-04-14 at 9.08.14 PM.png]]
### Clinical Features
- Rapid onset confusion
- Limb weakness
- Conjugate gaze palsy
	- [[Locked In Syndrome]]
- Dysarthria
- Hypotension
- Hyperreflexia
