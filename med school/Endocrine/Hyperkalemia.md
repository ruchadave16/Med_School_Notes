- Normally, K+ concentration maintained by renal excretion and cellular uptake through Na+/K+ pump

>[!Warning] $>$ 6–7 mM = medical emergency 
#### Causes
- Reduced renal excretion 
	- [[Renal failure]]
	- [[ACE Inhibitors]]
	- [[spironolactone]]
- Escape of intracellular K+
	- [[Metabolic acidosis]] (inorganic acid),
	- Tissue breakdown
	- Crushing injury
	- Transfusion of expired blood 
	- Hemolysis
#### Physiology
- Reduces equilibrium potential (normally K+ is found in high quantities intracellularly)
	- ↓ [[Cardiac Ventricular Action Potential|cardiac action potential]]
	- Stops Na+ channels from resetting from inactivated state -> closed but activateable
- This reduces *fast Na+ channels* conduction

#### Symptoms
- Arrhythmias 
#### ECG
- Widened QRS (slow electrical propagation)
- tall T wave -- enhanced repolarization K+ current
