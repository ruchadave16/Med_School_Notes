*Gland connected to [[hypothalamus]] by stalk (infundibulum) made of 2 glands*

Located in [[brain]] in sella turcica 
- Suspended by **infundibulum** from [[hypothalamus]]
#### Components
- [[Anterior Pituitary]] (Adenohypophysis) – glandular tissue
- [[Posterior Pituitary]] (Neurohypophysis) – nervous tissue

![[Screenshot 2026-10-19 at 1.20.28 PM.png]]

#### Common Disease Manifestations
- Anatomical: [[bitemporal hemianopia]], headaches, nausea, vomiting
- [[Pituitary apoplexy]] -- acute hemorrhage into pituitary gland -> emergency
- Excessive production of hormones -- adenomas
- Ischemia, surgery, radiation, inflammation, etc. can cause pituitary damage

>[!Clinical]
>[[Diabetes Insipidus]]
>- ↓ [[Vasopressin|ADH]] causes water to not be reabsorbed by kidneys
>- High thirst and dilute urine
