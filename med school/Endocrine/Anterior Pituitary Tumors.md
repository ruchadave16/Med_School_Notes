- Incidence = 35-60 yrs
	- Functional = they produce hormones.
	- Nonfunctional = they do not produce hormones.
	- Silent = hormone production is detectable in cells, but there are no clinical manifestations of hormone excess
- Microadenomas = less than 1 cm in diameter
- Macroadenomas = greater than 1 cm in diameter 
#### Lactotroph Adenomas
- [[Prolactin]]-secreting
- Most common
- Amenorrhea, galactorrhea, ↓ libido, impotence
- Infertility

#### Somatotroph Adenomas
- [[Growth Hormone|GH]] secreting -> 2nd most common
- [[Gigantism]] in children, [[acromegaly]] in adults
- Metabolic abnormalities -> [[diabetes mellitus]]
- ↑ production of **IGF-1** 
	- *IGF-1 levels used to diagnose*

#### Corticotroph Adenomas
- [[ACTH]] production causes ↑ [[cortisol]] causing [[Cushing Disease]]
