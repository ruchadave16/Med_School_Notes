*Enoxaparin, Dalteparin, Tinzaparin*
#### Action
- Enhanced activity of [[antithrombin III]]
- Shorter carbohydrate chain means it primarily inhibits Factor Xa 
	- Minimal activity against thrombin
#### Uses
- [[Deep Vein Thrombosis|DVT]]and [[Pulmonary Embolism|PE]]
- Bridging anticoagulation during perioperative [[warfarin]] interruption
- [[Prenatal Period|pregnancy]] anticoagulation
#### Reversal
- Partial with [[protamine sulfate]]
#### Dosing
- Subcutaneous preferred
- Rapid onset within few hours
- Longer duration than [[heparin]] (4-6 hrs)
- Dosing = 1-2x daily

**Monitoring**
- Anti-Xa level may be used in special cases (e.g., renal impairment, pregnancy, obesity)
#### Adverse Effects
- Bleeding -- partial reversal with [[Protamine sulfate]]
- [[Heparin Induced Thrombocytopenia|HIT]] -- lower risk than [[heparin]]
- Renal clearance
#### Contraindication
