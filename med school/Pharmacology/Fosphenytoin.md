#### Action

#### Uses
- [[Focal seizures]]
- [[Generalized tonic clonic seizures]]
- [[Status epilepticus]]

#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- **0th order kinetics** so small change makes a huge impact

#### Adverse Effects
- Gingival hyperplasia
- Ataxia, diplopia, nystagmus
- Hirsutism
- Teratogenic

**Phenytoin causes [[purple glove syndrome]]** so only give fosphenytoin
