*[[Vasodilators]] used to relieve and prevent angina by reducing myocardial oxygen demand and improving coronary blood flow*

*[[Nitroglycerin]], Isosorbide*
#### Action
- [[Nitric oxide]] donors
- NO activates guanylate cyclase -> increases cGMP
- Dephosphorylation of myosin light chains -> smooth muscle dilation
#### Uses
- Nitroglycerin -- Acute angina relief (sublingual)
	- Adjunct in hypertensive emergencies
	- [[Acute heart failure]] with pulmonary edema
	- IV for pulmonary edema in preeclampsia
- Isosorbide
	- Chronic angina prophylaxis

#### Contraindication
- [[PDE Inhibitors]] (PDE-5) like sildenafil

- Acute circulatory shock
