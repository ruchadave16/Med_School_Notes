#### Action
- Endothelin-1 receptor antagonist 
	- ETA = vasoconstriction and smooth muscle proliferation
	- ETB = clear endothelin and promote vasodilation

[[Bosentan]] = ETA/ETB antagonist
[[Ambrisentan]] = ETA antagonist
[[Macitentan]] = ETA/ETB antagonist
