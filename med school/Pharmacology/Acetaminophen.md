- Metabolized by [[liver]], small fraction makes toxic NAPQI
#### Action
- Weak COX inhibition in CNS
- Minimal peripheral anti-inflammatory
- Reduces PGE2 -> lowers fever
#### Uses
- All ages
- Safe for pts getting anti-coagulants
#### Dosing
- 10-15 mg/kg/dose
- 4-6 hr dosing
#### Adverse Effects
- Generally minimal GI impact <- fewer than NSAIDs
- Hepatotoxicity
	- Normally NAPQI -> GSH but in overdose, (7.5-10g), high formation of NAPQI
	- Hepatic necrosis
	- Liver injury signs = RUQ pain, ↑ liver enzymes, jaundice

**Treatment of Toxicity**
- NAC (N-acetylcysteine) to restore glutathione stores
	- GIVE within 8-10 hours
#### Contraindication
- Chronic alcohol use
- Malnutrition
- Liver conditions
