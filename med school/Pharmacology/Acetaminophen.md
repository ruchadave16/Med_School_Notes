#### Action
- COX inhibition in CNS
- Minimal peripheral anti-inflammatory
- Reduces PGE2 -> lowers fever
#### Uses
- All ages

#### Dosing
- 10-15 mg/kg/dose
- 4-6 hr dosing
#### Adverse Effects
- Generally minimal GI impact
#### Contraindication
- Hepatotoxicity with multiple dosing
