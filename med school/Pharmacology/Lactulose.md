#### Action
- Contain large, poorly absorbed ions or molecules that draw water into the colon or rectum through osmosis to stimulate a bowel movement
- ↓ NH4 levels by converting ammonia (NH3) into ammonium (NH4+)
#### Uses
- [[Hepatic Encephalopathy|hepatic encephalopathy]] -> FIRST LINE
- Osmotic laxative
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Dose so that 2-3 soft movements per day

#### Adverse Effects
- Bloating
- Abdominal cramping
