#### Action
- Activates adenosine receptors -> AV block
- Stops re-entry circuits in AV dependent tachycardias
#### Uses
- Paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia ([[SVT]])
- Diagnostic agent in tachyarrhythmias
- Pharmacologic stress testing
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Rapid IV push and then flush with saline

#### Adverse Effects

#### Contraindication
- AV block
- Sick sinus syndrome (unless paced)
- Asthma/COPD
- Preexcited atrial fibrillation
