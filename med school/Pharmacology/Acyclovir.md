Also famciclovir/valacyclovir in same class
#### Action
- Nucleoside analog
- Converted to acyclovir monophosphate after uptake by thymidine kinase
	- **Enzyme is NOT in human cells**
- Converted to acyclovir triphosphate -- inhibitor of DNA polymerase
#### Uses
- Against [[HSV1]] and [[HSV2]]
#### Resistance
- ↓ or altered thymidine kinase
#### Dosing

#### Adverse Effects
- Nephrotoxicity -- NEED hydration
#### Contraindication
