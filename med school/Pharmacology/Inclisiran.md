#### Action
- siRNA that targets PCSK9 mRNA, binds to it and induced degradation
	- ↓ PCSK9 synthesis so ↑ LDL receptors and ↑ LDL clearance
- Works inside the [[liver]]
#### Uses
- Heterozygous [[Familial Hypercholesterolemia]]
- High-risk primary prevention patients not at LDL goal with statins ± ezetimibe
- Statin-intolerant patients
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Subcutaneous injection
	- Initially, then at 3 months, then every 6 months
- No drug interactions
#### Adverse Effects
- Nasopharyngitis
#### Contraindication
