#### Action
- Ca2+ channel inhibitor
- GABA enhancer
- Na+ channel inhibitor
#### Uses
- Broad spectrum [[epilepsy]]
- [[Migraines]] prevention
- [[Bipolar I]] -- acute mania
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- **[[Cytochrome P450s|cytochrome P450]] inhibitor**

#### Adverse Effects
- Hepatotoxicity
- Pancreatitis
- Weight gain, hirsutism
- ↑↑ teratogenic - 1-2% have neural tube defects
#### Contraindication
