#### Action
- ↓ [[VLDL]] synthesis
- ↑ [[beta oxidation]]
- ↓ [[lipogenesis]] and ↑ clearance
#### Uses
- Severe [[Hypertriglyeremia]] -- Lovaza
- Adjunct to [[statins]]
	- Safe in pregnancy
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
**High doses (≥2–4 g/day)**

- Omega-3 acid ethyl esters (Lovaza)
	- EPA + DHA
	- Prescribed
- Icosapent ethyl (Vascepa)
	- EPA only
	- Reduces CV events if added to [[Statins|statin]] therapy
	- Does NOT raise LDL
- Fish oil supplements (OTC)
#### Adverse Effects
- GI symptoms
- ↑ bleeding risk
- ↑ LDL-C for the ones with DHA
#### Contraindication
