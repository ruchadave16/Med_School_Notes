#### Action
- Prodrug, Works best in acidic environments
- Affects membrane transport
#### Uses
- Active [[Tuberculosis|TB]]
	- Shortening therapy from 9–12 months to 6 months
#### Resistance
- Mutations in the pncA gene (pyrazinamidase)
- Resistance is common in multidrug-resistant TB (MDR-TB)
#### Dosing

#### Adverse Effects
- Hyperuricemia
- Hepatotoxic
- Arthralgia, rash, GI upset
#### Contraindication
