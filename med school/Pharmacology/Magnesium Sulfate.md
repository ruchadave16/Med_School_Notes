#### Action
- Stabilizes excitable membranes
- Slows L-type Ca channels
#### Uses
- [[Torsades de Pointes]]
- [[Eclampsia]] or [[preeclampsia]] seizure prophylaxis
- Adjunct in severe [[asthma]] / [[COPD]]

#### Resistance

#### Dosing

#### Adverse Effects
- Hyporeflexia
- Respiratory depression
- Hypotension (especially with IV overuse)

#### Contraindication
- Use carefully if renal impairment
