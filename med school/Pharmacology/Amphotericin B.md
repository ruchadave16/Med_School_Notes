#### Action
- Binds to [[ergosterol]] and makes holes in cell membrane 
	- Ions leak out and cell death
#### Uses
- Broad spectrum antifungal -- covers majority EXCEPT aspergillus
- First line for [[mucor]]
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
Ambisome = liposomal version, commonly used
- Higher CNS penetration
#### Adverse Effects
- Electrolyte imbalance and nephrotoxicity
#### Contraindication
