*[[Cilostazol]], [[Dipyridamole]]*
#### Action
- Block [[phosphodiesterase]] III (enzyme that breaks down [[cAMP]])
- ↑ cAMP so ↓ response to ADP -> ↓ platelet aggregation
- Also ↓ breakdown of cGMP in smooth muscle so [[vasodilation]]
#### Uses
- Cilostazol
	- ↑ walking distance for pt with [[PAD]] and intermittent claudification
	- Use with [[aspirin]] for stroke prevention
- Dipyridamole
	- Use with [[aspirin]] to prevent ischemic stroke
