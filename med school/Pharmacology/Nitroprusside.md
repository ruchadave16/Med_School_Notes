#### Action
- Converts to [[Nitric Oxide|nitric oxide]] -> ↑ cGMP 
- Vasodilation of arterioles and veins equally
#### Uses
- [[Hypertensive emergency]]
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Rapid onset and effect (IV)
#### Adverse Effects
- [[Cyanide]] toxicity
- **BP and metabolic monitoring in ICU setting**
#### Contraindication
