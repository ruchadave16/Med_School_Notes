*Abciximab, Eptifibatide, Tirofiban*
#### Action
- During platelet activation, GPIIb/IIIa receptors are expressed on the platelet surface
- These bind to [[fibrinogen]] and allow cross-bridging
- Blocking these stops binding of fibrinogen
#### Uses
- Adjunct for PCI
- High-risk NSTEMI/unstable angina
- Bridging therapy before surgery
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- IV only, short term use in hospital setting

**Monitor platelet counts**
#### Adverse Effects
- Bleeding
- [[Thrombocytopenia]]
- Hypersensitivity rxn  -- abciximab
#### Contraindication
- Active internal bleeding
- Hemorrhagic stroke
- Thrombocytopenia
- Major surgery in 4-6 weeks
