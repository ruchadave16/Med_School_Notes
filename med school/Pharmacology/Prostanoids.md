*Local acting [[Lipids|lipid]] mediators that come from [[arachidonic acid]]*
- [[Prostaglandins]] (PGs) -- inflammation, pain sensitivity, fever
- [[Prostacyclin]] (PGI2) -- vasodilation, ↓ platelet aggregation
- Thromboxane A2 ([[TXA2]]) -- ↑ platelet aggregation, vasoconstriction

![[Screenshot 2026-03-31 at 8.33.11 AM.png]]

- PGH2 (Intermediate) made after COX1 and COX2
- This is precursor for all others
	- [[PGD2]]
	- [[PGF2a]]
	- [[PGE2]]
	- [[PGI2]] (prostacyclin)
	- [[TXA2]]
#### Receptors
- [[G Protein Coupled Receptors|GPCR]]

#### Clinical Use
- [[Misoprostol]] -- PGE1 analog
	- Used to prevent NSAID gastric ulcers and to induce labor
- [[Dinoprostone]] -- [[PGE2]]: inducing labor
- [[Carbaprost]] -- [[PGF2a]]: postpartum hemorrhage
- [[Alprostadil]] -- PGE1 analog
	- [[Patent ductus arteriosus]] (PDA) in neonates with congenital heart defects
- [[Epoprostenol]] -- [[PGI2]]
	- [[Pulmonary arterial hypertension]]
- [[Latanoprost]] -- [[PGF2a]]
	- First line for [[open angle glaucoma]]
