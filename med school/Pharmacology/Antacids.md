#### Action
- Chemical neutralization of gastric acid in stomach
	- Aluminum Hydroxide 
	- Calcium Carbonate (Tums)
	- Magnesium Hydroxide
#### Uses
- Rapid relief of heartburn (symptomatic) and dyspepsia

#### Adverse Effects
- Aluminum Hydroxide -> constipation
- Calcium Carbonate -> hyperacidity in rebound, [[Hypercalcemia]]
- Magnesium Hydroxide -> diarrhea
