#### Action
- [[Thrombin]] is a platelet activator through PAR-1 (receptor) on platelet surface
- Reversible inhibition of PAR-1 reduces platelet activation
- Stops this without affecting fibrin generation
#### Uses
- Prevention of thrombosis in pt with [[Myocardial Infarction|MI]]or [[PAD]] history
- Use with [[aspirin]] or [[clopidogrel]]
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Halflife = 8 days
- Oral use
- [[CYP3A4]] metabolism

#### Adverse Effects
- Bleeding risk especially intracranial hemorrhage
#### Contraindication
- Stroke, TIA, intracranial hemorrhage history
- Active pathological bleeding
