Original:
- Alpha: [[Epinephrine]] ≥ [[Norepinephrine]] > > [[Isoproterenol]]
- Beta: [[Isoproterenol]] > [[Epinephrine]] ≥ [[Norepinephrine]]
#### $\alpha1$: a1A, a1B, a1D
- Vascular smooth muscles – contraction
- Pupillary dilator – contraction to dilate 
- Pilomotor smooth muscle – hair erect
- Prostate $\alpha1A$ – contraction
- Heart – ↑ contraction force
#### $\alpha2$: a2A, a2B, a2C
- Platelet – aggregation
- Adrenergic and cholinergic terminals – stop transmitter release
- Some vascular smooth muscle – contraction
- Adipocytes – stop lipolysis
### $\beta 1$
- Heart – ↑ force and rate of contraction
	- ↑ HR (chronotropic effect)
	- ↑ AV velocity (dromotropic effect)
	- ↓ action potential length
	- ↑ contractile force (inotropic effect)
	- ↑ relaxation rate (lusitropic effect)
- Juxtaglomerular cells – ↑ renin release

**Mechanism**
- [[G Protein Coupled Receptors|Gs]] dissociates into $\alpha$ and $\beta \gamma$
- $\alpha_s$ activates adenylyl cyclase -> catalyzes transformation of ATPs into cAMPs
- cAMP interacts with *funny channels* of [[Pacemaker Cells|pacemaker cells]]
	- Faster initial decay
- cAMP activates PKA
	- PKA catalyzes the phosphorylation of L-type Ca2+ channels
		- [[Pacemaker Cells|Pacemaker cells]]: spike of depolarization is faster (chronotropic effect)
		- [[Cardiac Muscle|Cardiac myocytes]]: plateau (inotropic effect)
	- PKA phosphorylates slow rectifier K+ channels
		- shorter action potential
	- Phosphorylates [[phospholamban]] reducing its inhibition of Ca2+/ATP into SR
		- Lusitropic effect
- AV node conduction ↑ -> dromotropic effect
####  $\beta2$
- Respiratory, uterine, vascular smooth muscle – relax
- Skeletal muscle – tremor
- Liver – glycogenolysis, [[gluconeogenesis]]

#### $\beta3$
- Adipocytes – lipolysis
- Detrusor – relaxation
