*[[Riociguat]]*
#### Action
#### Uses
- [[Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension|PAH]] by stimulating sGC even when 0 NO
- ↑ cGMP production so ↑ vasodilation and ↓ resistance
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Oral, 3x daily
#### Adverse Effects
- Hypotension
• Dizziness
• Headache
• Gastrointestinal upset
• Teratogenicity
#### Contraindication
- [[PDE5 Inhibitors|PDE5i]] -- hypotension
