#### Action
- Inhibit 14-a methylase (converts lanosterol -> ergosterol)
#### Uses
- [[Cryptococcus]]
- Most Candida
- Voriconazole for [[Aspergillus Fumigatus]], [[scedosporium]]
#### Resistance
- Repeated use ends up causing mutation of genes for the enzyme that this targets
#### Dosing

#### Adverse Effects
- Interaction of Fluconazole with cyclosporine, sirolimus and tacrolimus
	- Fluconazole inhibits CYP450 and increases drug level
- Voriconazole -- phototoxicity
- Ketoconazole -- hepatotoxicity
- Itraconazole for dimorphic fungi
#### Contraindication
