#### Action
- Converted to metabolites
- Inhibits thymidylate synthase so ↓ dTMP so ↓ DNA synthesis
- Incorporates into RNA
#### Uses
- Solid tumors: colon, breast, ovarian cancer
- Topical for basal cell carcinoma

#### Dosing
