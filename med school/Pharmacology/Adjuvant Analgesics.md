*Meds that can enhance analgesia and are helpful for neuropathic pain or chronic syndromes*

**[[Gabapentin]] and [[pregabalin]] = 1st line for neuropathic pain**
#### [[Sodium Channel Inhibitors]]
- [[Lamotrigine]] -- neuropathic pain, mood stabilization
- [[Carbamazepine]] -- choice drug for trigeminal neuralgia
#### [[Tricyclic Antidepressants|TCAs]]
- [[Amitriptyline]] -- Neuropathic pain, fibromyalgia, migraine prevention
- [[Imipramine]] -- Diabetic neuropathy, postherpetic neuralgia
#### [[Serotonin Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitors|SNRIs]]
- [[Duloxetine]] -- Diabetic neuropathy, fibromyalgia, chronic musculoskeletal pain
- [[Venlafaxine]] -- 2nd line for neuropathic pain
#### [[Antiepileptic Drugs]]
- [[Gabapentin]] -- Postherpetic neuralgia, diabetic neuropathy, fibromyalgia
- [[Pregabalin]] -- Neuropathic pain, spinal cord injury pain
#### [[Anesthetics]]
- [[Lidocaine]] 5% patch -- Postherpetic neuralgia, localized neuropathic pain
- [[Capsaicin]] -- Neuropathic pain
