- Binds directly to pyrophosphate binding site of DNA polymerase
	- Stops nucleotide binding
- Inhibits ALL herpes and HIV reverse transcriptase
#### Uses
- [[Cytomegalovirus|CMV]] retinitis
- [[HIV]]
#### Resistance
