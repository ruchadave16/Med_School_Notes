#### Action
- Bind to negatively charged [[bile acid]] in intestine to stop reabsorption
	- Excretion of bile acid in stool
- Liver uses up more cholesterol for [[bile acid synthesis]]
	- ↑ LDL receptors so LDL clearance ↑ 
#### Uses
- [[Hypercholesterolemia]]
- Adjunct with [[statins]]
- **CAN USE in pregnancy**
	- No systemic absorption
#### Classes
- Cholestyramine (powder)
- Colestipol (tablet)
- Colesevelam -- better tolerated GI 
#### Dosing
- Binds other oral meds (e.g., digoxin, warfarin, thiazides) → separate administration by ≥1–2 hours
#### Adverse Effects
- GI upset
- ↓ Vitamin A, D, E, K
- Drug interactions
#### Contraindication
- Avoid in patients with high triglycerides
