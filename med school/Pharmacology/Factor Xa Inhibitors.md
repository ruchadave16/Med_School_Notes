*-xaban suffix*
#### Action
- Selective inhibition of Factor Xa in common pathway of [[coagulation cascade]]
- No conversion of prothrombin -> [[thrombin]]
#### Uses
- [[Deep Vein Thrombosis|DVT]]and [[Pulmonary Embolism|PE]]
- Stroke prevention for non-valvular afib
- Postoperative thromboprophylaxis
#### Reversal
- [[Andexanet alfa]]: A decoy protein that binds Factor Xa inhibitors, allowing natural Factor Xa to function normally.
#### Dosing
- Oral administration
- Rapid onset, duration = 10--14 hrs
- Typically once or twice daily

**No monitoring**
#### Adverse Effects
- Bleeding risk
- Metabolized by [[CYP3A4]] and [[P Glycoprotein]]
#### Contraindication
