#### Action
- CMV has viral protein kinase that phosphorylates ganciclovir to make 5' monophosphate then triphosphate
- Inhibits ALL herpes DNA polymerase
#### Uses
- [[Cytomegalovirus|CMV]]
- Generally ALL herpes virus DNA polymerase
#### Resistance
- Mutation in gene encoding UL97 phosphotransferase -- does initial phosphorylation
- Mutation in DNA polymerase -- UL54
#### Dosing

#### Adverse Effects
- Pancytopenia
#### Contraindication
