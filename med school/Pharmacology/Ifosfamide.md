#### Action
- [[Alkylating Agents]] -- cross links DNA by alkylating guanine bases
#### Uses
- Solid tumors -- breast, ovarian cancer
- Hematologic cancers: Leukemia, lymphoma
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Hepatic activation (prodrug)
- Prevent hemorrhagic cystitis by co-administering with **MESNA** -- binds and inactivates acrolein
#### Adverse Effects
- Hemorrhagic cystitis -- caused by toxic metabolite [[acrolein]] accumulating in the [[Urinary Bladder|bladder]]
	- PREVENT: MESNA
- Neurotoxicity
- Myelosuppression
#### Contraindication
