*Drugs that reversibly block pain nerve conduction without affecting consciousness*
#### Properties
- Weak bases with pKa 7.5 - 9
	- Lipophilic version can diffuse across membrane
	- Once inside, it becomes protonated and binds to intracellular portion of receptor
	
>[!Note] In **acidic** environment, ↑↑ drug portion is ionized and stays outside, less effective
#### Mechanism
- Block volt-gated Na+ channels to stop depolarization
	- Binds to open and inactivated forms of these channels <- binds to pain "actively firing" nerves more than resting nerves
- No action potential propagation

**Vasoconstriction**
- [[Alpha agonists]] added to cause vasoconstriction <- mostly [[Epinephrine|epinephrine]]
	- ↓ blood flow so ↑ local concentration and ↓ systemic effect
	
>[!Warning] Do NOT use in areas with ↓ collateral circulation 
##### Differing Sensitivity

>[!Note] Pain > Temperature > Touch > Proprioception > Motor

1) Fiber Diameter
	- ↓ diameter blocked faster than larger fibers (shorter axon to block)
2) Myelination
	- Myelinated blocked more than unmyelinated
	- Myelinated only need to block at Nodes of Ranvier
3) Firing Rate
	- **Rapidly** firing are blocked more than slower ones
4) Type of Fiber
	- Autonomic B fibers = most sensitive
	- Pain fibers ($A\delta$ and C) = faster than the larger fibers
	- Large motor ($A\alpha$) = least sensitive

#### Different Anesthetics
- [[Lidocaine]] -- medium duration
- [[Bupivacaine]] -- long duration
- [[Ropivacaine]] -- long duration
- [[Procaine]] -- short duration
- [[Tetracaine]] -- long duration
#### Use Cases
- Topical Anesthesia -- directly applied
- Infiltration anesthesia -- inject into tissue without targeting nerve
- Peripheral nerve block -- apply near peripheral nerve or plexus
- [[Epidural block]]
- [[Spinal block]]
- [[Caudal block]]
#### Negative Effects
- CNS Toxicity
	- Lightheadedness, dizziness, visual or auditory disturbances
	- Seizures or coma if serious
- CV Toxicity
	- Myocardial depression 
	- Hypotension
	- Arrythmias
- Allergic reaction
	- More common with ester based 
- Local toxicity
	- Direct nerve injury
	- Transient Neurologic Symptoms
