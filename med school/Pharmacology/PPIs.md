*Omeprazole, pantoprazole, esomeprazole*
#### Action
- Inhibit the final step of acid production
- Irreversibly inhibit the H+/K+-ATPase enzyme on [[Parietal Cell|Parietal cells]]
#### Uses
- First line for [[GERD]] and [[Peptic Ulcer Disease|peptic ulcers]]
- Parts of [[H. Pylori]] treatment
- [[Zollinger-Ellison Syndrome]]
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- 30-60 min before 1st meal 

#### Adverse Effects
- Chronic use -> nutrient deficiencies, [[B12 Deficiency]]
- [[Osteoporosis]]
- [[hypomagnesemia]]
- ↑ [[C. Diff]]infection rate
- **fundic gland polyps and increased pneumonia risk.**
#### Contraindication
