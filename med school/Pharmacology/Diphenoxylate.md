#### Action
- $\mu$ agonist that DOES cross BBB
#### Uses
- [[Diarrhea]]
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Give with [[Atropine]] to minimize CNS effects
#### Adverse Effects
- Constipation
- [[Toxic megacolon]] in infectious diarrhea
#### Contraindication
- Contraindicated in bacterial enterocolitis caused by invasive organisms.
