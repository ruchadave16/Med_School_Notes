- [[Beta blockers]]
#### Action
- Block beta receptors so ↓ conduction of SA and AV nodes
	- β1-selective agents (e.g., metoprolol, atenolol) → block β1 receptors in the heart
	- Non-selective agents (e.g., propranolol, nadolol) → block β1 and β2 receptors
	- Mixed α/β agents (e.g., carvedilol, labetalol) → also block α1 receptors, causing vasodilation
- Acts on phase 4 of action potential in [[pacemaker cells]]
- **rate control**
#### Uses
- [[Afib]] / [[atrial flutter]] for rate control
- [[SVT]]
- Ventricular ectopy or nonsustained VT
- Prevention of death post [[Myocardial Infarction|MI]]
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- [[Esmolol]]: ultra-short acting, ideal for acute IV use
#### Adverse Effects
- Bradyarrhythmias, AV block
- Fatigue, depression, sleep disturbances
- Erectile dysfunction
- Bronchospasm (especially with non-selective agents)
- Masking of hypoglycemia in diabetics
- Rebound tachycardia or angina with abrupt withdrawal
- 
Boxed Warning: Avoid sudden discontinuation — taper over 1–2 weeks
#### Contraindication
- Sinus bradycardia
- AV block (without pacemaker)
- Decompensated heart failure
- Severe asthma/COPD (non-selective β-blockers)
- Cardiogenic shock
