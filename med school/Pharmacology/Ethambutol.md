#### Action
- Inhibits arabinosyl transferase -- layer in cell wall
- Increased cell wall permeability
	- Stops growth, doesn't kill
#### Uses
- [[Tuberculosis|TB]]
	- Discontinued if TB strain is sensitive to INH and RIF
#### Resistance
- Mutations in embB gene
#### Dosing
- Discontinue if no resistance
#### Adverse Effects
- Optic neuritis

>[!Note] Requires baseline and periodic visual acuity testing
#### Contraindication
These ppl can't report eyesight changes
- Very young children
- Unconscious patients
