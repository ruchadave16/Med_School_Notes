#### Action
- Inhibition of ATP citrate lyase (enzyme in [[cholesterol synthesis]] pathway)
- ↓ cholesterol so liver upregulates LDL receptors → ↑ LDL clearance from blood→ ↓ LDL cholesterol
- Only in **liver** so LOWER muscle side effects
#### Uses
- Adjunct to [[statins]] or combo with [[Ezetimibe]]
- Statin intolerant pts
- Heterozygous [[familial hypercholesterolemia]]
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
Prodrug: activated only in the liver 
- No drug interactions

**Dose**
- Oral tablet 1x day
- Bempedoic acid + ezetimibe (combo tablet) also exists, oral 1x day
#### Adverse Effects
- [[Hyperuricemia]]
- Tendon rupture -- more common in older adults or those on corticosteroids
- Monitor LFTs
- Rare muscle symptoms
#### Contraindication
