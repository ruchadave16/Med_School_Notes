#### Action
- Directly bind to and inhibit [[thrombin]], stopping clot formation and propagation
#### Uses
- [[Heparin Induced Thrombocytopenia|HIT]] -- argatroban, bivalirudin
- Anticoagulation during PCI -- bivalirudin
- Stroke prevention for non valvular afib -- dabigatran
- Venous thromboembolism prevention -- dabigatran
#### Reversal
- Prothrombin complex concentrate (PCC)
- Tranexamic acid (antifibrinolytic)
#### Dosing
- IV/SubQ: [[Bivalirudin]], [[Argatroban]]
	- Monitored in hospital setting
- Oral: [[Dabigatran]]
	- No monitoring needed
	
**Don't discontinue randomly**
#### Adverse Effects
- Bleeding
- GI symptoms (dyspepsia with dabigatran)
- Hypersensitivity rxn with lepirudin
