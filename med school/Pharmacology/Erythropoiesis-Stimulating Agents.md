- *Darbepoetin alfa (Aranesp)
• Epoetin alfa (Epogen, Procrit, Retacrit)*
#### Action
#### Uses
- [[Chronic kidney disease]] anemia
- Cancer chemotherapy anemia
- [[HIV]] or [[AIDS]] anemia
- Chronic inflammatory diseases
- Pre-surgical anemia
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Confirm iron stores are adequate first (check 3x first)
- Initiate if Hb < 10 g/mL
#### Adverse Effects
- Hypertension, especially if renal failure
- Iron deficiency
- Thrombosis
- Tumor progression
#### Contraindication
