*Clopidogrel, Prasugrel, Ticagrelor, Cangrelor*
#### Action
- Platelet activation causes ADP release
	- Binds to P2Y12 receptors
- Activation of P2Y12 = ↑ expression of GPIIb/IIIa receptors -> fibrinogen-dependent
platelet aggregation
- These block ADP-mediated platelet activation and aggregation
#### Uses
- [[Acute coronary syndrome]] (NSTEMI, STEMI, unstable angina)
- Post PCE (stent) along with [[aspirin]]
- Secondary prevention of MI / stroke
- Aspirin allergy
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Clopidogrel = prodrug, needs [[CYP2C19]] activation
	- Different genetic polymorphisms
- Prasugrel = prodrug which is more potent but ↑ bleeding risk
- Ticagrelor = faster onset with no activation needed
- Cangrelor = IV drug
	- Rapid effect
#### Adverse Effects
- Bleeding
- Dyspnea (Ticagrelor)
- Thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura -- **Rare but serious**
	- clopidogrel
- [[Neutropenia]] (Ticagrelor)

#### Contraindication
