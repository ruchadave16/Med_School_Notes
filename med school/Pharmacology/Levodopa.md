#### Action
- Precursor to [[dopamine]]
- Absorbed in GI by L-AA transporter
- Crosses [[Blood Brain Barrier|BBB]]
- Converted to dopamine in CNS by DOPA decarboxylase
#### Uses
- [[Parkinson's Disease|Parkinson's]]
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Use with [[Carbidopa]]
#### Adverse Effects
- Peripheral conversion is useless and causes side effects
- Nausea, vomiting
- Arrhythmias, hypertension
- Dyskinesias, personality change 
#### Contraindication
- [[Psychosis]]
- [[Closed angle glaucoma]]
- [[MAO Inhibitors]]
