#### Action
1)  ↓ excessive neuron firing at seizure focus point
2) ↓ spread of seizure activity to other regions

Targets: Voltage gated Na+ channels, Ca2+ channels, and K+ channels
#### Pharmacokinetics
- Oral administration well absorbed
- Highly protein bound and changes in binding can change drug levels

**Hepatic metabolism** -- [[Cytochrome P450s|cytochrome P450]]
- Strong [[Cytochrome P450s|cytochrome P450]] inducers: these increase metabolism of other drugs and reduce their effect
	- [[carbamazepine]]
	- [[phenytoin]]
	- [[phenobarbital]]
	- [[primidone]]
- Moderate inducers:
	- [[Oxcarbazepine]]
	- Eslicarbazepine
	- Rufinamide
	- [[topiramate]]
	- Felbamate
- Enzyme Inhibitors: inhibit metabolism and raise other drug levels in blood
	- [[Valproate]]
	- stiripentol
- Minimal effect
	- [[levetiracetam]]
	- [[gabapentin]]
	- [[pregabalin]]
	- [[lacosamide]]
	- [[tiagabine]]
	- [[vigabatrin]]
	- [[ethosuximide]]

- Topiramate
	- Cognitive slowing
	• Weight loss
	• Kidney stones
- Lamotrigine
	- Stevens Johnson

**Suicidal behavior warning with abrupt discontinuation**
#### Contraindication
Valproate in pregnancy
