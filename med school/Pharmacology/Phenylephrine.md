*$\alpha1$ agonist*
- Can activate $\beta$ receptors at very high dose
#### Action
- Activates $\alpha1$ receptor on vascular smooth muscle
- Peripheral vascular resistance ↑ 
- ↑ BP
#### Uses
- Manage severe hypotension
	- [[Shock]] if norepi causes arrhythmias or if others don't work
- Mydriatic (pupil dilation)
- Nasal decongestant
- Priapism, acute ischemic 
- Preferred for maternal hypotension during C-section
#### Administration
- Give through central line
#### Dosing
- Acidosis can reduce effectiveness
- Significant interactions
#### Adverse Effects
- 
#### Contraindication
- Hypersensitivity
- Severe hypertension
- Bradycardia or heart block
- Severe coronary artery disease or heart failure
