**Mechanism**
1) Identify type / mechanism
	-  Is it bradycardia or tachycardia?
	- Is it supraventricular or ventricular?
	- Is it due to abnormal automaticity, triggered activity, or a reentry circuit?
2) Non-Pharmaceutical Approaches
	- Vagal maneuvers for SVT
	- Electrical cardioversion for unstable tachyarrhythmias
	- Pacemakers or ICDs for bradycardia or high-risk ventricular arrhythmias
	- Catheter ablation for reentrant rhythms like [[AVNRT]] or atrial flutter
### Categories
1) Class I
	- [[Class Ia Antiarrhythmics|Class Ia]]: [[Quinidine]], [[Procainamide]], [[Disopyramide]]
		- Moderate block Na+
		- ↑ AP duration
		- ↑ QT
	- [[Class Ib Antiarrhythmics]]: [[Lidocaine]], [[Mexiletine]]
		- Mild Na+ block
		- ↓ AP duration
	- [[Class Ic Antiarrhythmics]]: [[Flecainide]], [[Propafenone]]
		- Strong Na+ block
		- No change to AP duration
2) [[Class II antiarrhythmics]] -- [[Beta Blockers|Beta blocker]]
	- ↓ SA/AV node, ↑ PR interval
	- [[Metoprolol]], [[Esmolol]], [[Atenolol]]
3) [[Class III Antiarhythmics|Class III antiarrhythmics]]-- [[Potassium Channel Blockers]]
	- ↑ AP duration, ↑ QT interval
	- [[Amiodarone]],  [[Sotalol]], [[Dofetilide]]
4) Class IV -- [[Non-dihydropyridines|Non-DHPs]]
	- [[Verapamil]], [[Diltiazem]]
	- ↓ AV condution, ↑ QT interval
5) Other
	- [[Adenosine]] -- acute SVT
	- [[Digoxin]] -- rate control in heart failure with afib
	- [[Magnesium sulfate]]
### Goals
- Rhythm Control
- Rate Control
- Prevent arrhythmia recurrence
- Reduce risk of sudden cardiac death
- ↓ adverse effects
- ↑ quality of life
