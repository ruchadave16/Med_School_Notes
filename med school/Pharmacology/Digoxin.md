*Cardiac glycoside that inhibits Na+/K+ ATPase pump*
#### Action
- Inhibits Na+ / K+ ATPase pump -> ↑ Ca2+ intracellular 
- ↑ vagal tone -> slows AV conduction
#### Uses
- Treatment of [[Congestive Heart Failure|CHF]]
- [[Afib]] -- rate control
- [[Atrial flutter]] -- rate control
- [[SVT|Supraventricular tachycardia]] (fetal) off label
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- IV or oral
#### Adverse Effects
- Visual changes (halos)
- GI symptoms
- AV block
- Ventricular arrhythmias

Risk increased with renal dysfunction, electrolyte imbalance, drug interactions
#### Contraindication
- [[VFib]]
