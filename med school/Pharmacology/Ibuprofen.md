#### Action
- Nonselective COX1 and 2 inhibition
- ↓ PGE2 centrally and peripherally
#### Uses
- Sustained fever reduction
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
Only for > 6 months
10 mk/kg/dose
#### Adverse Effects
- GI irritation
- Dehydration

#### Contraindication
