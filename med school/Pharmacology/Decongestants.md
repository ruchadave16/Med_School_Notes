#### Action
- [[Sympathomimetic Drugs|Sympathomimetics]] for nasal congestion
- Work by being a1 agonists on smooth muscle in nose -> vasoconstriction
#### Classes
1) Topical Agents
	- [[Oxymetazoline]]
	- [[Phenylephrine]] (nasal)
	- [[Naphazoline]]

These are rapid and short lasting, can have rebound congestion

2) Systemic Agents
- [[Pseudoephedrine]]
- [[Phenylephrine]] (oral)
- Slower, longer lasting

Affects: BP ↑, Palpitations, Insomnia
