1) Crystalloids
	- Solution of small molecules
	- First line for volume resuscitation
2) Colloids
	- Larger molecules like [[albumin]] that increase oncotic pressure
	- Use for rapid plasma expansion, but more expensive

#### Distribution
- Normal saline, LR, Plasmalyte → ~25% stays intravascular; ~75% shifts to interstitial space
- 5% dextrose → distributes mostly intracellularly (not useful for resuscitation).
	- Use for [[hypoglycemia]]
- 3% NaCl for [[hyponatremia]] or ↑ [[ICP]]
- Albumin → stays primarily in the intravascular space (more volume-effective).
	- Cirrhosis with Spontaneous Bacterial Peritonitis
	- Large-volume paracentesis
	- [[Hypoalbuminemia]]
