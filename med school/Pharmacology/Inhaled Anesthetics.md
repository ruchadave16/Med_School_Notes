*Used for maintaining [[General Anesthetics|general anesthesia]]*

- Distribution depends 
	- Partial pressure gradients
	- Solubility (blood gas partition coefficient)
		- Nitrous oxide, desflurane have low
		- Halothane has high
	- Anesthetic concentration: Minimum Alveolar Concentration
	- Ventilation rate <- ↑ ventilation means more rapid induction
	- [[Cardiac output]] <- ↑ CO means higher uptake peripherally, slower rise in brain PP
- Elimination depends
	- Solubility
	- Duration of exposure
	- Metabolism / excretion
### Volatile Anesthetics
- Halothane
	- Greater hepatic metabolism -> Halothene-induced hepatitis
- Enflurane, isoflurane, desflurane, sevoflurane
- Most are fluorinated ethers
### Gaseous Anesthetics
- [[Nitrous oxide]]
