[[Muscarinic Antagonists|Muscarinic antagonist]] (quaternary)
- Acts only peripherally, no cross BBB
#### Action
- Asthma COPD bronchodilation (M3 agonist)
- Shorter lasting
#### Uses
- Acute [[asthma]] exacerbations
- Given with [[albuterol]] in emergency
#### Administration
- Slow onset (30-60 minutes)
- Duration = 6-8 hrs
#### Dosing

#### Adverse Effects
- [[Glaucoma]] if reaches eyes
#### Contraindication
