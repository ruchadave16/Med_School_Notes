#### Action
- Stop [[purine synthesis]]
- Incorporate 6-MP into DNA so strand damage
#### Uses
- Leukemia, lymphoma
- Immunosuppression
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- **Metabolized by xanthine oxidase**
	- ↓ dose if given with [[allopurinol]] and [[febuxostat]]

#### Adverse Effects
- Myelosuppression
- Hepatotoxicity
#### Contraindication
