**Goal**: Lowering LDL-C is the primary goal of lipid-lowering therapy

>[!Note] For every ~38 mg/dL (~1 mmol/L) reduction in LDL-C, the risk of major cardiovascular events drops by ~20% over 5 years.

1) Risk Assessment
	- https://professional.heart.org/en/guidelines-and-statements/prevent-calculator

2) Lifestyle Adjustments
	- Weight loss: Aim for 5–10% reduction in body weight 
	- Exercise: 40 minutes of moderate-intensity activity 3–4 times/week 
	- Cholesterol-lowering diet: Emphasize fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and healthy fats; limit saturated fats 
	- Increase soluble fiber: Improves LDL-C clearance
	- Avoid alcohol: Especially if triglycerides are elevated

3) Check a fasting lipid panel:
	- At baseline
	- 4–12 weeks after starting therapy or dose change
	- Every 3–12 months thereafter

>[!Note] 2026 guidelines: starting [[statins]] at age 30 for patients with LDL-C levels at or above 160 mg/dL, a strong family history of premature ASCVD, or a high 30-year risk of ASCVD—even if their 10-year risk is low

#### Medication Classes
1) Hepatic Cholesterol Synthesis Inhibition
	- [[Statins]] inhibit HMG CoA reductase
	- [[Bempedoic acid]] inhibits ATP-citrate lyase

2) [[PCSK9 Inhibitors]]
	- [[Inclisiran]] = siRNA therapy
	- [[Evolocumab]] and [[Alirocumab]]

3) Intestinal Cholesterol Absorption Blockade
	- [[Ezetimibe]]

4) Triglyceride Reduction via Lipid Metabolism Regulation
	- [[Fibrates]]
	
5) [[Bile Acid Resins]]

6) [[Niacin]]

7) [[Omega-3]]
