#### Action
- Inhibits late sodium current in ischemic cardiac myocytes
	- ↓ intracellular Na so ↓ Ca influx from Na⁺/Ca²⁺ exchanger
	- ↓ Ca = ↓ ventricular wall tension = ↓ myocardial oxygen demand
- Blocks potassium currents -> can prolong QT interval

**No impact on HR or BP**
#### Uses
- Chronic [[stable angina]] (add-on)
- Off-label = Stable ventricular arrhythmias (in select cases)

#### Adverse Effects
- QT prolongation
- Dizziness, headache
- [[Torsades de pointes]]

#### Contraindication
- [[Cirrhosis]]
- CYP3A inhibitors/inducers (ranolazine is metabolized by CYP3A)
- Prolonged QT interval
