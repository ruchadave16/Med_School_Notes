*Meds used to dissolve clots in life-threatening situations*
- [[tPA]]
- [[Reteplase]]
- [[Tenecteplase]]

![[Screenshot 2026-05-25 at 1.48.21 PM.png]]
#### Action
- Bind to [[fibrin]] in a clot and convert plasminogen -> [[plasmin]], which dissolves fibrin
- Breaks down structure of the clot
#### Uses
- **Emergency** when reperfusion can stop tissue death
	- [[STEMI]] if no PCI in 120 minutes
	- Massive [[Pulmonary Embolism|PE]] with hemodynamic instability
	- Acute ischemic stroke < 4.5 hrs
	- Catheter occlusions
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Narrow therapeutic window
#### Adverse Effects
- High risk of bleeding
- Orolingual angioedema
- Hypersensitivity rxn
- Cholesterol embolization syndrome
#### Contraindication
- Recent surgery
- Bleeding diathesis
- Uncontrolled hypertension
- [[ACE Inhibitors]]
- [[Antiplatelets]] can increase bleeding risk
