- [[Sodium Channel Blockers]]
- *Rarely used today, *
#### Effect
- Moderate Na⁺ channel blockade of fast sodium channels→ slows Phase 0 depolarization + prolongs repolarization
- Also blocks some K+ channels
#### Uses
- Supraventricular and ventricular arrhythmias (less common today)
#### Drugs
- [[Disopyramide]] --- Maintenance of sinus rhythm in atrial fibrillation (esp. vagally induced); [[hypertrophic cardiomyopathy]]
- [[Procainamide]] -- Acute atrial arrhythmias with preexcitation, VRT; stable VT
	- Remains useful in acute, preexcited AF or stable monomorphic VT.
	- **Lupus like syndrome**
	- [[Agranulocytosis]] and [[aplastic anemia]]
- [[Quinidine]] -- Conversion and maintenance of sinus rhythm in AF/flutter (rarely used today); ventricular arrhythmias
	- **Hepatotoxicity**
#### Effects
- All can cause QT elongation
- Proarrhythmia -- need ECG monitoring
- Anticholinergic effects
- Worsening EF
#### Contraindications
- Heart block (without a pacemaker)
- Long QT syndrome
- Severe structural heart disease
- Myasthenia gravis, glaucoma, BPH (for disopyramide—anticholinergic toxicity)
- SLE history (avoid procainamide)
- Significant electrolyte abnormalities (e.g., hypokalemia, hypomagnesemia)
- History of [[Torsades de Pointes]]
