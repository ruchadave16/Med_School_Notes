*Epoprostenol, Treprostinil, Iloprost*

**Epoprostenol IMPROVES SURVIVAL**
#### Action
- Stimulate IP receptors so ↑ cAMP 
	- Vasodilation and anti-proliferation
#### Uses
- Advanced [[Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension|PAH]]
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Epoprostenol = IV infusion
- Treprostinil = inhaled, IV, oral, SC
- Iloprost = inhaled
#### Adverse Effects
- Flushing
• Jaw pain
• Diarrhea
• Hypotension
#### Contraindication
