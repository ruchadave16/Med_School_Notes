#### Action
- Inhibits Niemann-Pick C1-like 1 (NPC1L1) protein in [[brush border]] of small intestine
	- ↓ cholesterol absorption
	- ↓ hepatic stores of cholesterol
	- Upregulation of LDL receptors so ↑ clearance
- No impact on [[triglycerides]]
#### Uses
- Second line after [[statins]]
- Preferred over niacin or fibrates when the goal is LDL reduction without raising TG or
causing flushing
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- **No** drug interactions

#### Adverse Effects
- Mild GI
- Hepatotoxicity rare (monitor LFTs if statins also used)
- Myalgias 
- Allergic reaction
#### Contraindication
