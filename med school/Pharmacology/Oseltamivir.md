#### Action
- HA binds to sialic acid to start infection, and NA cleaves this link to allow virions to be released
- This **INHIBITS NA**
	- Blocks virion from being released
#### Uses
- [[Influenza Virus|Influenza]] A and B
#### Administration

#### Dosing
Start within 48 hours of symptom onset for best efficacy
- Oral (oseltamivir)
	- Preferred in hospitalized
- Inhaled (zanamivir)
#### Adverse Effects
- Nausea, vomiting (oseltamivir)
- Bronchospasm (zanamivir)
#### Contraindication
