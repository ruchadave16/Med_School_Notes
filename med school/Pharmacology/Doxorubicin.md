#### Action
- Intercalates into DNA and inhibits [[topoisomerase II]], causing DNA strand breaks
- Makes free radicals contributing to cytotoxicity
#### Uses
- Solid tumors
- 
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Prevention of cardiotoxicity: Co-administration of [[dexrazoxane]], an iron chelator
#### Adverse Effects
- [[Dilated cardiomyopathy]] / [[congestive heart failure]]
- Myelosuppression
- Alopecia
#### Contraindication
