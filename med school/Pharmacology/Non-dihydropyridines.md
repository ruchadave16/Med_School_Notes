*Verapamil, diltiazem*
- Class IV [[Antiarrhythmics|antiarrhythmics]]
#### Action
- Cardiac [[Pacemaker Cells|pacemaker cell]] effect 
	- Blockage of L-type Ca channels
- ↓ HR, contractility, and ↓ AV conduction
- Class IV [[Antiarrhythmics]]
#### Uses
- Atrial arrhythmias (e.g., atrial fibrillation/flutter)
- Stable angina
- Vasospastic angina
- Hypertension 
- Verapamil:
	- Also used for [[Migraines|migraine]] prophylaxis, [[Cluster Headache|cluster headache]], ventricular arrhythmias

#### Dosing


#### Adverse Effects
- Bradycardia, AV block
- Constipation (especially with verapamil)
- Exacerbation of heart failure
- [[Hyperprolactinemia]] (verapamil)
#### Contraindication
- Bradycardia
- Hypotension
- [[WPW Syndrome]] and other pathway mediated tachycardias
- Heart block ([[AV block]])
- [[Sick sinus syndrome]]
- Cardiogenic shock
- [[HFrEF]] - **Avoid in combo with [[Beta Blockers|beta blocker]]**
- Recent MI
