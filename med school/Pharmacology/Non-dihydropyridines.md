*Verapamil, diltiazem*
#### Action
- Cardiac [[Pacemaker Cells|pacemaker cell]] effect 
	- Blockage of L-type Ca channels
- ↓ HR, contractility, and ↓ AV conduction
#### Uses
- Atrial arrhythmias (e.g., atrial fibrillation/flutter)
- Stable angina
- Hypertension 

#### Resistance

#### Dosing

#### Adverse Effects
- Bradycardia, AV block
- Constipation (especially with verapamil)
- Exacerbation of heart failure
- [[Hyperprolactinemia]] (verapamil)
#### Contraindication
- Bradycardia
- Heart block
- HFrEF
