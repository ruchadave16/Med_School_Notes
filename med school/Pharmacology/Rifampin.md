#### Action
- Binds DNA-dependent RNA polymerase
- Bactericidal extra and intracellular TB
#### Uses
- [[Tuberculosis|TB]] -- Sterilizing agent that stops rapidly dividing
- [[Meningitis]] since it has CSF penetration
#### Resistance
- Mutations in rpoB gene 
- NEVER USE ALONE
#### Dosing
- CYP450 interactions
#### Adverse Effects
- Orange body fluids
- Hepatotoxic
#### Contraindication
- Rifabutin if [[HIV]] since less interactionsp
