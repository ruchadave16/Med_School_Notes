*Alirocumab, Evolocumab*
#### Action
- Normally PCSK9 binds to LDL receptors and promotes degradation of them
- Inhibitors stop LDL receptor degradation so ↓ LDL dramatically
#### Uses
- Can lower LDL by **50–70%**, even when added to statins
- [[Familial Hypercholesterolemia]]
- Statin-intolerant patients
- Adjunct to maximally tolerated statin + [[ezetimibe]] therapy
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Subcutaneous injection (every 2 months or monthly)
#### Adverse Effects
- Myalgias (rare)
- Neurocognitive (very rare)
- Rare allergic rxn
#### Contraindication
- High cost
