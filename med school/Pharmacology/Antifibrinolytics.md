***Tranexamic acid**, Aminocaproic acid*
#### Action
- Stabilize blood clots by stopping fibrinolysis
- Binds to lysine site on plasminogen, preventing [[plasmin]] formation
- ↓ inflammation by ↓ complement activation
#### Uses
- moderate [[TBI]]
- Postpartum hemorrhage
- Perioperative bleeding
- Bleeding disorders like [[von Willebrand Disease]]
- Dental procedures for pts on anticoagulants
- [[Hereditary angioedema]]
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Within 3 hrs of bleeding onset
#### Adverse Effects
- Hypersensitivity
- [[Thrombosis]] related events
- Rare: vision changes and retinal occlusion
#### Contraindication
- Active clotting
- [[Subarachnoid hemorrhage]]
