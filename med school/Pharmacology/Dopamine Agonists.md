#### Mechanism
- Directly stimulate D2 receptor
- No enzymatic conversion
- Longer duration of action than levodopa
#### Uses
- Monotherapy in early [[Parkinson's Disease|Parkinson's]]
- Add w [[Levodopa]]/[[Carbidopa]] in late stage
#### Types
- Non-ergot
	- Pramipexole
	- Ropinirole
- Ergot (more side effects)
	- [[Bromocriptine]]
	- Pergolide
