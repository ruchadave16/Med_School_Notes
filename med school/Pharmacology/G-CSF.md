*Filgrastim, Pegfilgrastim*
#### Action
- Recombinant human G-CSF used to shorten periods of neutropenia
- ↑ [[Neutrophils|neutrophil]] production
- Mobilizes CD34+ progenitor cells from [[bone marrow]] -> peripheral blood for stem cell collection
#### Uses
- Patients after autologous stem cell transplantation
- Patients on high-dose chemotherapy
- Congenital neutropenia
- Aplastic anemia
- Myelodysplasia
#### Resistance

#### Dosing

#### Adverse Effects
- Bone pain (moderate to severe)
- Splenomegaly
#### Contraindication
- [[Sickle Cell Anemia]] (can cause severe vaso-occlusions)
