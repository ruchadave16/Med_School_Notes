*Common pain med used that work by inhibiting COX-1 and COX-2*

- Traditional NSAIDS inhibit both 
- COX-2 inhibitors can reduce inflammation while not having impact on other organs
	- Reduce GI toxicity but can INCREASE CV by disrupting PGI2 and TXA2 balance
	- Minimal impact on platelet aggregation and bleeding risk
	
**Class**
- Salicylates -- [[Aspirin]], diflunisal
- Acetic acid derivatives -- Indomethacin, [[ketorolac]], diclofenac 
- Propionic acid derivatives -- [[Ibuprofen]], naproxen, ketoprofen 
- Fenamates (fenamic acids) -- Meclofenamate 
- Oxicams (enolic acids) -- Piroxicam, meloxicam 
- Selective COX-2 inhibitors -- Celecoxib
#### Effects
- Anti-inflammatory -- Inhibition of prostaglandins
- Pain relief -- ↓ [[PGE2]] synthesis
	- In peripheral tissues, PGE2 increases nociceptive pain and lowers activation threshold so NSAIDs inhibit production
	- PGE2 also centrally increase excitability in dorsal root
	- Not as helpful for neurogenic pain
- Fever reduction -- ↓ [[PGE2]] synthesis
#### Negative Symptoms
- GI toxicity from inhibition of [[PGE2]] and [[PGI2]]
	- ↓ mucus secretion
	- ↓ bicarbonate secretion
	- ↓ gastric mucosal blood flow
- Nephrotoxicity because PGE2 and PGI2 maintain renal blood flow
	- ↓ prostaglandins → afferent arteriole vasoconstriction → ↓ renal blood flow → ↓ GFR
	- Can cause acute kidney injury
- Cardiovascular effects
- Bleeding risk from ↓ [[TXA2]]
- ↑ Thrombosis from COX-2 inhibitors since ↓ [[PGI2]]
- [[Hypertension]]
#### Uses
- Chronic inflammation
- Acute pain and injuries
- Fever 
- Dysmenorrhea
- Headache disorders
#### Limitations
- Do NOT change disease progression
- GI toxicity from impact to COX-1
	- **PPIs reduce risk, [[Misoprostol]] is PGE1 analog, and COX-2 selective don't have this risk**
- Renal effects
- CV risk for COX-2 specific
	- Avoid in pts with ↑ risk for CV
