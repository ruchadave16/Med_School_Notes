*Gemfibrozil, Fenofibrate*
#### Action
- Activation of PPAR$\alpha$ which is a nuclear regulator for [[lipid metabolism]]
	- ↑ LPL expression so ↑ [[triglyceride]] clearance
	- ↑ [[beta oxidation]] in liver so ↓ [[VLDL]]
	- ↑ HDL synthesis
#### Uses
- **First line** for severe [[hypertriglyceridemia]] (TG > 500 mg/dL)
- Mixed [[Hyperlipidemia|Dyslipidemia]] (use with [[statins]])
- Prevents [[pancreatitis]]

#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Gemfibrozil = shorter, more drug interactions
- Fenofibrate = longer acting, use with [[statins]]
#### Adverse Effects
- [[Rhabdomyolysis]] -- risk ↑ with [[statins]]
- [[Cholelithiasis]] -- ↓ cholesterol breakdown so ↑ biliary cholesterol saturation
- GI disturbance
- Rash 
- Liver enzyme ↑ -- **monitor LFTs**
#### Contraindication
- Don't combine with [[Bile Acid Resins|bile acid resins]] -- ↑ risk of gallstones
