*[[Furosemide]], Bumetanide*
#### Action
- Ascending limb (thick) 
- Block Na+/K+/2Cl- transporter
	- Increase Na+ and water excretion
#### Uses
- Volume overload 
- [[Acute Heart Failure|acute left heart failure]]
- ↓ peripheral edema or pulmonary congestion
- [[chronic heart failure]] management
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Furosemide 40–80 mg IV starting dose for acute heart failure
	- Torsemide might have higher impact

#### Adverse Effects
- Rare ototoxicity
- Hypokalemia, hypocalcemia, dehydration
#### Contraindication
