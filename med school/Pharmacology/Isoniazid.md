#### Action
- Prodrug
- Inhibits Fatty Acid Synthase II (FAS-II) complex -> Inhibits mycolic acid synthesis
#### Uses
- Active [[Tuberculosis|TB]]
#### Resistance
- Mutations in katG (prevents activation)
- Mutations in inhA gene (target enzyme)
#### Dosing
- Activated by the mycobacterial catalase-peroxidase enzyme KatG
- Give with [[Vitamin B6]] to prevent neuropathy
#### Adverse Effects
- Hepatotoxicity
	- Production of metabolites that are dangerous
	- Toxicity varies based on genetic acetylation
- Peripheral neuropathy  -- solve by supplementing with B6
- Drug-induced lupus-like syndrome
#### Contraindication
