#### Action
- Enters cell through passive diffusion
- Selective to anaerobic
- Ferrodoxin / flavodoxin reduces nitro group to radicals -> toxic metabolites that damage DNA
#### Uses
- Anaerobic bacteria
- Parasitic infections
	- Giardiasis, Amebiasis (intestinal & extraintestinal), Trichomoniasis
	- Amoebic liver abscess
#### Resistance
- Can use [[tinidazole]] in case of resistance
#### Dosing

#### Adverse Effects
- Disulfiram type reaction with alcohol
- Nausea, vomiting, headache, abdominal discomfort
#### Contraindication
