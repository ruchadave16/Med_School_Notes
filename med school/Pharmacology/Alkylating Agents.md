#### Mechanism
- Add alkyl group to DNA -> DNA cross-linking + strand breaks
- Cell cycle–**independent**
#### Side Effects
- Myelosuppression
- Alopecia
- GI toxicity
#### Agents
- [[Cyclophosphamide]]
- [[Ifosfamide]]
- [[Nitrosoureas]]
- Busulfan -- ablate bone marrow before [[stem cell transplant]]
- Procarbazine -- [[Hodgkin lymphoma]] and brain tumors
	- Side effect =  disulfiram-like reaction, [[MAO]] inhibition
