Sumatriptan, Rizatriptan, Zolmitriptan, Eletriptan
#### Action
- [[Serotonin]] (5-HT1B/1D agonists)

5-HT1D and 5-HT1F = presynaptic on trigeminal sensory
- Release calcitonin gene-related peptide ([[CGRP]]), Substance P, Neurokinin A
- ↓ inflammation and pain transmission

5-HT1B on cranial blood vessels
- Vasoconstriction
- Counters vasodilation of migraine
#### Uses

#### Resistance
- If one doesn't work try another triptan (not exclusive to one another)
#### Dosing
Provide at full dose
#### Adverse Effects
- Paresthesia
- Flushing
- Chest tightness
#### Contraindication
- [[Coronary Artery Disease]]
- [[Hypertension]] not controlled
- History of stroke or TIA
