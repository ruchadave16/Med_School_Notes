#### Action
- Competitively inhibits dihydrofolate reductase (DHFR) -> no regeneration of tetrahydrofolate
	- Stops [[purine synthesis]] and dTMP
#### Uses
- Leukemias, lymphomas, sarcomas
- [[Ectopic pregnancy]], autoimmune
#### Resistance
- ↓ transport into cells
- ↑ efflux transporters
- ↑ dihydrofolate reductase expression
#### Dosing

#### Adverse Effects
- ↓ bone marrow (reverse with [[leucovorin]])
- Hepatotoxicity
- Mucositis
- Pulmonary fibrosis
- Nephrotoxicity
#### Contraindication
