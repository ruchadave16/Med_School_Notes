#### Action
- Uncompetitive antagonist of [[NMDA Receptors]]
- Reduces Ca2+ influx 
- Binds to allosteric site inside ion channel but only when channel is open
- **Selective blockage** -- effecting in situations of glutamate excess
- Low affinity and rapid drop-off 
#### Uses
- [[Alzheimers Disease|Alzheimers]] but only moderate to severe
#### Resistance

#### Dosing
- Use with [[Cholinesterase Inhibitors|AchE inhibitor]]
#### Adverse Effects
- Headache
- Dizziness
#### Contraindication
